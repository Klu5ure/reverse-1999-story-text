The morning began with soothing silence,
punctuated only by a soft, cool breeze.
And soon after, the first rays of sunlight broke through in full,
announcing the first gasp of spring.
A young Daoist rests atop a leafy tree, feathered by the spring breeze and shaded by the leaves, slowly drifting toward sleep.
**Paper Heron**: Hm?
Faint voices from below rouse her.
**Paper Heron**: ...
She leans in to listen carefully.
**Paper Heron**: Master? Finch?
Though drowsy from her rest, she leans closer still, trying to make out what the voices are saying.
Only to lose her balance and fall into endless darkness.
*[image: Inn, Chun Town]*
The sky above is a dull, pale gray. The first lunar month has just ended, and despite the melting of the ice and snow, the air feels colder than it should.
The innkeeper slowly paces about the courtyard with her hands behind her back before deciding to rest in an empty chair.
**Innkeeper**: It's freezing! Why hasn't that boy thrown more wood on the fire? Hm? Where is that lout, anyway?
*bang*
**???**: Didn't anyone teach you that there are rules out here?
**???**: I don't care about your rules. I follow my own principles!
**Innkeeper**: Is it too much to ask for a single day where no one draws their weapon in my inn?
The innkeeper sighs and slips away from her barely warmed chair, moving deftly around the corridor to hide herself.
**???**: *cough* Madam ...
**Innkeeper**: Ah! I was wondering where you'd gone off to. Avoiding your work, no doubt!
**Waiter**: This is hardly my fault! Besides, it's not as though we have any real customers today.
**Innkeeper**: Shh, be quiet! They might hear you!
**Waiter**: Alright, alright! I'll just take a quick look to see what's happening. Oh my, that man has a knife in his hand, but the girl he's facing actually looks calm, relaxed even.
**Waiter**: Wait, that peculiar mule of hers seems familiar.
*clang*
The innkeeper pulls the curious waiter back down and glares at him.
**Innkeeper**: What are you doing?! Are you trying to get the both of us killed?!
**Innkeeper**: *groan* Please don't break my elm tables and chairs. I only bought them this winter. Why must they start a fight just as we're closing for the night?
A sudden yawn comes from a forgotten corner.
**???**: Closing time already?
The young Daoist in red lifts her head from her arms. A crease from her sleeve has left a faint mark on her cheek.
She stands up and brushes off her clothes but looks reluctant to leave.
**Daoist**: But I haven't finished my mung bean cakes.
**Innkeeper**: Forget about the cakes. You'd better find somewhere to hide. There's going to be a fight!

**Daoist**: Hmm.

**Daoist**: *yawn* So sleepy. This will take a while. Wake me when you close.
**Innkeeper**: What? This is no time for a nap!
The Daoist gives a casual wave of her hand, her head already resting on the table once more.
While the Daoist sleeps soundly, tensions run high near the gate.
**Girl with a Mule**: Even if they don't aspire to be a hero, a Daoist should never use their skills to cheat people!
**Man with a Knife**: Ha! Who spoke any words of heroes?
**Man with a Knife**: I won't let some irritating little bug lecture me! Come on, let's see what you're made of.
**Girl with a Mule**: It takes no strength to know right from wrong, and this is a wrong I cannot walk away from!
**Man with a Knife**: Big talk, little bug. Let's go!
**Man with a Knife**: Urgh!
The man, pushed back a step into the courtyard, nearly topples before steadying himself.
**Girl with a Mule**: Bug's waiting. Come on!
From his hiding spot, the waiter slaps a nearby wall in realization.
**Waiter**: I remember her! She was involved in that trouble at the pawnshop last month.
**Waiter**: Apparently, the pawnbroker Wáng was pressuring a customer to sell their family heirloom. That girl happened to pass by, so she intervened to try and reason with him. After exchanging only a few words, it escalated into a full-blown fight!
**Innkeeper**: Oh, that girl! She showed up just before the Dushuo Festival, if I'm not mistaken.
**Innkeeper**: I heard she was looking for the, the, what was it again, Qi flows?
**Waiter**: Whether she found it or not, she's certainly causing a world of trouble. Neither pawnbroker Wáng, talisman-seller Lǐ, nor the Head of the Armed Escort, Mr. Zhào, could stand against her.

**Innkeeper**: She's certainly bitten off more than she can chew this time.
**Innkeeper**: ...
**Innkeeper**: Oh no, they're coming this way! Come, let's get out of here!
**Innkeeper**: Um, you there, are you coming?
**Daoist**: Hmm.
The sleeping Daoist only shifts place in response, clearly more interested in staying where she is.
As the fight draws closer, the innkeeper lets out a weary sigh, grabs her bumbling waiter by the collar, and rushes into the kitchen.
**Man with a Knife**: *sigh* So, you've got a few tricks up your sleeve after all. Not bad.
**Girl with a Mule**: If you return the money you swindled and swear to clean up your act, I'll let you go!
**Man with a Knife**: Ha! And what makes you think I'll stay true to my word?
**Man with a Knife**: That money is long gone. And I wouldn't be so confident if I were you.

**Girl with a Mule**: Watch out!
Gritting her teeth, she pivots and throws her longsword, sending it flying through the air and knocking the knife aside. She breathes a subtle sigh of relief.
*clang*
The longsword clatters to the ground, the sound almost lost in the man's bellowing laughter. With another sneer, he draws a second blade and brings it down in a heavy slash.
**Daoist**: Now that's a little much, don't you think?
The blade stops mid-descent, its edge caught between a pair of fingers.
White Qi coats the Daoist's fingertips. She turns and smiles at the stunned girl.
**Daoist**: Apologies for interrupting. I'm sure you can handle him yourself, but I just hate it when people don't play fair.
**Daoist**: I should've—No, my cake!
She swiftly brings her index and middle fingers together, tracing a sigil in the air. At once, the blade-knocked cake sails back onto the table.
**Daoist**: Phew! That was close.
**Man with a Knife**: A Daoist?
The man looks her over and notices the white jade sword at her side.
**Man with a Knife**: So you had a powerful master waiting for me, did you?
**Daoist**: Powerful master? Who? Where?
**Man with a Knife**: You're nothing but a fraud! Despicable little bug!
**Girl with a Mule**: I know nothing of her!
**Daoist**: Uh, I really only came for the cakes. Trouble showed up, so I stepped in. Nothing more, nothing less.
**Daoist**: Now that you've stopped, perhaps we can talk this out like reasonable people.
The man lashes out with another strike, harmlessly cutting through the air above the Daoist's head.
**Daoist**: *sigh* Not a talker, I see.
**Daoist**: With   the   flow   of   Qi,   the   mind   opens   wide!
