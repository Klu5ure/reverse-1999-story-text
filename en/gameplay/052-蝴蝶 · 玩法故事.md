**Ulrich**: She is also exhibiting mild symptoms of Storm Syndrome. Fortunately, the observation point was far from the epicenter of the "Storm," though thankfully her distance from the epicenter greatly reduced its effects. She handled it well.
**Coalwall**: Fortunate indeed.
**Coalwall**: Perhaps that, too, was part of the design.
The ferrofluid does not grasp her meaning.
**Ulrich**: This kind of work is simply unsuitable to fragile humans.
**Coalwall**: Mr. Ulrich, I would not wish for even you, an Awakened, to violate protocol by returning to safety at the very last moment before the "Storm" falls.
**Coalwall**: All for a single set of data.
The ferrofluid rests placidly at the center of his helmet.
**Ulrich**: But there are certain dangerous tasks we have no choice but to undertake, and we Awakened are simply better equipped.
**Ulrich**: Let's hope Researcher Rachel wakes up soon. Otherwise, our natural physics department may be reduced to just Adler. One may as well just declare the whole thing defunct.
**Ulrich**: Now then, I'm off to read Lorentz Butterfly's latest paper.
**Ulrich**: She's calculated several coordinates where supernatural phenomena may occur, suggesting a connection to the core mechanism of the "Storm."
**Ulrich**: Though I really do wonder how these provocative letters always manage to bypass security and end up on my desk.
**Coalwall**: Supernatural phenomena. That is indeed a compelling direction.
Ulrich turns toward her in a sideways glance.
**Ulrich**: You surprise me, Coalwall; I thought you had little interest in exploring arcanum theory.
**Coalwall**: Ah, well, I've simply come to understand something.
**Coalwall**: Her true objective is surprisingly simple.
The words bounce off of his glass exterior without understanding.
**Ulrich**: Who do you mean? Lorentz Butterfly?
**Coalwall**: Forget it.
**Coalwall**: Mr. Ulrich, if I had no interest in arcanum theory whatsoever, I would never have joined Laplace.
**Coalwall**: After all, the "Storm" is unquestionably an arcane phenomenon.
**Coalwall**: But I don't believe science has become obsolete in the face of that fact. The rules of the universe have simply changed beyond recognition.
**Coalwall**: It's time for science to embark on a new path.
**Ulrich**: By integrating arcanum?
**Coalwall**: Exactly.
**Ulrich**: In that much we are agreed. At Laplace, most consider Institutum Lorentz an affront to science. I, however, remain undecided.
**Ulrich**: As Lorentz Butterfly wrote in her letter:
**Lorentz Butterfly**: **Only through pneuma can we understand Chaotic Energy. Only by monitoring the ebb and flow of Chaotic Energy can we "measure" the "Storm."**
**Ulrich**: I don't entirely understand her methodology, but it may yet reveal the truth of our world.
**Ulrich**: Hmm. I'm curious about this Lorentz Butterfly. What sort of person do you think she is?
Coalwall gazes at her sleeping companion beyond the glass in quiet contemplation.
**Coalwall**: A genius, one unconstrained by rules, norms, maybe even basic manners. But a genius—that much no one can deny.
