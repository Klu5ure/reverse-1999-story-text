**Marsha**: Adelheid, why are you out here alone? Where are the other members of our Order?

**Marsha**: ... Master Hermann was loyal to his oaths and to us. He must have been attempting to expose them.

**Adelheid**: We had been betrayed.
**Marsha**: ...
**Adelheid**: With the armistice coming, we withdrew from fighting to determine what to do next.

**Adelheid**: I think they understood the futility; maybe they even realized how foolish they were being. But when all else falls apart, people often turn to nostalgia and tradition. They faced into the bleak future and chose to believe in an idyllic past.

**Adelheid**: Centuries of wealth accumulated by our Order and all the rewards of its glory were gone.
**Adelheid**: Our treasures are doubtless being melted down to fuel their schemes.

She knows every room, every corridor, every pillar of that castle.
It was her home.
She imagines all those familiar things being torn down, stripped, sold for parts, and melted into scrap. A firestorm of anger and humiliation wells up inside her, but years of training kick in, and she calms herself with her arcane skill.
**Marsha**: ... I hope the castle itself will be restored.
The emotions fall away, and reason takes over; something clicks in her mind.
**Marsha**: Is that why you haven't left this place?
Adelheid's lips curve.
**Adelheid**: Ach so, then you have come to me on behalf of your Foundation.

**Marsha**: The houses here are all abandoned. Most left in a hurry; there should still be some things left behind.
**Marsha**: We can borrow this house for the night.

**Adelheid**: Has your Foundation given you leave to come along with me?
**Marsha**: I've reported back to my commander and to Mr. Razor. At any rate, my task was to convince you to leave Sen; I would say this is an interpretation of those orders.

**Marsha**: ... I could not bear to take another soldier's life.
**Marsha**: The path of knighthood should not be one of endless slaughter on the battlefield.
**Marsha**: If I returned to the Order, if I had stayed a knight. I would have been forced to fight, kill, and die.

**Adelheid**: I've dealt with this "Foundation" of yours before. I admit their intentions seem noble, and they have done some good. Yet, I also know they've been recruiting soldiers and amassing military strength.

**Marsha**: ... I agree entirely.
**Marsha**: But I have chosen to put my faith in them, in what my own eyes have seen of their work.

**Adelheid**: What you've done is without precedent in our Order.
**Marsha**: I understand.

**Marsha**: I never truly abandoned my vows as a knight.
**Marsha**: And worry for the sake of the Order ...

**Adelheid**: After I reclaim our treasured relics and return them, I will begin a search for all our scattered knights.

**Marsha**: Grand Master, may I share my thoughts with you?

**Marsha**: That way, our knights can be freed from further bloodshed and return to the Order's greater purpose.

**Adelheid**: ... That is not who we are, Sister.

**Marsha**: *sigh*
