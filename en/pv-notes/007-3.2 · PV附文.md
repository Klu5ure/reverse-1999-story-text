The cursed blood in your veins ...
has lured in the shadows.
And the fate you face ...
has arrived as it must.
Adèle, what were those things ...?
Damn—look outside!
What's happening here ... It can't be real.
You mean we're in some kind of dream? That all this is ... false?
Whatever it is, we'll soon find out!
By fire, what is false is purged.
*gasp* I remember!
Adèle, you must destroy it.
Célia! *pant*
... The French Blue? But how?
If Célia's in danger ... I have to find her!
Please guide me, Madame Bouanich.
Eight-hour workdays!
Better wages! Paid vacations!
I see her ... swallowed by the city.
If you go to her, the shadow of death will follow close behind.
...?!
Beryl Bouanich ...
You foresaw all this, didn't you?
The river of fate is beyond our control.
Yet there are those who attempt to steer its course.
Prove it to me,
Adèle.
To see where this river flows—
That is why I witness ...
your story.
