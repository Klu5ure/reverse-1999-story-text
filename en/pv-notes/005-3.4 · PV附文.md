## 01 · COL-203400 · 【3.4】不老春版本PV

Who are you? Why are you telling me this?
What do you know about the Method of Longevity?
And whose memory did I just see?
Take this!
I hear an immortal will appear at the fair to grant longevity.
Idle folk, Daoists, Yao ... All manner of people have come to town.
We may have taken human forms, but we live only a fraction of their lives.
Chief Cheng, the School of Mei will see justice done.
Then it was they who started this rumor ...
I founded this School so that we may gather strength in numbers.
With the sword and array at our disposal, the subjugation of the beast is a surety.
We humbly request your guidance!
What do you know of our struggles and desires?
The final hour approaches; the Array is ready!
Tonight, beneath the Mei tree, let us await longevity—together!
The path lies untraveled, but the end has begun!
Seek salvation in longevity!
Release me! Every moment is precious!
Heron, I've been thinking ...
Years pass by you in the blink of an eye, don't they?
If you want the sword, then kill me!
... While I—I won't even live a full cycle of the seasons ...
Do you still seek longevity, knowing all that might happen?
