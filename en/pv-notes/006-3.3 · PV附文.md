## 01 · COL-103300 · 【3.3】第十二章版本PV

They're holding position at the outskirts of the city. It's almost like ...
Like Igor is waiting for us there.
Blood pours across the steppe.
So much killing ... haven't you had enough?
You may hide from this battle, but what about the "Storm"?
The Cavalry Corps can't shelter you forever, Merel!
Many of our soldiers call this land their home; they're eager to see it after the "Storm."
You expect me to abandon this place?
But we don't belong to this era.
The Cavalry Corps cannot intervene in local conflicts!
You have two weeks to withdraw!
I can show you what Urd left behind, Timekeeper.
Why help us?
To atone for our sins ...
And to defend our homeland.
For that, I'll need your help too.
It isn't about the bloodshed.
But if killing Igor paves my way to "immortality," then why the hell not?
Timekeeper ... Timekeeper!
I know, the age of cavalry is over.
Tales of chivalry have come and gone.
But we can still fight.
For the steppe!
For the Don River!
To defend the ones we love—and the future.
