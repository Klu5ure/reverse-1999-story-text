## 01 · COL-203600 · 【3.6】人们向何处去PV

The age of humans has come to an end.

With the lost research on the "Storm" and the long absence of an LSCC director,

the Foundation is considering appointing a director of their own choosing ...

Focus on the matter at hand!

Adler already said that they're pressuring us for results!

I did hear the Foundation has been quite "lively" lately.

We just need to submit something that'll shut them up!

Not just something. The *Pre-Storm Protocol*. And soon.

But there are still some problems that haven't—

They're simulated data. Forget your "humanitarian care"!

Think about it clearly, Adler.

The pieces in the toybox are not objectively different from their real-world counterparts.

Is that the case this time as well?

This is a simulation of the "Storm." Everything will reset. Nothing will change.

If you've already concluded that this is meaningless, why did you return?

Time waits for no one, dear brother. I have to go eventually.

Wait, I also—

Greta—!

You cannot comprehend arcanum.

Thus, you cannot open this door.

None of us even knows what's in there, and you're saying you're going in alone?

There must be a theory that unifies everything in the universe.

Adler, just by observing it, you have become a new influencing factor on the core.

No matter what you see, don't approach it. We mustn't ...

Every puzzle has a solution.

And the world is just another crossword.
