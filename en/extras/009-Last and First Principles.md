## 01 · COL-203620 · 【3.6】人们向何处去13.2

**Enigma**: Zukunft.

The metal melts into a soft liquid, and the indecipherable symbols fall away.

He has solved it.

And his reward is an unbearable truth.

**Enigma**: Urgh!

**Enigma**: This is ...

He can no longer see the arcane glow, yet the burning behind his eyes returns all the same.

His vision blurs. His arms begin to tremble. Everything around him collapses inward, like a crushed cardboard box.

**Enigma**: These intense arcane fluctuations, I've seen them before.

**Enigma**: Damn it, first I need to—

The metal returns to its original form. The secret was revealed for only a moment, but that moment was enough.

The lock isn't there to keep knowledge in. It's there to protect those too ignorant to survive it.

**Enigma**: I must have seen something like this before, I ...

**Enigma**: I'm ... Yes. It was when they tried to read that incantation aloud—

The first circle.

The miraculous knot.

The thing you will remember till the day you die—

Enigma's fingers fly across the keys, pulling up files from the Laplace database.

**Enigma**: "... High concentrations of asymmetrical nuclide R were detected in certain locations."

**Enigma**: "Upon inspection, these concentrations were not correlated with any external factors."

**Enigma**: "These locations did not follow any particular distribution pattern, nor did they overlap with the results of our geological surveys."

**Enigma**: "Based on our current research, the arcane fluctuations at these locations are similar to those of an activated Hofmann Knot, both sharing the same tremor frequency ..."

**Enigma**: "Some posit this as a blessing of sorts, resulting from the worship of a supreme existence. For example, the School of Apeiron's belief in the Boundless One spared them from the effects of the 'Storm' ..."

**Enigma**: "And Ms. Amundsen's self-described worship of the Mother Spirit is most likely connected to the mutation she underwent ... All of these locations had records of relatively extended periods of deity worship ..."

**Enigma**: Hahahaha, tsk!

**Enigma**: "Supreme existence"? "Deity"?

**Enigma**: If there were a unifying theory to describe the entire universe, even these so-called gods would be scientifically explained!

Puppets manipulated by invisible strings.

Enigma leans forward, head bowed. He stares at the cold metal core in his hands.

Doubt and mockery harden into anger, which then instantly morphs into something stronger.

Curiosity.

**Enigma**: What a helpful little assistant you are.

*[image: In the Suitcase]*

**Lucy**: The news you brought back was very interesting, Simone.

**Assistant Simone**: Madam Z has always maintained a cooperative attitude toward Laplace. It should have come as no surprise to you.

The glowing bar of her "eye" flickers. This is the latest expression Madam Lucy has learned, roughly the equivalent of a human wink.

Data analysis has shown it can lighten the mood and convey warmth, making it particularly well-suited for conversations between friends.

**Lucy**: I did not expect Laplace would receive such unique research materials.

**Lucy**: The details contained in the core of that toybox have created a new basis for greater exploration and research into the "Storm." Perfecting the immunity ritual has become a more likely possibility.

**Lucy**: The report also mentions that researchers Adler and Medicine Pocket collaborated to develop an antidote for Picrasma extract.

**Lucy**: I did not use the wrong names, correct?

**Assistant Simone**: Correct. Your data loss has alleviated considerably.

**Assistant Simone**: Although a significant portion of data still remains irrecoverable with our current abilities, it's only a matter of time until we find a solution, Madam Lucy.

**Lucy**: Good. Then, I will apply for some even more complex research tasks when the time comes.

She nods seriously.

**Lucy**: I have finished composing a response to Researcher Adler's letter.

**Lucy**: First, the antidote is undoubtedly an invention with immense practical value, allowing Picrasma extract to be used to greater effect.

**Lucy**: However, their testing methods went against regulations. They have violated Articles 51 and 106 of the *Laplace Laboratory Code of Conduct*.

**Lucy**: While I congratulate Researcher Adler on his official appointment as director, I must also warn him that he will appear on this month's public notice board for these violations.

**Assistant Simone**: In other words, everyone will see his appointment and his breach of regulations announced side by side.

**Assistant Simone**: Based on my estimates, this has a 92% chance of occurring. Approaching certainty.

Simone smiles.

**Lucy**: Hmm. There is something else I do not understand.

**Lucy**: Does Laplace currently have a large surplus of paper? All this information could have been relayed via communication device.

**Assistant Simone**: It's generally considered that letters are a more formal form of notice.

**Assistant Simone**: Any other thoughts on his appointment? Would you like me to monitor Researcher Adler's performance during his first three months as director?

**Lucy**: There is no need to scrutinize this specific matter, Simone.

Another flicker of her "eye." She runs her fingers over the letter on the desk.

**Lucy**: I am still functioning. Thus, there are still people who seek answers to the world's unsolved mysteries, and those who work to uncover its unknown principles.

**Lucy**: We continue to walk the path of progress.
