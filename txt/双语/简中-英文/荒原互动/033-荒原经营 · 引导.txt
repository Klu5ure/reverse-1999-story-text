01  【2.2】荒原经营引导剧情01

Accompanied by the faint glow of an arcane skill, an ambitious guest has come to visit the world inside the suitcase.
伴随着神秘术的微弱辉光，一位野心勃勃的客人到访箱中的世界。

Eternity: Welcome, my esteemed guest.
温妮弗雷德：欢迎，我们的贵客。

Ms. NewBabel: Nice to meet you, Ms. Winnifred, Ms. Vertin. I have to say, this land is even more magical and imaginative than I'd expected.
新巴别塔：很高兴见到你们，温妮弗雷德女士，维尔汀小姐。这片土地比我预想的还要神奇，也更充满想象力。

Ms. NewBabel: Yes. The sunlight and air are quite ideal. The children are going to love it here.
新巴别塔：嗯……阳光和空气都很适宜，孩子们会喜欢这儿的。

Ms. NewBabel: Ahem, Loretta, sit down. Stop circling our guest.
新巴别塔：嗯哼～洛蕾塔——坐下，别绕着客人打转。

Ms. NewBabel: My apologies, Ms. Vertin.
新巴别塔：向您表示歉意，维尔汀小姐。

Vertin: It's alright. I think she's just curious.
维尔汀：没关系，我想它只是感到好奇。

Eternity: Ms. Vertin is also quite interested in your children, Ms. NewBabel.
温妮弗雷德：维尔汀小姐同样对你的孩子们颇有兴趣呢，新巴别塔女士。

Ms. NewBabel: Of course, of course. I'd be happy to introduce you to them. Which should we start with? Arnold, Heather, Pamela ...?
新巴别塔：当然，当然，我很乐意向二位做进一步的介绍。从哪一只孩子开始呢，阿诺德、希瑟、帕梅拉……

A figure in green suddenly pounces in from outside the hall.
一个绿色的身影忽然从厅堂外扑了进来。

Sotheby: You little rascal, I knew you were hiding here!
苏芙比：小坏蛋，我知道你肯定是藏在这里——

Ms. NewBabel: Hm? Hello, little lady.
新巴别塔：嗯……？你好，年轻的小淑女～

Sotheby: Ah! I'm ever so sorry, ladies. I didn't know you were having a tea party.
苏芙比：啊！抱歉，女士们，我不知道你们在开茶话会……

She hastily curtsies to them and turns tail before finally remembering her current mission.
小淑女慌忙提起裙摆，朝众人鞠了一躬。但在转身离开前，她终于想起了自己当下的使命。

Sotheby: Oh right! Ladies, have you seen a strange critter at all?
苏芙比：对了，女士们，你们见到过一只很奇怪的魔精朋友吗？

Ms. NewBabel: Heh heh. I think I know who you're looking for.
新巴别塔：呵呵……我想我知道你在找的是哪个孩子了。

Out in the Wilderness, lively, sticky, fluffy, and scratchy critters are creating a commotion.
荒原上，活泼的、粘稠的、毛绒绒与刺挠的魔精们闹作一团。

Sotheby: Huh? There weren't this many critters here a moment ago! Ah! Bad friends! That's Mr. Rabies's straw!
苏芙比：咦，刚才这儿可还没有这么多魔精呀！——啊！坏朋友们，快放下爱宠先生的稻草！

Desert Flannel: This wasn't in the job description. See, this is why I said we should be promoting electronic pets!
沙丝绒：我领的薪水里可不包括这种活儿……看，这就是为什么我说应该推广电子宠物！

Desert Flannel props up the shrubs and saplings that have been knocked down by the critters.
沙丝绒将被撞倒的灌木和树苗扶了起来。

Medicine Pocket: Aghh! Damn critters! You think you're a match for my teeth?!
兔毛手袋：嗷！这些该死的家伙！你们是在向我的牙口挑衅吗？！

Eternity: Ms. NewBabel, were your children always this ... fond of exercise?
温妮弗雷德：新巴别塔女士，你的孩子们原本就这么……热爱运动吗？

Ms. NewBabel: Not usually. They're lively, but they know how to keep it under control. I wonder what's going on ...
新巴别塔：并非如此，它们平日里虽然活泼，但会小心地控制好尺度。是什么影响了它们……

The clock tower chimes. Ms. NewBabel looks up as a gentle rain of fresh Dust falls from it.
新一刻的钟声敲响，新巴别塔抬起头来，钟楼缓缓洒下新的微尘雨。

The Cruzan family's arcane skill is remarkably effective.
来自克鲁山德家的神秘术效果显著。

The critter's wild partying abruptly stops as Ms. NewBabel starts chanting, finally giving the unwilling guests a moment to catch their breaths.
魔精们的疯狂派对在新巴别塔的吟诵声中骤然停止，被迫出席的宾客们也终于得到了喘息之机。

新巴别塔：但请允许我为自己辩解一番，维尔汀小姐。这并非是件全然的坏事，至少，这其中藏着一个“商业机会”。

Vertin: Business opportunity?
维尔汀：……商业机会？

新巴别塔：您打算如何处置这些捣蛋鬼？

Vertin: I'm not sure yet. I think I'll have to apply for some resettlement funds from the Foundation first.
维尔汀：暂时还没有打算。我想，我得先向基金会申请一笔安置经费。

Ms. NewBabel: No need to go through all that trouble, dear.
新巴别塔：不必那么麻烦，维尔汀小姐。

Ms. NewBabel: They aren't hopeless destroyers; they just need some proper guidance. I can influence their behavior patterns with my arcane skill and boost a few production methods, too.
新巴别塔：它们并不是些无可救药的破坏分子，只是需要一些恰当的引导。我可以用我的神秘术影响它们的行为模式，以及可能的生产方式……

Vertin: Guidance?
维尔汀：……引导？

Ms. NewBabel: Exactly. We can teach them to channel their pent-up energy into work, engaging in production, or cultivating sections of the Wilderness.
新巴别塔：我们能够教导它们工作，从事生产或是开垦新的荒原，以消耗它们过于旺盛的精力。

Ms. NewBabel: See this as my first gift to you—a mutually beneficial solution, Ms. Vertin.
新巴别塔：这是我送给您的第一份礼物——一个互惠互利的解决方案，维尔汀小姐。

Ms. NewBabel: Let's move somewhere quieter to discuss the details.
新巴别塔：让我们到更安静的地方去好好地聊一聊吧。

Ms. NewBabel: Critters are very useful creatures. They can help us produce all kinds of things that will benefit all kinds of people.
新巴别塔：我们的魔精能够协助我们生产出许多东西，它们能让更多人受益。

Ms. NewBabel: They're not terrifying monsters—they're friends in a different form.
新巴别塔：它们不再是令人恐慌的怪物，而是我们另一种模样的朋友。

Ms. NewBabel: Just think. If we do this, people from all over the world will come to understand critters, rather than hate them.
新巴别塔：人们能因此对魔精产生更准确的认知，而非敌视与憎恶——

Ms. NewBabel: Eventually, they could even join thousands of households, just like any ordinary pet.
新巴别塔：它们甚至能像普通宠物一般，走入千家万户。

Vertin: It sounds like a feasible plan.
维尔汀：听上去是个可行的方案。

Ms. NewBabel: I'm glad you think so, dear. I believe we can start a test run here, on our patch of the Wilderness.
新巴别塔：很高兴您能这么想，维尔汀小姐。我想，我们可以先在这儿，在我们的荒原进行一次试点运行。

Vertin: We'll need to come up with a name for this concept.
维尔汀：现在我们也需要为这个创想拟定一个名字了。

Vertin: Let's use your current company name, Ms. NewBabel's Critter Rehabilitation Center.
维尔汀：就用你现在公司的名字吧，“新巴别塔”魔精公司。

Ms. NewBabel bows slightly, and the critter at her feet gives a soft whimper.
新巴别塔微微欠身，脚边的魔精轻声呜咽。

Ms. NewBabel: I won't forget your kindness, dear.
新巴别塔：我不会忘记您的善意的，亲爱的。

The businesswoman straightens up, claps her hands, and gives a confident smile.
女商人直起身来，拍了拍手，露出一个胸有成竹的微笑。

Ms. NewBabel: Now, let's get down to business. We have a company to run.
新巴别塔：现在，让我们忙起来吧——我们可是有家公司需要运作呢。
