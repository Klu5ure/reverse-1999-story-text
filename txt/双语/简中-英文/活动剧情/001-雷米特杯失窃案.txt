"HEART OF THE OCEAN"
COLLECTION OF SUSPICIOUS INFORMATION
“海洋之心”
                   可疑信息征集专栏

At that time I was groping for my glasses on the floor. When I look up, I saw it. The ominous, fleeting shadow. I also found an evidence of its appearance on the floor: a dark animal hair, different to humans'. Undoubtedly, our thief is a black animal, or some kind of a flying dragon. I've read about them from the books, they like shiny objects, like jewels or nicely decorated glasses.
                        —A friendly tourist who has high myopia


According to my theory, the
thief is an adult male who
has very poor manners. He
has a bewildered black beard
which falls out easily. He has
strong hands though, strong
enough to knock over a robot.
                         —An awkward detective trying a bit too hard to convince his listeners

As it turns out, the security robots are far from reliable. These tin heads can never replace us. They are clumsy, and use up a lot of oil, which is hardly environmentally friendly, isn't it? The big men in the parliament should really look into this company and see if there is anything fishy. That's why we poor securities voted for them ...
                          —An aggrieved security guard (former)
当时我正在寻找落在地上的眼镜，抬头的时候就看见了那团可怕的、一晃而过的黑影。我捡到了重要证据：一根黑色的毛发。它的触感十分特别，绝对不属于人类。毫无疑问，行窃者是一头黑色的野兽，或是某种
传说中的飞龙。我在书上看到过，它们大都喜欢亮晶
晶的东西，像是宝石、漂亮眼镜之类的。
                                     ——高度近视的游客朋友


据我推测，行窃者是一位举
止粗鲁的成年男性，留着一
把缺乏修剪的、易于脱落的
黑胡子，拥有一双有力的手，
甚至能打昏铁皮机器的脑袋。         
          ——顽固的蹩脚侦探

事实证明，安保机器人缺乏可靠性，这些铁皮脑袋永
远无法替代我们的存在。它们既不够灵活，还会消耗
大量的燃油，老实说，这对环境可不太友好。议员老
爷们应该重点关注这家公司的资质，至少为了那些可
怜的安保员们的选票……               
                              ——愤愤不平的安保员（曾经）

Working Principles:

It is known that sound is produced by vibration and propagates in the form of waves, and different sounds have different frequencies of vibration, so certain sounds can produce fixed waves under certain conditions, thereby representing certain patterns.

Operating Steps:

First, place the square metal sheet, hereinafter referred to as the plate, on a stable and well-fixed smooth solid surface.

Second, the sound element is made to contact the plate and emit sound at a specific frequency.
