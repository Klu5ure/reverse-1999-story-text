01  白屋 / The Cage

Cheng's House, Ancient Zhu County
古祝县·程宅

The cold sky presses down on black tiles. Black tiles press down on white walls.
冷澈的天压着黑色的瓦，黑瓦压着白色的墙。

At the foot of these walls is a courtyard. Though swept clean that morning, a light dusting of snow has already begun to reclaim it.
墙下是清晨新扫过的院子。不过几个时辰，上面便又积了薄薄一层雪。

Chéng Héguāng carries firewood into the house and lights the stove.
程和光抱着木柴进屋，给炉灶生了火。

Cheng Heguang: Copying documents at the Yamen still earns me around 30 wen a day.
程和光：在衙门抄书，折下来一天仍是三十文左右。

He takes the ledger and inkstone down from a nearby shelf, dips his brush in ink, and carefully records the day's income and expenses.
他从架子上取下账本与砚台，取笔蘸墨，细细记录今日的收支。

Cheng Heguang: One bass sold for seven wen.
程和光：售卖鲈鱼一条，赚得七文。

Cheng Heguang: Expenses: leaf celery, four wen. One jin of salt, five wen. Medicinal herbs, ten wen.
程和光：支出：芹菜四文，盐一斤五文，药材十文。

Cheng Heguang: Hm?
程和光：嗯……

Someone steps lightly behind him and drapes a coat over his shoulders.
有人轻步走到程和光身后，给他披上一件衣裳。

Mother: Back already? *cough* My, every winter feels colder than the last.
母亲：回来了？咳……冬天一年比一年冷了。

Cheng Heguang: You should be in bed. I only went to see Doctor Wú for some Baibu; he said you should be resting.
程和光：您不该起来的。我刚去吴郎中那里抓了些百部，他之前也说静养——

Mother: Alright, alright. But why did you go to him again? Don't you usually go to Jishitang?
母亲：好了，你怎么又去他那里？平时不是去济世堂么？

Cheng Heguang: I wanted a new prescription. I heard he'd just made a new batch of Dazao pills.
程和光：我想请他开副新方子——我听说他又新制了些大造丸。

Mother: Did you get some?
母亲：拿到了吗？

Cheng Heguang: ...
程和光：……

Mother: *sigh* I told you not to go there! In fact, I said we should've cut ties with him long ago.
母亲：唉，我早不许你去！早说要断绝来往。

Mother: Started up with his usual nonsense, did he? Refusing to let you inside, using some "illness" as an excuse? *cough, cough*
母亲：他是不是又对你说些浑话，不许你进门，说是因为病气……咳咳。

Chéng pauses for a moment and closes the ledger.
程和光顿了一下，合起账本。

Cheng Heguang: It's no matter. I have the medicine. I'll start brewing it now.
程和光：无妨。药买回来了，我这便熬药。

Mother: What did he say?
母亲：他说了什么？

Cheng Heguang: ...
程和光：……

At the doorway, the branches of a Mei tree shake off a few clumps of snow.
门口的梅枝抖落几抔雪。

Thankfully for him, a sudden knock at the door interrupts the awkward silence.
一阵叩门声适时地打断了尴尬的沉默。

Mother: Expecting someone?
母亲：有客人？

Mother: We haven't had a visitor since your uncle returned to the countryside.
母亲：自你二叔搬回乡下，家里好久没来人了。

Chéng puts the ledger back on the shelf.
程和光将账本放回架上。

Cheng Heguang: I'll take a look.
程和光：我先去看看。

Chéng trudges back through the snow to the courtyard gate and raises the latch.
程和光踏雪走回院门，抬起门闩。

A familiar military officer stands outside.
门外站着一位熟悉的武官。

Cheng Heguang: Commander Lì?
程和光：厉守备？

Li Huifeng: Hmm. Héguāng, I hope I'm not intruding.
厉晦锋：嗯。和光，没打扰到你吧？

Cheng Heguang: Not at all. I just returned from the clinic and was preparing to brew some medicine.
程和光：我刚从医馆回来，正打算熬药。

Li Huifeng: How is your mother these days?
厉晦锋：……令堂的病情如何了？

Cheng Heguang: Consumption is not easy to treat.
程和光：痨病难治。

Cheng Heguang: Are you here on official Yamen business?
程和光：您找我，是衙门有事？

Lì Huìfēng offers a faint, amiable smile.
厉晦锋对他露出了一个可称和蔼的笑容。

Li Huifeng: The Yamen's affairs have nothing to do with me.
厉晦锋：衙门的事不归我管。

Li Huifeng: I'm here to offer you an opportunity.
厉晦锋：我这次来，是为了给你一个机会。

Cheng Heguang: ...!
程和光：……！

Chéng returns indoors, clutching a yellowed sheet of paper.
程和光攥着一张泛黄的纸返回屋内。

The lid of the pot on the stove has already been lifted, the cooked rice porridge inside sending steady curls of steam into the air.
炉灶上的锅盖已被掀开，熬好的米粥正冒出腾腾的热气。

Out of habit, he takes the ladle from his mother's hand.
他习惯性地从母亲手里接走了汤勺。

Cheng Heguang: Let me take care of this.
程和光：这些事我来做就好。

Mother: Who was it at the door, then?
母亲：刚刚是谁？

Cheng Heguang: It was Commander Lì.
程和光：是厉守备。

Chéng brings the porridge to the wooden table and casually passes the paper to his mother.
程和光把粥端上木桌，顺手把那张纸递给母亲。

Cheng Heguang: Tomorrow, I'll ask if Auntie can come lend her hand for a while.
程和光：……我明日去找二姨，拜托她来照顾一阵。

Cheng Heguang: It will be a long journey.
程和光：我要出趟远门。

A few sparrows flit past the window, chirping as they flutter out of the courtyard.
几只麻雀掠过窗外，叽叽喳喳飞出了院子。

Street, Riverside Village
水乡·街道

This small village, governed by another county of Huanzhou Prefecture and nestled between mountains and rivers, has simply come to be called the Riverside Village.
程和光：这是涣州府别县治下的一个小村落，依山傍水，故称水乡。

This Riverside Village looks much like Zhu County. At its entrance, Chéng Héguāng sees the familiar rise and fall of black tiles once again.
程和光：水乡的风貌与祝县相似。程和光走到村口，又一次看见了黑瓦所勾勒的起伏轮廓。

Only, today's early afternoon is not quite as peaceful as usual.
程和光：只是今日的晌午不如往日般宁静。

Lead Villager: My brother's been mauled by a tiger! He's damn near on his deathbed! And you dogs have the gall to stand here, pushing us around?!
乡民首领：我兄弟被大虫咬伤，命悬一线！你们这些走狗还在这推三阻四！

Cheng Heguang: ...?
程和光：……？

To the left of the village entrance stands an exceptionally grand courtyard, with a memorial Paifang outside bearing the words "Loyalty, Filial Piety, Integrity, and Righteousness."
村口左侧是一处华贵非常的院落，门外还竖立着“忠孝节义”的牌坊。

A group of villagers have gathered beneath the Paifang, their faces flushed as they shout at the household guards blocking their way.
牌坊下已经聚了些面红耳赤的乡民，正对着拦阻的家丁叫嚷。

Cheng Heguang: Likely the residence of a local gentry.
程和光：……应是本地士绅的房子。

Chéng approaches without a word, slipping in among several bystanders.
程和光沉默靠近，混入周围几个看热闹的闲汉中。

Stern Guard: Doctor Xiù was paid for with the family's hard-earned silver! She's inside treating Master Hú. How dare you try to barge in!
严肃的家丁：秀郎中是我家真金白银请来的！她正在里面给朝奉开方把脉，岂能容得你们乱闯！

Lead Villager: Doctor Xiù is the village physician, not the Hú family's private attendant!
乡民首领：秀郎中是咱们水乡的医生，又不是你胡家自己的！

Lead Villager: What could be more urgent than a life-or-death situation?! What right do you have to stop us? What are you hiding in there?
乡民首领：什么病比人命还紧急！你凭什么拦着我们？你家有什么不能见人的宝贝吗？

Lead Villager: Tell Doctor Xiù there's a patient out here on the verge of death! I know she'll come out to save him!
乡民首领：你进去和秀郎中说，这有个快死了的病人——她一定会先出来救人！

Stern Guard: You'll just have to find another doctor. If you set off now for the county, you can make it there before nightfall.
严肃的家丁：你就非要看这一个大夫不成？不如早早动身去县里，还来得及天黑前进城。

Lead Villager: We already hauled him all the way down the mountain to Doctor Xiù's clinic, only to be told she was here!
乡民首领：我们下山就去了秀郎中的医馆。伙计说她在你们家，我们便一路颠簸走到这——

Lead Villager: Just look at him! Does he look as though he'll survive a trip to the county?!
乡民首领：你看他，还像是能挨到县里的样子吗？！

The injured villager is slumped over his companion's back, his hastily bandaged left arm soaked with blood.
受伤的乡民伏在同伴背上，草草包扎的左臂已是血肉模糊。

Stern Guard: That's none of our concern. We had no hand in his injuries.
严肃的家丁：这些我不管，又不是我们伤了他。

Stern Guard: If you continue causing a scene outside Master Hú's house, we'll call the bailiff over to have you arrested!
严肃的家丁：你们再这样围着胡朝奉的院子生事，我们就要叫保长来抓人了！

The lead villager explodes in fury.
为首的乡民怒喝一声。

Lead Villager: Go on, call him! You're all in cahoots with one another anyway!
乡民首领：叫吧，叫吧！你们原本就是一伙的！

Lead Villager: Hú Hǎiyuè is so afraid of dying. He summons a doctor every time he nicks his skin.
乡民首领：胡海岳怕死，手划破一道口子也要请郎中开药——

Stern Guard: Watch your mouth, you slanderer!
严肃的家丁：你别血口喷人！

Lead Villager: Our man's been mauled half to death, and you won't even let him through the door!
乡民首领：咱们的人被咬成重伤，连门槛都迈不进去！

Calm Villager: They waited until land prices were low and took two acres of my land in exchange for just one tael of silver.
冷静的乡民：他家趁着田价贱，用一两银子就买了我两亩地……

Disheartened Villager: Last year, I had a funeral to arrange and was desperate for money. The Hú family loaned it to me but charged five percent interest.
沮丧的乡民：去年我因为丧事，急需用钱。胡家放债，最后竟然要收我五分的利息……

The lead villager snatches a bow from his companion's hands and aims it at the household guards. A wave of shock ripples through them.
乡民首领夺过同伴手里的弓箭，竟对着家丁拉开了弓。

Lead Villager: The Hú family's out to ruin us all! First they snatch up our fields, then they hound us with debt! They're driving us to death!
乡民首领：胡家就是故意欺负我们！不是买田就是追债，不想让我们活了！

Lead Villager: Fellow villagers, tell me, does this sound fair to you?!
乡民首领：乡亲们，你们说这公平吗！

Villagers: No!
乡民们：不公平！

The rest of the villagers shout, their hoes and clubs raised high.
其余乡民愤怒地应和，举起手中的锄头和棍棒。

Lead Villager: We're done waiting! We're not taking it anymore!
乡民首领：我们等不了！我们不忍了！

Lead Villager: One way or another, I am going through that door!
乡民首领：我今天非要进门不可！

Villagers: We'll not be pushed around! Burn down the gate! We'll force our way in!
乡民们：咱们也不是好欺负的，把他家大门烧了！闯进去！

The household guards draw their swords, raising their shields into a solid wall.
家丁们抽出腰刀，举起盾牌，组成一堵密不透风的人墙。

Stern Guard: Go ahead and try! This is nothing short of rebellion.
严肃的家丁：那你们就试试！简直是要造反——

Before the words even finish leaving the guard's mouth, an arrow flies toward his forehead.
话音刚落，一支羽箭已经向着家丁的眉心飞来。

But before it can strike, it is cleaved in two by a flash of steel.
——只是未及射中，便被刀光劈为两半。

Chéng sheaths his blade, stepping between the guards and the villagers.
程和光：程和光收刀入鞘，挡在家丁与乡民中间。

Cheng Heguang: This man is dying! What harm is there in letting him inside?
程和光：人命关天，放他们进去又有何不可？

Stern Guard: Who are you?!
严肃的家丁：你是谁？！

Calm Villager: He's not one of us.
冷静的乡民：不是我们的人……

The crowd exchanges glances like a theater troupe interrupted mid-performance, unsure of what to do next.
人们面面相觑，像是被打断了演出的戏班，不知如何是好。

Stern Guard: You—
严肃的家丁：你——

The gate is suddenly thrown open.
紧闭的大门被人猛然推开。

???: What is going on out there?!
？？？：外面在吵什么！

02  访虎 / The Tiger

Calm, unhurried footsteps emerge from the courtyard.
从院子里踏出来的步伐不慌不忙，平稳轻盈。

Cheng Heguang: Seems they've had martial arts training.
程和光：应该是习武之人。

The guards hastily make way, their voices softening with deference.
家丁慌忙往旁边避让，语气也恭敬起来。

Stern Guard: D-Doctor Xiù, have you finished examining Master Hú?
严肃的家丁：秀、秀郎中？您瞧完朝奉的病了？

Doctor Xiu: You mean his night blindness? Exactly how much time do you expect me to waste on that man?
秀郎中：一点夜里看不清东西的小毛病，还要多久来治？

Doctor Xiu: And you, out here making a racket and swinging your weapons about while I'm trying to write a prescription!
秀郎中：倒是你们——我正写着方子，却在这吵吵嚷嚷，动刀动枪！

Doctor Xiu: Care to explain yourselves? Hm?
秀郎中：……嗯？

She takes the steps two at a time, passing Chéng without so much as a glance.
她停住话头，径自走下台阶，从程和光的身旁穿过。

The villagers all lower their weapons, allowing her to approach the gravely injured man. She narrows her eyes as she examines him.
乡民们放下武器，任她走到伤者面前眯眼观察。

Doctor Xiu: How did this happen?
秀郎中：怎么伤的？

Disheartened Villager: We were hunting in the mountains and ran into a tiger. My brother, he fell behind and—
沮丧的乡民：我们进山打猎，遇上了老虎。我兄弟落在最后——

Before he can finish, Doctor Xiù turns and strides back toward the gate.
秀郎中不待听完，便疾步走回大门。

Doctor Xiu: Get him in here!
秀郎中：马上抬进来！

Stern Guard: But Doctor Xiù! He's covered in blood! How could we possibly carry him into Master Hú's courtyard?!
严肃的家丁：秀郎中！这人浑身是血，怎么能抬进朝奉的院里？！

Doctor Xiu: It's bad luck for someone to die at the gate, is it not? I'll ask Master Hú for a room myself!
秀郎中：在大门口死了人不是更晦气？我自去让你家朝奉给我分间房！

She pauses, then turns back impatiently.
她顿了口气，不耐烦地转身催促。

Doctor Xiu: Well? Unless you wish for this man to die, I suggest you bring him in!
秀郎中：还不抬进来，等死吗？

Lead Villager: Ah, r-right.
乡民首领：啊，是、是。

Lead Villager: Quickly, let's get him inside!
乡民首领：快把他背进去！

Two villagers rush the wounded man inside, streaking the entrance steps with blood as they ascend.
两个乡民匆忙把伤者背入院内，在门口的台阶上留下一路血痕。

Cowardly Guard: Should we wipe it up? If it dries, it'll be harder to clean.
怯懦的家丁：要擦吗？等下干了就不好擦了……

Stern Guard: Of course, you dolt! Unless you're looking for a scolding, you'll wipe it this instant!
严肃的家丁：擦啊！擦啊！不擦等着挨骂吗？

The guards fetch water from the courtyard and begin hurriedly scrubbing Master Hú's steps.
飞扬跋扈的家丁们从院内打了水，忙着擦拭胡朝奉的台阶。

Lead Villager: Hmph. Despicable!
乡民首领：哼……小人。

Cheng Heguang: All's well that ends well, it seems.
程和光：应该无事了。

Chéng is just about to leave when the lead villager steps in to block his path.
城中居民Ⅲ：程和光正待离开，却被乡民首领拦住了去路。

Lead Villager: Wait.
乡民首领：等等。

Lead Villager: Who are you? Why did you cut down my arrow?
乡民首领：你是谁？为何要拦下我的箭？

Cheng Heguang: Only a passing traveler. Your arrow would never have pierced that guard's rattan shield, but it would have given them a reason to retaliate.
程和光：我只是个路过此地的旅人。你的弓箭劣质，无法射穿藤牌，反而给了对方回击的理由。

Cheng Heguang: Your recklessness will cause nothing but trouble.
程和光：激动挑衅，并非上策。

Lead Villager: I may have lost my temper, but I've put up with them and their shoddy treatment for far too long to care about the consequences! If they fight, then we fight!
乡民首领：我那也是一时心急！要打就打，忍他们许久，谁管得了那么多！

Cheng Heguang: And if you'd made the first move, you'd have been deemed the primary culprit. Were someone to die, your punishment would be death.
程和光：你先动手，便是殴斗的主犯。如果出了人命，主犯可是要判斩刑。

Lead Villager: And what of it?! You think I fear death?! Hah!
乡民首领：那又如何！不过就是死，我难道怕吗！

Lead Villager: The way you speak—it's obvious you come from noble stock. How could someone like you understand our struggles?
乡民首领：你这人说话文绉绉的……想必也是官宦子弟，怎么会懂我们的苦？

The disjointed back-and-forth frays Chéng's patience.
错位榫卯般的对话让程和光有些烦躁。

Cheng Heguang: I'm not—
程和光：我并非——

During their exchange, several village guards rush over, stirring up a cloud of yellow dust under the Paifang.
谈话间，几位保丁疾奔而来，在牌坊下扬起一阵土黄色的烟尘。

Headman: What's going on here?!
保长：出了什么事！

Master Hú's personal guards hurry forward to greet them, directing their accusations at the villagers sitting cross-legged by the gate.
家丁们连忙上前招呼，指责在门外盘腿而坐的乡民。

Stern Guard: It's these troublemakers! They've dirtied Master Hú's steps!
严肃的家丁：就是这些人闹事！弄脏了朝奉的台阶。

Calm Villager: We didn't mean to cause trouble. Someone was mauled by a tiger and urgently needed treatment!
冷静的乡民：我们不是蓄意闹事，只是因为有人被恶虎咬伤，急需医治！

Headman: Another tiger attack? We've lost three people this month to those beasts!
保长：老虎？这个月都因为老虎死了三个人了！

Headman: We have warned you over and over not to travel into the mountains. Why go up there?
保长：乡里三令五申不许进山，你们怎么还上去？

Lead Villager: With this year's feeble harvest, what choice did we have? Five years ago it was floods, and now it's drought! Grain has grown more expensive than gold!
乡民首领：还不是因为今年收成不好。五年前发洪水，今年又旱，粮价比金子还贵！

Lead Villager: If we don't hunt, what are we to eat?
乡民首领：不打猎，我们吃西北风吗！

Calm Villager: My land has all been taken, and we have no grain stored for the winter. Hunting rabbits is the only way we can scrape together a half-decent meal.
冷静的乡民：我家的地都被并了，冬天没存粮，猎只兔子还能凑合一餐……

Disheartened Villager: If only ... If only an immortal would appear again, as they did five years ago, handing out grain to us all.
沮丧的乡民：就是……要是和五年前一样，有仙人撒粮米就好了……

After a moment's hesitation, Chéng steps past the village guards and offers the headman a modest bow.
程和光犹豫片刻，绕过保丁，走到保长面前，轻作了一揖。

Cheng Heguang: I've had some formal weapons training, including hand-to-hand. If you aim to head into the mountains to hunt the tiger, I will offer my assistance.
程和光：我学过些拳脚刀法。如果几位要上山打虎，我可以从旁协助。

The village guards exchange amused glances.
保丁们互相看了眼，好像是听着了什么好笑的事情。

Headman: That mountain forest isn't meant for travel. Only a fool would go there without a good reason. Had these hunters not been so greedy, this wouldn't have happened.
保长：那山林并非官道，平日并无行人。若不是这几个猎户贪吃，怎么会被咬伤？

Headman: The tiger attacked them in the forest, not the village. We can't go traipsing into the forest hunting tigers. We'd never get any real work done!
保长：恶虎也没跑到乡里来肆虐行凶。若是山林里每只老虎都要打，我们还做不做其他事了？！

With a dismissive wave, he turns back to scold the grime-covered villagers.
保长对着他摆了摆手，回身责骂这几个灰头土脸的乡民。

Headman: That's enough! Go back to your homes! And no one is to go up the mountain again!
保长：还不快散了！不许再上山了！
