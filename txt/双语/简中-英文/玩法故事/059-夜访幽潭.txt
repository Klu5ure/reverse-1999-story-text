01  开场

"A Night Visit to the Lake" Flash Gathering

Hope you have a great night at the expo, filled with screams and shrieks
“ 夜 访 幽 潭 ” 闪 烁 集 会

期 待 您 能 在 惊 声 尖 叫 中 度 过 一 个 美 妙 的 博 览 之 夜

Horropedia: ... What's this?
恐怖通：……这是？

Horropedia: I don't remember an ad like that in my subscription to Monster Weekly... Huh...?
恐怖通：我可不记得我订购的《怪物周刊》内有过这样的小广告……嗯，嗯嗯……？

There is a familiar odor. Horropedia examines his palms, dusty and covered in mold.
一股熟悉的气味，恐怖通检查自己的手掌，菌丝粉尘遍布。

Horropedia: Okay, I know this stuff. Looks like Green Lake is inviting us again.
恐怖通：好吧，这玩意儿我熟，看来绿湖又在邀请我们了。

Horropedia: But we abducted its owner. Who else could have done it?
恐怖通：但是，它的主人都被我们拐走了，那儿还有谁能这么做呢？

Blonney: Hey, can you get out of the way? I want to grab... Ahem, the fashion magazine I ordered.
金蜜儿：嘿，你可以让开了吗？我还要拿……咳，拿我订购的时装杂志呢。

Blonney: The sound of footsteps comes closer and closer, mingled with the pounding of hooves upon the ground.
金蜜儿：脚步声越来越近，混杂着蹄子敲击地面的声音。

Jessica: A fashion magazine! A fashion magazine!
洁西卡：时装杂志！时装杂志！

Jessica: Fawn's head pokes out from the side, eagerly searching.
洁西卡：小鹿儿的脑袋从一旁探出来，兴致勃勃地寻觅着。

Jessica: Jennifer, we're looking for the September issue this time, right?
洁西卡：珍妮弗，我们这次要找的是九月刊，对吗？

Horropedia: She's into fashion now?
恐怖通：她现在喜欢上时装了？

Horropedia: I guessed that she was a curious girl who was willing to experiment, but I didn't expect her to find a new hobby so soon...
恐怖通：虽然我能猜出她是个对什么都好奇，愿意尝试的女孩儿，但我没想到她会这么快又找到一个新爱好……

Blonney: Don't make that "one less horror enthusiast, boo-hoo" face.
金蜜儿：别摆着一张“恐怖爱好者又减少一个了，呜呜呜”的脸惋惜了。

Blonney: Blonney pulls out the September issue and handed it to Jessica.
金蜜儿：金蜜儿抽出那本九月刊，递交给一旁的洁西卡。

Blonney: It's a deranged bridal magazine. The goth aesthetic has taken over everything, including the wedding dresses... You remember the ghost bride, I presume?
金蜜儿：这是本神经兮兮的新娘杂志，那些哥特狂人占领了审美，包括新娘礼服也不放过……我想，你还记得那个鬼新娘吧？

Horropedia: Oh! Nice!
恐怖通：哦！很好！

Horropedia: Can I borrow this magazine later?
恐怖通：这本杂志，一会儿也借我瞧瞧吧！

Blonney: It's Jessica's magazine. Don't ask me, ask her.
金蜜儿：这是洁西卡的杂志，你不该问我，该去问她。

Horropedia: Nice! Jess -- Huh?
恐怖通：很好！洁西——哦？

Jessica: "A Night Visit to the Lake"... Flash Gathering?
洁西卡：“夜访幽潭”……闪烁集会？

Jessica: What's this little card? Jennifer?
洁西卡：这张小卡片是什么？珍妮弗？

Jessica: This smells like Green Lake. Did you do this? But we've spent the last few days together, so you probably haven't had time to make this card...
洁西卡：我在上面闻到了绿湖的味道，这是你准备的吗？可我们这几天都待在一起，你应该没有时间准备这张卡片……

Blonney: A card?
金蜜儿：卡片？

Blonney looks down and sees a card in the open magazine in her hand.
金发姑娘低下头，在自己手中翻开的杂志中，也瞧见了一张卡片。

Blonney: I didn't do this, Jessica. I got a card, too.
金蜜儿：这不是我准备的，洁西卡，我也收到了这张卡片。

Cautiously, she picks up the little piece of paper and glances at it. She turns it over and reads something her companions have not seen.
她谨慎地捏起那张小小的纸片，看过，再翻面，读出了她的伙伴们不曾瞧见的内容。

Blonney: "Monster Chronicles"... A book club?
金蜜儿：“怪物编年史”……读书会？

Jessica: What's that?
洁西卡：那是什么？

Blonney: I think it's a book club based on a villain from a horror movie...
金蜜儿：我想这是一场以恐怖电影里的反派为主题的读书会……

Blonney: Members... Big Trouble Girl, Avant-garde Critic, New Director, Top Collector and...
金蜜儿：特邀成员……大麻烦小姑娘、先锋评论家、新晋导演、最佳收藏家与……

Blonney: ... The survivors of Green Lake.
金蜜儿：……绿湖的幸存者们。

Blonney: Ah...
金蜜儿：啊……

Think, think, think.
思考，思考，又思考。

Blonney: That seems to be referring precisely to us.
金蜜儿：这看起来说的正是我们。

Blonney: Jessica, give me a hand.
金蜜儿：洁西卡，扶我一下。

Jessica: Huh? What's the matter? Jennifer?
洁西卡：哎呀？你怎么啦？珍妮弗？

Blonney: This piece of paper has a spider on it, and the signature--
金蜜儿：这张纸上画着蜘蛛，还有那个签名——

Blonney: Oh-- Oh, my!
金蜜儿：天啊——天啊！

It is rare for her to get so excited, but girls like her do get excited in this sort of situation.
她很少如此兴奋，但和她一样的女孩儿在此情此景之下都会如此兴奋。

Blonney: This is an invitation to UTTU! An invitation from UTTU, the greatest fashion and arcanist information magazine!
金蜜儿：这可是《UTTU》的邀请啊！最伟大的时装与神秘学家信息杂志——《UTTU》的邀请呀！

Blonney: If I die, this will go on my tombstone. Oh, my word...
金蜜儿：如果我死了，这件事儿必须得写在我的墓碑上，天啊……

Blonney fell limply against Fawn, who naturally caught her.
金蜜儿瘫软着靠在小鹿姑娘身上，后者自然地接住了她。

Jessica: U... TTU?
洁西卡：U……TTU？

Jessica: Well... Together, these letters look like... A crying puppy!
洁西卡：嗯……这些字母放在一起，看起来就像是……哭泣的小狗！

Jessica: Are we going back to Green Lake to see some crying puppies?
洁西卡：我们要回绿湖去看一些哭泣的小狗吗？

Horropedia: Wait... UTTU, and Flash Gathering, I think I've heard stories about them.
恐怖通：等等……UTTU，还有闪烁集会，我好像听说过关于它们的故事。

Horropedia: It was a book club that took the arcanist world by storm. Everyone was fascinated by their books and cards.
恐怖通：那是一场风靡神秘学界的读书会，人人都为那些书本和卡片着迷。

Horropedia: But this craze hasn't caught on with the friends of the Foundation. After all, we have little time for leisure activities...
恐怖通：但这股热潮还没能燃进基金会的朋友们中，毕竟我们总是没什么自由活动的时间……

Blonney: What are you talking about? Don't you want to go--?!
金蜜儿：你在说什么？难道你不想去吗——？！

Horropedia: No, what I'm saying is... It's different now.
恐怖通：不，我要说的是——今时不同往日。

Horropedia: My guardians are no longer the faculty or the Foundation, but Vertin and the Tooth Fairy. We can go wherever we want.
恐怖通：我的监护人不再是教员或基金会的上级，而是维尔汀与牙仙女士，所以我们想去哪儿，就能去哪儿。

02  《失落的世界》 / "Lost World"

Lakeside
绿湖湖边

Jessica: Hm... I don't think the bottom of the lake is a good place to sleep.
洁西卡：唔……我想，湖底不是一个睡觉的好地方。

Blonney: The bottom of the lake?
金蜜儿：湖底？

Jessica: Yes, look, Jennifer. The book ends with a mention of monsters living at the bottom of the lake.
洁西卡：是的，你看，珍妮弗。这本书的最后提到了生活在湖底的怪物。

Jessica: I've been to the bottom of Green Lake, too. I thought life would be different there, but...
洁西卡：我也去过绿湖的湖底，我以为在那生活会有不同，可是……

Jessica: I had more nightmares while I was there.
洁西卡：可是我在那儿却做了更多噩梦。

Jessica: I dreamed that I was back in the past, only sleeping in a cage or on a glass plate, doing blood tests from time to time and taking medicine every day...
洁西卡：我梦到我回到了过去，只能在笼子或玻璃板里睡觉，时不时需要抽血，每天都需要吃药……

Vertin: You don't have to live that life anymore.
维尔汀：你现在不用再过那种生活了。

Jessica: ...
洁西卡：……

Jessica: ... Yeah, I don't have to live that life anymore.
洁西卡：……没错，我已经不用再过那种生活了。

Horropedia: Yes, you've left Zeno.
恐怖通：是的，你已经离开芝诺了。

Horropedia: I've since heard stories about the Youth Army. They say there's a research center at the bottom of the lake that can control the cognitive barriers around the camp and control the weather.
恐怖通：我后来也听说过一些少年军的事，他们说湖底有个研究中心，可以控制营地四周的认知壁垒，还能调控天气。

Horropedia: I think you found it. And you found the remote control for everything there.
恐怖通：我想你是找到了那里，又从那里找到了一切的遥控器。

Jessica: You mean... The little button?
洁西卡：你是说……小按钮？

Jessica: Since we got the little button, my friends and I are much happier.
洁西卡：自从有了小按钮，我和我的朋友就高兴多了。

Jessica: We have more variety in our games, and we can stop the rain when we don't want to get wet.
洁西卡：我们做游戏的花样更多，不想要浑身湿漉漉的时候也可以停下雨水。

Jessica: Ah--I see. I should make friends with them.
洁西卡：啊——我明白了，我应该和它们成为朋友。

Blonney: Friends? With who?
金蜜儿：朋友？和谁？

Jessica: With the big guys in the book. They should leave the place where they get nightmares and come join me.
洁西卡：和书里这些大家伙，它们应该离开会做噩梦的地方，来和我一起。

Jessica: My grass bed is big and cozy. No one up here hates them or carries syringes and little pills!
洁西卡：我的草床很大，很舒服。上头没有人讨厌它们，也没人带着针管和小药片！

Blonney: Oh... I'm sorry, dear.
金蜜儿：嗯……很遗憾，亲爱的。

Blonney: You can't be friends with them.
金蜜儿：你不能和它们做朋友。

Jessica: Why not?
洁西卡：为什么？

Blonney: Because it's just a movie. The one I made, remember?
金蜜儿：因为那只是电影。我拍的那个，记得吗？

Jessica: I was the female lead.
洁西卡：我做女主角。

Blonney: Yeah, that's the one. Everything in that movie was set up by people like me.
金蜜儿：没错，就是那个。那里面的东西都是像我这样的人安排的。

Blonney: Like when I chose to make Annie the "last girl"...
金蜜儿：就像我选择让“安妮”成为“最后的女孩”……

Jessica: That was me!
洁西卡：是我！

Blonney: You played her. You were only "Annie" in my movie--
金蜜儿：是你演的。你只在我的电影里才是“安妮”——

Jessica: But it was me too.
洁西卡：可那也是我呀。

Blonney: ... It's a little hard to explain, dear. In short, you can't be friends with them.
金蜜儿：……这有些难解释，亲爱的。总而言之，你没有办法和它们做朋友。

Blonney: But instead, I've got a Water Monster toy here, a limited edition! Maybe it can be your friend!
金蜜儿：但作为代替，我这儿还有个水怪玩具，限量款的！或许它能给你当朋友！

Jessica: I want a sewn up monster.
洁西卡：我想要一个被缝起来的怪物。

Blonney: N-no problem, we'll just take it apart and sew it up!
金蜜儿：没……没问题，我们把它拆开，然后缝好就行了！

Jessica: No, we have to find the guys in white and have them take it apart and sew it up.
洁西卡：不，我们得找到那些穿白衣服的人，让他们拆开它，然后缝起来。

Blonney: Fine. It looks like I've taught you how to get into character. Next, we'll have to learn how to get you out.
金蜜儿：好吧，看起来我已经教会了你怎么入戏，接下来我们得学会怎么出戏了。

03  《阴翳飞溅》 / "The Splashing Shadow"

Lakeside
绿湖湖边

Blonney: I love the location and the elusive projection that appears in the magazine. It almost perfectly captures the history of horror.
金蜜儿：我喜欢这个场地搭配，还有杂志中出现的、难以捉摸的投影画面，它几乎完美地展现了恐怖影史。

Blonney: But it's still the graphic shock that gives the purest enjoyment!
金蜜儿：但果然，还是画面上的震撼最能给人纯粹的享受！

Horropedia: Mm-hmm! It's the music, just listen to it. The ambient music here, and the chords. Don't forget, these are the epitome of the classics.
恐怖通：嗯嗯——是音乐，你听，这里的场地音乐，还有同期声，别忘了，这些才是经典中的经典。

Horropedia: Horror movies can not go without this sound any more than a Caesar salad can go without canned fish.
恐怖通：有声恐怖电影不能失去这个，就像凯撒沙拉里不能没有罐头鱼。

Horropedia: A long discordant note paired with distinctive chords seems simple enough, but--
恐怖通：一长段不和谐音搭配特殊的同期声，这操作听起来很简单，但是——

Horropedia: Think of the sound of hard soles on a wooden floor, or a blade sinking into flesh and bone.
恐怖通：想想那些硬鞋底踩在木地板上，刀刃陷进肉和骨头里的声音。

Blonney: The screams of women, the sound of the wind blowing through branches, scratching against the window.
金蜜儿：女人的尖叫，狂风刮过树枝激烈拍打窗框的动静。

Horropedia: They are all indispensable.
恐怖通：缺一不可。

Blonney: Blood! And plasma!
金蜜儿：血浆！还有血浆！

Blonney: No horror movie can do without plasma!
金蜜儿：任何恐怖片都不能错过血浆！

Horropedia: Of course, it is just syrup and food coloring⁠—cost-effective "special effects".
恐怖通：当然了，糖浆加色素，性价比极高的“特效”。

Blonney: It seems that we can agree on this⁠—
金蜜儿：看来我们在这上面已经达成了一致——

Blonney: I never thought I'd hang out with a nerd.
金蜜儿：真想不到我会有和书呆子相谈甚欢的一天。

Horropedia: There are endless possibilities. A horrifying monster in one chapter might become a homicidal maniac in the next.
恐怖通：万事都有新可能，就像是恐怖鬼怪迈向新篇章成了杀人狂。

Horropedia: And now, murderers are being left behind~
恐怖通：而现在呢，杀人狂又被人们扔到身后了～

Blonney: Hm...
金蜜儿：嗯……

Blonney: Times change, monsters change... I guess it's an interesting topic.
金蜜儿：时代的变迁，怪物的改变，我想这是个有趣的议题。

The blonde girl pulled out her notebook and scribbled a few notes.
金发女孩儿掏出笔记本，草草地勾勒了几笔。

Blonney: What do you think the next fad will be? Religious horror, which is outdated but never gets old? Or maybe psychological horror like psychotic hysteria?
金蜜儿：你觉得下一个流行趋势会是什么？过时，但也永不过时的宗教恐怖，又或者是会引起心理惊悚的疯癫癔症？

Horropedia: Looks like we've got plenty of time to talk.
恐怖通：看起来我们有的是时候聊了。

Sitting down, the young girl patts her knees and opens her backpack.
坐下身子，青年拍拍膝盖，打开了背包。

Horropedia: Luckily I always carry emergency rations in my backpack.
恐怖通：还好我总在背包里揣着压缩口粮。

04  《置之“死”地》 / "Left for Dead"

Lakeside
绿湖湖边

Blonney: This chapter is very well written.
金蜜儿：这个章节写得很棒。

The girl turns page after page, admiring the illustrations.
女孩儿反复地翻动书页，欣赏插图。

Blonney: The living dead is a great subject. I used to make up stories about the living dead.
金蜜儿：活死人是一个很好的主题，我过去常常创作关于活死人的故事。

Blonney: They are crazy, smelly and often have tragic pasts.
金蜜儿：他们疯癫、臭烘烘的同时，往往充满了悲剧性。

Blonney: It's good for the story.
金蜜儿：这对故事创作有好处。

Horropedia: That's why Jessica wrote the story about the ghost bride based on your story.
恐怖通：所以洁西卡才会按照你的故事，写出鬼新娘的故事。

Horropedia: Ah, I think that's why the ghost bride always wants to run after you--
恐怖通：啊，我想这也是为什么，那个鬼新娘总愿意追着你跑了——

Blonney: Ugh... When I wrote the story, I didn't think she would smell that bad...
金蜜儿：呃呜……我写故事的时候可没想过她闻起来真的会那么臭……

Blonney: By the way, Jessica--where did she go?
金蜜儿：对了，洁西卡——她去哪儿了？

Putting down the magazine, the girl looks around.
放下手中的杂志，姑娘四下观望。

Tooth Fairy: She disappeared a while ago.
牙仙：她消失已经有一会儿了。

The grass is shaking.
草丛摇动着。

Tooth Fairy: ...?
牙仙：……？

Elongated monster claws suddenly protrude out from the grass.
像是怪物一般细长的爪子从草丛中伸出。

It transforms into two black shadows and pounces on the crowd swiftly with a terrible flash of green light.
它化作两道黑影，快而迅捷地扑向人群，连带着一丝可怖的绿光闪烁。

Jessica: Hiss! Grrr, rawr!
洁西卡：嘶哈！咕咕嘎嘎！

Vertin: I'll cut out your hearts and eat your guts.
维尔汀：我要挖出你们的心脏，吃掉你们的肠子。

Jessica: Whoa! Whoa! Waaaaah--AH!
洁西卡：哇啊！哇啊!呜呜哇哇——哇！

Vertin: Fear me, you mortal! I am...
维尔汀：恐惧吧，普通人，我是……

Jessica: Jessica stares intently at the figure in front of her and gives a small but hard nod.
洁西卡：洁西卡紧盯着面前的人，小幅度地、用力地点了点头。

Vertin: I am the King of the Living Dead!
维尔汀：我是活死人之王！

Horropedia: ...
恐怖通：……

Blonney: ...
金蜜儿：……

Blonney bowed her head and lowered her voice.
金蜜儿低下头，压低了声音。

Blonney: What are they doing...?
金蜜儿：她们……这是在干什么？

Horropedia: ... I'm guessing it's a "ghost imitation" prank.
恐怖通：……我猜，这是“鬼怪模仿”恶作剧。

Horropedia: I know what Fawn is doing, but I don't understand what Vertin is doing. It seems like she's doing an interpretation of something, like...
恐怖通：我知道小鹿姑娘在干什么，但是我不明白维尔汀在干什么，她好像在做翻译，就像……

Tooth Fairy: It's like Little Pickles with Doggie.
牙仙：就像小皮克勒斯与“Doggie”。

Horropedia: Ah!
恐怖通：啊！

The young girl's expression changes from confusion to realization.
年轻人们的表情从困惑变为了恍然大悟。

Fawn stomps her feet impatiently on the other side.
另一侧的小鹿则是焦躁地跺了跺蹄子。

Jessica: Grr?
洁西卡：咕咕？

Vertin: That wasn't part of our rehearsal... But I think it means "Why aren't they scared?".
维尔汀：这不在我们的排演过程里……但我想这句话的意思是“他们为什么不害怕呢？”。

Jessica: Grr!
洁西卡：咕咕嘎！

Blonney: A-alright... Just give me a minute to prepare.
金蜜儿：好，好吧……稍等我准备一下，立刻，立刻就来。

Taking a few deep breaths and putting her hands to her neck, Blonney shudders and falls to the ground.
深呼吸几次，把手放上脖颈，金发姑娘忽然颤抖起来，跌倒在地。

Blonney: AHHH--!
