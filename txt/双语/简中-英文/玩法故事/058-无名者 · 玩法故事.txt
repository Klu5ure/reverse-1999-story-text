01  【3.7】无名者玩法01

Mental Autolysis
自我溶解酶

Mesmer Jr.: Relax. This is just a psych evaluation.
小梅斯梅尔：你可以放轻松，这只是一次心理诊察。

Consultation Room, Rehab Center
康复中心·诊疗室

Mesmer Jr., busy preparing the exam room's equipment, only glances at her patient.
康复中心的诊疗室，人工梦游治疗师没有看向面前坐姿端正的客人，专心调整着仪器。

Ms. Stranger: Don't worry, Ms. Mesmer. I understand this is a rather difficult situation for you.
无名者：请放心，小梅斯梅尔小姐，我很理解你的难处。

Ms. Stranger: Someone who has joined Manus Vindictae more than once is hard to trust—one who's worn the mask twice, doubly so.
无名者：一个反复加入重塑之手的人难以得到信任，特别是她还两次戴上了面具。

Ms. Stranger: But Foundation secrets aside, I'll tell you whatever you wish to know.
无名者：除了基金会的机密，我可以回答你的任何问题。

The camera's light locks onto its subject, illuminating her pupils gold.
无名者：摄像头的光线对准了受问者，将她的瞳孔照成了金色。

Mesmer Jr.: I'm a therapist, Ms. Stranger, not an interrogator.
小梅斯梅尔：不，我不是在审问你。

Mesmer Jr.: We just need to ensure the Manus mask hasn't left you with any dangerous after-effects.
小梅斯梅尔：我们的目的是确保重塑之手的面具没有给你留下什么危险的后遗症。

Mesmer Jr.: You don't have to answer every question truthfully. I won't be fact-checking you. Just treat this like a normal, everyday conversation.
小梅斯梅尔：你不用每个问题都如实回答我，当成一场聊天就好。

Once she's triple-checked the pupil readings, Mesmer Jr. turns her attention to the questionnaire.
人工梦游治疗师确认了三回瞳孔的成像无误后，拿起了手中的题册。

Mesmer Jr.: First question. You're walking barefoot across a snowfield, white as far as the eye can see.
小梅斯梅尔：第一个问题，你正赤足在雪原中行走，面前是一望无际的白色。

Ms. Stranger: Is this supposed to be about the Antarctic incident?
无名者：这是什么关于南极事件的暗示吗？

Mesmer Jr.: No, it's just a standard question to measure your emotional response.
小梅斯梅尔：不，这只是习题的一部分，旨在测试你的感情起伏。

Mesmer Jr.: Back to the scenario. You're hungry. You haven't eaten in three days. A snow hare approaches you.
小梅斯梅尔：你感到饥饿，因为你已经三天没有进食了，这时有一只雪兔来到你的脚下。

Mesmer Jr.: It seems to think you're one of its own. It curls up beside you, sharing its warmth.
小梅斯梅尔：它似乎把你认成了同类，蜷缩在你的脚边，和你分享着温暖。

Ms. Stranger: This is cruel, but given it's a question of survival, I'd grab it.
无名者：这可真残忍……我想为了生存，我会抓住这只亲近人的兔子。

Mesmer Jr.: Next question. You're sunbathing at sea when a sudden wave drags you into unfamiliar waters.
小梅斯梅尔：下一个问题，你正在海上享受日光浴，突然升起的大浪却将你卷到了未知的海域……

The subject's emotions are as controlled as Mesmer's voice—textbook, even.
提问者的吐词中没有感情，受问者的感情则标准得像是教科书。

Question, answer, question, answer ... About 40 camera flashes later, the room finally goes quiet.
问答声在诊室内无间歇地反复，摄像机的快门闪烁约四十次后，诊室迎来了短暂的寂静。

Mesmer Jr.: *sigh*
小梅斯梅尔：哎……

Ms. Stranger: Were my answers that bad?
无名者：我的回答很糟糕吗？

Mesmer Jr.: Not at all. Both your responses and emotional display are within acceptable ranges.
小梅斯梅尔：并不坏，你的回答和你表现出的情绪都在偏差范围内。

Mesmer Jr.: But they're too consistent. Keeping micro-reactions that uniform across these kinds of questions is, frankly speaking, unheard of.
小梅斯梅尔：但是都过于标准，正常人做不到在面对同类问题时保证所有微反应一致。

Ms. Stranger: That would be the fruits of my training.
无名者：你是对的，我受过的教育要求我做到这些。

Ms. Stranger: I'd usually add a little laugh here, a frown there, but perhaps recent events have left me tired.
无名者：也许是我最近太累了，通常我会在表情之间加入一些微小的变化。

Mesmer Jr.: If I sign off on this report, every department is going to suspect I faked it.
小梅斯梅尔：如果我在这份诊断报告上签字，每个部门都会怀疑我造假。

Mesmer Jr.: You need to answer with genuine emotion.
小梅斯梅尔：你必须用真实的情绪来回答我的问题。

Ms. Stranger: I would if I could, Ms. Mesmer.
无名者：我明白你的意思，小梅斯梅尔小姐，你真的难倒我了。

Ms. Stranger: But such training sticks. You might as well ask me to change the way I breathe.
无名者：我受过的教育就是如此，这恐怕像呼吸习惯一样难以改变。

Ms. Stranger: Would it help if I tried answering similar questions with different emotions?
无名者：不过也许我可以试着用不同的情绪回答同类的问题？

Mesmer Jr.: I'm not signing off on a falsified report, Ms. Stranger.
小梅斯梅尔：这同样没有意义……在伪造报告时可能有用，但我不会允许。

Mesmer Jr.: Lie down. We'll continue under guided Artificial Somnambulism.
小梅斯梅尔：请你躺到床上去，诊察改在人工梦游中进行。

Ms. Stranger: Of course. My memories are yours to explore.
无名者：没问题，我的记忆随时向你开放。

She reclines as though she's settling in for a cozy afternoon nap.
受问者坦然地躺下，像是要迎接一场美梦。

Artificial Somnambulism, start.
……人工梦游，开始。

Ms. Stranger: So, where shall we begin?
无名者：那么，我们从哪儿开始呢？

Mesmer Jr.: Are you talking to me?
小梅斯梅尔：你在和我说话吗？

Ms. Stranger: There's no one else here, is there?
无名者：当然，这里应该没有其他人？

Mesmer Jr.: At this setting, you shouldn't be conscious.
小梅斯梅尔：按照目前设置的睡眠深度，你不应该保有清醒意识。

Ms. Stranger: Further fruits of my training, I'm afraid. Couldn't risk divulging secrets in my sleep.
无名者：我受过非常严密的教育，以防在说梦话时暴露机密。

Mesmer Jr.: Lucid dreaming can be an early sign of dissociation, not something to be proud of.
小梅斯梅尔：清醒梦不是值得夸赞的能力，它可能只是精神分裂的前兆。

Ms. Stranger: It's not a matter of pride; it's just how things are. But don't worry about me. In all the years I've been like this, it's never caused any problems.
无名者：不必担心我，这个症状已经持续好几年了，它没有造成任何问题。

Mesmer Jr.: I should know to expect the unexpected at this point.
小梅斯梅尔：……我还能在神秘学家身上期待什么呢？

Mesmer Jr.: Anyway, I'll record everything as it is.
小梅斯梅尔：总之，我会如实记录你的异常表现。

Mesmer Jr.: Let's start with your earliest childhood memory.
小梅斯梅尔：请从你能想起的童年开始回忆。

Ms. Stranger: That would be my days at the SPDM.
无名者：那让我们从第一防线学校开始，我的母校。

From the void of her mind emerges a classroom with spotless black-and-white-checkered floors.
黑暗中浮现的首先是黑白格子地砖铺就的教室。

SPDM
第一防线学校

Instructor: Today is your first official day here at the SPDM.
教员：今天是你们正式在第一防线学校上课的第一天。

Instructor: I've read your files, but I'd like each of you to introduce yourselves.
教员：我已经了解了你们的信息，但我希望你们每个人都做一个自我介绍。

Instructor: It will help you get to know each other and understand yourselves better.
教员：这能够帮助你们互相认识并了解自我。

Instructor: We'll go in seating order. When it's your turn, please stand and introduce yourself. Name and arcane skill will do.
教员：现在，按照座位次序开始点名，点到名的就站起来介绍自己的神秘术和姓名。

Chairs scrape rhythmically against the tile as the nervous children stand and sit.
多数拘谨的孩童挨个起身，坐下，桌椅和地面碰撞的声音有节奏地响起。

The instructor watches, nodding after each introduction, until the rhythm is broken by the girl sitting next to the window. She rises from her seat in one smooth motion and notably without scraping.
教员看着这一切，满意地点头，直到坐在窗边的女孩利落地起身，却没有制造任何多余的声音，交响曲被突然中断。

Kora: My name is Kora. My arcane skill is conjuring fire.
柯拉：我的名字是柯拉，我的神秘术是……制造火焰。

Ms. Stranger: I remember lying when I enrolled, claiming I could conjure fire.
无名者：我还记得，我在入学时骗了教员，说自己的神秘术是制造火焰。

Ms. Stranger: It wasn't until I dodged the arcane aptitude exam three times that they realized something was wrong.
无名者：一直到我第三次找借口躲过神秘术小测，教员们才注意到问题所在。

Instructor: Kora, do you know why I asked to speak with you?
教员：柯拉，你知道我为什么要找你谈话吗？

Kora: Yes, ma'am. It's because I'm an arcanist.
柯拉：我知道的，老师，因为我是个神秘学家。

Instructor: You're a good child, Kora. There's nothing wrong with how you were born.
教员：……你是个乖孩子，作为神秘学家来到世上不是你的错。

Instructor: You've achieved top marks ever since you arrived here, surpassing all your fellow classmates.
教员：你的同学们也都是神秘学家，而你有着比他们都优异的成绩。

Instructor: Even in a human school, you'd be a top student.
教员：就算是在人类学校中，你也可以做一个优等生。

Instructor: I know that I've taught you all not to misuse arcane skills.
教员：我知道，胡乱使用神秘术是不好的，这是我教导你们的。

Instructor: But that doesn't mean I want you to suppress your skills. On the contrary, I hope you can learn to control and wield them as you would any other tool.
教员：但我希望你学会控制它，而不是一味地抑制它。

Instructor: Everything else about your performance is excellent. If you follow my guidance and show control over your arcane skill in the next test—
教员：你的其他各项成绩都很好，只要按我教的做，在下次测试时证明你的神秘术控制能力——

Instructor: I can request to move you up a grade. You'd be able to graduate early, go out, and see the world. Wouldn't you like that?
教员：我就可以申请给你跳级，你不想早点毕业，去外面的世界看看吗？

Ms. Stranger: But come exam time, I just stood there in the middle of the field, like before, silent, motionless.
无名者：结果下一次测验时，我还是沉默地站在操场中央，一动不动。

Ms. Stranger: That's when the school started to suspect I wasn't really the "Kora who can conjure fire."
无名者：学校这才开始怀疑我并不是会喷火的柯拉。

Mesmer Jr.: I've never heard of anyone like that at the SPDM.
小梅斯梅尔：我从未听说第一防线学校有过这样一个人。

Mesmer Jr.: A case like yours would've made waves. Why has no one ever mentioned you?
小梅斯梅尔：像你这样的情况会引起大轰动，为什么没有人提起过你？

Ms. Stranger: The Foundation erased my records, deleted my name from the graduate list, and even removed my face from every group photo.
无名者：基金会消除了我的档案，毕业生名单中没有我的名字。

Ms. Stranger: For a spy, having a past is a liability.
无名者：我的脸被从每一张集体照片上抹去，拥有过去对间谍来说只是破绽。

Mesmer Jr.: I'm sorry for what you went through, but this still doesn't make sense.
小梅斯梅尔：我对你的境遇表示同情，但这还是说不通……

Ms. Stranger: I wouldn't dwell on it. For all I know, this may not even be a true memory, or at least, not one of mine.
无名者：请不要在意。毕竟这可能不是我的记忆。

Mesmer Jr.: You mean ...
小梅斯梅尔：你的意思是？

Ms. Stranger: My long-term memory has been unstable lately. Too many people's pasts are jumbled in my head.
无名者：最近我的长期记忆有些混乱，太多人的过去混在我脑子里了。

Ms. Stranger: Mhmm.
无名者：嗯……

Ms. Stranger: I tried recalling some classmates' names, but all I got were John, Smith, and Doe.
无名者：我试着回忆了几个同学的名字，但只想起了约翰、史密斯，还有多伊。

Ms. Stranger: Seems it is indeed a false memory.
无名者：看来这段记忆确实是个误会。

Mesmer Jr.: That would point to a psychological disorder.
小梅斯梅尔：这代表你有精神病，我很肯定。

Ms. Stranger: Hmm. This part—this feels real.
无名者：嗯……这一段，这一段似乎是真实的。

The scene blurs again, reshaping itself in the void.
记忆的景象再度模糊，于黑暗中重新塑形。

Ms. Stranger: I should've been an ordinary girl from Iceland.
无名者：我应该只是个来自冰岛的普通女孩。

Ms. Stranger: My favorite thing in the world was Bolludagur—cream bun day. I'd whack my parents' backsides with a paper-covered stick, shouting "bolla, bolla, bolla!" to get a cream bun.
无名者：生活中最大的乐趣是在奶油泡芙日用纸棍打父母的屁股来换取点心。

Ms. Stranger: My second favorite thing was going to Broadway with them.
无名者：第二大乐趣是和他们一起去百老汇。

Mesmer Jr.: Wait, Broadway?
小梅斯梅尔：不好意思，百老汇？

Ms. Stranger: Oh! I'm sorry. Iceland doesn't have Broadway, does it?
无名者：噢！真是抱歉，冰岛应该没有百老汇？

Ms. Stranger: Let me correct that. I grew up in New York.
无名者：请让我再更正一次，我是在纽约长大的。

Ms. Stranger: Or was it San Francisco? I remember there was a big Chinatown near my home.
无名者：又或者是旧金山？我记得家附近有很大的唐人街。

Ms. Stranger: My Chinese wasn't very good, so I'd always end up ordering too much.
无名者：可惜我的中文不是很好，在唐人街的餐馆点餐时总是点错份量。

Ms. Stranger: Delicious as the grilled meat with chimichurri was, there was no way I could finish seven portions on my own.
无名者：加了奇米丘里酱的烤肉虽然好吃，可我一个人也吃不完七份。

Mesmer Jr.: Chimichurri isn't Chinese food, though. It's Argentinian.
小梅斯梅尔：奇米丘里酱不属于中餐，它是阿根廷的特产。

Ms. Stranger: You mean to say I'm from Argentina?
无名者：你是说……我的故乡在阿根廷？

Mesmer Jr.: *sigh*
小梅斯梅尔：哎……

Seeing she's getting nowhere, Mesmer Jr. cuts off the memory.
人工梦游治疗师无奈的叹息打断了回忆。

Mesmer Jr.: Well, we've established that your childhood memories are full of inconsistencies. That's enough for now.
小梅斯梅尔：你的童年回忆全是漏洞，我已经了解了这一点，这就够了。

Mesmer Jr.: Dwelling on them will only put more strain on you.
小梅斯梅尔：继续和童年纠缠只会增加你的精神负担。

Mesmer Jr.: I suggest we move forward. Focus on memories you're certain about.
小梅斯梅尔：我建议你向前看，从能够确定的记忆开始回想。

Ms. Stranger: The parts I'm certain about? Yes, I can do that.
无名者：确凿的部分？当然，我当然记得。

Ms. Stranger: Whatever my childhood was like, everything from one particular day onward, I've never forgotten.
无名者：不管童年怎么样，从那一天开始的记忆我不曾遗忘过。

Ms. Stranger: Because I met an alien that day.
无名者：我遇到外星人的那一天……

Mesmer Jr.: What?
小梅斯梅尔：什么？

Ms. Stranger: Just as I started to think I was dreaming, I heard heavy footsteps.
无名者：正当我以为自己在做梦时，传来了沉重的脚步声。

As her story unfolds, a pair of red lights flicker in the darkness.
随着故事展开，两对红色的光点开始在黑暗中闪烁。

???: The drug has taken effect. We can begin.
？？？：药物起作用了，我们可以开始了。

???: Are you awake? Are you comfortable?

The voices sound distant, like they're coming from deep underwater.
光点发出的声音仿佛自泥水中来。

Ms. Stranger: Where am I? Who are you?
无名者：我这是在哪儿，你们是谁？

Ms. Stranger: I'd heard enough stories to know what was happening. I'd been abducted by aliens. I was aboard their UFO.
无名者：年幼的我立即明白了，我被外星人诱拐了，我来到了它们的UFO中。

???: You won't be able to move for a while, so it's best you settle in ...
？？？：接下来你会有很长一段时间不能动，所以你最好趁现在调整到一个舒服的坐姿……

Ms. Stranger: I couldn't help but think of Colorado's cattle mutilation cases from the 70s. Aliens had abducted entire herds of cows, taken their body parts, and left the corpses to rot in the fields.
无名者：当时我以为自己会成为科罗拉多州的奶牛。

Ms. Stranger: My imagination ran wild. I saw myself drained of blood, dissected, organs removed, and then discarded as a hollow shell.
无名者：脑海中想象着自己被放血，解剖，取走内脏，变成干瘪的空心尸体的画面。

Ms. Stranger: But strangely, even as I thought of all that, I didn't feel afraid.
无名者：但神奇的是，我虽然在这么想着，却一点也感觉不到恐怖。

Ms. Stranger: Their glowing eyes in the dark, their low voices ... They made me feel calm.
无名者：外星人在黑暗中发光的眼睛，还有它们低沉的嗓音令我感到放松。

Ms. Stranger?: I know what you are. You're aliens.
无名者？：我明白了，你们是外星人……

Ms. Stranger?: Are you going to dissect me? Am I going to die?
无名者？：你们要解剖研究我吗？我要死了吗？

???: Yes, we're aliens. Are you not afraid of us?
？？？：是的，我们是外星人，你不怕我们吗？

Ms. Stranger?: Should I be?
无名者？：我……我不懂，我应该害怕吗？

Ms. Stranger?: What does fear even feel like?
无名者？：害怕……应该怎么做？

???: You're not afraid because you know you don't need to be, and you're right.
？？？：你不害怕，因为你明白你不需要害怕，这是对的。

???: We can be friends.
？？？：我们可以成为朋友。

Mesmer Jr.: You're sure they were aliens?
小梅斯梅尔：你确定那是外星人吗？

Ms. Stranger: Absolutely, Ms. Mesmer. They exist, and my abduction proves it.
无名者：我很确信，小梅斯梅尔小姐，外星人一定是存在的。

Ms. Stranger: They'd probably been on Earth for a long time, given how fluent their English was.
无名者：它们似乎在地球上潜伏了很久，因为它们都说着一口流利的英语。

Ms. Stranger: Though, I was unable to understand everything they said. Their vocabulary was a bit too advanced for my age.
无名者：不过用词都很生僻，小时候的我听不懂它们在说什么。

Mesmer Jr.: Aliens might well exist, and plenty of people in Laplace believe they do.
小梅斯梅尔：外星人确实有可能存在，科算中心内部也有不少人相信这件事。

Mesmer Jr.: But what I'm questioning right now is your mental state.
小梅斯梅尔：但我现在质疑的是你的精神状况。

Mesmer Jr.: Did you experience anything traumatic as a child? Like abuse or abduction?
小梅斯梅尔：你是否曾经历过类似虐待或绑架，或者其他不愉快的事件？

Ms. Stranger: Glad to see you again, Vertin. I was just sorting through my past.
无名者：见到你真高兴，维尔汀，我正在整理过去。

Ms. Stranger: It's not easy to separate the fabricated memories from the real ones. Would you be willing to lend me an ear?
无名者：分清伪造的记忆和真实的经历很困难，你愿意听我讲解，和我一起推理吗？

Vertin: Of course. You can always come to me.
维尔汀：当然，你随时可以来邀请我。

Vertin: If you're open to it, I also wanted to ask you about your plans moving forward.
维尔汀：以及，我是来询问你关于将来的规划的。

Ms. Stranger: Before I answer that, Vertin, do you remember when we first met?
无名者：……在回答这个问题之前，维尔汀，你还记得我们初次见面的时候吗？

Vertin: You mean at Tuesday's Motel?
维尔汀：你是说在蓝手帕旅馆？

Ms. Stranger: Was that my memory? I thought it was Kayla's.
无名者：哦？我还以为那只是凯拉的记忆，那是我的记忆吗？

Vertin: I think Kayla back then was you.
维尔汀：我想，当时的凯拉应该就是你。

Ms. Stranger: I see. Then, thank you. That clears up one misunderstanding.
无名者：既然你是这么说的，谢谢你帮我排除了一个误会。

Ms. Stranger: But the first time I recall meeting you was on the "Free Breeze." Do you remember?
无名者：可我印象里与你的初次见面，似乎是“自由海风号”……你还记得吗？

Ms. Stranger: What did you think of me back then?
无名者：那时你是怎么看待我的？

Vertin: Ms. Grace was a dangerous, manipulative member of Manus Vindictae.
维尔汀：淑女格蕾丝是一个邪恶的、蛊惑人心的重塑之手成员。

Vertin: Even if you showed some small kindness to Barcarola and Fatutu, it didn't erase your crimes.
维尔汀：即使你对芭卡洛儿和图图石子表现出了一些友好，也不能抵消那些罪行。

Ms. Stranger: And yet, even knowing what I'd done, you accepted my offer to cooperate in Antarctica?
无名者：可你在南极接受了我的合作邀请？明知我是黑色的棋子。

Vertin: At the time, stopping the Manus ritual was more urgent than holding you accountable.
维尔汀：当时需要尽快阻止重塑之手的仪式，不是追究你的责任的时候。

Ms. Stranger: Yes, I remember that.
无名者：是啊，我记得这些。

Ms. Stranger: You have a strong sense of right and wrong, and you try to keep moving toward the light, yet you're also remarkably tolerant.
无名者：你明白对与错，并向着白色的方向努力，却格外宽容。

Ms. Stranger: Like a fleeting, dreamy shade of gray—the most fascinating part of the Mothmen's cartoons.
无名者：就像是迷人的、转瞬即逝的、梦幻般的灰色，天蛾人卡通片中最精彩的部分……

Ms. Stranger: Now that you're without an excuse to delay holding me accountable, what will you do?
无名者：可现在已经没有借口延缓对我的审判了，你会怎么做？

Ms. Stranger: Or rather, what do you think I should do?
无名者：你觉得我应该怎么做，维尔汀？

Vertin: You already proved where your loyalties lie in Antarctica and during the attack on the Foundation. There's no need for any judgment from me.
维尔汀：你已经在南极和基金会遇袭时证明了你自己，我不需要再次审判你。

Vertin: If it's forgiveness you want, I can mediate on your behalf, but I can't make any promises.
维尔汀：如果你希望得到原谅的话，我可以为你进行仲裁和调解。

Ms. Stranger: I'm glad to hear you say that, but it's not the right time yet.
无名者：听到你这么说我很开心，维尔汀，但现在还不是时候。

Ms. Stranger: My memories are still a mess. Like the cowgirl in Kayla's memories—I don't think I ever met her.
无名者：我的记忆还很乱，比如凯拉记忆中的那个牛仔，我想我没有见过她。

Ms. Stranger: Yet, still, there's a lingering guilt I can't shake, and I don't think it's Kayla's.
无名者：可是总有一股内疚感放不下，这部分应该不属于凯拉……

Ms. Stranger: There are lots of memories like that.
无名者：像这样的记忆还有很多。

Ms. Stranger: So, it wouldn't be wise for me to meet the victims of my past identities for now. Doing so would disturb the peace inside the Suitcase.
无名者：所以现在让我与过去身份的受害者见面并不明智，箱子里的和平会被打破。

Ms. Stranger: Until I finish making sense of my memories and past, is it possible for a special room to be prepared? Somewhere no one can find me?
无名者：在我梳理完记忆，确认该用怎样的感情面对过去之前，能为我准备一个谁都找不到的房间吗？

Vertin: There are rooms in the Suitcase with entrances only I know. You can stay in one of those.
维尔汀：箱子里有一些只有我知道出入口的房间，你可以住在里面。

Ms. Stranger: Thank you for letting me hide a little longer.
无名者：谢谢你，愿意帮助我再逃避一段时间。

Vertin: Of course. In the meantime, is there anything you'd like to do? I can give you some ideas to pass the time.
维尔汀：在躲避期间，还有什么想做的吗？我可以给你一些消磨时间的建议。

Ms. Stranger: No, nothing comes to mind.
无名者：嗯……我没有什么想法。

Ms. Stranger: Funny. I told Arcana and Madam Constantine that the present shapes the future.
无名者：虽然我对阿尔卡纳和康斯坦丁都说未来是由当下决定的。

Ms. Stranger: But once you leave the past behind, it becomes hard to make choices in the present.
无名者：但离开过去之后确实很难做出抉择。

Ms. Stranger: Take something as simple as food, for example. Kayla cared only about feeling full. Lily loved sweets. Grace had a penchant for medium-rare steak.
无名者：就像饮食习惯，凯拉会追求饱腹感，莉莉喜欢甜食，格蕾丝钟爱五分熟的牛排。

Ms. Stranger: Their tastes may not have been mine, but without them, I feel lost.
无名者：她们的喜好未必是我的喜好，但抛开她们，我将没有判断自我的依据。

Ms. Stranger: Someone else might choose what to eat for breakfast in an instant, but for me ... Do I like toast with jam? Eggs and bacon? Just coffee? It's a daunting choice.
无名者：现在就连明天的早餐吃什么，对我来说都是个需要深思的问题。

Vertin: Don't push yourself to figure it out right away. You have plenty of time to try all sorts of things.
维尔汀：你有很多时间去尝试不同的口味，可以慢慢得出结果。

Ms. Stranger: Even the most mundane choices will shape my future, Vertin. If I fall in love with a certain flavor tomorrow, I might never explore all the others.
无名者：这是决定将来的大事，维尔汀，如果我在明天的早餐中爱上了某个味道，或许我就不会再尝试其他可能性了……

Ms. Stranger: If the future is mine to write, then isn't it natural to be afraid?
无名者：未来要由自己决定，难道我不应该胆怯吗？

Vertin: Hmm. Perhaps you could talk with Marcus? I think you'd find her perspective quite insightful.
维尔汀：关于这方面，也许马库斯能提供一些过来人的经验。

Ms. Stranger: If I'm going to place my trust in someone else, I'd rather trust you, Vertin.
无名者：如果要参考其他人的经验，我会比较相信你，维尔汀。

Ms. Stranger: Ah, that's it! Why didn't I think of it sooner?
无名者：是啊！我该早些想到的。

Ms. Stranger: I'll just leave it up to you! Can you make breakfast for me tomorrow?
无名者：我的决定就是——委托给你，维尔汀。你能为我准备明天的早餐吗？

Ms. Stranger: I promise, whatever you prepare, I'll eat it.
无名者：我发誓，不管你准备了什么，我都会吃下去。

Vertin: Of course. Leave it to me.
维尔汀：没有问题，我会为你准备明天的早餐。

Ms. Stranger: Finally, I've taken a step. I've made a decision that's mine and mine alone.
无名者：终于，我总算是迈出了一步，做出了一个属于我自己的决定。

Ms. Stranger: Ah ...
无名者：啊……

Whether from the weight of this monumental decision or simply the cold, exhaustion finally hits, and Ms. Stranger lets her eyes fall shut.
似乎决定了早餐算是完成了一件大事，又或许是在寒冷的雪地里待了太久，无名者在放松后袭来的疲劳感中合上了眼。

Vertin: Wake up, Ms. Stranger. You can't sleep out here in the snow.
维尔汀：醒醒，无名者，你不能在雪地里睡觉。

Ms. Stranger: It's alright. I feel much warmer now.
无名者：没关系……我感觉暖和起来了。

Vertin: That's frostbite. How long have you been lying here?!
维尔汀：那是冻伤的症状，你在这儿躺了多久？！

Ms. Stranger: I've been waiting for it to rain since this morning for the chance to toss my umbrella away and sing because I'm finally free.
无名者：我从今天早上起就在等待一场雨，然后我就可以扔掉伞在雨里高歌，因为我是自由的……

Vertin: Ms. Stranger! You mustn't fall asleep!
维尔汀：保持清醒！无名者！

Ms. Stranger: You know, Vertin. If I'd known earlier that the "Storm" brought a new identity as opposed to death, perhaps I would've sung in the rain long ago.
无名者：维尔汀……如果我早些知道“暴雨”带来的不是死，而是崭新的身份，或许我早就跳到雨中了。

Vertin: Ms. Stranger!
维尔汀：无名者！

Ms. Stranger: But now, I'm looking forward to tomorrow's breakfast. I just want to sleep until then.
无名者：但现在……我很期待你准备的早餐……只想一觉睡到明天……

Vertin: You mustn't—not here!
维尔汀：不要睡！

But in the end, there is no choice but to leave her dozing in the snow and alert some caretakers to her location.
最终，维尔汀还是让一些看护人员知晓了无名者的住址。
