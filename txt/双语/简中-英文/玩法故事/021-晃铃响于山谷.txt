01  序幕:山林尾声 / Prologue: Last Echoes in the Forest

...
……

The wind tears at what's left of his clothes. His body is like a hunk of naked flesh, crawling miserably along.
风撕扯着余下的衣衫，他的身体犹如一捆裸露的肉块，狼狈地爬行着。

The scarred chest and the bloodied feet have numbed him, but he keeps his hollowed eyes fixed on the steep, terrifying road ahead.
伤痕累累的胸口，鲜血淋淋的脚掌，而他已然麻木，空洞的眼睛目视——陡峭、可怖的前路。

？？？: Keep moving, to the higher place ...
？？？：还要前进，到更高的地方……

He mumbles through cracked lips.
他干裂的嘴唇，喃喃自语。

Where is here? None could tell, but the man knows he must go north, away from that village, away from that city, and into the rumored depths to find his final salvation.
这里是哪？无人知晓。男人只知道自己要一路向北，离开那个村，逃出那座城——去只有传闻中才出现过的深处，寻求最后的自我拯救。

By now he has successfully distanced himself from his homeland.
如今他已成功地远离了故土。

But he does not know if he will make it, or if he will become a feast for the vultures.
但他不知道自己是否可以到达，也许最后沦为秃鹫的美宴也不失可能。

The physical agony and torment let him temporarily escape the mental torture. To him, this is enough.
肉体的剧痛与折磨，让他暂时摆脱了心灵的酷刑。对他而言，这足够了。

He does not want the journey to end. Instead, he wants the penance to go on ceaselessly, like the fierce wind swirling on the cliff.
他不希求到达那一处终点，他盼求苦痛犹如吹过峭壁的狂风，永无止境。

？？？: This is...
？？？：这里是……

The uneven ground ends, and a dense forest lies ahead.
崎岖的地面走到了尽头，眼前是一片丛密的森林。

Birds hop high above in the treetops. He hears a faint rustling from the bushes and the gurgling of melting ice water.
树梢上的鸟跳跃，灌丛响起轻微的响动，融冰的流水潺潺。

？？？: Water...!
？？？：水……！

He buries his head in the icy river, the cold making his skull tremble, and rejoices with deep gulps.
他把头埋进冰冷的河水，寒意令颅脑战栗，而大口吞咽的喉咙欢喜。

Shamane: ...Ahhhh!
鬃毛沙砾：……哈。

Having guzzled down the water, he lies on the soft moss, gazing at the dreary sky.
吞饱了水，他仰躺在松软的苔藓上，直视着灰败的天空。

Shamane: Let's see how much farther you can take me.
鬃毛沙砾：让我看看，你还能把我带多远。

He digs out the sharp pebbles in his soles, tears off his left arm cloth to bandage the wound, and walks to the forest entrance.
他挖出脚掌里尖锐的石粒，撕下左臂上的布料绑住伤口，走到森林入口。

A huge tree towers over the entrance.
入口处，一棵高耸的巨树屹立。

The bark is peeled off, carved with a twisted, bizarre deity.
上面的树皮被削去，刻着一张扭曲、怪异的神像。

The deity's face is smeared with beast blood, seemingly warning those about to enter the forest.
神像的脸上涂满了兽血，似乎在警告着企图步入林中之人。

A hand full of wounds is placed on it.
一只布满创口的手覆了上去。

Shamane: After all, the poisoned arrows, deadly traps, and fierce animals…
鬃毛沙砾：毒箭、陷阱、野兽……

Fingers without nails brush over the deity's eyes.
失掉指甲的手指拂过神像的双眼。

Shamane: This face even looks welcoming to me, my friend.
鬃毛沙砾：你的警告和它们比起来——像出笑话，朋友。

He raises his hand, snaps off a thick branch, and staggers into the lush forest.
他抬手掰下一根粗枝，踉跄着进入了茂密的森林。

02  河流 / River

The river splits the land in two, like a crack in a canyon, turbulent and icy, never to be mended.
河流将陆地一分为二，犹如一条峡谷裂缝，它湍急、冷冽，永无弥合。

Without a doubt, the rapids will sweep away anything that steps into the current. While the remains will become a feast for the fishes, continuing to nourish this forest.
毋庸置疑，急流将带走任何动物。而遗骸则会变成鱼儿的美餐，继续哺育这座森林。

Shamane: Hm...
鬃毛沙砾：唔……

Shamane: It's not wise to wade this river.
鬃毛沙砾：这可不是能蹚过的河……

He looks at the river's source and its destination, with no end in sight.
他望向河流的归途与去处，不见有尽头。

Crouching down, he reaches his right hand into the rapids, feeling its breath and fury. But he frowns solemnly, withdrawing his hand.
蹲下来，他将右手伸进急流，感受着这条河的喘息与愤怒。接着脸色凝重，把手抽离了出来。

Shamane: Seems like I'm not welcome here, either.
鬃毛沙砾：……看来你也不太欢迎我。

Bowing his head, he looks back at the way he came.
他垂着头，回首望向来时的道路。

After a long while, he turns and faces the river, taking a deep breath.
良久，他转过身，望着河流，深呼一口气。

—Splash! He jumps in without hesitation.
——义无反顾地跳了进去。

...
……

...
…………

Shamane: Blech! Cough cough cough...
鬃毛沙砾：呕，咳咳咳……

Shamane: Washed ashore on the other side, he kneels on the ground, convulsing and vomiting.
鬃毛沙砾：被河流冲到对岸，他跪倒在地上，抽搐着呕吐出胃液和水。

Shamane: Ah... Ah... Choo!
鬃毛沙砾：哈……哈……呼。

Shamane: ...
鬃毛沙砾：……

Shamane: Just walk it off if you still can. Nothing to complain about.
鬃毛沙砾：……还活着就继续往前走吧，你没什么好抱怨的。

03  巨石 / Giant Rock

Where did it come from? And how did it end up here?
它从何而来？又为何驻足在这里？

Massive, immovable—anyone who sets their eyes on it would wonder about these things.
巨大、固执——端详着眼前这座庞然大物，没有谁会不发出这样的疑惑。

The huge boulder, carved with strange patterns, is wedged in the crevice by accident or not, blocking the only way forward.
这座凿有奇怪纹路的巨石立在夹缝中，巧合地堵住了唯一的去路。

It remains motionless, leaving only a gap the size of a fist.
它纹丝不动，留下的缝隙仅有一个拳头大小。

Shamane: ...It looks a bit taller than the sacred fig tree at home.
鬃毛沙砾：……唔，好像比家里的菩提树高那么些。

He pats the rock. Its smooth touch seems especially unfriendly now.
他拍了拍石头，光滑的触感在此时显得分外不友好。

Shamane: Hmm...it's not as easy to climb as that tree!
鬃毛沙砾：唔……这可没有——那棵树好爬——！

He looks for footholds on the boulder and moves up it skillfully, climbing bit by bit with his right hand.
他找寻巨石上的落脚处，熟练地踩了上去，右手一点点向上攀附。

Shamane: Phew, phew...good...Ah-!
鬃毛沙砾：呼，呼……好的……啊——！

The boulder below suddenly begins to shake.
脚下的巨石毫无来由地开始颤动。

Along with the strange movement, the patterns on the boulder emit light.
伴随着这异常的松动，巨石的纹路散发光亮。

Shamane: !?...!
鬃毛沙砾：！？……！

The boulder's shaking intensifies, until it jolts and throws him off violently.
巨石的颤动愈发剧烈，他颠簸着，狠狠地被甩了下去。

On the ground, a cone-shaped bone shard stands sharp, predictably piercing his calf.
地面上，一只锥形的碎骨尖锐地伫立着，毫无意外地刺穿了他的小腿。

Shamane: Ugh...!
鬃毛沙砾：呃……！

Blood soaks the leg cloth.
鲜血洇湿了腿上的布料。

Shamane: Tsk... How is this rock...
鬃毛沙砾：嘶……这石头怎么……

An unforeseen obstacle.
这是突如其来的抗拒。

Bearing the pain, he forcibly digs out the bone shard.
忍着剧痛，碎骨被他硬生生挖了出来。

Shamane: Phew...quite the temper.
鬃毛沙砾：呼呼……脾气不小。

04  荆棘丛 / Bramble Bushes

He probes carefully, leaving a drop of blood on his fingertip from the thorns.
他小心翼翼地探出手指，尖刺在指头上留下了一粒血珠。

Shamane: ...
鬃毛沙砾：……

The blood-red thorns have grown rampant. Within the thicket, numerous skeletons can be seen intact; the blood-soaked soil has turned dark crimson—this seems to be its sustenance.
血色的荆棘肆意生长，能看见荆棘丛深处有不少完整的骸骨，泥土被血浸成黑色——这似乎就是它们的养分。

Shamane: Seems like you've been feasting in the past years.
鬃毛沙砾：看上去你吞过不少东西……

The fingers avoid the tips, instead grabbing the coarse branches between the spines.
手指避开尖端，转而捏住尖刺之间粗糙的枝条。

Shamane: I wonder what your teeth can do to the stones, if they couldn't even break up human bones?
鬃毛沙砾：嚼不碎骨头的牙齿，能嚼碎石头吗？

Shamane: ...Let me think...
鬃毛沙砾：……我想想……

He gathers a pile of pebbles, picking out the hardest and sharpest rocks.
他抱来一堆石子，挑拣出最为坚硬与尖锐的石头。

Shamane: Ha-!
鬃毛沙砾：哈——！

The rock held high crashes down on the thorns, shattering sections of branches. Bright red sap splatters on his arms in a speckled pattern.
石头高高举起，砸在荆棘上。一段段枝节被砸断，鲜红的汁液迸溅在手臂上，星星点点。

Shamane: Huff— Huff—
鬃毛沙砾：呼——呼——

Shamane: Almost done.
鬃毛沙砾：……差不多了。

The thorns on their last legs, Shamane reaches in with his stick, prying open the broken thorns, and squeezing in sideways with his body.
荆棘奄奄一息，鬃毛沙砾伸出树枝，将断裂的荆棘挑开，侧着身体，向里面挤去。

But the thorns seem to sense the foreign intrusion and swarms around, their hooks piercing his ankles, abdomen, and aiming for his eyes...
但荆棘们仿佛感知到了异物的存在，其他区域的荆棘纷至沓来，它们钩伤他的脚踝、腹部，刺向眼球……

Shamane: ...!
鬃毛沙砾：……！

The lone right hand grips the climbing branches tightly.
孤零零的右手紧紧握住了攀附上来的枝条。

Shamane: At the very least...
鬃毛沙砾：至少……

Shamane: I can't stop here!
鬃毛沙砾：不能停在这里！

The spikes pierce deep into the skin.
尖刺没入皮肤深处。

Shamane: —!!
鬃毛沙砾：——！！

He moves forward, disappearing into the thorn bushes.
他向前，亦没入了荆棘丛中。

05  落幕:山林尾声 / Epilogue: Last Echoes in the Forest

Shamane: Whew...
鬃毛沙砾：呼……

After a day's bitter journey, his exhausted, battered body finally reaches a quiet place.
一天的苦旅。这副疲惫、遍体鳞伤的身躯，终于行至一处静谧之地。

The scabbed wounds ache faintly. He swallows a mouthful of bloody saliva, trying to deceive his howling stomach.
结痂的伤口隐隐作痛，他吞咽着血腥味的唾沫，试图欺骗哀嚎的肚子。

Shamane: Nearly there.
鬃毛沙砾：呼，呼……就快……

He looks up, the sky spinning in his eyes.
他抬起头，天幕在眼眸中旋转着。

Shamane: ...
鬃毛沙砾：……

Without any warning, his heavy eyelids droop—the sky draws its curtain.
没有任何征兆，他的眼皮铅重似的垂下——天空拉下帷幕。

Freezing darkness swallows the hills, invading his body, and soon his consciousness sinks into a dense darkness.
寒冷的黑暗吞没山林，侵袭肉体，意识也旋即陷入沉甸甸的黑暗。

He collapses to the ground like a cut log. The faint sounds quickly are dispersed by the wind and snow, disappearing into the woods.
他如一截断木倾倒在地。渺小的声响迅速地被风雪吹散，消失在了山林里。

06  序幕:片瓦无存 / Prologue: Reduced to Ashes

He vaguely hears a woman crying helplessly. The sound of her trembling vocal cords is familiar...
他隐约听见女人在无助地哭喊，颤抖着声带，这声音似曾相识……

The smell of burning wood rushes into his nose, and he hears a crackling sound beside his ears...
木头焚烧的气味蹿入鼻腔，他的耳边噼啪作响……

Shamane: ...!
鬃毛沙砾：……！

Shamane: This again.
鬃毛沙砾：又是这里……

He sees flames ablaze, engulfing the house before him, black debris floating before his eyes.
火焰汹汹燎起，吞噬着眼前的屋子，无数黑色碎屑在眼前飘散。

He stands before the dilapidated house, shadows covering his face, a sense of suffocation like the day the fire started.
他站在焰尖下摇摇欲坠的老宅前，阴影盖住他的脸，窒息感如同火焰燃起的昨日。

Shamane: I won't fall for this again. I will stand here and watch you burn to ashes.
鬃毛沙砾：我不会再上当……我就站在这，等着你烧成灰烬，什么也不留下。

Shamane: Ugh...
鬃毛沙砾：呜……

"What if this is real?" A voice whispers to him.
“如果这是真实的呢？”有声音向他窃窃私语。

Shamane: ...
鬃毛沙砾：……

The same voice, to which he once sacrificed his left arm in a state of despair.
是那道声音，他曾在惊惶中向它献上了自己的左臂。

Thus Mor Pankh was spared, but the powerful Sharma was no more in this world.
摩卢旁卡因此幸免于难，但世上从此再无那个强大的沙尔玛。

"You can still make a choice..."
“你还能做出选择……”

The wooden door in the house collapses, sparks splashing on his face, painful, scalding, then doused by tears.
老宅内的木门崩塌，星火溅射在他的脸上，疼痛、炙热，又由眼泪浇灭。

Sweat streams down his dirty face and he clenches his fists.
汗水在他脏兮兮的脸庞划下几道清晰的痕迹。他攥紧拳头。

"This may be the last chance, you know."
“这或许是最后一次机会，你知道的。”

"You try to abandon it all. To forget everything and numb yourself with physical pain, but the meaningless questions still swirl endlessly in your head."
“你妄图抛开、遗忘一切，用肉体的痛苦自我麻痹，无济于事的反问每天都在你的脑子里萦绕。”

"But is it really meaningless?"
“但真的，无济于事了吗？”

07  父亲 / Father

He used to look down on the family, disregarding his mission and wasting his outstanding talents.
他曾对家族不屑一顾，罔顾肩负的使命，荒废卓绝的天赋。

A veritable Eden, where arcanists and humans could coexist without hierarchy. That should be the shared future worth pursuing.
神秘学家与人类共存的伊甸园，没有高下之分的生活，那才应是众人所追寻的未来。

...He had never wavered in this belief.
……在这之前，他从未动摇过这个信念。

Shamane: Father...
鬃毛沙砾：父亲……

The crippled man supports himself on the table, sits on the floor and leans against the wall.
折了腿的男人，扶着桌子坐在地上，背靠着墙面。

The burning wood crashes down thunderously, shattering the table inches away. But the man is indifferent.
燃烧的断木轰然落下，将咫尺外的桌子砸了个稀巴烂。而男人无动于衷。

Kullu: Kapil, you d*****s! This isn't what we agreed to. Look at this fire, it's reaching our house!
库鲁：卡皮尔，你这个蠢蛋！这跟说好的不一样，瞧瞧这火，都要烧到咱们家了！

Kapil: I… I've no idea. Weren't we supposed to throw all the bottles out?...
卡皮尔：我，我不知道，不是要把瓶子都丢出去吗……

Kullu: D****t, those guys didn't tell us any of this! Forget it, let's run!
库鲁：该死的，那帮家伙没告诉咱这些！算了，先跑……

They stand in front of the gate, as if watching a circus act gone wrong.
他们站在大门前，仿佛这只是一场出了意外的马戏表演。

"These are your trusted human friends, good child." His father's voice echoed in his mind.
“这就是你所信任的人类朋友，好孩子。”——脑内响起的，是父亲的声音。

Shamane: ...
鬃毛沙砾：……

The guilt and grief have subsided over time.
愧疚与悲痛早在时光中沉寂。

Only the deep-rooted hatred remains.
唯有仇恨仍旧鲜明。

He goes over and tries to support the old man on the ground, as he had done time and again in his nightmares.
他走过去，像在以往的梦境中做过的那样——试图搀扶起地上稍显苍老的男人。

Shamane's Father: We don't need you here.
父亲：这里不需要你。

Rejected once again. His father's dignity and grace didn't fade in his memory, even after his death.
一如既往的拒绝。记忆中的威容与尊颜并没随着他的死亡而变得模糊。

Shamane's Father: You are no longer a Sharma... Take your hands off me.
父亲：你已经不是沙尔玛的一员了……松开你的手。

Shamane: Father...
鬃毛沙砾：……父亲。

Shamane's Father: ... Your mother and I, the entire family have invested years in your education ...
父亲：……我、你的母亲，整个家族曾对你倾注无数心血……

Shamane's Father: Yet you have let us down ... You are in no position to make any decision for this family.
父亲：而你辜负了一切——你已经没有资格涉足家族的任何抉择。

Before this, even when he was estranged from the clan, they never doubted him. Rather, they saw him as a wayward child.
在此之前，即便他与家族已经决裂，他们也从未怀疑过，只把他当作是一位离经叛道的孩子。

Shamane's Father: You think our reluctance to interact with humans is just stubbornness.
父亲：你以为，我们不愿与人类往来，只是因为固执已见。

Shamane's Father: You think we shut those humans out to cling to our land, intimidated, and afraid of losing this so-called dignity.
父亲：你以为，我们将那些人类拒之门外，是因为守着这三分地，诚惶诚恐，害怕失去所谓的尊严。
