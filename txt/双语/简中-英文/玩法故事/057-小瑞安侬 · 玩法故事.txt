01  【3.7】小瑞安侬玩法01

The Young Traveler
小小远行客

Inn
旅店

Hotelier: A single room'll be four coins a night; breakfast delivered to your door'll be one coin extra.
旅店招待：单人房一夜的价格是四枚硬币，如果需要把早餐送到客房门口，需要额外再付一枚。

Rhiannon: Alright, that makes five.
小瑞安侬：那么一共是五枚……

Rhiannon opens her coin purse and lets a few coins spill into her palm.
驯鸟女巫掂了掂钱袋，倒出几枚钱币落在掌心。

Adar Llwch Gwin Fledgling: Coo ...
小尘酿鹰：咕……

The fledgling perched upside-down on her wand spots the glint of treasure and stretches out to snatch it.
金属光泽吸引了额外的目光，倒挂在术杖上的小尘酿鹰摇晃着抻长脖子，想要够到女巫的宝物。

Rhiannon: Stop it.
小瑞安侬：嘿！别闹。

She flicks the bird's still-tender beak.
她屈指轻轻弹了弹小鹰半软的尖喙。

Adar Llwch Gwin Fledgling: Coo!
小尘酿鹰：咕……！

The fledgling gives an indignant little shake.
小尘酿鹰不服气地晃了晃身体。

Hotelier: We don't turn away anyone here—witches, shamans, druids ... Why, even vampires'll get clean, cozy beds if they've the coin for it.
旅店招待：我们不拒绝任何客人：女巫、萨满、德鲁伊……哪怕是血食怪，只要能付得起房费，就能换取一张温暖干净的床铺。

He glances at the two Adar Llwch Gwins.
他斜眼看向两只尘酿鹰。

Hotelier: Pets, though—those'll be two coins extra.
旅店招待：但是带宠物入住需要额外收取两枚硬币的费用。

Rhiannon: T-Two?! No, that's too much! Oh, how about this! I'll clean the room before I go. You won't find so much as a single feather—promise!
小瑞安侬：两枚？！这、这有些太贵了吧！我在离开前会把房间打扫干净的，我保证你们看不见任何一根羽毛。

A soft gray plume drifts onto the counter. Molting season strikes again.
娇嫩的灰色羽毛轻飘飘地落在柜台上，为换毛季增添了一丝尴尬。

Hotelier: ...
旅店招待：……

Rhiannon: ...
小瑞安侬：……

Hotelier: You know, we once had a guest who insisted on bringing his Ceffyl Dŵr inside. That water horse nearly flooded the whole room! Charged him for an entire new set of bedding, I did.
旅店招待：上回有位客人执意要带他的凯菲杜尔进客房，结果那匹水马差点把房间都淹了！我不得不向他收取更换整套床上用品的费用。

Hotelier: Be glad I'm only charging a coin per parrot.
旅店招待：你该庆幸你饲养的只是灰鹦鹉。

Rhiannon: They're not parrots.
小瑞安侬：他们不是鹦鹉……

She mutters under her breath but relents, setting seven gleaming coins on the counter.
她嘟囔着，将七枚闪闪发光的钱币放在了柜台上。

The lion cubs stamped on them roar silently at him.
钱币上的幼狮对着他无声咆哮。

Hotelier: And what would these be, miss?
旅店招待：……这是什么？

Rhiannon: Sharpodonties, sir.
小瑞安侬：利齿子儿，先生。

Hotelier: I know what they are, but we only take shillings, miss. Shillings!
旅店招待：我知道这是什么，但我们只收先令，小姐，先令！

Rhiannon: Shillings, um ...
小瑞安侬：先令……

This town may not bar its doors to arcanists, but it's far from being the witches' fair in Caernarfon.
尽管这座小镇并未对神秘学家关上大门，但这里不是卡那封的女巫集市。

Sharpodonties have never passed for legal tender in the mundane world. Folks around here are more familiar with the King's profile looking back at them as opposed to a snapping lion.
十三条棱边的货币始终不是世俗社会的一般等价物，在这里，国王的左侧脸才是能让人们脸笑开花的东西。

Rhiannon: R-Right.
小瑞安侬：对、对哦……

She smiles sheepishly and begins digging through her bag.
她讪讪地笑了笑，把手伸进行囊里摸索。

Spare clothes, biscuits, a jar of chocolate spread ... So many items, yet not a trace of any human-accepted coins.
换洗衣物、饼干、巧克力酱罐子……手指从各色杂物上一一滑过，却始终没能摸到此刻急需的救命金属。

Hotelier: You DO have shillings, don't you, miss?
旅店招待：你有钱吗，小姐？

Rhiannon: O-Of course I do! It's just ...
小瑞安侬：有的！当然有的！只是……

She scratches her cheek, embarrassed.
她尴尬地挠了挠脸。

Rhiannon: My mam threw me out, you see. It was all so sudden. I didn't really have the chance to pack proper money.
小瑞安侬：我是被家人赶出来的，没来得及准备好通用的货币……

Hotelier: So, you haven't any then.
旅店招待：所以你没钱。

Rhiannon: I, erhm ...
小瑞安侬：我……呃……

He pushes the Sharpodonties back to her.
旅店招待把七枚利齿子儿推回了她面前。

Hotelier: Best you go home and patch things up with your mam while there's still daylight? Better that than spending a night in the stable with your parrots.
旅店招待：趁着天还没黑，回家认个错吧。这样总不至于带着你的鹦鹉睡在马棚里。

Rhiannon: Oh, I know! How about I help you out around the hotel instead?
小瑞安侬：先生，我可以用劳动来换！

She's not ready to give up on a cozy bed just yet.
投宿的热情没有被浇灭。

Rhiannon: I know plenty of handy arcane skills—boiling water without fire, prepping ingredients without a knife, and making a bed before you can even say "fluff the pillows."
小瑞安侬：我会很多神秘术，让水无火沸腾，不用刀就能处理食材，或者瞬间叠好被子……

Hotelier: With all due respect, miss, this isn't some pragmatist's office. I won't be responsible for putting my neighbors out of a job.
旅店招待：这里不是实用主义的办公室，我不会让我的邻居们“技术性失业”。

Rhiannon: P-Pragmatist?
小瑞安侬：实、实用主义？

Hotelier: You know, pragmatism, it's all the rage with those big shots in London.
旅店招待：没错，这是伦敦那帮大人物们嘴里最新的议题。

Though the hotelier himself doesn't fully understand the debates he's read about in the papers, the buzzword leaves Rhiannon downright stupefied.
旅店招待也并不完全理解那些报纸上的辩论，但这些先锋词语很明显打晕了小瑞安侬。

???: Now now, what's all the fuss about?
？？？：做什么吵吵闹闹的？

Hotelier: Mrs. Owen, this young witch here's trying to check in without a shilling to her name!
旅店招待：欧文夫人，这儿有位小女巫没钱还想强行住宿！

Rhiannon: That's not. I wasn't trying to—!
小瑞安侬：强行住宿？！我才没有！

The old woman raises a finger to her lips.
老妪竖起食指抵在唇间，示意噤声。

Mrs. Owen: Come now. We don't make scenes at the front desk. Think of the other guests.
欧文夫人：在前台闹像什么话，万一吵到其他住店的客人怎么办？

Hotelier: This ...
旅店招待：这……

Mrs. Owen waves her hand.
欧文夫人挥了挥手。

Mrs. Owen: Go help in the kitchen. I'll take care of this.
欧文夫人：去厨房帮忙吧，这里交给我处理。

The hotelier gives a respectful nod and steps away from the counter. His deference speaks volumes about the woman.
招待恭敬地应声，随即退出了柜台。他毫不掩饰顺从与尊敬，这无声宣告了欧文夫人的身份。

Rhiannon: Ma'am, I'm so sorry. I didn't mean to cause any trouble. I just ...
小瑞安侬：夫人，非常抱歉！我不是故意的，我只是……

Mrs. Owen: Thrown out by Dobharchú Tywyll, were you?
欧文夫人：只是被多沃卡·缇威尔赶出门了，对吗？

Rhiannon: What?
小瑞安侬：什……

A bit of a mouthful for most, but to Rhiannon, that name carries a very different weight.
一个在现代英语中略显拗口的名字，但它对小瑞安侬而言，有着另外一层深刻的含义。

Rhiannon: You know—!
小瑞安侬：您、您认识——！

Mrs. Owen: That I do. Also had tea with Ivan, taught little Gwen her letters ... Why, I even went all the way to London to help Thomas get his hands on the latest bicycle.
欧文夫人：是的。我不仅认识多沃卡，还和伊凡喝过茶，教小格温认过字母，在伦敦帮托马斯买到了最新款的脚蹬车……

Rhiannon's mother, father, cousin, and uncle. And that cerulean bicycle—she'd fallen off it once herself.
那是小瑞安侬的母亲，父亲，表亲和叔父。她甚至从那辆靛青色的脚蹬车上摔下来过。

Her eyes are wide as saucers.
驯鸟女巫瞪圆了眼。

Mrs. Owen: And you, little Aderyn! Well, not so little now, I suppose. How have you been all these years? Old enough to travel on your own now, are you?
欧文夫人：还有你——阿德琳，你这些年过得还好吗？你是不是也到可以外出远游的年纪了？

Rhiannon: I ... Mam and I have been secluded for so long, and yet you still know my name.
小瑞安侬：我和妈妈已经远离人群很久了……但您甚至能说出我的名字来。

Rhiannon: I take it you're an old friend of the family?
小瑞安侬：您是……一位老朋友，对吗？

The Adar Llwch Gwin dangling from her wand stares at Mrs. Owen as though trying to burn a hole right through her.
倒挂在术杖上的尘酿鹰目光炯炯，几乎要在欧文夫人的脸上看出一个洞来。

Rhiannon: Hey, quit your staring! It's terribly rude!
小瑞安侬：别这么盯着人看……！太不礼貌了。

Mrs. Owen: It's quite alright, dear. I'm glad to see you and these little birds are getting along better now. Last time we met, you were little more than a babbling babe, and they were chasing you all over the place.
欧文夫人：没关系，看来你现在和这些小鸟儿相处得不错。回想我们上次见面时，你甚至还不会说话，就已经被尘酿鹰追得满地跑。

Mrs. Owen: Oh, how I miss those days, seeing the Tywyll children and their birds at the fair, laughter and chirps filling the streets.
欧文夫人：真怀念能看见缇威尔的孩子们和鸟儿一起逛集市，用鸟鸣和笑声装点街道的日子。

Mrs. Owen: Your family was quite the prominent one back then. Had that big estate right outside of town. Not like now.
欧文夫人：缇威尔那时也还是个响当当的大家族，一大家子人在领地里生活，和镇上的我们比邻而居，不像现在……

Rhiannon: ...
小瑞安侬：……

Mrs. Owen: Huh.
欧文夫人：噢……

Mrs. Owen catches herself a moment too late.
家族旧友迅速意识到了自己的失言。

Mrs. Owen: Forgive me, dear. You need a room for the night, don't you?
欧文夫人：原谅我，孩子。你需要留宿一夜，对吗？

Rhiannon: Yes. Glyndŵr dropped me off. I have two, um, I guess you could call them friends. We agreed to meet here in town.
小瑞安侬：是的。格林杜尔把我扔在了镇上，我还有两位，呃……朋友，我和两位朋友约好了在这儿见面。

Mrs. Owen: Oh, that heartless Dobharchú, sending you off without so much as a shilling. I'd love to put you up for the night, but once word gets out ...
欧文夫人：可狠心的多沃卡没给你准备先令。我也很想收留你一夜，可一旦开了这个头……

Rhiannon: Don't worry. I understand, Mrs. Owen.
小瑞安侬：我明白的，欧文夫人。

Rhiannon picks up her bag and gives a small bow.
小瑞安侬掂了掂行囊，向女店主微微欠身。

Mrs. Owen: Where are you off to, then?
欧文夫人：你要去哪儿？

Rhiannon: The ruins just outside town, ma'am. My friends and I would sometimes spend the night there when we were little.
小瑞安侬：镇外的遗址，夫人。小时候我和玩伴们常在那儿过夜。

Mrs. Owen: That won't do. That won't do at all!
欧文夫人：这怎么行呢。

Mrs. Owen takes her hands gently.
欧文夫人亲切地拉过她的手。

Mrs. Owen: Rest here a while. Have some tea and biscuits. And if you're tired, just let me know, and I'll show you to the break room.
欧文夫人：在这儿歇歇脚吧，喝喝茶，尝尝饼干。要是困了，我带你去休息室睡一会。

Rhiannon: This ...
小瑞安侬：这……

Rhiannon hesitates. Mrs. Owen's words carry a familiar warmth, yet they haven't seen each other in so long ...
她有些犹豫。虽然对方的话语中处处透露出对自己的熟悉，但她们实在是太久没有见面了——

So long, in fact, that Rhiannon can't find so much as a trace of her in her memory.
或者说，时间长到小女巫的记忆里已经找不到这位夫人的影子。

Mrs. Owen: I know, it's been so long I must seem like a right stranger to you. But we've got plenty of time to get reacquainted now, don't we?
欧文夫人：让我们重新认识，现在也来得及，我们还有大把时间呢。

Mrs. Owen: Come, have a seat. You can tell me about yourself, your remarkable little birds, or even your mean old witch of a mother, if you'd like.
欧文夫人：来吧，聊聊你，聊聊你的神奇小鸟儿，或者聊聊你那狠心的女巫母亲。

Mrs. Owen: She's complained to me more times than I can count—said you never wanted to set out on your journey. And yet, here you are! So, where has our little Tywyll bird decided to fly off to?
欧文夫人：你妈妈向我抱怨过好多次啦，说女儿不愿踏出远游的步子。可我们缇威尔的小鸟儿，现在这又是要往哪儿飞呀？

Hearing the secret she'd only ever told her mother spoken back to her shatters what's left of her defenses.
更加秘密的，只在母女之间传递着的心事打破了小女巫的防线。

Rhiannon: I can't believe she'd ... *sigh*
小瑞安侬：她怎么连这个都往外说……

Rhiannon: Guess you might as well hear the whole thing, then. It all started that morning, the day before my birthday.
小瑞安侬：一切要从我生日前的那一天早晨讲起……

02  【3.7】小瑞安侬玩法02

Comes a Gentle Wind
风从何处来

Aderyn: Here    I    humbly    stand   at   the   cleft   of   the   earth,   beseeching   Annwn's   mercy.
阿德琳：我立于大地之隙，祈求安温垂怜。

Aderyn: I   offer   my   flesh   as   pledge:   grant   me   sight   of   this   world   and   the   worlds   beyond!
阿德琳：我愿以肉身为凭，求终得见那未曾降生常世之灵！

Her eyes meet another's in the darkness, flashing with a golden hue.
她在黑暗中和金色的双眼对视。

Aderyn: Mhmm.
阿德琳：嗯……

Nothing. Not so much as a breeze.
无事发生，连风都未曾动过。

By the Mirror Lake
镜湖畔

Light shimmers across the water. The giant bird's half-translucent tail feathers catch the color of it and quiver.
只有光线蹦跳着落在了湖面上，巨鸟原本半透的尾羽应激般生长出了颜色。

Glyndŵr: ...
格林杜尔：……

Aderyn: Glyndŵr, did it work? Did the bond form?
阿德琳：格林杜尔，契约成功了吗？

The bird stares back at her without response.
巨鸟沉默地看着她，未作回答。

Aderyn: Again? Hmm. Did I mess the incantation up somehow? Where'd I go wrong?
阿德琳：又失败了？难道我的施术能力出了什么问题……

She draws a deep breath and points her wand at the lake.
她深吸了一口气，将术杖指向湖面。

Aderyn: Water ... Change the water.
阿德琳：水……让水改变的神秘术……

The calm lake ripples as her incantation spreads across it. Then the water churns, and what rises from its surface is no longer mist.
平静的水面被落入湖中的神秘术激活，一阵翻腾后，从湖面升起的不再是水雾。

Aderyn: ...
阿德琳：……

Colorful crystalline bubbles float upward, and the light they refract dances with the shadows on her face.
盈着琉璃色彩的泡沫缓缓上浮，光影的碎屑落进了少女的眼中。

*pop*
……“啪。”

The Adar Llwch Gwin tracks the rising bubbles with wide eyes. With but a flap of its wings, the bubbles burst one by one, falling back into the lake and taking the dreamy scene with them.
尘酿鹰睁着滚圆的眼睛，视线追着泡沫向上移动。它轻轻振翅，泡泡逐一裂开，童话仙境般的景色随之消失。

Adar Llwch Gwin Fledgling: Coo!
小尘酿鹰：咕！

The little bird beats its wings to take off from the wand and settle atop Aderyn's head.
小鸟扑腾翅膀，从术杖顶端起飞，悠悠落到了阿德琳的头顶。

Aderyn: Stop it.
阿德琳：别闹。

She lifts the fledgling from her hair and holds it close.
她抬手把尘酿鹰抱了下来，拢在了怀里。

Aderyn: I still can't bond with Glyndŵr.
阿德琳：我又失败了，我还是没能和格林杜尔缔结契约。

Aderyn: "A Tywyll of sixteen must journey far."
阿德琳：“十六岁的缇威尔须去远方。”

Aderyn: "With blessings and a dear companion's sight ..."
阿德琳：“带着祝福，带着密友的眼睛……”

Aderyn: How am I to carry on the tradition if I can't so much as ...
阿德琳：虽然不知道这个传统还能不能延续下去……

With a sigh, she returns the little bird to her wand.
她叹了一口气，把小鸟托回了术杖上。

Then she turns to face the still-silent Glyndŵr.
她转身看向沉默的巨鸟。

Aderyn: You still won't accept me. Am I ... Am I still not worthy, Glyndŵr? Is that it?
阿德琳：你一直不肯承认我，是我做得还不够好吗，格林杜尔？

Aderyn murmurs her worries as she strokes the Adar Llwch Gwin's smooth breast feathers.
女巫抚摸着尘酿鹰顺滑的胸羽，喃喃自语着。

Just as she's done after each of her many failed attempts before.
一如此前千百次施行契约术失败后一样。

Glyndŵr: ...
格林杜尔：……

Glyndŵr looks down at the dejected little witch and lets out a rumble unbefitting of a bird.
巨鸟垂眼看向沮丧的小女巫，喉中发出一阵不似鸟类的咕哝。

Aderyn: Nothing for it but to keep trying, I suppose.
阿德琳：我得再加把劲才行。

But that inner door, as ever, stays shut.
但显然，那未能敲开阿德琳脑子里那扇紧闭的门。

Aderyn: Let's go home, Glyndŵr.
阿德琳：回家吧，格林杜尔。

Glyndŵr: Coo.
格林杜尔：……咕。

Glyndŵr stoops obediently onto the ground, letting Aderyn climb onto his back.
巨鸟咕哝一声，乖顺地趴卧在地，好让小女巫爬上自己的脊背。

Tylwyth Teg Berry Garden
妖精莓果园

*bang*
“砰——砰——”

A sudden thudding against the wooden door startles the drowsy birds sheltering under the eaves of an otherwise quiet orchard.
果园一片寂静，撞击木门的不和谐音惊起了屋檐下困倦的飞鸟。

The vine-choked latch rattles under the impact, no longer strong enough to contain the fearsome creature within—
被树藤寄生的门栓在撞击中摇晃了几下，最终无力继续囚禁鸟房中的恶兽——

Which promptly rolls its small and rotund form out.
一颗周身布羽的小圆球从敞开的门中滚了出来。

???: Coo ...
？？？：咕……

Though freshly hatched, its feathered wings still wet and sticky, even, the hatchling waddles out for an adventure.
破壳不久的小小生灵，羽翼上尚留着些黏糊的胎膜，就摇摇摆摆地闯了出来。

Adar Llwch Gwin Fledgling: Coo?
