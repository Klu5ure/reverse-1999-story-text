01  美好年代 / La Belle Époque

La Belle Époque
美好年代

Countryside, Rouen
鲁昂乡村

The rural town of Rouen dozes in the afternoon sun. Inside the house, Agnès recites scripture in a steady, devout tone.
午后的鲁昂乡村瞌睡在阳光里。屋内，艾格尼丝语气平稳地念诵经书，透出虔诚的安乐。

Marianne rests beside her cousin, softly repeating the words, attempting to let their weight settle in her heart.
玛丽安娜伏在妹妹的手边，轻声跟读，试图让经文厚重地落到心里。

Agnès: "And he that searcheth the hearts knoweth what is the mind of the Spirit, because he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God."
艾格尼丝：“洞察人心的，知晓圣灵的意思，因为圣灵照着神的旨意替圣徒祈求。”

Agnès: "And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to His purpose ..."
艾格尼丝：“爱神的人得天主一切协助，就是照祂旨意蒙召的人……”

Marianne's attention wanes as a robin's call echoes from outside the window.
艾格尼丝的声音渐远，窗外知更鸟的鸣叫吸引了玛丽安娜的注意。

A bird said to carry the blood of saints through generations. Its song is meant to bring salvation—but all she feels is restlessness.
世代衔着圣人之血的鸟，歌声会带来救赎，但她只觉得烦闷。

Nothing seems to be able to take her mind off the quarrel that is still raging in the other estate. A quarrel caused by her.
她似乎被不断提醒着，一场因她而起的争吵此刻在另一座宅邸里尚未停歇。

Marie, stop it! Scripture requires the full attention of the mind. Focus. Be devout ...
……不！玛丽，别再想了，聆听经文时该怎么做？专心虔敬……

Agnès: Marie.
艾格尼丝：玛丽。

Marianne: Huh? ... Agnès, what's wrong?
玛丽安娜：……艾格尼丝，怎么了？

Agnès: I wished to ask thee the very same question. Thou didst rush to me, begging of me to read the scripture for thee, and yet ...
艾格尼丝：这该由我来问你呀。你匆忙地来见我，要我为你读经，可是……

Agnès: Marie, which chapter did I recount to thee this last moment?
艾格尼丝：玛丽，刚刚我读的是哪一章节？

Marianne: ...
玛丽安娜：……

Marianne: Agnès ... you know as well as I that I have never been as familiar with the scripture as you.
玛丽安娜：艾格尼丝，你是知道的……我……向来没有你熟悉这些文字。

Agnès gives her a long, disapproving look.
艾格尼丝不赞同地望着她，不发一言。

Marianne: ... *sigh* you're right, "You shall not lie to one another." The Lord does not look kindly upon such things.
玛丽安娜：……好吧，我不该撒谎，这是主所厌恶的。

Agnès doesn't speak, but her gaze turns gentle as she encourages her cousin to open up.
艾格尼丝没有打断她，以眼神鼓励玛丽安娜敞开心怀。

Marianne: You know, Agnès ...
玛丽安娜：你知道吗，艾格尼丝……

Marianne: Were we to head to Paris this very moment and wander along the Seine, we would see all manner of wonders from the four corners of the world.
玛丽安娜：如果现在去到巴黎，我们将在塞纳河畔见到来自世界各地的新东西。

Marianne: Auntie Suzanne says incandescent lights countless in number illuminate the night there, and great trains travel through tunnels deep beneath the ground.
玛丽安娜：姑母说，数千盏白炽灯会照亮黑夜，脚下地铁会一路疾驰。

Marianne: And the cinema! Oh my, Agnès—horses, true as anything, gallop on the screen ...
玛丽安娜：还有“电影”！马儿会在幕布上真实地奔跑……

Her thoughts had been suppressed too long. Now given the chance, they pour out like a river.
这些话压抑了太久，得到机会便像河水般涌了出来。

Agnès: Ah, yes! Thou spakest of this before ...
艾格尼丝：噢！是你之前和我说过的……

Agnès: The, um ... The Exposition Universelle, was it?
艾格尼丝：嗯……万国博览会，对吗？

Marianne knows her cousin is only feigning interest. The hustle and bustle of Paris has never stirred her as much as the scripture.
玛丽安娜知道妹妹对此并不关心，巴黎的喧闹远不如一句简短的箴言能让她心神澎湃。

But Agnès always shows just enough curiosity and concern to gently support Marianne.
但艾格尼丝会展现恰到好处的关心与好奇，体贴地托住玛丽安娜的情绪。

Marianne: Exactly! Oh, how I begged and begged that she take me. And finally, she agreed! She even said we would visit the Eiffel Tower.
玛丽安娜：没错！我太想去了，就拜托了姑母，她好心地答应了我，还说顺道带我去埃菲尔铁塔上看看。

Marianne: But Father detests the Exposition. He says technology serves only to distract us from the word of God.
玛丽安娜：可爸爸对万博会深恶痛绝，认为华而不实的技术，只会阻挡我们聆听神的箴言。

Marianne: ... Auntie Suzanne and Father quarreled still upon my leaving.
玛丽安娜：……我出门前，姑母还在和爸爸争吵。

Marianne: It was not my intent to bring strife to the family. Perhaps ... perhaps I should never have had such thoughts at all ...
玛丽安娜：这是我不愿看到的，或许……我真不该有这样的念头……

Agnès: Hmm ... arguments between the grown are a fearsome thing indeed. They hesitate not to slice at one another with words.
艾格尼丝：嗯……我也害怕大人们的争吵，他们总是不在意用语言伤害彼此。

Agnès: But such an act is against the Lord, it is plain. Our Father in heaven wisheth a life of peace for us, one without strife.
艾格尼丝：可这也明明违背了主的教导呀，天父要我们与人和睦，远离纷争。

Agnès: Nanny says we must all act in accordance with God's plan.
艾格尼丝：嬷嬷也说我们所做的一切都必然符合神的计划。

Agnès: Just as it is His will that I journey to the monastery in Saint-Lizier two days hence, perhaps it is His will that thou goest to Paris.
艾格尼丝：就像两天后，我要去到圣利济耶的修道院，说不定神的旨意也必要你去巴黎呢。

Agnès's words are fair and kind—not just to comfort Marianne, but to live out the word of God.
艾格尼丝的话语公正而善良，仿佛不只是为了宽慰玛丽安娜，而在于时刻践行神的智慧。

Marianne: Thank you, Agnès.
玛丽安娜：谢谢你，艾格尼丝。

Agnès: Mhm. Well, I do not believe any amount of reading will lift thee from thy haze.
艾格尼丝：呼……现在就算读再多经文，对你来说用处都不大啦。

Agnès: Come, let us stroll together in the orchard. The sweet scent of the apples is quite pleasant this—
艾格尼丝：一会儿去苹果园逛逛吧，我偶尔能闻到苹果熟透后的清香……

Before Agnès can finish her sentence, a door bursts open.
艾格尼丝的话还未说完，一阵匆忙的脚步声便从走廊传来。

Maid: There you are! Mademoiselle Marianne, please gather your things. Your aunt's waiting for you outside!
女佣：玛丽安娜小姐，收好东西，您的姑母正在屋外等着您呢！

Marianne: Why ...?
玛丽安娜：什么……

Maid: To take you to the Exposition Universelle, of course! Hurry now, before your father changes his mind!
女佣：就是您想去的万国博览会呀！快，趁老爷还没后悔！

Marianne shoots Agnès a surprised look. Agnès's mouth quickly curls into a warm smile.
玛丽安娜诧异地看向艾格尼丝，后者略显惊讶，但即刻露出了真诚的笑容。

Agnès: It is as I said, is it not, Marie? Thou hast been called to Paris, and I have been granted a peaceful afternoon in the apple orchard—
艾格尼丝：看，玛丽，现在你就要坐上姑母的车去到巴黎，而我将在苹果园享受我的午后——

Agnès: God watcheth over each of us.
艾格尼丝：神已为我们安排好了一切。

World Expo, Paris
巴黎万国博览会

The glass dome of the Grand Palais refines the sunlight to a crystalline brilliance amid its Eiffel Tower-esque ironwork.
大皇宫的玻璃拱顶将阳光淬炼成晶莹的质地，支撑这座宏伟建筑的繁复铁艺让人联想到年轻的埃菲尔铁塔。

Marianne cranes her neck in an attempt to see beyond the crowd on the Champs-Élysées, the halo of the new world reddening her face.
站在香榭丽舍大街上，玛丽安娜从拥挤的人流中抬起头，新世界的光晕染红了她的脸。

Marianne: Auntie! Is our next visit to the "Pavilion of Elegance" you spake of?
玛丽安娜：姑母！下一站是不是你说的“典雅厅”？

Marianne shoots an eager look behind at her aunt. She's struggling to keep up.
玛丽安娜迫不及待地望向身后急忙追上的苏珊娜。

Suzanne: Prithee, slow thy steps, child! If I lose thee, I shall have no words to explain it to thy father!
苏珊娜：慢些！要是弄丢了你，你父亲说什么我都哑口无言了！

Marianne: But Father allowed me but two days! We cannot linger, Auntie!
玛丽安娜：爸爸只给了我两天时间，当然不能在一个地方停留太久。

Suzanne: ... D'accord. I shall make certain to show thee all there is to see here.
苏珊娜：……我保证，一定带你把所有的东西都看完。

She sighs and grasps Marianne firmly by the hand.
苏珊娜叹了口气，走上前握紧小女孩的手。

Suzanne: This, ma chérie, is the Pavilion of Elegance. The works inside are what we now call "haute couture."
苏珊娜：前面就是典雅厅，里面展出的，都是现在被称作“高级定制服”的作品。

Upon entering the pavilion, Marianne is enraptured by the dazzling garments on display.
踏入展厅，繁复华贵的服装作品令她目眩。

Marianne: *gasp*
玛丽安娜：哇……

Suzanne: To the left of thee is Mr. Worth's design ...
苏珊娜：看你的左边，是沃斯先生的设计……

Delicate embroidered patterns, bustled skirts—every inch of his designs pushes the boundaries of design.
精致的刺绣花纹，优雅的裙摆轮廓，每一寸都在拓展设计的边界。

Until now, Marianne had never imagined that something so ordinary as clothing could express the spirit of a new era.
在此之前，玛丽安娜从未想过衣服本身可以成为新时代的一种诠释。

Suzanne: He was not only the first to establish a maison de couture, but also—
苏珊娜：……他不仅是第一位创立高级时装店的人，还——

Marianne: Auntie, how does one stitch such a pattern? And these pleats, how might they be sewn?
玛丽安娜：姑母，这些纹路是怎么绣上去的？还有这些褶皱，是怎么缝制的？

Suzanne chuckles.
好奇的小姑娘打断了苏珊娜的介绍，她哑然一笑。

Suzanne: ... Perhaps thou ought to explore the subject thyself, hm?
苏珊娜：……玛丽，要不要试着自己探索一下？

Suzanne: Paris doth create all manner of fashion magazines, some featuring such techniques. I shall mail them to thee.
苏珊娜：巴黎出版了不少时装杂志，有些会刊载这些高级工艺，我会邮寄给你。

Marianne: But Father—
玛丽安娜：要是爸爸发现了……

Suzanne: Now, now, thy father is not the final word on all matters. I shall find a way.
苏珊娜：我会想办法。况且，你父亲并不总是正确的。

Marianne looks puzzled, but her aunt gives her a playful wink.
玛丽安娜不解地看向自己的姑母，后者俏皮地眨了眨眼。

Suzanne: I fear thy father groweth ever more blind to the realities of our world. This here, in Paris, is the future shaped before our very eyes.
苏珊娜：他唾弃新时代的技术，对巴黎孕育的每一样事物都视而不见，可这些都已在改变我们的未来。

Suzanne: If we all were to live like thy father, would even a fraction of all this exist?
苏珊娜：试想看，如果每个人都像你父亲那样生活，眼前的一切还有可能出现吗？

Suzanne: Of course not. Those who cling to scripture cannot fathom such creativity.
苏珊娜：答案必然是否定的，恪守经文的人没有这样的创造力。

This "rebellious" family member voices her thoughts with ease.
这位“离经叛道”的家庭成员，轻而易举地说出了大胆的话。

Marianne: I ... I know not. I have never thought of things in such a way ...
玛丽安娜：我……我不知道，我从来没有想过……

Suzanne: I am simply asking thee to linger on it, ma chérie, for there is more to life than the monastery—
苏珊娜：不着急，你还有很长的时间来思考，我只是想告诉你——

Suzanne: Thou hast the power to choose—to live a "new" kind of life.
苏珊娜：你还可以选择一种“新”的生活方式。

Suzanne: Come along. We have yet to see the Palace of Electricity at Champ de Mars!
苏珊娜：来，抓紧时间，战神广场的电力宫也不能错过呢！

Marianne snaps out of her thoughts and follows her aunt back into the crowd.
玛丽安娜愣怔地跟上苏珊娜的脚步，再次汇入人流。

02  无腰线设计 / A Dress to Make

A Dress to Make
无腰线设计

Countryside, Rouen
鲁昂乡村

When she closes her eyes, that brief trip to Paris all those years ago appears as vivid as ever in Marianne's mind.
闭上眼，短暂的巴黎之游依旧鲜活。

For six years, Marianne did not dare defy her father, and thus she entered the Ursuline convent as planned.
回到鲁昂，玛丽安娜不愿再忤逆父亲，于是六年前她依照安排进入乌苏拉修女会学习。

To be kind, to shun conflict—these are virtues of the faithful.
与人为善，弃绝纷争，这是教徒所应有的品质。

Suzanne: Thou hast the power to choose—to live a "new" kind of life.
苏珊娜：你还可以选择一种“新”的生活方式。

Marianne: I can choose ...
玛丽安娜：选择……

Marianne: My, you've grown thick ...
玛丽安娜：原来已经这么厚了。

Marianne murmurs softly to herself, the hefty book clutched in her hands.
玛丽安娜捧着书册，低声感慨。

It's a scrapbook carefully put together from clippings of fashion magazines, so thick now it's long since become too heavy to carry around.
这是她从时尚杂志上小心裁剪制成的册子，厚重到早已不能随身携带。

Aunt Suzanne kept her promise. For six years, she mailed Marianne the magazines, disguised under gospel covers.
姑母没有食言，过去六年她不断给玛丽安娜寄来这些杂志，并以福音书的封面伪装。

Marianne, taking the lead from her, glued church pamphlets over the cover. Her father never noticed the "rebellion."
她学着姑母，用教会的宣传册粘贴成封面，父亲从未发现她的“叛逆”。

Marianne: ... Grant me courage through hardship, and guide me by Your will.
玛丽安娜：……请赐予我面临困境时的勇气，让我的决定受您的指引。

Marianne: Amen.
玛丽安娜：阿门。

For the first time, she leaves the book on the table for all to see and walks down her chosen path.
她第一次将这本册子大方地放在桌上，接着走向选择的岔路口。

Immanuel: Absurd! Hast thou forgotten thy heritage?
埃曼努埃尔：荒谬！你还记得自己是石像鬼吗？

Immanuel: Thou carriest the blood of the Gargoyles. There is naught but the word of God that may shield thee from utter corruption.
埃曼努埃尔：种族的血脉在你身上流淌，你就得时刻倚靠神的教诲让自己免于堕落。

Immanuel: First, thou didst cast off the few gifts offered by our bloodline, and now, thou wouldst reject the teachings of the monastery?
埃曼努埃尔：你不仅不愿花时间钻研血脉天赋，现在还想脱离修道院的教导？

Immanuel: Dost thou willfully drive thyself to demonhood?
埃曼努埃尔：你想变成真正的恶魔吗？

His hair may have grayed at the temples, but his commanding presence has remained utterly unchanged.
父亲的发鬓已经染白，但惯于主导所有的威严，并未随年龄减退。

Marianne clenches her fists, pushing past her fear to meet her father's rage-filled eyes head-on.
玛丽安娜握紧拳头，刻意忽略了胆怯，迎上父亲的怒气。

Marianne: True faith is the pure connection between oneself, God, and the Holy Spirit. To put too much reverence in the likes of churches and monasteries is to head directly into ruin.
玛丽安娜：信心在于认识神与圣灵，只想着敬畏教堂和修道院才会走向毁灭。

Marianne: Thus, no matter where I am, so long as I keep the Lord's grace in my heart, I will not fall into depravity.
玛丽安娜：哪怕我去的是公立中学，但我的心里始终确信主的恩赐，就不会步入堕落。

Marianne: Secular education will ink no stain on my bloodline, either, for reason is a far greater tool in discerning good from evil than our gift.
玛丽安娜：世俗教育也不会玷污我的血脉，新时代的理性能帮我更好地实践辨别善恶的天赋。

Immanuel: I shall not suffer thee to attend public school, no matter how thou dost argue.
埃曼努埃尔：……不管你怎么狡辩，我都不会同意你去公立中学。

Immanuel: This matter is finished.
埃曼努埃尔：这个话题到此结束。

The standoff is broken by the arrival of an elderly figure.
父女俩毫不退让，僵局直到一位年迈的老者到来才得以被打破。

Madeleine: Hmph ... Scripture is not a sword to be wielded in a quarrel.
玛德莱娜：咳……经文上的教诲，不该用于争论攻击吧。

Marianne: Grandmother!
玛丽安娜：奶奶！

Immanuel: Mother, thou ought to be abed at this hour. For what art thou—
埃曼努埃尔：母亲，您这时应该在午睡才是，怎么……

Madeleine: How could one sleep through such clamor?
玛德莱娜：你们吵架的声音这么大，我怎么睡得好呢？

Marianne: My apologies, Grandmother ...
玛丽安娜：对不起，奶奶……

She waves her hand dismissively.
白发苍苍的老人摆了摆手。

Madeleine: Let Marie go.
玛德莱娜：让玛丽去吧。

Immanuel: What?
埃曼努埃尔：什么？

Madeleine: Church schools closeth their doors one after another. It is not an easy labor to find undefiled teachings these days.
玛德莱娜：教会学校不断关停，现下早就难以获得纯净的教导了。

Madeleine: Allow Marianne to see it for herself. Only then will she truly comprehend thy words.
玛德莱娜：但如果玛丽没有亲身得证，她绝不会相信吧？

Madeleine: She is young; she will come to understand thee in time. Why let such conflict shake our halls and taint thy spirit for so small a matter?
玛德莱娜：她还这么小，等她长大自然懂得你现在的苦心，何必要在这里毁掉父女情谊呢？

Her words ease the tension in the room.
老人的言论缓和了厅室中剑拔弩张的气氛。

Madeleine: The ever-stubborn Marie, hast thou made up thy mind? Where dost thou wish to study?
玛德莱娜：倔脾气的玛丽妮特，你想好要去哪所中学了吗？

Marianne: Rouen Girls' School!
玛丽安娜：鲁昂女子中学！

Madeleine: So shall it be.
玛德莱娜：好，那就这样做吧。

Madeleine: Now, return to thy room and seek forgiveness of the Lord for thy behavior.
玛德莱娜：快回房祷告，刚才这番动静可不是神所乐意见的。

Marianne: Oh, thank you, Grandmother! Thank you dearly!
玛丽安娜：奶奶，谢谢您！

Immanuel: Mother, thou must not indulge her so!
埃曼努埃尔：母亲，您不能在这件事上这么纵容她！

Immanuel: Marianne hath already deigned to speak akin to the common folk; were she to place any more earthly wisdom above the true path, she shall never learn to discern vice from virtue ...
埃曼努埃尔：要是玛丽安娜将世俗的庸识误认为真道，那她就更学不会辨清善恶了……

Marianne has already skipped happily along to her room.
玛丽安娜领会到祖母的好心，顺着她的意思，雀跃地转身回房。

She is blissfully unaware of her father's anguished complaints.
父亲痛心而无奈的抱怨轻巧地被挡在门外。

画过圣号，念过祷文，玛丽安娜依言做完了祷告。

军事顾问：士兵，你短视且懦弱，根本不明白战争的本质。

Marianne: ... The true nature of war?
玛丽安娜：……战争的本质？

Marianne: I have seen the face of it. An excuse to spend lives like currency for your own gain ...
玛丽安娜：不过是借用军队便利地让人们替你送死……

Marianne: And to absolve yourself of all guilt for your heinous acts.
玛丽安娜：继而毫无愧疚地实现自己的私利。

Marianne: War is naught but a tool to exercise your ambition.
玛丽安娜：战争只是你用来宣泄自己狂妄和野心的手段罢了。

玛丽安娜：你不是为了祖国，你只是为了你自己！

Military Advisor: Heh ... And who do you think authorized me to make contact with Manus Vindictae?
军事顾问：呵……你以为是谁授权我站在这里与重塑之手接洽？

Military Advisor: Soldier, it is you who has betrayed your country and forgotten your duty.
军事顾问：士兵，是你背弃了自己的祖国，忘记了自己的尊严。

Military Advisor: You fear death and reject the honor of war!
军事顾问：你只知道恐惧死亡，却不肯接受征战的荣耀！

A raven glides down from a distant train car, lands lightly on the officer's shoulder, flaps its wings, and flies off.
一只渡鸦从远方列车车厢飞来，轻巧地落在军官肩上，它扇了扇翅膀，继而离去。

Military Advisor: ... Hmph. As expected, they've rejected the deal. Negotiations have failed.
军事顾问：……哼，他们果然拒绝了协议，和谈失败。

Military Advisor: So what now, soldier? War resumes tomorrow.
军事顾问：士兵，你要怎么办呢？从明天开始，战争依旧继续。

Military Advisor: And those who carry the glory of our nation in their hearts will be sent onward to crush our enemies.
军事顾问：它将载着所有渴望国家荣誉的人，一路前进，直至碾碎历史的仇敌。

Military Advisor: And you? Which will it be? Fight and become a hero, or turn back a coward?
军事顾问：你呢？你要选择成为逃兵，还是民族的英雄？

Marianne: No ...
玛丽安娜：不……

The ground shakes violently. Her insides burn.
地面再次剧烈抖动，火焰在内脏上肆意燔烧。

Marianne: Huff ... huff ...
玛丽安娜：呼……呼……

Marianne gasps for air.
玛丽安娜几近窒息。

At the end of a dark, narrow corridor, she sees the stone Gargoyle receding into the distance.
灰暗的窄道尽头，她看见石像鬼雕像逐渐远去。

Ladislas: ... We Gargoyles are the enactors of His divine judgment in a demonic guise. We are the guardians of faith ...
拉蒂斯：……石像鬼以恶魔之姿彰显神的威严，以污秽守卫圣洁……

Ladislas: Only when this war doth end shall Agnès return unto us ...
拉蒂斯：战争结束，艾格尼丝才会重回我们身边……

Agnès: Marie ... what immortal hand or eye could frame the cause behind these deadly ends?
艾格尼丝：玛丽，这场战争，究竟是为了什么？

Marianne: Agnès ...
玛丽安娜：艾格尼丝……

She closes her eyes, her finger trembling by the trigger.
她闭上眼，搭在扳机上的食指微微颤抖。

Military Advisor: So. You choose desertion.
军事顾问：所以，你选择做逃兵。

Military Advisor: You choose to kill your compatriot, your commander! You are the traitor!
军事顾问：你选择杀害你的同胞、你的将领，你才是叛国者！

Marianne: Whoso sheddeth man's blood ... by man shall his blood be shed.
玛丽安娜：……凡流人血的，他的血也必为人所流……

Marianne: To kill a fellow countryman is to willingly put a stain upon my soul. I confess to You ...
玛丽安娜：夺去同胞的生命，是我自寻污秽，我向您忏悔……

Military Advisor: I command you to lower your weapon!
军事顾问：士兵，放下你的枪！

Marianne: I shall ever bear the cross, and in death, l shall face Your judgment ...
玛丽安娜：我背负上我的十字架，死后愿意接受您的审判……

Military Advisor: Killing me will change nothing! You're just one person! What could you possibly do?!
军事顾问：你杀了我又能如何，你一个人又能做到什么！

Marianne: Lord ...
玛丽安娜：主……

I pray, do not abandon me!
请不要离弃我！

Military Advisor: Disgraceful ...
军事顾问：可耻的……

Marianne: I will put an end to this war.
玛丽安娜：我来终结这场战争。

Military Advisor: Rgh!
军事顾问：呃！

The bullet hits him square between the eyes. He drops, his star-stamped epaulettes stained with blood and dirt.
子弹正中额心，他倒了下去，星星肩章染上血迹与泥土。

Marianne wipes the tears from her face with her arm, her brow hardening into a look of determination.
她抹去脸上的眼泪，呆愣地站了一会儿。

Then, she steps forward into the forest, no longer as just Marianne, but as Sentinel.
随后，玛丽安娜向森林的更深处走去。
