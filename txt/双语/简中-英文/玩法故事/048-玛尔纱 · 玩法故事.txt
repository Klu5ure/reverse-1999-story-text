01  【3.3】玛尔纱玩法剧情01

On the Inside
解铃人

Hohes Schloss
高地城堡

Marsha: I, Marsha, before my brethren of the Order, do solemnly take this oath.
玛尔纱：我，玛尔纱·罗森哈特，在诸位骑士团同胞之前，立下誓言。

Marsha: I vow loyalty and obedience, to renounce all possessions, to care for my brethren, and never to forsake the Order.
玛尔纱：我承诺忠诚顺服，放弃一切财产，与众人共同生活，彼此照顾，绝不离团。

Marsha: I vow to hold the Hochland Order in reverence, to defend it faithfully, and to fulfill my duties with unwavering devotion.
玛尔纱：我将成为高地骑士团的捍卫者与永恒的朝圣者，虔诚贯彻骑士团所有使命。

Marsha: I vow to keep this oath without deceit, to heed the statutes of virtue, and to honor the counsel of my brethren—
玛尔纱：我将毫无欺诈地履行誓言，遵循良俗章程与同胞的劝勉——

Marsha: Until the day of my death.
玛尔纱：直至死亡。

A hand rests on her shoulder, bringing with it the weight of enormous responsibilities.
一只手搭上她的肩膀，偌大的使命与责任也一同落了下来。

Grand Master of the Order: In the name of our sacred Order and with the blessings of the Lord above, we accept your oath.
骑士团大团长：奉主与圣母的名义，我们如此接纳你。

Grand Master of the Order: We bestow upon you these gifts: bread, pure water, and your robes.
骑士团大团长：我们给予你面包、清水与清贫之衣。

She puts her hands together and allows the heavy cloak to settle on her shoulders.
她双手合十，等待厚重的披风覆于肩上。

Another weight shifts onto the cloak, a hand.
又有一只手搭上她的肩膀。

???: From today onwards, you walk the path of a knight.
？？？：今天起，你正式踏上这条骑士之路。

???: You will learn that it is as much a burden as an honor, Marsha ...
？？？：你须知，这并非荣耀而是重负，玛尔纱。

Marsha: I understand.
玛尔纱：我明白。

She clutches her hands together, feeling the warm moisture on her palms.
合十的手紧紧握着，直至欣喜的热汗将她的掌心沾湿。

Fallback Position, The Foundation
基金会·撤离点

The knight folds her hands once more.
骑士的双手再次合十。

A rare peace settles over the safe zone, yet it does little to soothe the dead and dying.
罕见的和平降临在安全区内，但并未为生命的凋零画上终止符。

Marsha: Brothers and sisters, you have proven your courage this day. Do not fear the darkness; I shall pray for you ...
玛尔纱：战友，你们已尽了忠诚与勇气的本分，请勿惧怕黑暗，我将为你们祈祷——

Marsha: May you rest in peace and in the light.
玛尔纱：你们将在平和与光明中安息。

She finishes her prayer and opens her eyes, composing herself once more.
祷告结束，她睁开眼，调整了一番心绪。

Since their long retreat, they've been forced to tend to the heavily wounded on the move, but for every soldier they save, another succumbs to infection.
大撤退后，有不少重伤者尽管接受了急救，但仍然没能挨过感染的侵袭。

Field Nurse: So many lives lost needlessly, if only we had proper facilities or just clean bandages ...
急救站护士：如果我们有更好的医疗条件，他们一定能活下来……

She lets out a heavy sigh. Marsha moves to comfort her.
疲惫的叹息中夹杂着懊恼。玛尔纱拍了拍对方的肩膀。

Marsha: We've done all that we could, Marguerite.
玛尔纱：我们已做了能做的一切，玛格丽特。

Marsha: Every life is precious, but it is not in our hands to decide who lives and who dies.
玛尔纱：铭记生命的重量，但不要过分苛责自己。

Messenger: Sister Marsha, Commander Creius is asking for you.
传令兵：玛尔纱医生，刻雷乌斯长官找你。

Marsha: I'm on my way.
玛尔纱：好，我马上就去。

Marsha looks over the dressing station once more before she leaves.
玛尔纱再次注视了一番急救站内的景象，这才转身离开。

Marsha: Commander Creius ...
玛尔纱：刻雷乌斯长官。

Creius: I've received a report from "Razor" Squad. They say they've encountered a Hochland Knight in Sen, not far from the frontlines.
刻雷乌斯：我刚刚收到“剃刀”小队的报告，他们在靠近前线的森城遇上了一位骑士团成员。

Marsha: ...!
玛尔纱：……！

Marsha: Someone from my Order?
玛尔纱：您是说，我过去的战友吗？

Creius: It seems so. "Razor" has attempted contact, but she made it clear she has no intention of evacuating.
刻雷乌斯：没错。“剃刀”试图与其接触，但对方十分排斥我们的接近。

Creius: I need you to go to Sen and find out what's going on.
刻雷乌斯：因此，我需要你前往森城，看看问题究竟出在哪儿。

Creius: We've temporarily put Manus Vindictae on the back foot, but it's only a matter of time until they come back in force. We can't have these sorts of loose ends blunting our efforts.
刻雷乌斯：如今我们暂时阻拦了重塑之手的计划，但仍需争取更多能对抗他们的力量。

Creius: I do believe you may be able to bridge the gap between the Foundation and your former comrade.
刻雷乌斯：你的身份，能帮助基金会拉近与骑士团之间的关系。

Marsha: I'll do all that I can.
玛尔纱：好，我会尽力的。

Marsha: But, the dressing station ...
玛尔纱：不过，急救站那边……

Creius: We're moving as many of the wounded as we can to the rear tomorrow.
刻雷乌斯：明天开始，存活下来的伤员会陆续转移到更后方的医院。

Creius: To real hospitals. They'll see proper care.
刻雷乌斯：届时有更专业的医疗团队提供救治。

Creius: There's a train to Sen in two hours. You're to leave as soon as possible.
刻雷乌斯：两个小时后会有一班火车开往森城，你要尽快动身。

Marsha: Understood, sir.
玛尔纱：我明白了，长官。

The knight touches her pauldrons as she leaves the office.
离开办公室，骑士摸了摸手臂上的盔甲。

Marsha: It's been so long ...
玛尔纱：已经很久没有和骑士团的人见面了……

She feels a wave of relief, but it's soon tinged with anxiety.
莫大的宽慰感涌上心头，其中还潜藏着一丝焦虑。

Train Station, Sen
森城·火车站

The train pulls into the station with a long whistle.
悠扬的鸣笛声后，火车缓缓进站。

Marsha feels the tension in her neck relax a little as she steps off with the rest of the crowd.
玛尔纱舒缓了一下僵硬的身躯，随着人群一同下了火车。

"Razor" Captain: Marsha, you're finally here.
“剃刀”：玛尔纱，你终于来了。

"Razor" stands waiting for Marsha at the station, a look of pained helplessness is written across his face.
前来接应玛尔纱的“剃刀”脸上流露出无可奈何的神情。

Marsha: Mr. Razor, please inform me of the situation in full.
玛尔纱：“剃刀”先生，现在情况怎么样了？

"Razor" Captain: You were told about the knight that has refused to speak with us, correct?
“剃刀”：想必你已知道，城内这位骑士并不愿与我们交流。

"Razor" Captain: Well, there's another problem; we've gotten word of extensive movement from Manus Vindictae in the region. An offensive, perhaps, or at the very least a sizeable troop redeployment.
“剃刀”：更棘手的是，这里靠近重塑之手的占领区，我们目前正在疏散人群。

"Razor" Captain: Our orders are to clear the area of all civilians, but your old comrade ...
“剃刀”：这也包括了你那位骑士团的同伴，但……

"Razor" Captain: She refuses to budge.
“剃刀”：她拒绝撤离。

He shakes his head begrudgingly.
想到那位固执的骑士，“剃刀”不免摇了摇头。

Marsha: She won't evacuate? Do you have any idea why?
玛尔纱：她不愿撤离？原因是什么？

"Razor" Captain: ... I think that's something only you can figure out.
“剃刀”：……我想只有你才能找到这个原因了。

"Razor" Captain: But if her replies are any indication, she seems intent on fighting Manus Vindictae.
“剃刀”：更令人头疼的是，她执意前往重塑之手的占领区。

Marsha: By herself? That seems entirely unreasonable.
玛尔纱：一个人前往占领区？这太危险了！

"Razor" Captain: Now you see why we wanted you.
“剃刀”：这也是我们的考虑。

"Razor" Captain: We know that the Knights of the Hochland Order are powerful. You're living proof of their capabilities.
“剃刀”：我们清楚高地骑士团成员的本领，玛尔纱，你就是鲜明的例子。

"Razor" Captain: But we can't just let her charge out alone against the Manus. It'll put our operations here at risk.
“剃刀”：但孤身一人遭遇重塑之手，发生意外的可能性太大。

"Razor" Captain: And we don't want unnecessary bloodshed.
“剃刀”：我们并不希望无谓的流血事件发生。

Marsha: Our vows are a sacred commitment; it's little wonder she would want to fight. Still, I don't understand why someone from my Order would refuse to cooperate.
玛尔纱：我不记得骑士团里有这么不讲理的人。

Marsha: There must be some reason she is being so stubborn. I wonder what she is after ...
玛尔纱：应该是有一些特别的原因，才会让她这么固执。

Marsha keeps that question in mind as she takes her leave from "Razor" and walks from the station into the city.
骑士百思不得其解。

02  【3.3】玛尔纱玩法剧情02

Where We Stand Now
如今的我们

Sen
森城

Sen has already seen the full brunt of arcane warfare, their aftereffects turning the streets into a twisted mockery of their former existence.
一座边境小城，被战争席卷后，剩下破败的残骸勉强勾勒出城镇的骨架。

But the Foundation succeeded in getting most of the population out before the fighting got too close, leaving the area like a strange lifeless moonscape.
基金会的疏散工作已经展开，街道上一派萧瑟模样。

Marsha: According to Mr. Razor, the knight should be somewhere around here ...
玛尔纱：按照地图的指示，“剃刀”先生就在这附近遇上了那位骑士……

Marsha stands at the crossroads, taking stock of her surroundings.
玛尔纱：玛尔纱站在十字路口，四下张望。

Closed doors, dark storefronts, occasionally life stirs in the form of passing refugees trudging their most treasured possessions toward the evacuation points.
周围门扉紧闭，入眼尽是提着行囊从家中离去的人。

Marsha: ... Has she already left?
玛尔纱：……那位骑士已经离开这儿了吗？

Woman: Staś! Wait!
妇人：——斯塔希！给我站住！

The shout comes from the end of the street, Marsha turns alert and ready for danger.
突然，街道尽头传来一位妇女着急的呵斥。

But no sooner does she turn than a tiny form slams into her leg.
玛尔纱刚一转身，腿上便猛地撞上了什么。

Boy: Watch it, lady! Don't you know there's a war coming?
小男孩：你——你怎么站在这儿挡路呢！

The boy tumbles out onto the street, along with his felt hat and everything he's holding.
男孩儿跌坐在地上，头上的呢帽与手中的东西也一并滚落在地。

Marsha: Entschuldigung (excuse me). Is everything alright, young man?
玛尔纱：真是抱歉，你还好吗，小朋友？

Marsha: Are you hurt at all?
玛尔纱：有哪里撞伤了吗？

Marsha crouches down to inspect the boy.
玛尔纱蹲下身来，仔细打量着男孩儿。

Boy: Leave me alone. Mama is going to catch me—
小男孩：你别管我，妈妈快要追上来了——

The boy gathers his things in a rush.
男孩儿敏捷地捞起身边掉落的物品。

Marsha: Hold on!
玛尔纱：等等！

She grabs the boy just as he is about to run, nearly causing him to stumble again.
玛尔纱将正欲逃跑的男孩儿一把拉得趔趄。

His mother runs toward them, out of breath.
不远处，男孩儿的母亲气喘吁吁地向这边跑来。

Marsha: Staś ... Can I call you that? What happened? Why are you running around out here?
玛尔纱：斯塔希……可以这样叫你吗？发生了什么，为什么要一个人往回跑？

Marsha: This place isn't safe. You should go home to your mother.
玛尔纱：你的母亲很担心你，这附近可不安全。

The boy twists with all his strength to break free from Marsha, his thin face flushing.
男孩儿用尽了力气也无法从玛尔纱手里挣脱，一张瘦削的小脸涨得通红。

Seeing that he can't escape, his shoulders slump in frustration.
半晌，眼见逃离无望，他挫败地塌下肩来。

Boy: I-I just want to deliver a gift!
小男孩：我、我只是想把临别礼物送出去而已！

Marsha: A gift?
玛尔纱：临别礼物？

Woman: Phew ... You little brat!
妇人：呼……你这臭小子！

The boy's mother at last catches up, puffing with both exasperation and exhaustion, and gives his head a smack.
男孩儿的母亲终于追了上来，无可奈何地在他头上敲了一记。

Woman: Thank you so much, madam. I swear I look away for just one second and he starts charging into trouble.
妇人：真是谢谢你！我一会儿没注意，他就悄悄跑了。

Boy: I wasn't charging into trouble! I had to deliver my gift! You always tell me to keep my word, well, that's what I'm doing!
小男孩：我才不是悄悄！我说了，要回去送临别礼物，约好的事就要做到！

Woman: There's no time for that. What if we miss the evacuation train?
妇人：都什么时候了，这样耽误下去，万一错过撤离的火车呢？

The boy pouts, but he has no argument.
男孩儿自知理亏，抿紧了嘴，眼睛里已噙了泪。

Marsha: Would you let me know what's going on here? Please, don't worry. I'm on a mission from the St. Pavlov Foundation.
玛尔纱：您介意向我说说情况吗？我来自圣洛夫基金会，今天会在城内办事。

Marsha points to the badge on her chest.
玛尔纱指了指胸前的徽章。

Marsha: If it's something as simple as delivering a gift, perhaps I can help?
玛尔纱：如果只是顺手捎一份礼物应该还是可以帮上忙。

Boy: Really?!
小男孩：真的吗？！

The boy wrings himself free and offers Marsha the item he had been carrying.
小男孩激动地挣开了母亲的手，将怀里藏得严实的物品递至玛尔纱面前。

Marsha: Is this ... a shield?
玛尔纱：这是……盾牌？

The construction is somewhat rudimentary, made of welded steel with an old belt for a strap, and it's on the smaller side, only as long as her forearm, but its purpose is unmistakable.
一块只有前臂长短的铁质盾牌，做工粗糙，背面固定着一截旧腰带。

Red paint twists into a shape across its rough surface; it's difficult to make sense of, but it seems to portray the silhouette of a building on a hill.
红颜料蜿蜒爬过表面的锈迹，隐约显出一座建筑的轮廓。

Woman: My little Staś had a bad fever. With the way things were, we were worried we wouldn't be able to get him to a doctor. But then we came across this woman.
妇人：斯塔希前几日高烧不退，我们正愁找不到药时，城里来了一位骑士。

Woman: Oh, she was wearing armor just like yours.
妇人：噢，她和你一样，身上也穿戴着盔甲。

The woman points toward Marsha's pauldrons, a bit surprised.
妇女看着玛尔纱的双臂，有些意外。

Woman: She healed Staś, and to repay her kindness, he wanted to give her a shield to match her armor.
妇人：是她治好了斯塔希。为了答谢她，斯塔希想要赠送一块和她身上的纹章一样的盾牌给她。

Woman: My husband ... he helped him make it from an old bucket.
妇人：他的父亲……用铁桶皮勉强做了出来。

Woman: With the evacuation order and all the packing, we haven't had time to give it to her. I put it away for later.
妇人：临走时，我收拾得匆忙，忘了这块盾牌已经被收进行李，也就没来得及送出去。

Woman: But then Staś happened to find it, and ... since then, I haven't had a moment's peace.
妇人：但斯塔希还是找出来了，就闹着要回去。

Marsha: ... The sigil on the shield, is it meant to be a castle on a hill?
玛尔纱：……您说的纹章，图案是不是高地上坐落着城堡的模样？

Woman: That's right. Are you ...
妇人：噢，是的。难道您……

Marsha: Yes. A fellow member of her Order. I'm looking for her.
玛尔纱：我是这位骑士的战友。我这次就是来找她的。

Marsha: Have you any idea where she is, and ... did you happen to get her name?
玛尔纱：您方便透露她目前所在的位置吗？以及，她的名字是……

Within an old but tidy house, a kettle begins to roll and cry with steam.
陈旧但整洁的民居内，沸腾的水壶呜呜尖叫着。

A woman in knightly armor sits on a wooden chair, cleaning her helmet with care.
骑士安静地坐在木椅上，细致擦拭着面甲。

It bears the scratches and scuffs of untold numbers of battles, yet it has clearly been meticulously maintained.
盔甲上已遍布经年累月的划痕，但仍然光洁，是骑士日复一日不曾懈怠的护养结果。

A knock at the door breaks the rhythm of her work.
敲门声突兀地响起。

???: Who is it?
？？？：谁？

Marsha: Sister Adelheid? Are you in there?
玛尔纱：请问是阿德海德修士吗？

The sound is soft and faint, filtered by the door, but Adelheid feels her heart skip.
门扉将声音稀释得轻而薄，阿德海德却蓦地停下了动作。

Marsha: It's me, Marsha.
玛尔纱：阿德海德，是我，玛尔纱。

Adelheid: ...!
阿德海德：——！

It's a voice she could never mistake.
这是她不会认错的声音。

Her palms feel numb as she moves toward the door; she almost involuntarily jerks it open.
手掌开始发麻，她三步并作两步跨至门边，猛地拉开门——

The face she sees behind the door is both everything she feared and hoped for.
一张熟悉但也陌生的脸。

A face she once thought gone and buried. A face that elicits grief and sorrow in every reflection. But now all she feels is joy.
阿德海德曾以莫大的悲伤与愧悔将这张脸深埋在心底，与许多不幸去世的同胞一道。

Marsha: Adelheid. It's really you ... I'm so glad.
玛尔纱：阿德海德，真的是你……

The woman in front of her is tired—maybe from a long journey—but breathing, radiating warmth.
眼前的人，面容略带疲倦——也许是因为长途跋涉，但无疑是健康的，带着鲜活的温度。

Not a skeleton, not some revenant or nightmare.
不是一具骷髅，也不是一具已然腐烂的尸身。

Living, breathing, at her doorstep.
而是活生生的人。

Adelheid: It's a miracle! Marsha, you're here! You're alive.
阿德海德：……天主在上！玛尔纱，你还活着？！

She nearly leaps toward her, her hands stretch outward, but she freezes in place from shock or awe or fear—she can't say.
她不自觉地伸出手，那似乎是一个拥抱的姿态，却因过度震惊和无措僵在了原地。

Yet Marsha reaches in and pulls her into a warm embrace.
玛尔纱没有丝毫犹豫，抱了上去。

Marsha: Yes, Adelheid, I survived "Dead Man's Hill."
玛尔纱：是的，阿德海德，我从“死人山”上活了下来。

Marsha: I'm so glad you did as well.
玛尔纱：我很庆幸，你也活着。

It is a true blessing, being able to reunite in these times of war.
战争年代，重逢是一件奢侈的事。

Adelheid lets out a long-drawn breath and nestles into the hug, before breaking from it with a reassuring pat.
阿德海德长长叹息着，重重拍了拍玛尔纱的背。

Adelheid: Come inside. The winter has come early this year; I'd rather not let the chill in.
阿德海德：进来说吧。初冬的风已能冻人的骨头。

Marsha: I have something for you, a gift of thanks from Staś.
玛尔纱：给，斯塔希说好要送给你的临别礼物。

The rough shield is delivered to the knight as promised. Adelheid takes it and turns it in her hands.

在场的每一位骑士，都曾参与过接纳礼，在众多同胞前宣誓，虔诚等待骑士的荣耀降临在自己肩上。

Marsha: I vow to hold the Hochland Order in reverence, to defend it faithfully ...
玛尔纱：我将成为高地骑士团的捍卫者与永恒的朝圣者……

Marsha: I vow to keep this oath without deceit, to heed the statutes of virtue, and to honor the counsel of my brethren—
玛尔纱：我将毫无欺诈地履行誓言，遵循良俗章程与同胞的劝勉——

Marsha: Until the day of my death.
玛尔纱：直至死亡。

Marsha murmurs her oaths beneath her breath, flooded by the memory.
玛尔纱喃喃着，当年的誓言从记忆深处翻涌而出。

Marsha: ... All the training I've done was in service of that day.
玛尔纱：……漫长而艰苦的训练，只是为了这一天。

Marsha: The day I became a "knight."
玛尔纱：成为“骑士”的这一天。

In the painting, Marsha's expression is solemn. She wears her ceremonial attire. Her folded hands tell of her expectation for the glory to come.
画面上的玛尔纱面容肃穆，身着骑士礼服，合十的双手间藏着对荣光的期盼。

Countless faces of knights, living and fallen, seem as though they could perfectly overlap with the girl's face.
无数骑士的面容，无论逝去还是幸存，都在此刻与画面上的少女重叠。

Hildegard: The knights departed from Hohes Schloss after the dubbing ceremony, carrying out their duties under the Grand Master's command.
希尔德加德：骑士们在接纳礼后，便要离开高地城堡，按照团长的指令履行职责。

Hildegard: We looked ahead to future missions, eager to prove ourselves, to return with glory earned by our swords.
希尔德加德：我们期待着未来的使命，期待自己能有所作为，期待着我们的剑能带回荣誉……

Until that "knightly glory" was laid to waste beneath the withering scythe of machine gun fire. Now, the ones that survive wander without purpose around its grave.
直到“骑士的荣耀”曝尸荒野，所有人围着它的坟墓徘徊。

But the young woman in that painting walks a different path—a narrow one, defined by her vows but no longer constrained to them.
只有画面上的女孩儿，独自走向了一条不受誓言允诺的羊肠小径。

A path too small for steeds to charge, too tight for armor to pass. So, she releases her horse, lays down her sword, and packs away her breastplate.
小径无法让马儿奔驰，也不便身着盔甲行走。于是她放生了马儿，取下骑士佩剑，将盔甲收进行囊。

Yet she keeps her pauldrons. Their silvery steel gleams as a guide, like the North Star, a reminder of what matters.
但臂甲仍留了下来，于是甲面上闪烁着的银光，就此化作了地面的北极星。

Adelheid: ... The Knights of Hochland must begin anew.
阿德海德：……骑士们的确需要一个新的开始了。

As the painting is uncovered, the distance between past and present clears away the dust of history. It opens a bloody wound long left untended. A rift that has kept them divided and weak.
画作被揭开之时，过去与当下的间隙无情地为骑士们切下身上历史的尘垢，鲜血淋漓，却又无比痛快。

Adelheid removes her cloak and gently covers the broken sword.
阿德海德取下自己的披风，妥善地将断剑包裹起来。

Adelheid: I never would have dared to dream this sword could be recovered.
阿德海德：没想到，在我放弃追寻之后，它们反而兜兜转转回到骑士们的手上。

Stranger still for it to have been found and returned by an organization she had long distrusted.
甚至是在她曾不屑与提防的基金会帮助下。

Adelheid: Though the sword is far from our Order's most precious relic. It's possible all of the items of true value are gone forever. Heh ... But it doesn't matter.
阿德海德：这并非骑士团最珍贵的物品，值钱的物件可能早已被转运走了。呵……这也不重要了。

The painting before them once proudly adorned their home's stone corridor.
画作被赠予骑士团后，装饰着城堡的长廊。

The candlesticks once burned in the meeting hall, witnessing the knights' worship and prayer.
烛台曾在会议厅燃起火焰，见证骑士们周而复始的礼拜与祈祷。

Apart from that sword, the rest of the items are humble things from the castle.
除了那把断剑外，这些曾是城堡里并不算起眼的东西。

Yet though they may be of little worth, they are rich in memories. Not all of them glorious, but small, tangible, and real.
但微不足道的物品却将骑士们过往的点滴铺展开，那并非总是光荣的时刻，反而琐碎，却足够真实。

Adelheid: Even so ... I feel our treasure has been restored.
阿德海德：也许……这才是真正价值连城的“财宝”。

Just like the shield Staś gave to Adelheid, the one she carries at her waist.
正如同阿德海德始终系在腰间，那块由斯塔希赠送的盾牌。

Adelheid: What defines a knight?
阿德海德：究竟是什么定义了“骑士”？

Adelheid asks herself.
阿德海德向自己发问。

Adelheid: ... To uphold our values and never bow to evil, to honor our brethren alive and dead through our words and our deeds.
阿德海德：……那只能是骑士从未向恶妥协的秉性，和拒绝与恶为伍的言行。

Marsha: "It is more of a heavy burden than an honor." You told me that after the dubbing ceremony.
玛尔纱：“这并非荣誉而是重负。”阿德海德，你曾在接纳礼后向我说过。

Marsha walks up to her mentor and comrade-in-arms.
玛尔纱走到阿德海德身边。

Adelheid: Yes. A knight's path is not one of extravagance and luxury. We learn to endure hardship and live simply. To keep our minds fixed on justice.
阿德海德：是啊，骑士之路不会通往奢靡的享受，骑士要学会忍耐困窘与清贫。维护正义、坚守仁慈向来不是简单的事。

Adelheid looks deep into Marsha's eyes. The future knighthood in her vision stands right beside her.
阿德海德注视着玛尔纱，她所想的骑士的未来正鲜明地站在自己身侧。

The answer is within reach.
答案触手可及。

Adelheid turns and faces the others.
阿德海德转向身后众人。

Adelheid: Brothers and sisters. After we leave this hospital, we will search for our scattered comrades.
阿德海德：修士们，从医院离开后，我们要去各地寻找失散的骑士团成员。

Adelheid: Along the way, I intend to follow Sister Marsha's advice. This age does not need mounted warriors, but it still needs the Order. We will find a new purpose: to help the vulnerable, to heal the sick, and to care for the wounded as we once did in days of old.
阿德海德：搜寻过程中，我将采纳玛尔纱修士的意见，尝试将骑士团重组为医疗救援组织。

Adelheid: The knights will distance themselves from the wicked games of war. We shall return to where we first began.
阿德海德：我们不会再奉陪那些战争游戏，而是回到自己的起点。

Adelheid: From this day on, we fight only to save lives—from the sickle of death.
阿德海德：未来，我希望骑士们只会为生命“征战”，只与死神为敌。

Adelheid: Our efforts will no longer be confined by nationality or creed.
阿德海德：因此，我们救援的目标将不受国家与民族身份的限制。

A murmur ripples through the knights, yet not a single voice rises in opposition.
在场的骑士们议论纷纷，忧虑居多，却没有反对。

Marsha listens quietly, surprised.
玛尔纱静静聆听，并未显得意外。

Hildegard: We will follow wherever you lead, Adelheid, but this is a significant change ...
希尔德加德：我们不会拒绝这个提议，但这事关所有人的未来……

Adelheid: That may be so. I am but the acting Grand Master, though it is my vision. I will convene a meeting of the Order so that we might decide on our path together.
阿德海德：那便召开骑士团会议吧，流程想必会漫长，但这也是必担的“重负”，不是吗？

Adelheid looks at "Razor."
阿德海德又看向一旁的“剃刀”。

Adelheid: If the Order chooses this path, there may come a day when we will seek to collaborate with the Foundation—provided the Foundation remains what it is today.
阿德海德：骑士团如果走在这条路上，总有一日会与基金会合作，只要基金会仍能维持如今的模样。

The squad leader bows his head in respectful agreement.
“剃刀”坦然地行了基金会的礼。

"Razor" Captain: May the peace be with us.
“剃刀”：愿和平与我们同在。

Adelheid: Sister Marsha, should that day come, I hope that you will be as you have been, a bridge between our organizations.
阿德海德：玛尔纱修士，我希望你能成为骑士团与基金会之间的桥梁。

Marsha: I will, Grand Master.
玛尔纱：我不会辜负这份使命。

The simple words are said as a solemn oath.
玛尔纱郑重地允诺。

A breeze sweeps across the plains, carrying the crisp scent of late autumn frost. As it passes through them, it seems to strip away the dust that has gathered on their Order.
一阵鲜活而热烈的山风拂过，那是来自朝阳的吐息，将骑士身上陈腐的余烬卷走。

The morning light shines on them, a promise of a new day, a new beginning.
世间万物由此显出其原有的轮廓，而骑士们也终于在这一刻，得以将现实的碎片放进“未来”的拼图之中。

Marsha raises her hand before her eyes. The brilliant dawn spills in through her fingertips.
玛尔纱抬手遮了遮眼，耀眼的光芒自指尖缝隙流淌在她面庞之上。

Marsha: Hmm ... what a good day it is.
玛尔纱：嗯……今天会是个好天气。
