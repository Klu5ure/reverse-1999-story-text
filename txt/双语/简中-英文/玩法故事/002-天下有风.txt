01  更漏长 / Dripping Inside the Hourglass

???: ...
？？？：……

???: ... I just ...
？？？：……方才……

Gradually regaining sight, the bird spies the furnishings of a simple office and the face of an old man staring back at him.
视野逐渐清晰，屋内的陈设与说话人的面庞随之切实地映入眼中。

The old man, with a face both worried and confused, seems to be reiterating a previous statement.
这是一张带有关切神色的脸，在皱纹中似乎还藏有少许疑虑。它属于正对葛天发问的男性老者。

Lizheng: So you've said your name was Gětiān Yànpíng, and you've come to this city in search of two lost belongings.
里正：依您方才所言，您名为燕平，此番入城是为寻两样随身物件。

Lizheng: Is that correct?
里正：是也不是？

The eagerness in the man's tone finally drags Gětiān's wandering soul back into his body.
略带急迫的语气正如一条缰绳，有助于拽回漫散的思绪。

Yanping: Yes. You may call me, Yànpíng.
燕平：正是。

Yanping: Forgive me, my mind is scattered—must be in need of rest after my trip.
燕平：也许是奔波劳累的缘故，我有些走神。但并不妨事。

Yanping: Whatever else do you need to know?
燕平：您还有何疑问？

Lizheng: Well, we've made a mock-up of your belongings. Please check if they match your description.
里正：噢，这是根据您的描述所绘制的图样，需得您确认一番。

Lizheng: We've never seen anything like these particular items in our city, so I'm afraid I'll have to ask you to tell us a little more about them.
里正：另外，此两样物件，城中以往并不曾有过。恐怕还需您多费些工夫，略为叙说其来历。

The old man puts the files aside and unfolds two pieces of yellowed paper.
老者将桌面的文书推往一侧，在葛天面前摊开两卷淡黄纸张。

Lizheng: Take your time. Let me know if there's anything incorrect.
里正：便在此了。您可细细查阅，如有缺漏之处，还请直言。

Yànpíng pins the pictures down with his wing and checks them over carefully.
燕平沉默着抬起一侧羽翼，压住纸张的边角，投以视线。

Yanping: Yes, it's a good rendition. You should know the wand is made of bone, and the pendant of jade.
燕平：……并无任何缺漏，与我随身的骨杖及玉佩一般无二。

Lizheng: So, are these valuable, in any way?
里正：这样说来，想必都是贵重饰物？

Yanping: No. Not as you mean it. The wand itself is no more than a common tool. Used to cast arcane skills, but no different from the calligraphy brushes, flagons, or brooms other people use.
燕平：并非如此。骨杖是我平日施术所用，与那些用毛笔、酒壶、笤帚施术之人一般，只是从身边取用的寻常物件。

Lizheng: Oh, so you use it to cast your arcane skills.
里正：原来是您的术杖。

The old man nods thoughtfully, before following with another question.
老者若有所思地点了点头，随即开口追问。

Lizheng: You just said it was a "common tool" ... So where you're from, arcanists don't need any sort of special wand?
里正：只是，依您所言“寻常”一词……在您的来处有许多神秘学家，他们所用的术杖也极为普通？

Lizheng: Flagons, brooms, swords, sticks, even, eh, you said calligraphy brushes? It seems like anything could be used to cast arcane skills?
里正：且不论“毛笔”，酒壶、扫帚，乃至刀剑棍棒，竟都可用于施展神秘术？

Yanping: I'm sure you've seen it for yourself. Arcanists are everywhere, even here I presume. Then you must know it is the caster's will that triggers the skill, the tool is only a medium, a matter of style rather than necessity.
燕平：自然。神秘学家随处可见，有何稀奇？施术不过随心念所动，又何须刻意挑选媒介？

Gětiān blinks at the man, confused at his strange ignorance of such basic precepts of arcanum.
话语中显露的差异使燕平有些疑虑，抬头望向老者。

The old man seems to read nothing into Gětiān's stare and continues.
而老者也并未回避他质询的目光。

Lizheng: Ah, well, we're sort of remote, so many things are different here ... I try not to assume.
里正：喔，大概是此地偏远，许多事难免不能与外界相同。

Lizheng: I didn't mean to offend. I only was curious, we have arcanists here of course, but we know little about those outside of our community.
里正：还望您不要见怪。城中也有许多神秘学家，我只是未曾想过，还有他处与此城相仿。

Yanping: The world is vast indeed ...
燕平：天下之大，事物亦繁多。不足为奇。

Lizheng: That's true.
里正：的确，的确。

Lizheng: So then this bone wand is your top priority. We are short of manpower right now, but I'll have it found.
里正：既然如此，看来这根术杖更为重要。
城中人力有限，我会安排他们加紧寻找此物……

Yanping: ... No.
燕平：……不。

Lizheng: Hmm?
里正：嗯……？

The old man wrinkles his face in surprise at the bird's response, yet it comes to rest back to a smile again.
这句否认使老者惊讶地抬起眉毛。但他很快就平静下来，再次挂上微笑。

Lizheng: As you said the pendent is of no great value. To be honest, it seems quite ordinary, I think it's unlikely it will be stolen.
里正：至于玉佩，您也不必担心。我便说得直些，这般普通物件，并无什么人有意拿取。

Lizheng: But the wand is a different case. The consequences of such a tool ending up in the wrong hands is certainly more severe.
里正：但您的术杖则不同。若不快些动作，恐怕会遇着有心人藏匿、偷占。这样便麻烦了。

Lizheng: Nonetheless, we will try to have them both returned to you.
里正：两枚物件，我们必定将其寻回，原样奉还。

Yanping: Thank you. I only meant that I can't say that the wand is a greater priority to me.
燕平：多谢。我只是一时难以肯定，骨杖是否“更为重要”。

Lizheng: Why is that? The wand is an arcane tool, I can only guess it must be more valuable ...
里正：这有何难？那骨杖到底也是神秘学物品，其价值……

Yanping: ...
燕平：……

The old man must have sensed Yànpíng's hesitation, so he cuts himself off to ask another more pointed question.
老者察觉到燕平的犹豫，打住了话头，另起一问。

Lizheng: Heh heh, of course, I should have asked first. Which one is more valuable to you?
里正：哈哈，也是我想得窄了。那在您看来，哪件东西价值更高，更值得珍重？

Yanping: ...
燕平：……

Gětiān replies with silence. Only the rustle of the feathers and the drip-drops of a water clock can be heard.
厅室之内仍然一片静默，仅有羽翼轻微抖动的窸窣声响，以及更漏中流水的清脆回音。

The worn stone floor is smooth and cold, proving uncomfortable to stand on compared to a branch.
地面的石板被磨损得发白，光滑而冰冷，并不如树木枝干那般易于安稳停落。

Gětiān folds his wings, with claws tapping on the stone floor gently.
燕平收拢翅膀，趾爪在石板上轻轻敲击着。

Yanping: I cannot say ...
燕平：我不知。

Yanping: I should need to think it through, before I give an answer.
燕平：此问还需多加思量。

Surprised by Yànpíng's response, the old man leans in with interest.
燕平的回答似乎并不在老者意料之中，但却使他颇感趣味。

Lizheng: I see. Please, take your time.
里正：这般，您尽可仔细思索再作回答。

Lizheng: We may need to wait some time for your belongings to be found, you're welcome to stay here and talk with me.
里正：寻物总归要费工夫，空坐也是无趣。您不若与我闲谈，在此稍待片刻。

Lizheng: I happen to need help with some questions of my own. Of course, you can ask me any that you have as well, whatever comes to mind.
里正：正好尚有些疑惑需向您请教。当然，您若有任何问题，也只管提出就是。

Yanping: By all means, go ahead.
燕平：但说无妨。

Lizheng: There is a young girl who runs a tavern in the city that has gone missing. We searched every corner but, still haven't found her.
里正：这几日，城中有一位酒坊娘子不见踪影，大家着急忙慌地寻过，无甚收获。

Lizheng: She was wearing a red dress with a blue-green top. Did you happen to see her before you entered the city?
里正：——您入城前，是否曾在周遭见过着红裙、青衫，约莫桃李年华的姑娘？

Yanping: No.
燕平：不曾。

Lizheng: She has a friend, a foreign merchant of the same age with red hair, very eye-catching. Would that help at all?
里正：那姑娘有位胡商友人，发色赤红，格外显眼，也是与她一般年纪。您又是否见过？

Yanping: No, it would not.
燕平：亦是不曾。

Lizheng: Please, search your memories ... they might have been near a bridge.
里正：您——请您再想一想，分明在一座桥边——

Yanping: A bridge?
燕平：……桥？

The word conjures up a memory. Indeed, there was a bridge.
这个字眼似乎能与记忆中的画面对应。他确实知道这样一座桥。

Yanping: Built over the cliff, broken in two. Many have tried to make it over, and nearly as many failed. At least the skeletons below tell me so.
燕平：那桥早已毁去，不可通行。桥下许多白骨，皆是想要跃过断口的人与动物所留。

Lizheng: And what about the other side of the bridge?
里正：桥的对面又是何处？

Yanping: I recall further on there are trees, yes ... then rivers, then mountains, and a heavy fog that separates this place from there.
燕平：山，树，河流，将这些景物与我们分隔的浓雾。别无其他。

Yanping: The ones you're looking for would have no reason to jump that gap, if they could even manage it ...
燕平：你要寻的二人，没有理由，也绝无可能跃过桥去。

He places a certainty in his tone, as if to stop the old man's questions, or at least convince him he in fact hasn't seen them.
他本以为，笃定的话语能令老者停止追问，也能说服自己。

Yet now he feels a rising uncertainty inside himself.
但疑虑仍旧像一阵令人不适的细小气流，在羽毛之下穿行。

Lizheng: We will have to expand the search then. *sighs* I hope they're alright.
里正：那便只有多朝几处去寻了。唉，还望她们平安。

Yanping: ...
燕平：……

He thinks back to the bridge, as well as the parting that happened there.
他记得那座桥，也记得在此处发生的交谈与离别。

There was a building on the other side of the gap, and yes, someone did jump across it.
桥的对面本应有建筑，也应当曾有在他眼前纵身跃过断桥的人。

Someone in a striking red dress, hard to miss against its plain surroundings.
——那身影在黯淡的木头与石块之上格外显眼，恰好是红色。

???: What about Jiǔ Niángzǐ? She jumped over the bridge. Is she never coming back?
？？？：……曲娘呢？她也过去了。但她是不是，不会再回来了？

???: Perhaps we will meet again one day.
？？？：也许在别的地方，会再见到的。

Yanping: Jiǔ Niángzǐ ...?
燕平：曲娘……？

The memory and the name pop up in his mind, but for now he has to put them aside.
他暂且将这个名字，还有这番突兀涌现的景象放置在脑海一角。

The old man remained locked in his stare all this time, before breaking the tension with another question.
老者始终关注着他的举动，此刻适时地开口插话。

Lizheng: Did you remember something?
里正：您可是想起了什么？

Yanping: Naught that is of any use.
燕平：琐事而已。

Yanping: My apologies. There is nothing I can do.
燕平：很遗憾，寻人一事，我恐怕无能为力。

Lizheng: Oh ... Never mind then. We should talk more about your ...
里正：这……也无妨，我们还是说回——

Yanping: My wand, perhaps?
燕平：——说回我的骨杖。

Yanping: I presume you wish to know how to use it and whether it may do harm to you.
燕平：或许你还想知道如何使用它，它是否会对你有所妨害。

Yanping: We have spent too much time circling about each other.
燕平：这些互相试探的问话，已经耗去太多时间。

Lizheng: Circling? No, no! I ...
里正：试探？不，不！这——

Yanping: Take no mind of it, I will tell you what I know.
燕平：无妨。我会将这些详尽地告知你。

Lizheng: ...!
里正：——！

The old man puts on a shocked face, its contours look strange for his age. Like a momentary glimpse of a younger man.
老者的脸上浮现出与他模样并不相符的震惊，看上去甚至有几分滑稽。

Yanping: As I said, this wand is not of any great power, but it has its uses to me and I would have it back.
燕平：正如此前所说，这枚骨杖于我而言并不是多么珍贵的物件，但也有其用处，不可失去。

Yanping: I will satisfy your curiosity. But then you must return it, at once.
燕平：因此，我选择满足你的好奇。希望你听完能将它归还给我。

Lizheng: Certainly. We are not thieves, after all.
里正：……自然。我们并非盗匪，不会强占他人财物。

Yanping: As I am just a visitor here, and you, my host. All I can do is make a humble request.
燕平：毕竟，您是此地的主人，而我是来客。我只能这般请求。

Somehow the request comes across as more of a polite warning.
与其说是请求，这更像礼貌的警示。

Yet the old man seems not to sense the implication at all. He heaves a sigh of relief and nods along with Gětiān.
但老者仿佛对其中暗含的意味毫无察觉，反而是松了口气般，连连点头应下燕平的话。

Lizheng: No problem, no problem at all.
里正：理应如此，理应如此。

Yanping: Good, things are simpler when the truth is out in the open. I will tell you about my wand, but first you need to answer my question.
燕平：把话说开，一切便简单了。答过骨杖一问后，我也有一问要还与你。

Yanping: I can plainly see your bones don't fit your appearance.
燕平：在我看来，你的骨相与这副形貌并不相符。

Yanping: Are you disguised as someone else?
燕平：——你可有假借他人身份？

Lizheng: Hmm. You already have your answer, don't you?
里正：唔。你既然问出口，想必已有答案。

Lizheng: But I will answer your question only after you answer mine.
里正：待你说完，我定会给出交待。

He slightly nods and signals to Yànpíng.
他微微颔首，朝燕平做了个手势。

Lizheng: Please.
里正：请吧。

02  流水今日 / A Flood of Memories

Getian's Clan Mate: Life in the mountain is indeed carefree.
葛天族人：山中之日，果然悠闲自在。

Getian's Clan Mate: Listen, there's the wind, the rustling leaves, and the chirping birds, all much more beautiful than that awful racket that humans make.
葛天族人：像这般立在枝头，听听风声、叶声、鸟鸣声，岂不比市井喧闹要好得多？

Getian's Clan Mate: Do you think so, Yànpíng?
葛天族人：——你说是么，燕平？

The bird called Yànpíng moves carefully to the trunk of the tree, so he could hide in the shadow.
被喊到名字的鸟儿谨慎地往树枝一侧挪了挪，以便将自己纳入叶片的荫蔽下。

Yanping: I've never left the mountain. Which is more annoying, the noises made by humans, or those made by animals?
燕平：我不曾往山下去过。市井喧闹之声，比之兽类打闹的喧哗，如何？

Getian's Clan Mate: To you, I think they would both be annoying, just in different ways.
葛天族人：按你的性子来看，那便是各有其扰人处。

Getian's Clan Mate: I admit, listening to the daily conversation of humans and their haggling in the market can sometimes be interesting.
葛天族人：若要我说，人们在坊市中的叫卖与交谈，倒是别有趣味。

Yanping: Is that so?
燕平：这样。

Birds like any intelligent creature can have different personalities and hobbies.
即使是同族，彼此间的性格与喜好也不尽相同。

One can tell the differences between them by listening carefully to their chirps. One might be loud and talkative, while the other makes only a few short sounds in reply.
在槐树之下驻足细听，便可分辨出鸟鸣的差异。密集而明亮的声音之下，偶尔会掺杂几个单薄的音节。

Getian's Clan Mate: They do invent many new and useful things, some that maybe you will need one day.
葛天族人：山下人常有些新奇的创造，学来说不准就能派上用场。

Getian's Clan Mate: Like bricks. Our nests have been much better protected since they showed me how to make them.
葛天族人：自从得了他们制砖瓦的法子，我们的巢穴就没再让雨水泡散架了。

Yanping: That's true.
燕平：确实如此。

Getian's Clan Mate: And the technique of making beautiful melodies with bamboo and wood with holes, or the doodles they draw that can hold their words ...
葛天族人：还有让那些竹条、木板发出优美音律的技巧，把说过的话记录下来的涂鸦……

Getian's Clan Mate: They can even measure time, using something they call a "calendar."
葛天族人：甚至有用于度量四季更替、时间流逝的一套规则，他们称之为“历法”。

Getian's Clan Mate: They can see into the essence of ordinary things and dream up wondrous creations from them.
葛天族人：本是寻常事物，在他们看来竟又有了其他意味。

Getian's Clan Mate: If you look at the world like a human does, you will probably be shocked by its vastness.
葛天族人：用山下人的眼光观察四周，恐怕会觉得天地实在辽阔。

Yanping: It is.
燕平：嗯。

Getian's Clan Mate: ... Yànpíng.
葛天族人：……燕平。

Getian's Clan Mate: I know you don't care about humans, but I feel I should tell you everything I want to say.
葛天族人：我知道你一向对这些不感兴趣，但我也总觉着，需找个机会将要说的话全说干净。

Yànpíng moves back to where he had stood before, right next to the other bird. Their wings rubbing against each other, with feathers puffing out.
听到这句话，燕平自树荫下迈步，挪回原来的位置。两只鸟儿站得近，翅膀挤挨着，蓬出几根不安分的羽毛。

Yanping: So ... you are leaving too.
燕平：你也要下山了。

Yanping: Well, it's only a matter of time. Good luck.
燕平：这不过也是早晚之事，祝你顺利。

Getian's Clan Mate: Thank you.
葛天族人：借你吉言。

In a rare moment of silence in the tree. One of the birds stretches his wing to cover the other.
枝头难得迎来了片刻安静。其中一只鸟儿伸出翅膀，代替树叶投下阴凉。

Getian's Clan Mate: You're still unwilling to leave the mountain ... Is it because you can't bear to stay away from home?
葛天族人：你不愿下山，是因为不舍这片土地？

Yanping: I simply have not found a reason to leave.
燕平：我只是……寻不到应当如此的缘由。

Getian's Clan Mate: Your time has not yet come, I guess, but that's fine, we need someone to take care of our nests and belongings ... I'll leave my books and instruments to you.
葛天族人：唔，也许是时机未到。这样也好，得麻烦你照料大家的巢，还有那堆收集的小物件……我的书本和乐器便托给你了。

Getian's Clan Mate: You are the first one born in the Central Plains after we migrated. I think you will adapt to the environment here faster than we do.
葛天族人：你是第一个在中原出生的，或许会比迁徙而来的我们更适应这儿的风土吧。

Getian's Clan Mate: We are so used to living high in the sky. When we look down, both the cities and the people are no different from dirt piles. Only by living among them can we truly experience the prosperity described in the poems.
葛天族人：我们所习惯的地方太高了。从高空往下望，城、人，都只是灰扑扑的土堆——但若生活其中，大概能真正体会到诗文中所写的繁盛景象。

Getian's Clan Mate: I think you will leave the mountain one day.
葛天族人：我想，总有一天，你会动身下山。

Yanping: But not for now, at least.
燕平：至少不是近日。

Yanping: It's not yet time for me to think of such things.
燕平：现在谈论这些，实在是为时过早。

Getian's Clan Mate: Just as well ... You glide slowly just like always.
葛天族人：好吧，好吧……你这慢悠悠的性子。

Getian's Clan Mate: Hopefully I'll be useful to our people one day. I don't want to become another lazy bone always snoozing beneath the tree ...
葛天族人：希望我最后是块有用的骨头，别像旁边树上那家伙似的，尽记了些絮絮叨叨的废话……

The seasons changed, beasts and birds came and went away. And the traces they had left behind were gone in less than a day as the leaves fell.
逢着时节更替，山中鸟兽来去频繁，今日树底的足迹，及至明日，往往隐入叶丛，不复再见。

茶叶，墨水，纸张。金属水壶，木质长桌，砖石铺就的地面。所有的人工造物。

He was a newborn chick, and the mountain, the brook, and the forest were his shell. Now the shell has been gently cracked, and he flows out into a bowl.
山峦、溪水与林木曾是将他环绕的卵壳，如今它们已被轻轻敲破，于是他骤然被倾入一只碗中。

That bowl might be roughly made, but the liquid inside is clear and good, reflecting the sky, the mountains, and everything ...
碗壁粗糙，但其中液体清亮，映出天地悠悠。

Getian: Perhaps this explains why people in the human world love liquor.
葛天：山下人对杯中之物的喜好，或是由此而来。

Getian: I should have drunk with them, when I had the chance.
葛天：当时，也应与他们……同饮一回的。

The bird closes his eyes.
鸟儿闭上眼睛。

There is still time to enjoy the moment, before he wakes up from this dream.
在人工梦游结束之前，尚有片刻可用于怀念。

Getian: Life beyond the mountain is indeed chaotic.
葛天：山下的确喧闹，几乎片刻不得清净，灯火也易扰了眼睛。

Getian: I never intended to stay long in the city after that transformation mystery was solved.
葛天：我本打算解决城中一事后，便不再逗留。

Getian: But now, I'm even further away from home.
葛天：如今，却是身在此处，离族地越发遥远。

Getian: Perhaps I will never return home again.
葛天：想来，或许再难回返。

Getian: Even so it was good to see it once more ... if only in a dream.
葛天：也恰好借着这幻境，再见上一见。

Gětiān sits on the scholar tree, just as he had before. With the wind still blowing through his feathers.
鸟儿仍然栖在槐树的枝干之上，风也依旧拂动着他的羽毛。

But he feels now that he is no longer as he was.
只是未曾动身，与选择回返，两者的心境恐怕不再相同。

Getian: If time went back to that day, I would still fly down the mountain.
葛天：……但若是再作抉择，应当仍择下山一途。

Getian: The owner of that tavern was young. Stubborn, because of her youth. But she has always been true to herself.
葛天：那位酒坊娘子，年纪尚轻，且颇有些执拗。但所行皆是出自本心。

Getian: She grew up in that city, growing close to her neighbors, she loved them like they were her own kin.
葛天：她与城中众人，恰如同族，彼此友爱。也是因着在此城生长，才使她始终有此为旁人思索的心思罢。

Getian: If she did jump over that bridge, she must be a real Xiángruì now. She must be pleased.
葛天：既过得那桥，也应了传说所言的“祥瑞”一词。她应当会高兴。

Getian: As for those two foreign merchants, they weren't scared away by all the trouble. They stayed till the very end.
葛天：那两名胡商，也曾与我坦诚相谈，面对城中混乱不曾走避，竟也留至最后。

Getian: That day, I saw the lanterns floating down the river glowing brighter than ever before.
葛天：况且，我也得以再见河灯顺流而下的亮光，比过往都更为明亮。

Getian: And that Hexagram of Water and Fire. The choice to be made. Xū, with water above and sky below. The Hexagram of waiting.
葛天：水火既济，已有抉择。水天需，逢时机。

Getian: I hope they left the city with what they wanted. Till this day, I still wonder which path that lady with unreadable bones ultimately chose.
葛天：她二人终究也有所获。只不知，那位骨相不可勘读的年长者，究竟择了哪一条路。

Getian: That young man was another interesting person. I wonder whom I will meet next.
葛天：今日所识的年轻人，也是有趣。不知稍后出了幻境，又能见到怎样的光景。

Getian: How would I know the limits of the mountain if I didn't leave it? Yet the world turns out to be another bowl.
葛天：若非身至山下，怎能彻底明了曾在山中。天地过客，皆要入此杯盏。

Getian: Fine. I shall listen to the wind before anything comes up.
葛天：也好。可趁无事，暂且听风。
