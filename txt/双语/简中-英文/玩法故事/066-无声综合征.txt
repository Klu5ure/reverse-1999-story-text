01  门之前 / In Front of the Door

The subtle fragrance of nectar works its way into your dream and wakes you from your sleep.
微弱的花蜜气味飘入梦中，将你从沉睡中唤醒。

Even with your eyes shut, you can tell this isn't the room you usually stay in.
还不用去看，你已经意识到周遭的环境并非你常待的房间。

选项 / Choices

1. Open your eyes.
1. 睁开眼

2. Try to go back to sleep.
2. 回归困意

3. Stretch your body.
3. 伸展身体

分支 1 / Branch 1

Still, no matter where you look, the space is colored Klein Blue—just like everywhere else.
不过眼前仍是铺满了克莱因蓝色的房间，哪儿都一样。

分支 2 / Branch 2

You try to sink back into the comfort of darkness, but the sweet scent stimulates your senses.
你试着回归舒适的黑暗，但花蜜的味道刺激着你的神经。

In the end, you're forced awake in a room drenched in Klein Blue.
你被迫醒来面对满是克莱因蓝色的房间。

分支 3 / Branch 3

You roll and turn as you stretch off the stiffness of a long rest, exploring the space as you do so.
你开始用身体丈量所处的空间，同时缓解长时间睡眠导致的僵硬。

It feels meaninglessly empty. You could roll around a few more times without hitting a thing.
房间内空旷得毫无意义，你甚至可以再打几个滚。

共同接续 / Shared continuation

A note hangs prominently on the door, its stark lettering immediately readable at a glance:
一张纸条以醒目的方式贴在门上，上面清晰地写着你也能看懂的文字：

On the other side of this door is your homeland, though one you've never seen before.
You may go around as you like until I return.
Remember to say hello at the Paris base. Try to be polite. 
Don't mention Manus Vindictae to strangers.

Have a pleasant trip, and stay safe.
门的后面是你理论上的故乡，虽然你从未见过它，在我回来之前，你可以自由活动。
记得去巴黎的据点打声招呼，要有礼貌，路上不要和陌生人提及重塑之手。

旅途愉快，注意安全。

02  再教育 / Encore Lesson

The ritual has ended. Beneath the Arc de Triomphe, only two figures are left standing among several headless bodies scattered across the ground. A female figure approaches them.
仪式已经结束，凯旋门之下只留下了两个站着的影子，以及几具倒下的无头躯干。一名女性的影子正向他们走来。

Ustrina: How fared the ritual? Where are the members of the Headless Society?
火渡船：仪式怎么样了？无头社的人呢？

Ustrina: The "Storm" approaches. I am here to bring you back to shelter.
火渡船：“暴雨”要来了，我来接你们回藏身处。

Taira: They're dead.
平：他们死了。

Ustrina: Seeking life through death is folly, like breaking a clay lamp for its wick.
火渡船：先接近死亡，再借此成为他们理想中的生命，就像毁掉一盏陶灯，只是为了取烛芯照明。

Ustrina: I do not understand their reasoning, but I do wonder what sort of wick they were hoping to find.
火渡船：我不明白，但我确实好奇结果——他们想要的烛芯长什么样？

Taira: Stop wondering. The wick is gone. They're dead, nothing more. The ritual failed.
平：别好奇，没有什么灯芯了，他们只是死了，仪式失败了。

Ustrina: Did you not cut off their heads as required?
火渡船：你没按要求砍下他们的头？

Taira: Not me. They chose Coppélia as the executor—claimed she was more suitable.
平：不是我，他们选了珐琅眼做执行人，说是她更合适。

Taira: But it seems Coppélia didn't meet their requirements either. They said so with their final breath.
平：但看来珐琅眼也不符合要求，他们咽气前是这么说的。

Big Hat: *cough* A pity ... It seems you didn't meet the requirements in the end.
大帽子：咳，咳咳……很遗憾，看来你终究不符合执行人的要求。

Coppélia: ㄚ... I ... did something wrong?
珐琅眼：ㄨㄛˇ，ㄨㄛˇ……我做错了吗？

Big Hat: Oh ... don't be upset. Any head still attached to a neck is enslaved to thought.
大帽子：哦……这没什么好伤心的，只要脑袋连在脖子上，就难免是思想的奴隶。

Big Hat: Whether it was a vulgar emotion or a grounding idea that got in your way, it doesn't matter much—it simply kept you from being perfectly useless, that's all.
大帽子：不管是哪种庸俗的情感与思想占据了你的头，导致你无法成为一个完美的无用之人，都很正常。

Big Hat: Or perhaps it was me who couldn't fully let go of human wisdom. This ritual ... haha ... it's just ridiculous! Hahahaha!
大帽子：而且也有可能是我无法彻底舍弃为人的智慧？甚至这个仪式也蠢得要命！哈哈哈哈！

Coppélia: ㄧ-Your head is ... glowing ...?
珐琅眼：ㄋ丨ˇ的头……它在闪烁……？

Big Hat: Beautiful, isn't it? Like the shining sun!
大帽子：很漂亮对不对？就像太阳一样散发着能量！

Big Hat: But it'll soon go out. I'll take my freedom the simple way now.
大帽子：不过它快要熄灭了，因为我将以一种更简单粗暴的方式赢得我的主权。

Big Hat: I wouldn't recommend it though—quite tedious, really. You'd better find another way.
大帽子：但我不推荐这种方法，它过于没劲，你就别模仿了。

Coppélia: ㄨ-What can I do?
珐琅眼：ㄨㄛˇ还能、还能做些什么？

Big Hat: Oh, you're already a wonderful person. Now you get to feel for yourself, think for yourself, and struggle through the pain ahead.
大帽子：你已经是个出色的人了。接下来你得自己去感受，自己去思考，然后在痛苦中挣扎了。

Taira: Then the flames on their necks flickered out. I'm almost certain they're gone for good.
平：然后他们脖子上的火球就熄灭了，我想是彻底死了。

Ustrina: Hm. A banal end. Bring Coppélia. We're leaving.
火渡船：原来是最无趣的结局。带上珐琅眼，我们该走了。

Coppélia: *sob*
珐琅眼：ㄨ，ㄨ……

Taira: Coppélia, do you hear me? Coppélia?!
平：珐琅眼，你听到了吗？珐琅眼？

Coppélia hears Taira's voice, but she can't tear her eyes away from the Big Hats lying motionless on the ground. Maybe the ritual didn't fail completely.
珐琅眼听到了平的呼唤，但她仍注视着倒下的大帽子的躯体，仪式似乎并没有完全失败。

She senses something—an invisible force awakened by their deaths.
大帽子的死亡确实唤来了某种无形的力量，她可以感受到。

It squeezes her heart, choking her throat, even making the moisture in the air sting her eyes.
这股无形的力量扼住了她的心脏，撬动着她的咽喉，甚至指挥起空气中的水分去刺痛她的双眼。

Coppélia: *wail*
珐琅眼：ㄨ哇啊啊啊啊——！

Then these things called grief and guilt make her legs give out. She collapses to the ground, crying uncontrollably.
然后名为丧失感与负罪感的力量使她双腿发软，不得不蹲在地上，控制不住地大哭起来。

Ustrina: What is this? What's wrong with her?
火渡船：怎么？为什么？

Taira: I have no idea. Coppélia, listen to me.
平：我不知道，喂，珐琅眼？

Coppélia: ㄥ ... My eyes sting, nose burns, throat ... on fire ...
珐琅眼：ㄉㄨㄥˋ，眼睛好痛，鼻子好酸，喉咙在着火……

Ustrina: How odd. Is this your doing?
火渡船：我不明白。是你让她哭得像壶沸水的吗？

Taira: Me? She was only following orders.
平：我不知道？她一路上都很服从命令。

Ustrina: Tears come with sadness, Taira. It's a rudimentary emotion.
火渡船：按照人的情感逻辑推断，一般来说，会哭是因为伤心。

Taira: What could she be sad over?
平：伤心？能有什么事？

Ustrina: Why do you ask me? I deal with the dead, or those knocking at its door. Their sadness is simple, but this? This is beyond my understanding.
火渡船：大多时候，我都在和死者或将死者说话，他们的伤心很单一，所以我也不明白。

Ustrina: My work lies in the stilling of souls, not the drying of tears. This is yours to deal with.
火渡船：但我只擅长让灵魂永远地闭嘴，不擅长暂时的，得你来。

Taira: Uh ... Listen, Coppélia, this failure doesn't fall upon your shoulders. The Headless Society refused to join us, but you did as we asked. No punishment awaits you.
平：呃……没能招募无头不是你的错，珐琅眼。你忠实地执行了命令，砍下了他们的头，没有人会责罚你。

Taira: Their ritual was foolish, and they paid the price for it ...
平：那群无头只是为他们愚蠢的仪式付出了生命的代价……

Coppélia: ㄅ-But the Big Hats ... I don't hear them anymore. They're gone. ㄙ-Said they would get back up, but didn't.
珐琅眼：ㄎㄜˊ，可是，大帽子的声音，听不到了，没有了……他们说得不对，他们没有再、再站起来……

Realizing her newly made friends have turned into silent, lifeless objects, Coppélia's crying threatens to drown out the thumping rain.
意识到自己刚认识的朋友已经变成了不会动的沉默物件，珐琅眼的哭声甚至有盖过雨声的趋势。

Meanwhile, her temporary guardians still don't understand what's wrong.
而一旁的临时监护人显然没有意识到问题所在。

Ustrina: You spoke wrongly.
火渡船：你说错话了。

Taira: How, exactly?
平：我说错了什么？

Ustrina: I don't know, but she's crying harder. Perhaps it was your tone.
火渡船：我不知道，但她哭得更大声了，所以你说错话了。

Ustrina: The "Storm" is nearly upon us. Get her up. Now.
火渡船：“暴雨”真的要来了，你必须快点让她站起来。

Taira: Then lend me a hand! I'm hardly adept at dealing with crying children!
平：那就来帮我，我不知道怎么照顾孩子！

Ustrina: She is seventeen, Taira. Hardly a child.
火渡船：我得纠正你，她十七岁，广义或狭义上都不能算孩子。

Ustrina: And I don't know how to deal with her either. As I said, this is not my domain.
火渡船：而且我也不知道怎么照顾她，我重申一遍，我只擅长让灵魂永远地闭嘴。

Taira: Why couldn't it have been Lyra or ... ah, no matter.
平：为什么来接应的不是歌唱家或者苦——算了。

Taira: At least help me carry her back.
平：至少出点力，和我一起把她架回去。

03  人性的底层 / Upbeat of Humanity

Coppélia sinks Taira's blade deep into his chest. His human body crumples to the ground, while the crab on his face continues to twitch. The Big Hats, who have been silently watching, begin to clap.
平的刀刃刺穿了它自己的胸膛，人类的躯干部分倒在地上，剩下蟹的部分仍在挣扎。自始至终都在一旁围观的大帽子们鼓起了掌。

Big Hat: Well done! You have claimed your autonomy, wresting free from your rulers.
大帽子：多么可贺，你从管束者的手里夺得了主权。

Big Hat: A shame though, that you're now trapped in a cage of thoughts, tied down by a taboo called a conscience. You've lost sovereignty over the universe.
大帽子：但又多么可悲，从此你将陷入思想的囚笼，被名为良知的禁忌束缚，全宇宙的主权已经离你而去了。

Big Hat: Looks like you're no longer fit to be our ritual's executor. So, what will you do now?
大帽子：恐怕你也没法担任仪式的执行人了，接下来你打算怎么做？

Coppélia: ㄚ-I don't know ...
珐琅眼：ㄨㄛˇ不知道……

Big Hat: A brilliant answer!
大帽子：绝妙的回答！

The big hat, still hiding whatever's beneath, adjusts its brim, putting away its dramatic gestures.
从不露出脑袋的大帽子扶正了自己的帽檐，收起了夸张的肢体动作。

Big Hat: I mourn the beast that once ruled over humanity, and I honor the tool that has become human again.
大帽子：我在这里为支配人的动物默哀，也向回归人的工具致敬。

Big Hat: No ritual today. No use sticking around.
大帽子：仪式无法举行，我已经没有留在这里的必要。

Big Hat: I'm off to the carnival at Champ de Mars. People there are rejecting their work and returning to their animalistic nature—mustn't miss that.
大帽子：接下来我要去参加战神广场的狂欢节，拒绝工作的人们似乎正在回归兽性，这可不能错过。

Coppélia: Can I come too ...?
珐琅眼：ㄨㄛˇ也想去……

Big Hat: No, you can't. You've only just become human. No reason to go back to being a clueless animal.
大帽子：那不行，你刚成为人，回归无知的野兽对你毫无意义。

Big Hat: What you need now is to wrestle with your thoughts, feel the pain of it all, and then dream up a future right in the middle of it.
大帽子：在思考中痛苦，然后于痛苦中畅想未来才是你现在应该经历的。

Big Hat: I believe this might be the last time we ever meet, so farewell!
大帽子：再见了！虽然我们估计再也没有机会见面了！

The Big Hat finally lifts its hat, giving a proper tip of it, but through the heavy rain Coppélia can't make out a face.
大帽子终于摘下自己的帽子，行了个标准的脱帽礼，但逐渐变大的雨使得珐琅眼没能看清他的样貌。

This downpour seems intent on washing away every melody in the city.
而雨越来越大，仿佛要将城市中的旋律全部冲走。

Coppélia reaches out, feeling the raindrops fall from above. She knows there's nothing scary about this kind of rain, but that soon, it will reverse its direction.
珐琅眼伸出手，感受着雨落下，自上而下的雨并不可怕，她知道这一点，但很快雨就会颠倒过来。

Normally, she'd run to her comrades for shelter, but she knows they wouldn't agree with what she did today. She can no longer stay with those who have no conscience.
以往她应当立刻回到同胞的所在地躲雨，但她明白，没有同胞会赞同她今日的选择，她再也无法和没有良知的同胞共处。

She must find her own place to hide.
她必须自己寻找能够避雨的屋檐。

Coppélia: ㄨ-What ... What should I do now ...?
珐琅眼：ㄣ……怎么办……

She hears the crab moving behind her but keeps her eyes focused on the road ahead.
身后的蟹正在悄悄移动，但她现在只能专注于自己的前路。

Coppélia: Huh?
珐琅眼：ㄣˊ？

Then, she catches the faint scent of nectar in the wet air, the same one from when she first awoke.
接着她在潮湿的空气中闻到了花蜜的香气，与醒来时相同的气味。

???: Come. The rain is about to start.
？？？：走吧，就要开始下雨了。

Coppélia: But ㄚ-I can't go back ... I ㄉㄧˋㄙ—disobeyed a lord ...
珐琅眼：ㄎㄜˊ，我回不去了……我违抗了一个大人……

Coppélia: I don't know. ㄏㄚˊ-Have I done wrong?
珐琅眼：ㄨㄛˇ不知道，我做错了吗？

The flowers on the woman's body playfully blink at Coppélia. She glances with moist eyes at the crab scuttling away in the distance.
女人身上的花朵调皮地朝珐琅眼眨了眨眼，她用湿润的目光瞥视了一眼那远去的蟹。

???: You'll have plenty of time to wonder whether your choice was right or wrong.
？？？：你还有很多时间来思考这个选择的正误。

???: For now, just remember—there is always more than one place to shelter from the rain.
？？？：至于现在，避雨的屋檐不止有一个。

???: Come with me. This time, we'll not return to that meaningless blue room.
？？？：我们走吧，不过这次，我们不会再回那间蓝得一无是处的空房间了。

04  误读 / Misinterpretation

By the time Coppélia breaks free from Ustrina's control, the ritual is already over.
珐琅眼终于摆脱了提灯大人的控制，然而仪式已经结束。

She staggers over to a Big Hat lying on the ground.
她踉踉跄跄地扑到了一个躺倒的大帽子旁。

Coppélia: ㄚ ... ㄚ-I came for the ritual too. Am I late?
珐琅眼：ㄨㄛˇ……ㄨㄛˇ也来参加这个仪式……是来晚了吗？

No reply comes from beneath the hat. The light his head emits is fading.
帽子下没有声息。他的头仍在发出微弱的闪烁，却也在慢慢熄灭了。

Then, she hears some questions, clear as the pitter-patter of raindrops.
一连串的疑问声，像雨滴一样敲响在珐琅眼耳边。

Ustrina: You would go so far as to fight us, just to save them?
火渡船：你不惜与我们对抗，是想救他们？

Ustrina: Is it because they made you a promise? Offered you something of a value even you can understand?
火渡船：这群人许给了你什么好处吗？连你也明白其价值的那种？

Ustrina: Or is it because the ritual failed, and you're angry you won't receive payment? Money does have a tendency to make even peaceful people turn violent. Is it the same for you?
火渡船：还是说因为仪式失败了，你拿不到报酬而生气？人们常常因财物的得失而行为失控，你也会这样吗？

Taira: What are you going on about?
平：你在说些什么？

Ustrina: I am simply wondering what drives her. She wouldn't be the first to lay down her life for wealth, nor would she be the last.
火渡船：我在合理推测她的动机。人为财死，这也是很常见的。

The two voices sound so close and yet so distant. Coppélia doesn't understand the words, but her hands, still clutching the hat's brim, can't help but tremble.
两位大人的说话声忽近忽远，珐琅眼听到了，却无法分辨其中的含义。
她的手颤抖着，捏在帽子边缘。

Coppélia: ㄨ-What else can I do? Could you tell me?
珐琅眼：ㄨㄛˇ还能做什么，能不能告诉我……

Ustrina: Hmm. From the sounds of it, my assumption was wrong. I suppose it's to be expected—reading the living has never been my forte. But I digress.
火渡船：听起来我的推断错了。我不擅长分析活人的情感……算了。

Ustrina: All we can ascertain is that she wishes to complete a ritual that would, in essence, bring about her own death, no?
火渡船：我们唯一知道的，就是她想去完成一个实质上是去送死的仪式对吧？

Though the rain pours down on them, it doesn't put out the flame in Ustrina's chest. If anything, it feeds it.
雨滴落下，火渡船胸前的火焰似乎受其鼓舞，反而愈发明亮。

A sense of unease stirs in Taira's pincers. He shakes the raindrops from them only for even more to take their place.
有一种不好的预感在蟹钳上悄然滋生。平想甩开钳子上的水滴，但雨滴已经变得更加密集。

Taira: Never mind what she's thinking. We're done here—help me carry her back. Since when do you have so much to say, anyway?
平：别管她在想什么了。任务结束，快帮我把她架回去，往常怎么没见你有这么多想法？

Her flame, unfazed by Taira's words, dances as she crouches and grips Coppélia's shoulders, forcing her to face its cold glow.
那丛火焰跃动着，全然没有理会蟹脸同胞的话语。她蹲下身，扶住了珐琅眼的肩膀，强迫后者抬起头。

Ustrina: It was their promise of death that fed your emotions, wasn't it, Coppélia?
火渡船：珐琅眼，是他们给你许诺的死亡，让你获得了这些情感的养料，对吗？

Coppélia: Coppélia stares into the flame. Her stiff fingers drag the hat from the dim head.
珐琅眼：珐琅眼呆呆地注视着火焰，僵直的手指拽落了那顶帽子。

Ustrina: No answer? It matters not. I have another method of verifying this conclusion.
火渡船：我有一个办法可以验证这个推断。

Ustrina: If you wish to shed this mortal coil, then come—let us take part in an even greater ritual.
火渡船：接近死亡的途径不止一种。跟我来，我们可以去一场更伟大的仪式。

Taira: You're ...! Have you, too, lost your mind?!
平：你是想……！你也疯了？

The patterns on Taira's shell writhe with indignation. He turns and storms away.
蟹壳的纹路因恼火而更加扭曲，平转过身，向着相反的方向走去。

The Seine looks nothing like it did just a few hours prior. Now, people march along its banks, their strange festivities melting into and blurring with the pouring rain.
河道上的景象与几小时之前迥然不同，人们在塞纳河的两岸游行，怪异的欢庆场景和雨幕连成模糊的一片。

The ferryboat rocks against the ripples created by their passion.
那些狂热也推涌着河水，将渡船拍打得微微摇晃。

Ustrina, standing at its bow, breaks the silence.
意识唤醒者站在船头，难得地主动开口。

Ustrina: You see, they're always like this, cheering slogans for tomorrow, raging over today's wages. First, they build their own cages; then, from within those bars, they seek the key.
火渡船：你看，他们一直这样。为明天的口号而高兴，为今天的工钱而愤怒，先为自己寻求束缚，再为自己寻求解脱。

Ustrina: They pile endless kindling beneath their own feet to fuel their shifting emotions, until at last they bury themselves in the pyre's flames and call it liberation of the soul.
火渡船：他们往自己脚下堆砌了无尽的柴薪，支撑着变幻的喜怒——直到他们将自己葬身于火焰中，宣称获得了灵魂的自由。

Ustrina: A scene I've witnessed countless times, ever since I was but a lamp. A scene I've grown so accustomed to.
火渡船：当我还是一盏灯时，我就对这些习以为常了。

Ustrina: But your feelings are different. They're pure—still tender and new. For a novice like me, that makes them far easier to learn from and understand.
火渡船：但是你的情感又不太一样，你的情绪很纯粹，也还很崭新。对我这种初学者来说，你这种情感肯定更好学、更好理解。

Coppélia stares at the water. She's never heard Ustrina speak this much, and stranger still, she's neither questioning nor scolding her.
珐琅眼茫然地看向水面。提灯的大人很少说这么多话，既没有提问，也不像训斥。

Coppélia: ㄚ-I ... I don't know. The Big Hats were kind. They said that I could do it. ㄚ-I need to—
珐琅眼：我……不知道。大帽子们很好，他们说……我可以做到，我要——

Ustrina's flame lights up the surrounding river, making Coppélia feel as though she's riding atop a drifting lantern.
提灯大人的火焰点亮了周围的河水，珐琅眼感觉自己似乎正坐在一只漂荡的灯盏上。

Coppélia: ㄉ-Death is ... transcending order. It is an experience, an enlightenment, for our sovereignty ...
珐琅眼：死、死亡是，超脱秩序。死亡是，体验，新启发，为了我们的主权……

Ustrina: Haha. You actually believe that drivel of theirs.
火渡船：哈哈。你还真的信他们那一套。

Ustrina: Oh? That sound I just made is what they called "laughter," is it not? An expression of happiness or amusement, I believe. Yes ... Say more—I can feel something new stirring. I was right to bring you along.
火渡船：哦，我发出了他们在……笑的声音，这应该是我开心的表现。你再多说几句吧，我的确有了一些新的冲动……把你带过来果然是正确的决定。

Coppélia: ㄧㄝ-Yes. Death is ... the right decision. A fearless death is the finest ...
珐琅眼：ㄕˋ，是的，死亡是……正确的决定，无畏的死亡，是绝佳的……

The rain intensifies, drowning out the voices on the shore. Coppélia tightens her grip on the hat. She sees the flame's azure glow at the corner of her eye, yet its owner's voice seems much further away.
雨越下越大，岸边的声音也逐渐被掩埋在雨水里。珐琅眼紧紧攥住手里的帽子，余光里只剩下火焰湛蓝的光泽。大人的声音像是从远方传来。

Ustrina: Hmm. I find I no longer care about my unanswered questions. That, too, is a new sensation. And yet, the moment I stopped caring, the answer revealed itself to me.
火渡船：对。我现在也不在意未竟的疑惑了，“不在意”也是一种崭新的感觉，然而，不再在意后，答案就向我敞开——

Ustrina: Lamp oil and kindling were never the key to burning bright, nor were they the key to feeling. The key is rain—for every flame yearns for a deluge to snuff it out.
火渡船：灯油或柴薪都不是燃烧的关键，也不是情感的钥匙。一场雨才是——火的愿望就是熄灭在雨中。

Ustrina: And this is a proper downpour. Just look at you—drenched to the bone.
火渡船：雨真大。你脸上都是水。

Coppélia: ㄚ-I will ... be snuffed out too?
珐琅眼：ㄨㄛˇ也要……熄灭了吗？

Rainwater streaks down her face. She looks back down at the hat, still held tight in her stiff, wet hands. It feels like a dark cloud now, its tears spilling upward.
脸上都是，雨水。
珐琅眼低下头，怀中的帽子似乎也是一朵乌云，它的泪水正在向上逆流。

Bittersweet: Before the "Storm" arrived, I told Ustrina to bring Coppélia back with her. But in the end ... *sigh* Even Taira could offer no explanation on his return.
苦蜜井：……“暴雨”来临前，我明明已经嘱托了火渡船，让她带着珐琅眼一起返回。可终究……回来的平也说不出个所以然。

Hummingbird: You have my condolences. If it's any consolation, I believe she will soon traverse death and find you once more.
蜂鸟：我对此表示遗憾。相信不久的将来，她可以跨越死亡与你重逢。

Bittersweet: Sweet child. The sorrow trailing your words tells me this faint wish is heartfelt. I take it your research has seen some progress?
苦蜜井：好孩子，你尾调中的悲伤告诉我，这份缥缈的祝福必定发自真心。这么说来，你的研究似乎有了进展？

Hummingbird: Yes. Professor Roseau's work—his study of the Depths of Myth is what brought me to Paris.
蜂鸟：罗西教授关于迷思海的研究给了我一些启发。坦白说，我来巴黎，就是为了迷思海。

Bittersweet: Ah, the trove of the subconscious. A most fascinating place. But it isn't made up of humans' and arcanists' myths alone. Entities ancient and mysterious make their home there, too.
苦蜜井：迷思海确乎是一处潜意识宝库，但那里并非只由人类和神秘学家的迷思构成，其上还有古老未知的存在。

Bittersweet: Don't rush in before you've fully mastered the revival ritual. What awaits you there is far more beautiful—and far more dangerous—than an inverted Paris.
苦蜜井：在你完全掌握“起死回生”的知识前，不要贸然去尝试，因为那里远比倒置的巴黎更加美丽和危险。

Bittersweet: Ah, forgive my presumptuousness. My words are nothing more than the ramblings of a florist who has just lost her precious seedling, loath to see others disappear for nothing.
苦蜜井：请原谅，就当这是一个刚刚失去珍贵种子的花匠在絮聒吧。我不希望再有人因此而无谓消失。

Hummingbird: No need to apologize; I'll bear your warning in mind. Still, I can't help but wonder—how much do you know about this treasure trove?
蜂鸟：谢谢你，我会谨记这一点，但我不禁好奇，你对于这座宝库还了解多少？

Bittersweet: A decent amount. An old friend often visits me in dreams to talk about it. I can introduce you if you'd like.
苦蜜井：不瞒你说，我的一位故友经常在梦中与我讨论迷思海的点滴，我可以为你引荐她。

Hummingbird: I appreciate your generosity. Would you care to walk with me along the river? The evening is still young. We have plenty of time to talk.
蜂鸟：感谢你的慷慨，介意和我一起到河边走走吗？天色尚好，我们可以详聊。

05  自我的超越 / Self-Transcendence

With a deafening rumble, the giant creature falls. It lies there motionless, save for the twitching metal pipes on its body.
巨大的怪兽在轰鸣声中倒下了，只有它身上的金属音管还在抽搐。

Voices from behind the door reach Coppélia before she can catch her breath. Fortunately, the concert hall offers plenty of places to hide.
来不及缓口气，门外已经传来大人们的说话声。还好，演奏厅里有很多可供珐琅眼躲藏的地方。

Lyra: What a mess! Still hiding, Coppélia? First you're angry, and now you're frightened?
歌唱家：这里也一团糟。躲着做什么，珐琅眼，生完气又感到害怕了？

Lyra: I didn't take you for this much of a fool. Don't tell me you're going to make me come find you myself.
歌唱家：没想到你愚蠢到这个程度。还要让我亲自来找你吗？

Bittersweet: Such panic. If I didn't know better, I'd assume they'd all found their own "Félicienne." What happened here?
苦蜜井：看大家慌得像无头的苍蝇一样，我以为他们都找到了自己的费利西安呢。这里发生了什么？

The familiar scent of nectar eases Coppélia's nerves. Even so, she tries to follow the conversation outside rather than show herself.
熟悉的花蜜香气缓解了珐琅眼的紧张，但她还是不敢把头探出去，只是更努力地去听外间的对话。

Lyra: Right on cue. Every time that little tuning fork steps out of line, there you are. Come to pour out that bottomless conscience of yours again? Don't tell me you've actually developed a taste for playing nanny.
歌唱家：我就知道你会来，每次小音叉一犯错你都会立马蹦出来，又在泼洒你泛滥的良知吗？你不会真的当保姆当上瘾了吧？

Bittersweet: Manus Vindictae treats all arcanist children as equals. I can only hope you haven't forgotten that.
苦蜜井：重塑之手对神秘学家的孩子一视同仁，希望你不要忘了这个古老的初衷。

Lyra: Yet arcane talent itself is not equally shared.
歌唱家：但神秘学天赋的分配向来厚此薄彼。

Lyra: Take this organ, for instance. I channeled the full force of my orchestra into creating it, nurturing it, tuning it, and for what? So it can make some wretch's meager abilities just the least bit useful?
歌唱家：看看这架管风琴吧，为了让一些可怜人仅有的、蚕豆大小的才华发挥作用，我倾尽乐团之力，才得以创造它、喂养它、塑造它。

Lyra: I devoted no less of myself to its creation than you've devoted to any of your little "seedlings." And that devotion saw it become more than a mere node in the "Windweave." It turned it into a chorus of countless talents, saw it nearly reach the boundaries of beauty!
歌唱家：我对它注入的心血不比你对你那些孩子的少。它不仅仅是个风网的组件，还是无数天赋最后的和鸣，它几乎已触及美的边界！

Lyra: And Coppélia has what it takes to understand it. Such talent should be handled with care.
歌唱家：而珐琅眼本来有理解它的天赋，足以得到重用的天赋。

Bittersweet: Of course, you've always seen Coppélia as a fine instrument. She has trembled through every performance, always done precisely what you ordered, no more and no less.
苦蜜井：当然……你一直都把珐琅眼当作一架好乐器。她在演奏中一向战战兢兢，从不做任何你没吩咐的事。

Lyra: Such are the results of my constant tuning and correction. Alas, she must be disciplined this time. Only then will she know where the boundaries lie.
歌唱家：那正是因为我不厌其烦地调试和纠正。这次她也必须接受惩罚，从而明白行为的边界。

The conversation stops, and footsteps follow. Coppélia squeezes her eyes shut and shrinks further into the corner.
对话停止了。轻捷的脚步声随之响起，角落的少女闭紧双眼，努力把自己蜷缩得更小。

But the efforts do nothing to drive the footsteps away.
然而这努力在驱走脚步一事上如此徒劳。

The warning signs of capture—a piercing "wind" that scatters like a barrage of arrows—arrive before anything else.
一些被捕的预兆已经先一步抵达——一种尖锐的、像利箭一样四射开来的“风”。

Coppélia remembers the vibrations and how they reverberate through her. She remembers how Lyra, even with her back turned, could catch her every move, every beat, every breath ...
珐琅眼记得这种震颤，也记得小个子大人即使背对着她，也能察觉她的每一个动作、每一次节拍，甚至每一次呼吸……

The "wind" tells Lyra everything.
“风”会帮小个子大人得悉一切。

It swirls around Coppélia like the opening notes of every tuning session ... Yet this time, it carries a faint fragrance.
这些“风”似乎绕着珐琅眼打了个转，像每一次调试的前奏，只比以往多了一点细微的芳香。

Coppélia: ...
珐琅眼：……

Lyra: I'm honing your talent—you merely lack the means to understand! You know not the refinement a perfect instrument must endure!
歌唱家：好了，这不过是打磨天赋的一环，你不了解！你完全不了解完美的乐器理应接受怎样的雕琢！

Lyra: So quit your hiding! Pour your anger and fear into the music, Coppélia!
歌唱家：把你的愤怒和害怕都放在乐曲上！你怎么敢期待躲过去？珐琅眼……

熟悉的训斥声，以及逐渐远去的脚步声。珐琅眼茫然地抱紧手臂，发现自己仍然好端端地躲在墙角。

随后是关门声。另一阵脚步声走近，一只手搭在她的肩头。

苦蜜井：还好你一直带着那支花蜜，我凭此才很快找到了你。

珐琅眼仔细嗅着让她安心的气味，感到一种飘然的恍惚，而女人却挥散了残余的香气，她用温和的眼光注视着珐琅眼。

苦蜜井：别被香气迷惑住，它不是为你准备的。

苦蜜井：走吧，花种一旦长出新芽就不该再流连于暗室中。去阳光下吧，你可以尝试的事还有很多。

珐琅眼：ㄨㄛˇ……要去哪里？

苦蜜井：另一个能为你提供庇护的屋檐，你不必再担心被歌唱家找到。

珐琅眼：庇护的……屋檐？

苦蜜井：是的，那里也会有引领旋律的老师、你的同伴，不过与这里不同，那里是自由的。

珐琅眼：自由？……自由！我记得……自由归于我们！休假和福利！

苦蜜井：嘘，可不要被风听到。我们现在就动身，离乐团越远越好——对了，你对蜡过敏吗？
