01  掌中雀 / Sparrow in Hand

Back to Earth
重返人间

Sparrow in Hand
掌中雀

Library, Suitcase
箱中图书室

Two figures are reading, illuminated by the soft lights of the library.
图书室内，柔软灯光下的阅读时间。

They sit in comfortable harmony, a tacit understanding between them.
一种舒适的默契和平衡，在空间中流淌。

Зима: …
冬：……

Sonetto: …
十四行诗：……

The only sound is the turning of pages.
安静得仅余书页翻动的声音。

Sonetto: …
十四行诗：……

It is a silence that will break as their mutual agreement ends.
沉默作为一种由社群共识维持的状态，终究会因共识破灭结束。

After all, this girl has a question forming in her mind.
毕竟，女孩一直思考着如何开口。

Sonetto: Excuse me, Mr. Зима ...
十四行诗：冬先生，请问……

It seems the man is in a world of his own. He does not even notice her voice.
——对方似乎沉浸在自己的世界里，丝毫没有察觉。

The girl lowers her tone. It is shorter, urgent, and almost piercing.
女孩于是压低了声音。更短，却更急促。几乎振聋发聩。

Sonetto: Mr. Зима!
十四行诗：冬先生！

зима and Зима snap out of their trance.
“冬”与冬一齐，被震得醒来。

Зима: W-What?
冬：什，什么？

Sonetto: Sorry, maybe I shouldn't ask you here ...
十四行诗：抱歉，虽然也许不应该在这里提问……

Sonetto: ... but you have the most extensive knowledge of poetic imagery of anyone I know. I have a question that's been bothering me for a while—
十四行诗：但您是我认识的人当中，对诗歌意象了解最为广泛的。有一个问题已困扰我许久——

The chickadee on his shoulder shakes its head in surprise and takes flight.
山雀慌乱地摇头，甚至再次飞了起来。

She takes no notice, fully focused on receiving her answer.
可惜，急于寻求答案的女孩并未留意。

Sonetto: I've been wondering ... What's your interpretation of "a sparrow in hand" and "a goose in the sky"?
十四行诗：我想知道，您是怎样看待，“掌中之雀”和“空中之雁”这两种意象的？

Sonetto: Is it simply referring to these two animals literally, or does it include all the extended symbolism from previous works?
十四行诗：“雀”与“雁”，是仅仅实指语义上的这两种动物，还是包括了它们在以往作品中延伸的、所有的虚指含义？

Sonetto: Or perhaps, as some suggest ... this is some kind of highly directional metaphor?
十四行诗：或者还是如部分观点所说……这是某种指向性极强的隐喻？

Зима: …
冬：……

Зима silently picks up a pen, preparing to write his response in the book lying open in front of him.
沉默中，冬提笔，似乎打算将自己的看法，写在面前摊开的书上。

Sonetto keenly perceives his intent.
而这意图几乎立刻被敏锐地察觉了。

Sonetto: Ah—please don't do that.
十四行诗：啊——请不要这样做。

The chickadee tilts its head, seemingly confused. Perhaps even a little annoyed.
山雀歪了歪头，表情流露出许多不解，和一点点被捉弄的恼怒。

Sonetto: These books are from Timekeeper's collection ...
十四行诗：这些都是司辰的藏书……

Sonetto: ... If you write on its pages in ink, it'll spoil the experience for the next reader.
十四行诗：而且在上面用墨水写字，可能也会影响之后其他的借阅者。

Зима: ... S-sorry.
冬：……抱，抱歉。

Зима: It's not ... stone ... anymore. I ... forgot ...
冬：这不是……不再是……石头。我……忘记了……

Sonetto: It's because you don't have any blank paper. Here, use this notebook.
十四行诗：原来是因为您没有空白的纸了。这儿有一些没用过的笔记本，您可以试试……

Sonetto: Just like your piles of parchment, I've filled many notebooks. Writing down my thoughts ... helps me clear my head.
十四行诗：就像您这些满满当当的羊皮纸一样，我也已经写满了许多笔记本。记录想法……可以帮我理清思绪。

Зима: I ... u-use parchment ... to write.
冬：我……用，用羊皮纸，写。

Sonetto: Of course. Parchment is a very good choice.
十四行诗：当然。羊皮纸也是很好的选择。

Sonetto: I think there's some over there ... I'll go look for it. Hold on.
十四行诗：我记得这边还有一些——可以找找。请稍等。

Зима: …
冬：……

Sonetto: It should be here ... Ah, found it.
十四行诗：应该在这里的……啊，找到了。

When she turns around, a breath of cold air sweeps past her.
当她转身，感觉到一阵冷然的气息掠过。

The book remains open on the desk. The quill, its owner, and the chickadee named "зима" have all disappeared.
书还在桌面上摊开。羽毛笔同他的主人，与名为“冬”的山雀，一起消失了。

Sonetto: Mr. Зима ...?
十四行诗：冬先生……？

Sonetto: Did I say something wrong ...?
十四行诗：是我说错了什么吗……

Archives, Foundation
基金会档案室

Sonetto: Every time I ask him a question, it seems like he doesn't want to answer.
十四行诗：每次提问，他似乎都不愿回答。

Sonetto: And what did he mean by "stone"?
十四行诗：“石头”，又是指什么呢？

Sonetto: Well ... I could take a look at his file. Maybe it'll help me avoid making the same mistake again.
十四行诗：好吧，只是看一下。也许了解原因，下次就能规避这种错误了。

Sonetto: I'll just take a little peek ...
十四行诗：只是看一下——

Sonetto: ...?
十四行诗：……？

The file contains only a few pages, despite covering half of his life within it.
档案只有寥寥数页。一个人的前半生即被囊括其中。

02  黑林私语 / Whispers in Black Forest

Whispers in Black Forest
黑林私语

Sonetto stands under the overhead light, slowly reading aloud the words in her hands.
十四行诗站在顶光下，将手中的文字轻缓地读出声来。

Sonetto: "In 1912, the poet was charged with 'insulting the highest authorities.' He was sent to Eastern Siberia, then transferred through various prisons, and eventually exiled to a solitary island."
十四行诗：“1912年，诗人被定以‘侮辱最高当局’罪名，押送至东西伯利亚，随后通过转运监狱，流放孤岛。”

Sonetto: "Over the next decade, he lived there in isolation ..."
十四行诗：“此后十余年，定居于此……”

Sonetto: "His poetry ... was inseparable from ... the animals on the island ..."
十四行诗：“与岛屿上的动物……诗歌……形影不离……”

In the forest, Зима sits on a rock in a clearing.
林中，冬正坐在空地的岩石上。

A group of animals surrounds him. A small forest gathering.
许多动物环绕着他。此处，俨然一个小型的森林集会。

Зима: ..."Believe not in the unbelievable,"
"And touch not that which is untouchable,"
冬：——“对不可相信的话，请别相信”

“对不可触碰之物，请别伸手”

A brown bear beside him places a thick paw on his arm, making the parchment in his hand wobble.
身边，棕熊抬起厚实的手掌，搭到他举着羊皮纸的小臂上。

Зима: Sir, please don't touch ...
冬：先生，请别伸手……

Зима: There should be no pause here. It interrupts the meter.
冬：这里，不该停顿。韵脚会乱。

Зима: Ahem.
冬：咳。

Зима: "Look not back on the path you did not take,"
"Iron wings, a warning to the hunter makes."
"Yet in his greed, he disregards it still."
冬：“对不可后悔的路，请别回头”

“铁翅对猎人，如此告诫”

“可这猎人，轻信且贪婪”

On his left, a fox paws his sleeve disapprovingly.
左边，铅灰色的狐狸不满地衔住他的衣袖。

Зима: Please behave. Wait until I'm done ...
冬：请认真些，先听完……

Зима: ..."Try as he might he cannot reach his prey,"
"Futility destroys his world this day."
冬：——“不得所求”

“便妄图抹杀所有”

A sable drapes itself around his neck. It whispers in his ear.
黑貂亲热地围着他的脖子，窃窃私语。

Зима: ... Yes, you're right. The hunter is simply incorrigible.
冬：……是的。您说得对。这位猎人先生，简直无药可救。

Зима: And ... I'm afraid there are still many such people in the world.
冬：并且……恐怕现在，仍有这样的人。

Зима: May they reap what they sow ...
冬：愿他们，自食其果……

In the background of this harmonious scene, a reindeer shakes its antlers.
融洽的景象中，站在身后，驯鹿轻轻摇晃巨大的双角。

Her dissent nearly knocks the poet off the rock upon which he is sitting.
一些反对意见的苗头，险些把诗人从岩石上晃了下去。

Зима: Ah, miss ...!
冬：啊，小姐……！

Зима: I ... ahem, ahem. I understand.
冬：我，咳咳。我明白。

Зима: You're saying that the hunter is just trying to survive ... I ... I simply don't agree.
冬：但您说，猎人，也只是为了生存……这我……我不能同意。

Зима: It is out of vanity, not hunger, that he refuses to return empty-handed.
冬：是虚荣，使他拒绝空手而归，不是饥饿。

Зима: Are there truly gems in a pigeon's belly? The branch it perches on cannot bear, um, the weight of a man ...
冬：鸽子的腹中，真的会有宝石吗？它所站的枝桠，也承受不起，呃，一个男子的重量……

Зима: The hunter aims to persecute the pigeon, yet it warns him. When he falls into the snow, the snow embraces him.
冬：猎人，要迫害鸽子，鸽子却告诫他；跌落进雪地，雪地也接纳他。

Зима: Truly ... forgiving.
冬：真是……宽容。

Зима: Yes, it reminds me of ...
冬：对呀，这令我、想起……

Зима: "How can we not love earth more than heaven?"
冬：“我们怎能不爱人间，更胜过天堂？”

Зима: Nature is the fabric of our world.
冬：自然，是人间的远景。

Зима: We are like potted plants ... placed amidst its great garden.
冬：我们像盆栽……被放置其中。

A chickadee flies from afar, landing firmly and accurately on his shoulder.
山雀从远处飞来，稳当、准确地落在他肩膀上。

Surprise and intimacy. A pair of long-lost friends.
惊喜，亲昵，一对久违的友人。

Зима: My friend!
冬：我的朋友！

Зима: You've been gone for so long. I had begun to think that you had fallen into a ... a hunter's trap.
冬：你似乎去了，太久。我快以为你，是落入了哪……哪个猎人的陷阱。

The surrounding animals immediately become alert.
动物们几乎立刻便警戒起来。

Зима: But there are no hunters here ...
冬：虽然，这里并没有什么，“猎人”……

Зима: Just us.
冬：只有我们。

The hickadee shakes its head and chirps, displeased.
山雀却摇头，吱吱喳喳，模样并不畅快。

It looks desperate for the full attention of its friends.
它看上去急切地需要来自朋友的全部注意力。

Зима: What did you say?
冬：你说什么？

Зима: Wha—
冬：什……

Зима: ...!
冬：……！

Зима: ... No, I, ahem! Ahem, ahem. I should ... go ...
冬：……不，我，咳！咳咳。我得，走了……

The news leaves him trembling. Зима's papers scatter to the ground, but he is too hurried to pick them up.
这消息令他浑身震悚，手中稿纸散落一地，甚至来不及捡拾。

He leaps down from the rock, puts out the fire, and disappears into the depths of the forest.
冬跳下岩石，踏灭了篝火，随即往针叶林的更深处躲去。

His friends, of course, follow close behind.
“朋友们”，当然也随他跑走。

"According to the poet's own account, the years on the island were marked by 'bitter cold and loyal companions.'"
“据诗人本人描述，岛上的岁月伴随着‘苦寒，与忠诚的朋友’。”

Sonetto: "'Many friends ...'"
十四行诗：“‘很多朋友……’”

By now, she has fallen into a nearby chair and is sitting in contemplation. Her gaze lingers on the file.
女孩思索着，视线停留在档案上。

"These quiet years lasted at least until the mid-1920s."
“而这段平静岁月，至少持续到20年代中叶。”

"Until a human journalist from the government set foot in that land of the Far East."
“直到当局的人类记者踏上了这片极东的土地。”

03  我自愿流放！ / Self-Exile

Self-exile
我自愿流放！

Journalist: It's so ... desolate.
记者：真是荒凉……

Journalist: Compared to this place, Tobolsk is like the Leningrad of Siberia.
记者：和这里比起来，托博尔斯克简直就像西伯利亚的列宁格勒。

Journalist: There must be a bounty of untapped resources here.
记者：这地方一定还有很多未开发的资源。

Sailor: I'd be more focused on finding a place to stay if I were you.
船员：别想太多。先赶紧给自己找个落脚的地方吧，年轻人。

船员：这儿只有监狱，孩子。

Journalist: ... Prisons?
记者：……只有监狱？

Sailor: You heard me. Not even a pigeon would stop to crap in this cesspit.
船员：是的，监狱。欢迎来到这鸟都不停下来拉屎的鬼地方。

Journalist: So, where are these prisons?
记者：那最近的监狱在——

Sailor: You're in one, mate.
船员：你的脚下。

Journalist: Ha! I get it. You're messing with me.
记者：……您不愿告诉我，也不必这样捉弄我。

Sailor: Tsk. You came all this way, and you don't even know a single thing about this place. You're dead meat.
船员：来到这种地方，却什么也不知道。你会丢命的。

Journalist: I just came here to investigate ...
记者：我来这里，正是为了调查……

Sailor: Head south. Or buy another ticket and come back with us. That's my suggestion.
船员：往南走，或者再买张票跟我们一起回去。这是我的建议。

Journalist: I ... can't.
记者：这可……不行。

Journalist: When will your ship come back again?
记者：您的船下次来，是什么时候？

Sailor: Side barges are centrally managed ... It could be 15 days. Maybe longer.
船员：边部驳船由区中央统一管理……可能得等上十五日。或者更久。

Sailor: Until then, do your best to stay alive.
船员：在那之前，先活着吧。

"The government journalist discovered the poems and stories while searching for shelter."
“四处寻找容身之所的当局记者，发现了那些诗歌和故事。”

"They were written on pieces of wood, stones, and scattered parchment."
“在木片上、石头上，在散落的羊皮纸上。”

"Ecstatic at his discovery, the journalist set out to identify the author and collect them all."
“记者欣喜若狂，决定找到作者，好将它们全数收下。”

Sonetto: Mr. Зима ... doesn't seem like someone who could handle something like that.
十四行诗：冬先生……似乎没办法应付这样的事。

Journalist: If it weren't for these stone-paved roads, I swear, I would have marked this place as a "no man's land."
记者：如果没有这些石头铺成的路，我发誓，我一定会把这儿记录成“无人区”。

In an opening not far away, crude shacks, some empty or collapsed, stretch out like long scars torn into the earth.
不远处，空地上的简陋棚屋，或空置，或坍塌，连成一片，像地表被撕开的长长伤疤。

At the center lies an extinguished campfire.
这疤痕突兀地静止在这儿，与熄灭的篝火堆一同沉默。

Journalist: What about those people who were exiled about a decade ago? Did they all escape?
记者：十多年前被流放的那些人呢？难道全部逃走了？

记者：那些现存记录里，流放政策的尾声……

记者：不过，这种环境……无论谁来，肯定都时刻想着逃走。

记者：算了。先观察一下生活痕迹，也许会有发现——

Journalist: Huh? The fire. It's still warm.
记者：咦？篝火竟然还有余温？

Journalist: Is this ...?
记者：这是……？

Half-burned parchment is scattered around the campfire in a scene of attempted destruction. He picks up one sheet.
看上去有些年头的羊皮纸，散落在篝火周围。一种毁尸灭迹未遂的现场。

Journalist: ... Parchment paper with poetry on it and a still-smoldering campfire!
记者：——这些稿纸，这些诗句！尚有余温的篝火！

Journalist: Someone is definitely here!
记者：这儿一定有人来过！

Journalist: Who could it be? Who would write poetry in a place like this?
记者：是谁？谁在这种地方写诗？

It is an amazing discovery. The next occurrence, however, is even more surprising.
惊人的发现。可惜，意外总是更令人措手不及。

A bird swoops over and snatches the parchment from his hand like an eagle plucking a fish from the ocean.
鸟儿从他手中抢走稿纸，就如同从雕塑的眼睛上衔走宝石一样轻易。

记者：那是我的！我发现的！你这个坏东西！

Journalist: Give it back!
记者：还给我！

Humans who neglect to exercise are no match for wild animals.
疏于锻炼的人类，并非野生动物旗鼓相当的对手。

What this human now faces is a primitive yet most difficult-to-resist ambush.
更何况，这个人类遇上的，是最为低级，同时也最难以招架的偷袭与群殴。

Primitive, but efficient. It is orderly and prepared.
原始，但高效。整齐划一，有备而来。

Journalist: What's going on!?
记者：这突然都是怎么了！

Journalist: How are there so many ...!?
记者：怎么这么多……！？

The birds, dedicated to driving off the intruder, give him no answer.
致力于齐心协力驱逐外来人的鸟儿，并不会回答他的问题。

Bird: *chirps*
鸟：啾啾——

Journalist: P-please, let go of my clothes!
记者：请，请放开我的衣服！

Journalist: Where are you taking me?
记者：你们要带我去哪儿？

Journalist: The parchment!? I don't want the parchment! Give me back my notebook ...
记者：羊皮纸！羊皮纸我不要了！把笔记本还给我……

Journalist: No! That's the brooch my mother left me!
记者：不！那是我母亲留给我的胸针！

In such chaos, opportunities are fleeting.
混乱中，脱身的机会稍纵即逝。

He must grab it and take back what is his!
抓住它，抢回属于自己的东西！

突然爆发的挣扎，显然也具有惊人的、且可持续好几秒的威慑力。

鸟群的动作有短暂的停顿。随后，一个人影自林中休憩处缓步挪出来。

04  唾手偶得 / An Easy Discovery

An Easy Discovery
唾手偶得

Journalist: …
记者：……

Journalist: "... We grow, yet no one knows,"
记者：“……我们生长，却无人知晓”

Journalist: "Like poplars in the winter."
记者：“像寒冬的白杨”

Journalist: Did you write that?
记者：这是您写的吗？

Journalist: It's beautiful. But ...
记者：真的很美。但……

Зима: ...?
冬：……？

Journalist: Could you please ask your bear friend to stand a little further back?
记者：您能不能先让您的熊朋友，站远些？

The huge brown bear stands stubbornly between the two, a mighty guard.
巨大的棕熊，像一个威猛的护卫，执着地伫立在两人中间。

Зима: ... S-sorry.
冬：……抱，抱歉。

Зима: Mr. Medvedevich, please, stand ... on my left.
冬：梅德维奇先生，请、您站……到我的左手边来。

她抬头，甚至不必转向。桌上的羊皮纸已经被“冬”衔住边角展开来。

Sonetto: ... Ah.
十四行诗：……啊。

Of course, a poet would present a poem.
诗人当然会留下诗。

Poetry can give voice to the dead.
诗歌是死去的语言。诗人便使死者复生。

Poetry can be a prophecy or simply an expression of one's thoughts and emotions.
某种谶语，或仅写下所想所感。

In one hundred feet of snow,
A dead branch a quill will be, sufficient
To write about the moon's passage both night and day;
To write about the frosted needles caught in treetops, the sound of the wind;
To write about the nothingness of night and the all-embracing sea.
They light a bonfire,
Crackling, flickering,
And before the fire leaves their eyes, they sing,
What did you see?
The rocks do not reply,
But a young voice says,
百尺长的雪地
枯枝也是羽毛笔，足以
写月亮行走过，昨夜，今日
写树梢勾住的，霜针，风声
写什么也没有的夜色，包容万物的海
也燃篝火
噼啪，闪烁
在眼睛寂灭前，唱着
“你看见了什么？”
石头并不回答
只有，一个年轻的声音

"Humanity."
“人间”

зима's small, distinct black claw marks are stamped on the corner of the parchment.
一枚来自“冬”的，小而清晰的黑色爪印，踩在羊皮纸的末端。

A budding sprout, the branching veins of a leaf, ...
初发的嫩芽，或叶脉上长开的分叉。

The two signatures form a journey that has never been taken.
两枚签名，组成一场未曾踏出的旅行。
