01  很久很久之前 / In Days of Yore

The Ninth Virtue
第九条美德

In Days of Yore
很久很久之前

Paimpont Forest
潘蓬特森林

The drizzle, which had fallen for several days without end, stopped just before dark.
连绵数日的细雨，在天黑前停止了。

A horse's hooves thud against the wet earth and moss.
马蹄叩击在湿润的泥土与绿苔之上，发出沉闷的响声。

Dense branches obscure the sky. Occasionally, pheasants and turtledoves swoop across the trail ahead—this is Paimpont Forest.
茂密的山毛榉遮天蔽日，偶尔有野鸡和斑鸠跳过眼前的小径——这里是潘蓬特森林。

Arriving here means their trip is nearing its end.
到达了这里，就意味着这次的行程接近尾声。

Ganelon: Company, halt—we'll make camp here today.
加尼隆：所有人停下——今天我们就在这儿驻扎。

Ganelon: According to the dictates of our order, there is no need to fast today. The deer that Sir Roland hunted can be taken out and roasted.
加尼隆：按规定，今天不必斋戒。先前由罗兰骑士猎下的那只鹿，可以拿出来烤了。

Happy cheers arise from all sides. Only in one corner is there deep silence—there, a figure sits squat.
周围响起了雀跃的欢呼声，唯有一角暗沉的寂静——那是一个蹲坐的人影。

It is Sir Roland, the leader of their band and His Majesty Charlemagne's most trusted defender—soon to be named the Margrave of Brittany to serve as their paramount lord and ruler.
那是罗兰骑士，队伍的领军者，最受查理曼陛下信任的捍卫者，以及——即将成为布列塔尼要塞军事总督的受勋者。

Ganelon: Sir Roland, we expect to arrive at the castle on the morrow … But sire, if you worry about an ambush, we might continue under the cover of darkness in order to arrive earlier.
加尼隆：罗兰阁下，我们预计在明天就会到达军事要塞……如果你担心有偷袭，也可以趁夜晚行进，尽早到达。

Ganelon: You know—in the eyes of many in Brittany, your head is now worth more than its weight in gold.
加尼隆：你知道的——在布列塔尼人的眼里，你的头颅比等量的黄金更贵重。

The seemingly downcast youth rises to his feet.
看似低落的青年站了起来。

Roland: No, Sir Ganelon, you mistake me.
罗兰：不，加尼隆，你误会了。

Roland: I'm thinking—
罗兰：我是在想——

Roland: What should I say if were to find a deer without any eyes?
罗兰：你见过没有眼睛的鹿吗？

The question alarms Ganelon, who shakes his head slowly.
熟悉的问法让加尼隆产生了警惕，他缓慢地摇了摇头。

Roland: I have no-eye deer.
罗兰：是无眼鹿【No idea】。

Ganelon: …
加尼隆：……

Ganelon: Sire, enough of these jokes of yours.
加尼隆：阁下，别再琢磨你的那些笑话了。

Ganelon: As for deer, the buck you hunted will soon be prepared.
加尼隆：你还记得吗？前几天猎到的那头牡鹿。

Ganelon: Unless you wish to continue eating only bread and beans, I suggest you join us and have some.
加尼隆：如果你不想继续吃白面包和豌豆，那就过来吧。

Dusk is falling.
暮色四合。

The knights cut down a few cedars near their camp—in the damp weather, only their dry and oily heartwood is sufficient for starting a fire.
骑士们砍伐了一些杉木，用斧子把木材劈成小块——在湿润的天气里，树心的木材干燥且富含油脂，是生火的好材料。

Flames leap up, casting a warm orange glow. A young knight in their company brings over the prepared venison.
火苗燃起，映照出一片暖融融的橙色。
一位见习骑士将处理好的鹿肉端了上来。

Knight I: I've marinated the meat as best I could in our circumstances. If we had fennel and cardamom, we might sprinkle some on while it roasts. The heat would bring out the flavors nicely.
骑士Ⅰ：我把肉块腌好了，如果有茴香和豆蔻，可以在烤的时候撒上，高温会激发它们的风味。

Knight I: Of course, the best spices would be black pepper and cumin …
骑士Ⅰ：当然，最好的调味料还是黑椒和孜然……

Knight I: But such extravagance ... We'll probably only taste the likes of those spices again when His Majesty deigns to celebrate with us.
骑士Ⅰ：那太奢侈了……也许只有等查理曼陛下为诸位庆功时，才能品尝到这些香料的味道了。

The knights skewer the meat at an angle over the fire pit.
骑士们将肉块以一个斜角插在了火堆旁的土地中。

The buck's fat is rich and heavy. As time passes, it melts in the heat, releasing a wonderful aroma.
肉块中的油脂丰腴厚重。随着时间的推移，它们被高温所融化，散发出美妙的香气。

*Rumble—*
“咕咕——”

Knight I: My lord, look at how the fat is dripping.
骑士Ⅰ：主啊，这块肉的油脂都溢出来了。

Knight II: I can't even remember the last time I had fresh meat.
骑士Ⅱ：……我已经想不起来上一次吃肉是在什么时候了。

Roland: This morning.
罗兰：在今天早晨。

Knight II: This morning?
骑士Ⅱ：早晨？

Roland: Did our Lord not transform bread and wine into flesh and blood?
罗兰：主说过，“这是我的身体和血，是为众人免罪而舍弃和流出的。”

Roland: Tis true. Our morning meal was not the Holy Communion, but if unleavened bread can serve as a fitting vessel for our Lord, it must surely be the equal of any venison.
罗兰：虽然那并不是一次圣餐礼，但我们确实食用了面包与无酵饼。

A brief, eerie silence follows, only to be broken with a scoff.
一阵短暂、诡异的沉默。

Knight II: Shall I laugh now, or wait until after the cardinal has your head on a pike?
骑士Ⅱ：……您的幽默感总是如此突然，我现在是该笑吗？

Roland: If I have offended, then I beg your pardon.
罗兰：我深感歉意。

Roland: But let me ask a new question.
罗兰：但我有一个新的疑惑。

Knight II: Yes, my Lord Margrave?
骑士Ⅱ：罗兰阁下，请说。

Roland: I've told not less than thirteen jokes today, and I must imagine that at least one of them was clever.
罗兰：我在今天已经连续讲述了十三个笑话，其中不乏一些灵机一动的精妙之作。

Roland: Tell me then, why has no one laughed?
罗兰：为什么没有人愿意笑一笑呢？

Knight I: Sir Roland, I swear, that pun about the deer was the wittiest I've heard in some time! It was only that in the moment, I forgot to laugh.
骑士Ⅰ：罗兰阁下，我敢发誓，那是我近几天听到过最巧妙的双关语！

Knight I: But I'm afraid I didn't quite catch the meaning of the joke you made about "unleavened bread"?
骑士Ⅰ：只是这个无酵饼的笑话——您愿意解释一下吗？

Roland: Is that so? The cardinal will be busy indeed, young sir. Have you forgotten that the bread of our Communion becomes flesh? So then, our bit of bread this morning could well become meat, if our Lord deigned it so. It is ... a little blasphemous, I must admit.
罗兰：嗯……当然，无酵饼在圣餐礼中代表着肉。我的意思是，我们在早晨已经吃过肉了——这个幽默基于一点小小的冒犯。

Knight I: Hahahaha!
骑士Ⅰ：哈哈哈哈哈哈哈哈哈！

Knight II: …
骑士Ⅱ：……

Ganelon: …
加尼隆：……

Ganelon: The devil take you, Roland. These jokes of yours are—Not—Funny!
加尼隆：见鬼，这些笑话根本就不！好！笑！

Ganelon: A lie repeated a thousand times still yet does not become the truth—even if you told forty jokes, not a one would be funny!
加尼隆：谎言即使重复一千遍也不会变成真理——你哪怕说上四十个笑话，也是照样地不好笑！

Roland: Ganelon, I think you must not understand the word "diligence."
罗兰：加尼隆，我认为你对“勤奋”这个词存在误解。

Roland: As the proverb says, "Practice makes perfect." Anything, if pursued persistently enough, will surely lead to success.
罗兰：就像谚语中所说的那样——“熟能生巧”。任何事情，只要足够坚持，一定能有所成就。

Ganelon: Rather than discussing the definition of diligence, I would prefer you not turn yourself into a joke.
加尼隆：……相比于研究勤奋的定义，我更希望你不要把自己变成一个笑话。

Ganelon: "Many small streams make a river." Knights, is there anyone here capable of telling some half-wise decent jokes to our Lord Roland?
加尼隆：“涓涓之水，才能汇成江河。”——骑士们，有人愿意讲点正常的笑话给罗兰阁下听吗？

Knight II: I have one!
骑士Ⅱ：让我来！

…
……

This was to be the very first instance of the "Comic Knight Festival."
这是“骑士笑话节”的雏形。

Born on a night in the 8th century, as feast among a band of true companions.
它诞生于公元8世纪的一个夜晚，由一群骑士所发起。

Each among them had come from different backgrounds and held various positions, but there was no doubt they could serve as excellent judges.
骑士们的出身各不相同，职位也各有高低，但毫无疑问，他们都是优秀的评委。

After all, a sense of humor doesn't need to be taught. It precedes any rules and structure—the only truth is humor is what makes one laugh.
毕竟，幽默不需要教习，它的存在先于任何规则——每个人天然地具有对幽默的审判力。

As one knight finishes his joke.
一名骑士结束了他的笑话。

Another starts the next.
一名骑士又开始了他的笑话。

And on it goes until, at last, a sprinkle of fennel is scattered over the venison, forming a mouth-watering aroma of citrus and pine gum.
直到有人在鹿肉上撒了一把茴香，它发出了类似柑橘和松脂混合的香气。

Knight I: The meal is ready.
骑士Ⅰ：鹿肉可以吃了。

The edges of the meat are charred brown and sizzling with grease.
肉块的边缘已经被烤成了焦褐色，正吱吱地冒着油花。

The young knight turns chef for the evening and smears fresh wild honey on the meat.
见习骑士将新鲜的野蜂蜜抹在了肉块上。

Knight I: My turn.
骑士Ⅰ：轮到我说了。

Knight I: Oh, but if only we had a pound of Roquefort cheese …
骑士Ⅰ：唔，如果有一磅罗奎福特奶酪就好了……

Yet the savory aroma of roasted meat serves as more than enough. He swallows his piece with a satisfied gulp.
不论如何，肉类的焦香总能令人感到放松。他的口腔略感餍足，于是喉头一滚，将鹿肉吞入腹中。

Knight I: Unlike the rest of you, I will tell a proper joke with some theatrical flair, where I shall take on the dramatis personae of one among us.
骑士Ⅰ：和诸位不同，我要讲一个基于假设与扮演的笑话。

Knight I: Though this joke may not be to everyone's tastes, I would use it to express admiration and praise for a worthy hero …
骑士Ⅰ：虽然这个笑话可能略有失礼与无趣，但我想借此表达自己对英雄人物的向往与赞美……

Then, in a booming practiced voice, he begins his joke.
随后，他用所有人都可以听清楚的音量，开始了他的笑话。

Knight I: There once was a knight both true and faithful, whose great deeds much envy provokes! But where others would boast, his friends were most grateful, that the worst of his faults were his jokes!
骑士Ⅰ：有这样一名骑士，他有着数不清的功绩，却依然乐呵呵地与同僚打成一片，毫无架子。

Knight I: I speak, of course, of myself! Roland, your liege on the eve of our arrival in Brittany, for soon I will be named—
骑士Ⅰ：那就是我，即将前往布列塔尼，受封的骑士罗兰——

Knight I: That is this very day, I …
骑士Ⅰ：今天，我……

*whoosh*
“咻——”

Roland: …!
罗兰：……！

—An arrow.
——一支箭。

Knight I: Ah—
骑士Ⅰ：啊——

An arrow has pierced through the young knight's throat. The light fades from his eyes as he falls to the ground.
见习骑士喉部中箭，他的眼睛如同鹿肉上正在凝固的油脂，渐渐地失去了光泽。

Ganelon: We're under attack!
加尼隆：……敌袭！

Roland: Knights, to your swords! Avenge our comrade!
罗兰：骑士们，拿起你们的剑！为同伴报仇！

02  辉光管 / Nixie Tube

Nixie Tube
辉光管

Somewhere in the Suitcase
箱内一隅

In the hall, a slender plant stands, with green flower buds clinging to its stalk.
厅中，草本植物摇曳着它纤长的枝叶，嫩青色的花穗还攀附在柔软的茎秆上。

Its branches sway in a metered rhythm.
它们以恒定的节律摇晃着。

Oliver Fog: Here's what happened.
雾行者：……事情是这样的。

Oliver Fog: A few days ago, I asked A Knight to tell me the story of Pepin the Short.
雾行者：前几天，我想让未锈铠先生给我讲讲丕平三世的故事。

Oliver Fog: So I knocked on the door to his room, but no one answered. Then I went to check the patio, but he wasn't there either.
雾行者：我敲了他的房门，没有人应声，我又找遍了庭院，他也不在。

Oliver Fog: Next, I asked Vertin, but … She didn't know either.
雾行者：于是我询问了维尔汀小姐，但……她也并不知情。

An-an Lee: Ah! I understand, Sir Fog! So you want to know where Sir Knight has gone.
泥鯭的士：啊～我懂你啦，雾sir！你是想知道铠sir去哪儿了？

An-an Lee: Well, it is true I am the very best when it comes to ghost hunting! But Sir Knight is not exactly a ghost. He's more of a haunting character …
泥鯭的士：捉鬼我最擅长啦！不过铠sir他不算鬼喔，他应该算“日用品”那类……

Oliver Fog: Apologies, I suppose I don't quite understand your arcane skill, but … perhaps you still have a knack for finding people.
雾行者：抱歉，我对你的神秘术并不了解，但……也许你有什么找人的诀窍。

Oliver Fog fishes out a few Sharpodonties from his pocket.
雾行者从口袋里摸出几个利齿子儿。

Oliver Fog: This is work. I know the rules.
雾行者：——这是工作，我明白规矩。

Oliver Fog: I did not prepare much, but maybe I can pay a deposit first.
雾行者：我的准备不多，但也许可以先支付定金。

An-an Lee: Huh? What's this?
泥鯭的士：咦？这是？

It dawns on her.
她恍然大悟。

An-an Lee: Oh, how generous! In that case, I'll certainly help you.
泥鯭的士：哇，老板豪气！我一定帮你帮到底。

An-an Lee: Besides catching ghosts, asking Lord Lao Zi for a little direction is a simple matter …
泥鯭的士：捉不得鬼，帮你问下太上老君也是easy job啦……

She takes the two pieces from Oliver's hand.
她从雾行者的手心取走了其中的两枚。

An-an Lee: And it is a kind gesture to help out a friend …
泥鯭的士：帮朋友忙，自然是友情价……

An-an Lee: Hm, what was it that Grandma taught me again?
泥鯭的士：唔，阿婆怎么教我的来着？

An-an Lee: Whatever. As long as you truly believe it, that's all that matters!
泥鯭的士：……算了，心诚则灵就好了嘛！

An-an Lee cups three arcade tokens in her hands, clasping them together and shaking them forcefully.
泥鯭的士用手拢起三个游戏厅的硬币，合掌之后，用力地摇晃着。

An-an Lee: Spirits of heaven and earth …
泥鯭的士：天灵灵，地灵灵……

Oliver Fog: …
雾行者：……

Oliver Fog: At first, I thought A Knight might have been on a mission outside … So, I specifically asked Ms. Vertin.
雾行者：我一度怀疑未锈铠先生在外执行任务……为此，我特地询问了维尔汀小姐。

Oliver Fog: But Ms. Vertin said that A Knight hasn't been on any missions recently.
雾行者：但维尔汀小姐说过，未锈铠先生近日并没有外派任务。

Oliver Fog: But he had asked Vertin for several days of leave.
雾行者：不过，他向维尔汀请了很多天的假。

Oliver Fog: I wonder if he has some important matters to attend to?
雾行者：——是有什么重要的事情需要去处理吗？

An-an Lee: Hm … yes, I see him!
泥鯭的士：嗯……我倒是有看到他噢！

The young ghost hunter begins her soothsaying.
年轻的占卜师开始了回忆。

An-an Lee: I see that Sir Knight is as he was before. He still loves telling those dreadful jokes.
泥鯭的士：铠sir呢，还是同以前一样，爱讲那些古灵精怪的笑话。

An-an Lee: But—it seems he has been collecting something recently! They look like small colorful banners and trumpets …
泥鯭的士：不过——他最近好像在收集一些东西哦！像什么小彩旗、小号角……

An-an Lee: It's not clear to me what he's doing!
泥鯭的士：也不知道他要做什么！

She stops her hands and examines the answer the coins give.
她停下了手，观看硬币们所给出的答案。

An-an Lee: Hmm …
泥鯭的士：嗯……

An-an Lee: The first hexagram, Lesser Yin; the second hexagram, Lesser Yang …
泥鯭的士：第一爻，少阴；第二爻，少阳……

An-an Lee: The sixth hexagram, Old Yin ... Changing to the Hexagram of Earth over Water.
泥鯭的士：第六爻，老阴……地水师，上六变卦。

An-an Lee falls silent.
泥鯭的士陷入沉思。

An-an Lee: Oh, ho!
泥鯭的士：哦呦！

She has learned her grandma's exaggerated manner of shouting.
她学着记忆里阿婆的样子夸张地大叫着。

An-an Lee: Oh dear, that's terrible, Sir Fog!
泥鯭的士：大镬啦， 雾sir！

Oliver Fog: What does all of this mean?
雾行者：这是？

An-an Lee: The Hexagram of Earth over Water shows that Sir Knight is going to war and that the war will be both unpredictable and hazardous.
泥鯭的士：地水师，说明铠sir要去打仗，这个仗会打得状况百出，前途堪忧啊。

An-an Lee: And this changing of the Hexagram shows ... there may be traitors among Sir Knight's forces, so he must be extra cautious.
泥鯭的士：而这个上六变卦……说明铠sir的军队里可能有反骨仔哦，要加倍小心才行。

Oliver Fog: Going to war?
雾行者：……打仗？

Oliver Fog: In the suitcase?
雾行者：在箱子里？

Oliver Fog: This sounds rather more like a tall tale …
雾行者：这听起来更像是故事……

An-an Lee: Hmm ... The war indicated in the hexagram isn't some mere skirmish.
泥鯭的士：唔……卦象上显示的打仗，不是普通的小打小闹哦。

The branches of the potted plant stop their rhythm and begin to tremble.
恒定的节律消失了，盆栽中的花枝微不可察地颤抖着。

And though perhaps it is only old circuits, the fluorescent lights in the hall begin to flicker faintly.
也许是电路老化，大厅中的辉光管正以一个微弱的频率闪烁。

The orange light in the vision pulses slowly, like twilight in a memory.
橙色的光芒缓缓涌动，如同一次追忆中的黄昏。

Oliver Fog: Perhaps …
雾行者：难道……

The pair look at each other.
二人面面相觑。

Oliver Fog: … Ms. An-an, do you know what era it is outside right now?
雾行者：……安安小姐，你知道外面现在是什么时代吗？

03  俏皮话城邦 / A Burg of Quips

A Burg of Quips
俏皮话城邦

Roland wrings out the water that is pooling on the hem of his clothes.
罗兰拧干衣摆上的积水。

Roland: Rain again …
罗兰：又是下雨……

Roland: Hmph … Where is everyone?
罗兰：呼……人呢？

The trail left by their attacker had disappeared.
偷袭者留下的痕迹消失了。

And his fellow knights had disappeared along with it.
同行者也随之不见了。

Roland: Hm?
罗兰：嗯？

Hoof beats tramp across the mud nearby.
远处传来了马蹄声。

He holds his breath and listens.
他屏息凝神。

The figure of a knight appears in the distance, causing Roland to draw his sword defensively.
远方出现了一个骑士的身影，于是他抽出了剑。

???: … Roland?
？？？：……罗兰？

Roland: Oliver! You nearly drove me out of my wits!
罗兰：奥利弗！你吓死我了！

Oliver: Haha …
奥利弗：哈哈……

Roland mounts his horse, and the pair ride side by side in the rain.
二人在雨中并肩而行，进行了简短的交流。

Oliver: So—
奥利弗：所以——

Oliver: Was it really Ganelon who suggested a festival of jokes? He doesn't seem the type to enjoy comedy.
