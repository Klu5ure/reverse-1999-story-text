01  跨时代尖叫 / A Cry Across Time

The Last Film
最后的电影

A Cry Across Time
跨时代尖叫

Congratulations, Bette.
恭喜你，贝蒂。

Bette: For me?
贝蒂：这是给我的？

Bette: Oh my god, I can finally stop pretending to be someone else on the big screen.
贝蒂：天呐，我终于可以不用在电影里装成别人了……

Bette: Victim C
贝蒂：受害者C

Bette: Ah!
贝蒂：啊！

Bette: What's happening?
贝蒂：怎么了？

What was that?
那究竟是什么？

No matter how hard she tries, Bette can never pinpoint where the chaos began.
再如何回想，贝蒂都难以分辨出混乱究竟是源自哪里。

It feels like reality itself is caving in, as if someone had knocked over a dollhouse with one swift blow.
那是一场全方位的现实崩溃，宛如娃娃屋被人一掌扇倒。

What's going on?
到底发生了什么？

The world is falling apart. Raindrops rise from the ground, defying gravity, while her thoughts plummet, free-falling into the pavement of the film set.
世界开始瓦解，雨滴从地面上升了起来，与之相反，自己的认知却似乎因为从高处坠落而溶进了片场的水泥地。

The scene flips. A woman's scream echos in her ears, sharp and frantic. But it isn't Bette's voice. It belongs to someone else.
翻转，女性的尖叫仍在耳边回响，但那不是贝蒂的声音，是另一个人的。

Bette: Have I gotten too deep into my role and forgotten where the script ends and reality begins?
贝蒂：是我入戏太深，分不清剧本和现实了吗……

Bette: Or was it something the camera captured?
贝蒂：还是……那其实是能被摄影机记录下来的东西？

She covers her mouth and nose with her hand, as if trying to suppress her rising panic.
贝蒂用手掩住了自己的口鼻，以此暗示自己压下心中的恐慌。

Foundation Staff Member I: Miss Bette, is this a good time?
女性职员I：贝蒂小姐，现在方便吗？

Reality at last interrupts Bette's stream of frantic thoughts. More pressing than fear, she needs to return to the present.
来自现实的声音打断了贝蒂的思索，比起心中的恐惧，她更需要先处理眼下的事务。

Bette: Ahem ... Come in.
贝蒂：咳……请进。

A woman in a black-and-white business suit walks in, holding a thin folder in her right arm. Her face hidden under a white mask.
门后是一名戴着白色面具，身着黑白色职业裙装的女性，她右手抱着一本薄薄的文件夹，款款走了进来。

Seeing the masked woman, Bette instinctively touches her upper lip.
看见她被面具遮住的面容，贝蒂下意识地摸了摸自己的上唇。

The mustache she had worn before was gone. The realization hits like an overwhelming sense of nakedness.
那里并没有自己一直佩戴着的绅士胡，猛然发现这点时，贝蒂感觉自己像是没有穿外衣。

Guest Room, Foundation
基金会客房

Foundation Staff Member I: Feeling any better after a night in your own bed?
女性职员I：今天是你出院的第二天，感觉好些了吗？

Bette: Me? Oh ... I guess so ...
贝蒂：我？哦……还好……

She is still alive, at least.
“还好”大概是指自己尚且无性命之忧。

Foundation Staff Member I: Your body has recovered well. You should be able to begin the intake process today.
女性职员I：你的身体恢复得很好，今天就可以开始走收容流程了。

Though she can't read her face behind the mask, the voice carries a certain kindness.
虽然戴着面具，但从说话的语调中，贝蒂听出对面的女性是一位和善的人。

She takes out a form and places it in front of Bette.
她从文件夹中拿出一张表格，将它放到了贝蒂的眼前。

Bette: Intake ...
贝蒂：收容……

Foundation Staff Member I: There's no rush. You have plenty of time to think it over. After all, you've been through a lot. You may need time to process it.
女性职员I：虽然今天就提出来了，但不必着急，你有着充裕的时间进行考虑，毕竟经历了这么大的变故，想必你现在正需要时间来梳理。

Bette takes the form and scrolls across it mechanically.
贝蒂接过表格，有些机械地读着表格上的文字。

Bette: ◇ St. Pavlov Foundation ◇

Name：_____________

Gender：_____________

Arcane Skill：_____________
贝蒂：◇ 圣洛夫基金会 ◇

姓    名：_____________

性    别：_____________

神秘术：_____________

How many times has she filled out forms just like this over the years?
如此常见的表格，在她20年的人生里，她已经填了几十遍。

But the words only pass over her lips. They never sink in, never become anything meaningful.
但那些名词只是从贝蒂的唇边溜过了，它们没能挤进贝蒂的脑中，转化成什么“意义”。

Bette: ...
贝蒂：……

Bette stops reading and stares blankly at the form.
贝蒂停下了阅读，茫然地看着表格。

Foundation Staff Member I: Do you have any questions?
女性职员I：还有什么问题吗？

Bette: Outside ...
贝蒂：外面……

Bette recalls the nightmare she had barely survived.
贝蒂回忆着前不久自己经历过的劫难。

Bette: What happened out there?
贝蒂：究竟发生了什么？

Hallway, Foundation
基金会走廊

Bette: ...
贝蒂：……

Bette: This ...
贝蒂：这……

The staff member leads Bette from the guest room and into the hallway. Here, she finally gets a glimpse beyond the Foundation's sterile walls at the new era awaiting her.
女性职员将贝蒂带到了客房外的走廊，在这里，贝蒂能脱离基金会风格的装潢，尽可能地窥见新时代的一隅。

Beyond the echoing footsteps and hushed voices in the hallway, a peaceful scene unfolds.
剖离基金会走廊中脚步与低语的回声，廊外是一片安详静谧的景象。

A deep blue lake stretching far across the horizon, a white sailboat drifting toward its center.
远处，入眼的是一片湛蓝的湖。湖上，一叶白帆缓缓地驶向湖心。

On the shore, a family sits on a pale pink picnic blanket. The children are running in and out of the shade of the trees, while their mother watches them from the edge of the grass.
岸边，一家几口正在粉白色的餐布上野餐，孩子们在树荫中奔跑，母亲坐在餐布和草地的边界注视着他们。

Foundation Staff Member I: The outside world is safe now. Once you've recovered, you'll be free to leave the Foundation, though few choose to.
女性职员I：外面现在很安全，你也可以选择在康复后离开基金会……不过，少有人会这么做。

The sunlight is soft, carving out crisp shadows without feeling harsh or glaring.
阳光和煦，能够将影子切割得很分明，却又不刺眼。

The air beyond is fresh, a stark contrast to the chlorine-laced sterility of the hospital. It feels like the real world.
空气也是如此的新鲜，与充满消毒剂气味的病房相比，这里更贴近人间。

But Bette frowns, staring at the idyllic scene in disbelief.
但贝蒂却皱着眉，难以置信地看着这片美景。

Bette: There's something strange here ... eerie almost.
贝蒂：这太奇怪了……甚至诡异。

Her pupils tremble, and the illusion of peace shatters—but this disaster is one only she can see.
眼瞳震颤，平和轰然欲坠，但这只是贝蒂自己的灾难。

Foundation Staff Member I: You're not the first to say that.
女性职员I：你并不是第一个这么说的人。

The world is still beautiful; yesterday no different from today. So what exactly have I lost?
世界是如此美好，昨天与今天并无分别，那我到底失去了什么？

Bette glances at the staff member beside her. Her voice calm, her masked face unmoving—yet Bette can tell she is still wearing that same rehearsed smile.
贝蒂看着身旁的职员，她的语调仍旧平缓，面具之下，她的脸颊肌肉牵动，似乎仍旧保持着那幅公式化的微笑。

Foundation Staff Member I: The world is mostly the same, but there are things that have changed.
女性职员I：世界还是这个世界，但确实有一些地方变得不同了。

Foundation Staff Member I: Hmm ...
女性职员I：嗯……

The staff member takes out a pen, resting the cap against her lips as she ponders for a moment.
女性职员掏出了一支笔，将笔尾抵在嘴角处，沉吟了一会。

Foundation Staff Member I: That house has a new roof, and the trees have grown taller.
女性职员I：那栋房子的房顶换了一批瓦，树也长高了一些。

Foundation Staff Member I: That park over there—its fence used to be black, the paint was peeling off. Now it's white.
女性职员I：那边那个公园的栅栏之前不是白色的，而是掉了漆的黑色。

She points at them with the pen, speaking as if these shifts were the most natural thing in the world.
职员用笔尾指着，似乎已经对这种变故习以为常，甚至还能心平气和地讲述二者之间的不同。

Her gaze returns to Bette.
她将视线移回到了贝蒂的脸上。

Foundation Staff Member I: If you want to understand what you've been through, you can attend the Foundation's information session once you've recovered. You'll get some answers there.
女性职员I：如果你想知道你之前经历的究竟是什么，可以在康复之后来参加基金会举办的说明会，在那里，你的疑问会得到解答。

Foundation Staff Member I: We've developed a standard recovery process for survivors of the "Storm."
女性职员I：对于那些躲过“暴雨”的幸存者，我们已经形成了一套标准的处理流程。

Foundation Staff Member I: It will help you move past the trauma, adjust to your new life, and if you choose, join us in assisting others.
女性职员I：这套流程既可以帮助你摆脱心理阴影、快速融入新社会，也便于你加入我们，为更多的幸存者提供帮助。

Foundation Staff Member I: You're free to go about the facility as you please. If you'd like, you might want to visit the other survivor who arrived with you.
女性职员I：我们并不会剥夺你自由行动的权利，如果你希望去见见与你一同送来的那位人类女性也可以。

Foundation Staff Member I: Welcome to the world after the "Storm."
女性职员I：总之，欢迎来到雨后的新时代。

Bette: "The other survivor"?
贝蒂：与我一同送来的人类女性？

The screams in her ears never really faded. If anything, they have grown sharper.
耳边的尖叫似乎从未散去，甚至愈加清晰了。

Foundation Staff Member I: Yes. I believe she was your colleague.
女性职员I：是的，她似乎是你的同事。

The staff member flips through the folder, searching for a particular page.
女性职员翻开了手中的文件夹，一页一页地寻找着。

Foundation Staff Member I: Her name is Jones.
女性职员I：她叫琼斯。

Bette: Jones ...
贝蒂：琼斯……

Jones: I hate this place.
琼斯：我真讨厌这里。

Jones braces herself against the fake brick-patterned wooden panel with one hand, pressing down her wind-blown hat with the other.
琼斯一手扶着贴上砖块图样的木板，一手摁着被风刮起的帽子。

Her elegant face is caked in thick, exaggerated makeup.
她那精致的脸上化着夸张又浓重的妆容，像是贝蒂小时候在雨洼里搅拌出来的石头汤。

Jones: It's so high up, and the walls feel awful! They just slapped these painted panels on and called it a day! Why should I have expected better when they call this mess on my face "makeup"?
琼斯：这里这么高，墙还做得这么粗糙！他们就只是把砖块画贴在了木板上而已，他们什么都分辨不出来，所以把我化成这个鬼样子！

Perched atop the tower set, Jones makes no effort to hide her frustration.
高塔布景之上，琼斯毫不遮掩地释放着她的怨气。

Jones: My eyes sting! This foundation is already running like a soup—I feel like I'm going to pass out!
琼斯：我的眼睛好痛！把这么浓稠的粉底糊在我的脸上，我感觉我要晕过去了！

Bette: I'll try to get this in one take so we can wrap up.
贝蒂：我会争取一遍过，这样我们就能早点从这里下去。

Bette keeps her head down, double-checking the harness around her waist, making sure it doesn't peek into the shot.
贝蒂低着头检查着绑在腰间的防护绳，以确保它们不会在镜头之中露馅。

In a few moments, she will be playing the male lead, leaping off the tower right in front of Jones.
再过一会，贝蒂就要扮成男主角的样子，从琼斯的面前一跃而下。

Jones: You'd better get it in one take, Miss Bette. I heard you're a real professional.
琼斯：最好我们能一遍过，贝蒂小姐，我听他们说你是很专业的。

Bette: Uh ... yeah.
贝蒂：嗯……嗯。

The grumbled compliment leaves Bette unsure whether it is genuine or sarcastic.
听着琼斯用如此埋怨的语气讲出赞赏自己的话，贝蒂一时之间也分不清琼斯究竟是什么意思。

She returns to the safety checks, not wanting to push Jones's temper.
她只好埋头检查安全措施，以免触了琼斯的霉头。

A top Hollywood star like her could have Bette fired with a snap of her fingers.
若是惹了琼斯不快，像她这样顶级的好莱坞明星，想要开除贝蒂是很轻易的。

Jones: When are they calling action? They—
琼斯：他们到底什么时候才会发开拍的信号？他们……

Her words are cut off abruptly, like someone sliced them clean through. Bette glances up in confusion, trying to see what has caught her attention.
琼斯的话像是被切面刀斩断了，贝蒂不免疑惑地抬头去确认琼斯是被什么吸引走了注意力。

Jones: What is that?!
琼斯：那是什么？！

Bette: Hm?
贝蒂：嗯？

Jones's body stiffens as she looks ahead.
琼斯的身体僵硬起来，她直挺挺地凝视着远方。

Jones: Something's wrong.
琼斯：出事了。

Bette: ...
贝蒂：……

Following the same eyeline, Bette turns toward something beyond her comprehension.
顺着琼斯的目光看去，贝蒂望见了一片她至今无法理解的景象。

The sky begins to take on a kaleidoscope-like panoply of colors, the phenomenon spreading out like a flood over the horizon.
怪异的感觉如同洪水蔓延，世界仿佛为贝蒂戴上了万花镜。

Reality begins to unravel around them, leaving the tower standing out like a strange island.
随着难以描述的异样逐渐肢解现实，布景高塔已如孤岛。

Until it collapses.
但随即，高塔也陨落了。

将贝蒂带到琼斯的病房后，女性职员便离开了。

Beyond the thick glass, a woman lies inside a capsule-like machine, her face wrapped in bandages. Several grayish-white square monitors are angled toward the head of the device, watching over Jones.
厚玻璃后，一名面部缠满绷带的女性躺在玻璃棺一般的仪器中，几块灰白色的方形显示器扭向仪器的头部，关切地看着琼斯。

整个房间只有机器滴滴作响的声音，女性似乎沉睡着，胸部的起伏若有若无。

听到了贝蒂的声音，女性向窗边轻轻地侧了侧头。

02  渐强符号 / Crescendo

Crescendo
渐强符号

Meeting Room, Foundation
基金会会议室

Foundation Staff Member II: The "Storm" is a temporal anomaly of unknown origin. Before it arrives, the world enters a state known as the Storm Syndrome ...
女性职员II：“暴雨”是一种原因未明的时空灾害，“暴雨”来临前，世界会陷入某种“暴雨症候”中……

A Foundation staff member sits at the speaker's table, delivering a monotonous, rigid explanation of concepts far beyond Bette's imagination.
一名基金会的工作人员坐在发言席上，机械又刻板地介绍着一些贝蒂从未听说过的概念。

The white-tiered conference hall is filled with other people—all survivors of the "Storm."
白色的阶梯议事厅中坐满了人，他们的身份如贝蒂一样，都是“暴雨”幸存者。

Foundation Staff Member II: Raindrops will be observed rising from the ground into the sky, and then almost simultaneously the entire era is replaced.
女性职员II：雨滴会从地面升上天空，随后，时代会在一瞬间更替。

Bette finds herself drawn away from the lecture and onto the faces around her; few seem as lost as she feels, as though they had known this disaster was coming, and now this lecture is merely filling in the details.
贝蒂观察着神色各异的人们，但少有人像贝蒂如此失落。他们仿佛早就知道了这场灾难的降临，现在正悠然享受着这份信息差。

Foundation Staff Member II: And the St. Pavlov Foundation provides ...
女性职员II：而圣洛夫基金会为大家提供了……

Bette begins analyzing each person's attire, trying to piece together the lives they had once led.
贝蒂一个一个地看着议事厅中的人，从他们的衣着中推测着他们曾经的人生。

Bette: Cornflower blue wool suit, perfectly tailored. Gold-striped brown tie, gold watch on the left wrist ... A stockbroker?
贝蒂：知更鸟蓝羊毛西装，裁剪合身，金丝棕条纹领带，左腕处戴着金表……证券人员吗？

Bette: Champagne dress with a white fur shawl, pearl necklace ... A wealthy housewife?
贝蒂：香槟色连衣裙搭配白色皮草披肩，珍珠项链……富太太吧……

Bette continues.
贝蒂漫无目的地环视着自己的周围。

Bette: Beret, fishing vest ... Maybe even a red clown nose, just like the director ...
贝蒂：贝雷帽，钓鱼马甲，说不定脸上还有个红鼻子，就像导演那样……

Bette: ...
贝蒂：……

Bette: If only it really were the director ...
贝蒂：要真是导演就好了……

This is not a good time to be alive.
这不是个好时代。

The streets at night feel like a jungle, the few remaining lights flickering like fireflies in the darkness.
夜晚的街道如同丛林，仅剩的几点灯光像是萤火虫一般在黑暗中飘忽着。

A man walks past one dimly lit alley after another.
男人路过了一处又一处。

With the economy in ruins, people go to bed early—not just to save on lighting, but to sleep through their hunger.
经济萧条之下，人们睡的很早，因为这样不仅不用点灯，而且可以用睡眠来挨过饥饿。

Man: Hunger ... won't matter soon. This will all be over.
男人：饥饿……反正这一切马上就要过去了。

The metal buttons on his top hat occasionally catch stray beams of light, and the tip of his cane scrapes against the walls as he walks.
高礼帽上的金属钮扣时不时反着不知何处射来的光线，文明棍的首端碰撞粗糙的墙壁，发出声响。

He pays no mind to the homeless huddling near the alley's entrance, striding toward a dimly lit restaurant.
他罔视巷口阴影处卧着的流浪汉们，脚步急切地走向半熄灯的餐馆。

The door is locked. Through the fogged-up glass, a faint silhouette moves inside.
门紧闭着，从门上透光的玻璃中时不时能看到一个不停走动的人影。

A flickering streetlight casts an uneven glow onto the asphalt, and the shadow beneath it grows and then swallows up the last slivers of light.
模糊的灯光铺洒在柏油路面，于灯光中移动的影子停了一下，随后逐渐变大，遮掩住了本就不明亮的光。

Click. The restaurant's sign goes dark.
“咔”，餐馆的招牌灭了。

Man: Wait!
男人：等等！

The man hurries forward, reaching the door.
男人快跑了几步，终于来到了餐馆门前。

Man: It's me!
男人：是我！

The shadow inside hesitates, then thick wooden boards are pulled aside, iron chains unwind, and finally, a lock is undone.
影子愣了一下，随后走向门口，卸下挡住门的厚木板，解开绕了几圈的铁链，才终于打开了锁住的门。

Restaurant Owner: Bette?
餐馆老板：贝蒂？

The apparent man grins and removes his hat, a thick, neatly braided plait falling out over his shoulder.
男人灿然一笑，摘下了高礼帽，一条蓬松厚实的麻花辫落在了他的肩膀上。

Man: Got any meat left?
男人：现在还有剩的肉吗？

The shape beneath the man's clothes begins to shift. In a moment, his silhouette transforms into that of a familiar woman.
男人衣服下的身形似乎缓慢变化着，不一会，男人便变成了女人的模样。

Bette: I could do for a steak if you've got any.
贝蒂：要是有牛排就最好了。

Restaurant Owner: Steak? What, you think this is the Ritz?

She flips directly to the page she's after. Even during Jones's bedridden final days, she never stopped reading it; the page had visible creases from overuse.
贝蒂直接翻到了那一页，即使在琼斯卧床期间，贝蒂也没有放弃排练自己的角色，因此那页已经有了不同于其他页的弯折。

Bette sets up the camera, checks the film and lighting, then steps in front of the lens.
贝蒂将摄影机架好，检查完胶片和光线后，她走到了镜头前。

Bette: Yes!
贝蒂：是

Bette: No!
贝蒂：不

Bette: Ah—
贝蒂：啊

Bette: For the first time, she isn't just rehearsing. The lines are captured; they're real.
贝蒂：贝蒂终于不再在脑中演练，而是真正将它表演了出来。

Bette: Just as they were written in the script, then Bette collapses back onto the bed.
贝蒂：像剧本写的那样，贝蒂倒在了床上。

Just three simple lines, no real acting required—a minor role in a film that would never be screened.
只是简单的三句，也不需要什么演技，它也只是一个永远也无法上映的
电影中的小角色。

Bette rolls off the bed, turns off the camera, then takes the reel of film and rushes out the door.
贝蒂翻身下了床，将摄影机关闭，然后拿出了那盘胶片，急急奔出门去。

The cemetery is empty. Those who rest here no longer need to care what is about to happen to the world.
墓地空无一人，这里躺着的人并不在意这个世界即将要发生什么。

Bette holds a delicate box with a clumsy ribbon tied in a lopsided bow.
贝蒂抱着一个精美的盒子，上面特意系上了歪歪扭扭的蝴蝶结丝带。

Bette: This is for you, Jones.
贝蒂：送给你，琼斯。

She places the box on the tombstone, and then, after a time, she shakes her head and opens it.
贝蒂将盒子放在了冰凉的墓碑前，直接打开了盒子。

Bette: It's our film. I finished it.
贝蒂：这就是我们的电影，我拍完它了。

Bette: But that isn't all.
贝蒂：但这并不是全部。

She pulls a blank film strip from her pocket, encased in a transparent glass frame. It is bleached in the sunlight, rendering it totally unusable.
贝蒂从裤子口袋中拿出了一条空白的胶片，它被贴在透明玻璃板中，胶片早已过曝不能使用。

Bette: I kept some for myself because I still don't know what I should be filming.
贝蒂：我给自己留了一些，因为，我还没想好我到底应该拍什么。

Bette: If we meet again, I'll bring it to show you.
贝蒂：如果我们还能见面的话……我会再把它拿给你看的。

Bette: But for now ...
贝蒂：但现在……

The cold gravestone offers no response. It lies unspeaking, unknowingly awaiting the impending "Storm."
墓碑没有做出任何反应，它就只是静静地听着，就像世界正平静地接受着即将到来的“暴雨”。

Bette: I finally understand now—the world won't wait for me to say "I'm ready" before it begins.
贝蒂：我已经明白了，世界不会等我喊“准备好了”之后才开幕。

Bette: So I can't stay in the past. I'll move forward, because that's the only choice.
贝蒂：所以我不会停在过去，我会向前，也只能向前。

The "Storm" countdown alarm echoes in the air as Bette gently wipes the tombstone.
伴随着“暴雨倒计时”的警报，贝蒂轻轻地擦了擦墓碑。

Bette: We'll meet again.
贝蒂：我愿意相信，我们还会再见。

She holds her stare at the engraved letters, the carved marks flowing like rippling waves, tracing the life of Jones like a river.
贝蒂望着墓碑上文字的凹痕，雕刻的痕迹似是潺潺的波纹，如河流般贯穿了琼斯的一生。

Bette: And when I see you ...
贝蒂：等你再回来的时候……

Bette shakes the film strip in her hand.
贝蒂晃了晃手中的胶片。

Bette: I promise—it will be spectacular.
贝蒂：我向你保证，它一定会很精彩。

The last film of the 1930s.
30年代最后的一部电影
