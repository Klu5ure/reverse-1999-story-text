01  阳光渗漏 / Flakes of Light

The Long Night Singing Its Dirges
直到长夜下旬

Flakes of Light
阳光渗漏

Monastery
修道院

Within the monastery, a crowd eagerly gathered on both sides of the wide-open courtyard gate.
修道院里，人群热切地簇拥在敞开的院门两侧。

At the forefront there stood a man, shifting in place as if anticipating something.
为首的男人翘首盼望着。

Michael: Uhhem!
迈克尔：咳——

As if catching sight of something, he coughed into his throat and walked forward to greet the new arrival.
像是望见了什么，他突然轻咳了两声，快步迎了上去。

His demeanor shifted quickly to something more flattering.
也迅速换上了一个更加谄媚的表情。

Michael: Lady Dikke, we're sincerely delighted to have you—
迈克尔：帕米埃老爷，我们热切地欢迎您的——

The young magistrate, striding through the door, waved her hand brusquely, as if to cut the welcome short.
大步跨进门的年轻法官迅速摆了摆手，毫不客气地打断了对方。

Dikke: You know why I've come; your pleasantries won't impact my assessment of the work here.
帕米埃：你应该知道我此行的目的，类似的发言并不会影响此地工作的评判结果。

Dikke: So save your proclamations of piety and duty for your flock.
帕米埃：你大可以把这些话吞回肚子。

Dikke: They may be more inclined to play these tedious games with you than I.
帕米埃：想必那些宣读结果的老爷们更擅长与你们玩这些无聊的把戏。

The man managed an awkward, pleading smile.
对方挤出一个尴尬的笑容。

Michael: ...
迈克尔：……

Michael: Thank you for your... suggestion.
迈克尔：感谢您的……建议。

Michael: The necessary records have already been organized; please follow me.
迈克尔：对应的卷宗已经整理好了，请跟我来。

Dikke seemed uninterested in following, her gaze wandering between the passing worshipers and the flickering candles.
帕米埃似乎并不打算跟上去，她的目光游离在往来的信众与烛台之间。

Michael: Lady Dikke...?
迈克尔：帕米埃老爷……？

The proud bishop raised his head, seeming quite satisfied with the lively scene inside the monastery.
主教先生骄傲地昂起头，似乎对修道院的热闹景象相当满意。

Michael: As you can see, there are many who still place their trust in us. They come to pray here each day...
迈克尔：如您所见，每天都有许多人前来祷告，他们十分信任我们……

Dikke: Hmm...
帕米埃：嗯——

Dikke: She chuckled briefly.
帕米埃：她轻笑了两声。

Michael: Do you doubt the sincerity of our faith or our convictions?
迈克尔：您在担心真实性吗？

Michael: I swear to you, My Lady; I would never bring dishonor upon our Lord...
迈克尔：请相信我，大人，我绝不会令主蒙羞……

The bishop raised his hand, deftly gesturing in front of his chest.
主教举起手，熟练地在胸前比划起来。

Dikke: Rest assured; I'm not interested in your oaths either, sir.
帕米埃：好了，我一点儿也不关心你的起誓，先生。

Dikke: Tell me, where might I find a tavern around here?
帕米埃：这里的酒馆在哪里？

Michael: Oh, Ah?
迈克尔：啊？

His stance shifted, he stood his ground and spoke up.
他坚决地站定，声音突然洪亮起来。

Michael: My Lady, surely it would be improper to be seen drinking during work hours!
迈克尔：老爷，在工作时间内，我们不应当醉酒！

Noticing nary a change in her expression, he continued cautiously.
窥见对方的表情并无变化，他又小心翼翼地说了下去。

Michael: Moreover, the only tavern to be found here is a haven for vice... it is home only to drunkards, mud-raked and covered with lice…
迈克尔：而且，这里的酒馆大多数时候是那些潦倒的酒鬼们的天堂，他们满身污垢，袍子上全是泥土和臭虫……

Michael: It would be unseemly of you to be seen there.
迈克尔：与您的身份实在不符。

Dikke: ...
帕米埃：……

At last, the bishop seemed to catch on, flashing a knowing smile.
聪慧的主教大人像是终于领悟了什么，他露出一个你我皆知的笑容。

Michael: Ah, but if you're in the mood for some good wine... I happen to have some...
迈克尔：但若是您希望寻找美酒……我正好有一些……

Dikke: You misunderstood me, Bishop Michael.
帕米埃：你误会了，迈克尔主教。

Dikke: I want only a straightforward answer.
帕米埃：我只不过希望得到一个更真实的答案。

Dikke: You failed to detail this in your records.
帕米埃：你的卷宗上没有写明的。

He smirked, laughing under his breath.
他讪笑了两声。

Michael: So then this a request from the court, or...?
迈克尔：这是裁判所的意思，还是……？

Dikke: It is a personal request.
帕米埃：这是我个人的意愿。

Michael: I see...
迈克尔：我明白了……

He finally eased up. The bishop stood tall, reclaiming his usual air of arrogance.
他终于放下心来。主教站直身子，恢复了往常的高傲。

Michael: No wonder you've become the youngest chief magistrate.
迈克尔：难怪您能成为最年轻的主裁判官。

Michael: You are not worried about upsetting the others?
迈克尔：不过，您不担心他人的不满么？

Michael: I've heard plenty of rumors about you.
迈克尔：我曾听说过许多……关于您的传闻。

The bishop feigned a look of paternal concern. To his dismay, he couldn't detect even a hint of hesitation in the young woman.
主教先生故作忧心地望着对方。遗憾的是，他没能发现哪怕丁点儿退缩的表情。

Dikke: That's something they'll need to consider.
帕米埃：那是他们需要考虑的事。

Dikke: No one is irreplaceable, Bishop Michael... myself included.
帕米埃：没有人是不可取代的，迈克尔主教……当然也包括我。

Dikke: The great plague claimed many lives. But we did little during that catastrophe, didn't we?
帕米埃：大瘟疫害死了许多人。可我们在这场灾难中并无作为，不是吗？

Michael: It wasn't our fault... there was nothing we could do...
迈克尔：这并不是我们的错……我们什么也做不了……

Dikke: Yet doing something is better than nothing.
帕米埃：但总比什么也不做好。

The daylight was blinding, yet she stubbornly raised her head into the sun, holding it above him with authority.
太阳几乎刺得人睁不开眼，她仍固执地抬起头来。

Dikke: I don't intend to vex you, Bishop Michael—
帕米埃：我并不打算为难你，迈克尔主教——

Dikke: Only that I feel the sun must spread its warmth to a few more places.
帕米埃：我只是希望太阳能照的地方再多一点而已。

Michael: ...
迈克尔：……

Dikke: Any more questions, Your Excellency?
帕米埃：你还有什么疑义吗？迈克尔主教。

Michael: No... I'll arrange for someone to guide you.
迈克尔：不……我这就去安排人手为您领路。

Dikke: Very well.
帕米埃：很好。

Michael: May the Lord bless you.
迈克尔：愿主保佑您。

02  赞美诗 / Song of Praises

Song of Praises
赞美诗

Outside, Tavern
酒馆 · 门外

The rowdy sounds from the tavern spilled out into the air.
酒馆里的聒噪声远远地传了出来。

But the ruckus outside the door wasn't just drunken banter.
但同样出现在门外的显然不止酒鬼们的喧哗。

Pfft—
“噗——”

George: Curse you! How much water did you sneak into this drink?!
乔治：见鬼！你到底在里面掺了多少水？！

Tavern Owner: Get lost, you babbling broke fool!
酒馆老板：滚出去，满嘴胡说八道的穷鬼！

The drunkard, licking his lips as he slyly pulled out the cork, was more than pleased.
贼头贼脑的酒鬼意犹未尽地舔了舔偷偷顺出来的瓶塞，咂吧咂吧嘴。

Tavern Owner: Quit causing a scene.
酒馆老板：别吵吵嚷嚷的。

Tavern Owner: I can feel those pitiful coins tumbling in your pocket; though they don't amount to much.
酒馆老板：摸摸你兜里那几颗可怜的硬币子儿，它们只值这么多。

George: Gi... Give me another... I can pay!
乔治：再……再来点，我买得起！

Leaning casually against the dirty wall in her pristine robes, someone chimed in.
一身干净的衣袍毫无顾忌地倚靠在墙边。

Dikke: Two glasses of your best white.
帕米埃：这儿最好的白葡萄酒，两杯。

The innkeeper slyly masked his surprise and briskly swung the tavern door open, inviting the magistrate in.
老板不着痕迹地收起了讶异的情绪，忙不迭地为法官大人推开了酒馆的大门。

Tavern Owner: Need anything else? It's snug, sure, but we've got everything you could want!
酒馆老板：您还需要点什么吗？咱们这虽然只是个小地方，但该有的都有！

Tavern Owner: And, of course, we offer services off the menu!
酒馆老板：当然也包括菜单之外的服务～

His suggestive wink went unnoticed; the speaker had no plans to step inside.
他的挤眉弄眼扑了个空，对方似乎并没有进去的打算。

Dikke: I'm looking for someone.
帕米埃：我要找一个人。

Dikke: Someone who knows their way around here.
帕米埃：一个对这片土地最为了解的人。

A scruffy man stepped forward with swaggering confidence.
旁边的邋遢脑袋赶忙凑了上来。

George: Me name's George, I'm the man you're after!
乔治：大人，我正是您要找的人！

George: Want the juicy details about noble affairs, or are you after some excitement?
乔治：您想知道些什么，贵族老爷们的风流事？还是想找点刺激的地方？

George: Trust me, my rates are fair...
乔治：放心，我的价格相当公道……

He thumped his chest with genuine conviction.
他激动地拍着胸脯，言之凿凿。

Dikke: Here, these are for you.
帕米埃：两杯都是你的。

George: Oh Lord, praise be to your name!
乔治：噢～我的老爷，赞美您！

George: To be trusted by a person of your stature; I vow you have made a wise choice!
乔治：像您这样的大人物，一定不会反悔！

Taking a deep breath of the lingering scent of wine, he turned his head away from the innkeeper.
他对着门后飘出的酒香深吸了一口气，又神气地拧过头去，看也不看面前的老板。

George: Care for it well! And remember, not a drop of water — I'm won't be so easily fooled!
乔治：给我好好地收着！记住，半滴水也不许掺进去——我可没那么好糊弄！

Tavern Owner: Tsk.
酒馆老板：嘁。

The figures moved through the streets and alleys.
一前一后的身影在街巷之间穿梭。

Dikke: Is this the market?
帕米埃：这是你们的市场？

George: Yes, Your Honor.
乔治：是的，尊敬的大人。

George: Merchants from other towns come here to buy and sell their wares. If you're willing to spend a bit, you can find all sorts of treasures.
乔治：其他镇上的商人们都会来我们这买卖特产，只要您愿意花点钱的话，总能淘到不少好宝贝～

George: As for that tall structure there... well, before it was abandoned, it was our tower.
乔治：至于旁边这个更高的地方嘛……还没荒废的时候，算是我们的塔楼。

He lowered his voice and leaned in.
他压低了声音，凑上前来。

George: Since the great plague, no one has taken care of it... Everyone says there are ghouls inside...
乔治：自从大瘟疫之后，这儿已经没人打理了……大家都说里面藏着食尸鬼……

George: My Lady, I advise you to stay away from there.
乔治：大人，您最好离那儿远一点。

Dikke looked around. The few people that passed by avoided eye contact, their faces marked by strange expressions.
帕米埃环视四周，来来往往的人很少，他们大多神色古怪，目光闪躲。

She traced the path of their gaze over to a mound nestled in an alley beneath the tower and a solitary cross peeking out from the shadows.
她顺着旁人的视线看过去，一座隆起的土堆藏在塔楼下的巷子里，只稍微露出了个孤零零的十字架。

Dikke: Hmm...?
帕米埃：嗯……？

Dikke: It seems this is not a market but a graveyard.
帕米埃：这里荒凉得不像个市场——倒像是个坟茔。

Without waiting for a response, Dikke walked towards the alley.
帕米埃没等对方接话，径直朝着巷子走去。

As she entered under the tower's shadow, the mound became fully visible
走进巷子，土堆整个显露了出来。

Withered wildflowers and tangled hay lie around it, the decaying wooden cross sticking out from above. Ants were crawling in lines over it.
边上随意扔着几朵枯萎的野花和打了结的干草，腐坏的木质十字架插在上面，偶尔有蚂蚁从上面爬过。

Dikke: Is this grave one of the market's specialties?
帕米埃：这座坟呢，也是市场的特产之一么？

George: This... it must be new... I haven't seen it here before...
乔治：这，这应该是……新的……之前我没见过这个……

George: Oh... Recently, there have been rumors about these ghouls. Perhaps...
乔治：对了……最近关于食尸鬼的传闻确实越来越多了，大家都这么说，说不定……

Suddenly, an approaching man pushed George aside, glaring harshly at him.
突然靠近的男人狠狠地瞪了站在一旁的乔治一眼，把对方挤到一边。

Malachi: Step back, filth! Do you dare taint the robes of our honorable magistrate!
马拉基：滚开，肮脏的家伙，你怎可玷污执法官大人的衣袍！

The man wheeled around on his heels, bowing in a respectful gesture.
他转过头来，露出一个肃然起敬的表情。

Malachi: Ah! Honorable Magistrate, I am Malachi.
马拉基：啊！尊敬的执法官大人。

Malachi: You've graced us with your presence, at last. Even now, we are patiently awaiting you at the monastery.
马拉基：您终于莅临此地，我们在修道院等您很久了。

Malachi: But, why are you out here all alone?
马拉基：不过，您怎么一个人在外面……？

Dikke: It's a habit of mine.
帕米埃：工作习惯。

He nodded, then feigning realization adopted a fearful expression.
他故作恍然地点了点头，又换上一副心有余悸的表情。

Malachi: This drunkard is a madman. If he said anything foolish to you, I beg you forgive him.
马拉基：这个酒鬼是个疯子，如果他跟您说了什么疯话，还请您见谅。

Malachi: ...
马拉基：……

He gave the other man a stern look, and his nose twitched.
他瞪了对方一眼，鼻头无意识地动了动。

Malachi: Huh? You smell of Languedoc? Where did you steal such good wine?—You've committed a crime, boy!
马拉基：嗯？你身上怎么有朗格多克的气味？你从哪偷的这么好的酒？——你这是犯罪，小子！

George: Pah, I stole nothing!... This was my hard-earned reward.
乔治：呸，我可没偷！……这是我的劳动报酬。

Malachi: We'll deal with this later!—Magistrate!
马拉基：一会再跟你算账！——执法官大人～

He put on a new smile to accompany his changed face.
他迅速地换上一副新的笑脸。

Dikke: I believe you have another matter to discuss.
帕米埃：我想你还有一份账得算算了。

Dikke: Unreported deaths, is that right?
帕米埃：一起未报告的死亡案件，对么？

Malachi: No, no, no, My Lady, you misunderstand.
马拉基：不不不，您误会了。

Malachi: Of course, we were planning to report this to you, but... we needed first to organize the complete records.
马拉基：我们正打算向您报告此事，只是……我们还需要整理完整的卷宗。

The man spoke haltingly.
他支支吾吾地打岔。

Dikke: No matter, I had no intention of combing through your tedious records.
帕米埃：无妨，我也并不打算关注你们的无聊笔录。

Dikke: You may state your case in person.
帕米埃：现在你可以当面陈述了。

Malachi: He ...
马拉基：这……

The sound of an impatient rapping of fingers rose and fell along Lady Dikke's sword, echoing into Malachi's ears.
敲击剑柄的声音忽高忽低地传到马拉基修士的耳中。

Dikke: You have nothing to fear. Please ... continue.
帕米埃：无须多心，你继续吧。

Malachi: ...The deceased was a monk... Brother Chester.
马拉基：……死者叫切塞纳，是一位……修士。

George: Ah! It's him...
乔治：啊——！居然是他……

Malachi shot him another glance.
马拉基又瞪了他一眼。

Dikke: Go on.
帕米埃：说下去。

George: I've heard some rumors; people say he's the one you've hidden away, the one that had turned into a ghoul...
乔治：我……听说过一些消息，大家都说他就是那个被藏起来的食尸鬼……

Malachi: Shut your mouth, or would you like to forfeit your rations?
马拉基：闭上你的嘴，还是说，你不需要之后的食物配额了？

George: ...
乔治：……

The man pulled back his neck.
酒鬼缩了缩脖子。

Malachi: According to our initial investigation, Chester appears to have died due to a drunken misstep...
马拉基：据我们的初步调查，切塞纳应该死于酒后失足……

He nervously scanned the surroundings.
他稍显慌张地环顾了下四周。

Dikke: Father Malachi, is there something you'd rather not discuss openly?
帕米埃：有什么不方便直言的么，马拉基神父？

Malachi: Several residents reported witnessing odd behavior from Brother Chester, that he had a peculiar offensive odor about him... some even claim to have seen him sneaking into the tower...
马拉基：许多居民都声称曾目睹过死者的奇怪行为，比如偷摸地溜进塔楼，身上总是有奇怪的臭味……

Malachi: And others say they saw him carrying a corpse through the town at night. Convinced that he was a ghoul.
马拉基：还有人说，他曾经在夜晚背着尸体进城。

Dikke: And is there any truth to these claims?
帕米埃：所有人都这么想吗？

Malachi's voice dropped.
马拉基的声音突然低了下去。

Malachi: Of course not, My Lady! Ghouls are nothing more than superstition... Yet, heaven only knows... Perhaps he was a wizard.
马拉基：当然不，大人！食尸鬼显然是不存在的——但说不准，他是个巫师……

Dikke: Huh...
帕米埃：呵。

Malachi: ...The elders think so too.
马拉基：……长老们也这么认为。

Dikke: So, then you assume his death came from a spell that backfired?
帕米埃：所以你们认定他死于巫师作法时的反噬？

He leaned in, whispering in a voice only the two could hear.
他凑上前来，用只有两人能听清的声音小声耳语。

Malachi: My Lady, it doesn't really matter what the truth is.
马拉基：大人，真相是什么并不重要。

Malachi: Should it be revealed that the monastery concealed a wizard, it wouldn't sit well with the townspeople.
马拉基：修道院里藏了个巫师，总归不是什么值得张扬的事。

His breath ran thin as he advised the young woman in front of him.
他无奈地提醒着眼前这位并不成熟的大人。

Malachi: Though the Great Plague is over, the people's trust in us has not yet been restored.
马拉基：更何况，哪怕大瘟疫已经过去了，但居民们对我们的信任却一直没能恢复。

Malachi: "Can the monastery really protect us? Can they?"—You must have heard such doubts.
马拉基：“修道院真的能庇护我们么？他们做得到么？”——您应该也听到过许多类似的说法。

Malachi: Whether a wizard or a ghoul, either one would profane the monastery by their presence.
马拉基：不论是巫师还是食尸鬼，对修道院来说，都是不正义的存在。

Malachi: It leaves us in a precarious position, I'm sure I need not say more.
马拉基：该怎么做，想必不需要我再多说了。

He nodded in approval, as if he were satisfied by his own words.
对方理所当然地点了点头，这让他对自己的演讲相当满意。

Dikke: You have your reasons.
帕米埃：充分的理由。

Malachi: Hmm...
马拉基：嗯——

Dikke: But I don't accept them.
帕米埃：我不接受。

Malachi: What...?!
马拉基：嗯……？！

He stared in astonishment at the young magistrate.
他愕然地盯着这位年轻的法官。

Dikke: As of now, I'm unsure where the truth lies. What I do know is that it won't be discovered in empty words or false advice.
帕米埃：我并不清楚真相现在藏在哪儿。我唯一确信的是，它肯定不在你我毫无价值的谈话里。

Malachi: You should know... the affairs of wizards are most elusive, it will be difficult for us to gather substantial evidence.
