01  流亡者 / The Exiles

The Master of Theatrics
戏剧与戏剧

The Exiles
流亡者

Charlie: Hmmm... Mr. Fool, how much further do we have to go?
夏利：那个……弄臣先生，我们还有多久的路程？

The Fool: My dear Troupe Master Charlie, to which path are you referring?
弄臣：夏利团长，您指的是哪里的路程呢？

The Fool: For the road to paradise has long been closed, no sign of it reopening any time soon.
弄臣：我们前往天堂的路途早已关闭，遥遥无期。

The Fool: But if it's the path to hell you're after, that remains wide open, ready for our carriage to speed down and down!
弄臣：若是前往炼狱的路途，则是畅通无阻，可供马车驰骋。

Charlie: Um... N-No, that's not what I meant...
夏利：呃……不，我的意思是……

The Fool: Hah! A jest, my dear, to lighten the mood on this long journey.
弄臣：呵呵……请您别紧张，这只是个用以调节旅途无聊气氛的小小玩笑而已。

The Fool: We've an hour left till we reach our destination.
弄臣：——距离目的地还剩下一小时的路程，夏利团长。

Charlie: One... a whole hour?
夏利：一，一小时？

The Fool: You seem rather surprised.
弄臣：您似乎看上去很惊讶。

Charlie: Well... yes.
夏利：是的……

Charlie: It’s only that we’ve already met quite a few people on our journey. I-I thought perhaps we were closer to town.
夏利：我们这一路上遇到了不少人，我本以为我们会距离城镇更近一点的。

Charlie: Also there’s something else... something off about this letter...
夏利：而且，我总觉得这封信有些怪怪的……

"Dear Troupe Master Charlie: we have heard of your troupe’s reputation. The folks in our city wish to see you perform at our upcoming festival in five day’s time."
“尊敬的夏利团长：久闻剧团大名，本市全体市民希望能有幸邀请剧团于五日后的节庆上演出。”

Charlie: It’s all-together strange, isn’t it? Who invites a theater troupe to perform at a time like this?!
夏利：太奇怪了！怎么会有人这个时候邀请剧团前往演出呢？

The Fool: More surprising is that you received the invitation at all.
弄臣：倒不如说，我反而对如今您只能收到这样一封信函而感到惊讶。

The Fool: Only a few years back, we'd have enough of these letters to fill a wall.
弄臣：在几年前，这类邀请函多得足够堆砌起一面墙壁。

Charlie: But that's... that’s long since past...
夏利：那也是以前的事情了……

Charlie: With all theater prohibited... Sending a letter like this is b-brave...
夏利：现在戏剧已经被全面禁止了……在这个时候发来这种邀请函，简直就是……

The Fool: Brave indeed. Openly defying the ban.
弄臣：简直就是——明目张胆地对抗禁令。

The Fool: But my dear Troupe Master, new laws do not restrain desires, they only make new lawbreakers.
弄臣：夏利团长，禁令能够禁止的只是人们的行为，而非欲望。

The Fool: It is only right that we respond to the people's desires, whether there be a law or not.
弄臣：而在这种时刻，我们更应当回应人们的愿望。

Charlie: But...
夏利：但——

The Fool: Did we not all agree on this decision, the whole troupe, including you?
弄臣：这是剧团所有人一致决定的结果，您也予以了认可。

Charlie: Still, I feel like we shouldn't go... What if it’s a trap?!
夏利：哎，我还是觉得我们不应该去的……这说不定是个陷阱！

Soldier: ...
士兵：……

Soldier: Pardon me, would you be the actors from the People's Troupe?
士兵：请问阁下是民众剧团的演员吗？

Charlie: S-soldiers!
夏利：士，士兵？！

Charlie: It is a trap! These soldiers m-must be here to arrest us!
夏利：咿呀呀！我就说是陷阱了！连逮捕我们的士兵都派过来了！

Charlie: Run away! Run away!
夏利：快，快逃！

The Fool: Wait, Troupe Master, no need to panic. These fine chaps don't seem interested in starting a fight.
弄臣：无需恐慌，团长，对方似乎并没有激起冲突的意愿。

A middle-aged man steps forward from among the soldiers.
一名中年人从士兵的身后走出。

Jeffrey: Allow me to introduce myself. I am Jeffrey, we’re here to welcome you on behalf of Autonoma.
杰弗里：请允许自我介绍一下，我是代表自治市前来迎接各位的杰弗里。

Jeffrey: Mr. Fool and I are acquainted; I believe he can attest to my identity.
杰弗里：我与弄臣先生曾是相识，我想他能够证实我的身份。

Charlie: Huh? S-so you're not here to arrest us?
夏利：诶？原，原来不是来逮捕我们的追兵吗？

Jeffrey: Of course not! Why I’m as big a fan of your troupe as anyone else! Why would we do that?
杰弗里：您说笑了，我本人也是剧团的忠实拥趸，怎么会做出这种事情。

Jeffrey: You must have seen all the people heading this way. They're local farmers and craftsmen on the way to Autonama for the festival.
杰弗里：这一路上的行人的目的地与您的是一致的。他们都是这附近的农民与手工业者，准备前往自治市参与节庆。

Charlie: Oh, yes... of course, like it said in the letter- still having a festival at this time feels a bit...
夏利：我虽然已经在信中知道了这件事情，但在这个时间举办节庆也太过……

Jeffrey: Dangerous?
杰弗里：太过冒险了？

Jeffrey: We're well aware, but this decision was made by the Mayor after weighing all the risks.
杰弗里：您说得对，我们也很清楚这一点，但这是市长先生评估风险后得出的结论。

Jeffrey: The Mayor thinks people need something to celebrate in these troubled times.
杰弗里：市长先生认为如今人心惶惶，正需要一场庆典来慰藉民众。

Jeffrey: To avoid clashing with the Duke's ban, we're calling this a "market fair!" At least, until the show begins.
杰弗里：当然，为了避免与护国公的禁令产生直接冲突，我们会对外宣称这是一场“市场集会”——直到演出开始之前。

Jeffrey: We'd appreciate it if the troupe could likewise be discreet.
杰弗里：关于这一点，还请剧团的各位代为保密。

The Fool: Why even the foolish groundhog knows to keep his head low when a hawk's on the hunt.
弄臣：即便愚蠢如土拨鼠，亦会懂得躲避鹰隼的追击时，不当发出声响。

Jeffrey: ...That's a sharp analogy, my friend.
杰弗里：……真是辛辣的比喻，朋友。

Jeffrey: But either way, I want to thank you for coming and for responding to our plea despite the dangers involved.
杰弗里：但不管怎样，我都要感谢你们的到来。感谢你们冒着生命危险，回应了我们的请求。

Charlie: Y-you're too kind. We're just grateful for the chance to perform...
夏利：您、您太客气了，我们很感谢您给予了我们演出的机会……

Jeffrey: The festival and your show will be held tomorrow. Until then, rest up and stay low.
杰弗里：节庆与演出将在明天举行，还请各位今日暂且休整。

Jeffrey: I've arranged a place to stay and some provisions for you. Follow me.
杰弗里：我为各位准备了休息场所与食物，请跟我来吧。

02  市场集会 / Only a Fete

Only A Fete
市场集会

As night descends the following day, the once tranquil streets burst into life, abuzz with activity.
次日的夜幕如约而至，往日沉寂的街道一转常态，变得人声鼎沸。

Bonfires crackled at the heart of the square, dispersing the chill and blending with the lively chatter, the scent of malt, and spices, to create a warmth that enveloped the whole crowd.
广场中央熊熊燃烧着的火堆驱散了寒冷，与嘈杂的人声、麦芽和香料的香气共同变为温暖的潮湿热气，落在人们的眼帘深处。

Resident: Could I have some bread? How much is it, today?
居民：给我拿一份面包，多少钱？

Vendor: All bread and meat are absolutely free today, courtesy of the City Council.
商贩：今天的面包与肉食由市议会提供，通通免费。

Resident: Free? But... what's going on here?
居民：不要钱？这是怎么一回事儿……

Before he could react, the vendor had already stuffed several loaves of bread into his hands.
不待他反应过来，面前的商贩就将几块面包塞到了他的手上。

Vendor: Here's your bread. And here, take a drink on the house.
商贩：这是面包，拿好了，我再送你一杯饮料。

The vendor bent down to pour an amber-colored ale from a nearby barrel.
随后，商贩弯下腰，从身旁的木桶中倒出了一杯琥珀色的饮料。

Resident: A drink? This almost looks like elderberry malt to me...
居民：饮料？怎么看着像是接骨木麦芽酒，让我尝尝……

As the resident took a sip from the cup. his face lit up.
市民接过杯子，品尝了一口。

Resident: This is elderberry malt!
居民：见鬼！这就是接骨木麦芽酒！

Resident: But, what are you doing? All liquors are banned now, even arcane brews...
居民：你在干什么！所有酒类都已经被禁止了，神秘学酒品也不例外……

Resident: Wait... could it be, is this all to celebrate the festival?!
居民：等等……难不成，你们是在庆祝节日？！

Vendor: Don't worry. It's only a "market fair" today, my friend.
商贩：别害怕，这只是一场“市场集会”，我的朋友。

The vendor gave a shrug that hid their smile.
商贩耸了耸肩。

Resident: A market fair? This looks more like a celebration to me!
居民：市场集会？可这明明是在庆祝节日！

Resident: I was wondering why they started lighting up the square. It seems to me you're celebrating Christmas again!
居民：从刚刚开始，我就在奇怪为什么广场会立着火堆了，这明明是过往节日才有的东西！

Resident: Have you lost your minds?! Did you forget about the Duke's ban on celebrations?
居民：你们疯了吗？！护国公明明已经宣布了庆祝圣诞节是非法行为！

Vendor: Not at all, relax friend. There's no celebration here, only a "market fair."
商贩：所以——这只是一场“市场集会”。

The vendor slyly winked at the nervous citizen.
商贩狡黠地朝着惴惴不安的市民眨了眨眼睛。

Vendor: And this? No alcohol here. Only "fermented elderberry syrup" freshly bought from an arcanist.
商贩：这也不是酒水，只是我们从神秘学家那儿买来的“发酵接骨木糖浆”罢了。

Resident: But it sure tastes like—
居民：可这明明就是——

Vendor: Oy, drop the act, mate. Haven't you been waiting for a "market fair" like the rest of us?
商贩：噢，别假正经了，老兄。你跟我们一样，都期待着这场“市场集会”，不是吗？

Resident: ...
居民：……

The citizen gazed down at the cup in his hand.
市民低头看了一眼手中的酒杯。

Resident: Well, if it's just a "market fair"... Just a 'market fair,' that's all it is...
居民：好吧，这只是一场“市场集会”……只是一场“市场集会”……

He muttered it low, as if convincing himself.
他喃喃自语，仿佛是在说服自己一般。

Raising the cup, he gulped the smooth malted drink down.
他举起酒杯，将杯中之物一饮而尽。

"Gulp, gulp, gulp..."
“咕咚咕咚咕咚——”

Resident: Ah! Haven't had elderberry malt in ages! Packs more punch than my old homemade beer!
居民：啊——好久没有喝过接骨木麦芽酒了！这可比自己偷偷酿的啤酒带劲多了！

Vendor: Remember that's "fermented elderberry syrup," mate. Drink your fill; we've got more than enough for today.
商贩：是“发酵接骨木糖浆”，朋友。喝慢点，今天饮料管够。

Resident: Oh, of course, only "syrup" how silly of me.
居民：噢，请原谅我的愚蠢。

Resident: What a wonderful "market fair" this is—all the better with some lovely "fermented elderberry syrup"!
居民：赞美“市场集会”——当然，还有我们伟大的“发酵接骨木糖浆”！

Charlie: S-So many people...
夏利：好，好多人……

Charlie: I haven't seen a place feel so... alive in ages.
夏利：我已经很久没有见到这么热闹的情景了。

The Fool: Of course, everyone's flocking here! It's in our nature, something we can't resist! Like moths to bright flame!
弄臣：人们当然会接踵而至！这是我们的天性，无法逆转的天性！

Jeffrey: We invited a great many singers and actors... Though, for obvious reasons, only a few could make it as planned.
杰弗里：我们邀请了不少歌手与演员……尽管出于各种原因，能够如约而至的人寥寥无几。

The Fool: It makes our planned performance all the more special, don't you think?
弄臣：正因为如此，这场演出才会显得格外珍贵，不是吗？

Jeffrey: You're right!
杰弗里：您说得对。

Jeffrey: I think the People's Troupe is what everyone's most looking forward to. That's why we've arranged your performance to be our grand finale.
杰弗里：民众剧团是其中最具分量、最受期待的演出者，因此我们将剧团的演出排在了最后一场。

Jeffrey: It should be a most spectacular show.
杰弗里：最为重磅的一场。

A wooden stage covered with tents stood at the square's center, poised to become the highlight of this unspoken festival.
广场的中央是一个由帐篷覆盖的木质舞台，很快，它便会化为这场心照不宣的节庆里万众瞩目的中心。

Charlie: You mean we—we're, the last performance?
夏利：我们？最后一场？

Charlie: If I'm not mistaken, our play would be...
夏利：如果我没有记错的话，我们演出的剧目是……

The Fool: Yes—You'll play an important role in it.
弄臣：是的——您会在其中扮演一个极为重要的角色。

Charlie: No, I absolutely can't do it!
夏利：不行的……我绝对不可能办得到！

Charlie: This... This is far too rash a decision! I'm sure I'll muck it up ...
夏利：这，这个决定太过草率了！我肯定会搞砸的……

The Fool: It was a collective decision, my dear Troupe Master.
弄臣：这是大家共同的决定，夏利团长。

The Fool: And we have full faith in your abilities.
弄臣：而我，也同样对您充满信心。

Old Man: Huh?
老人：嗯？

Old Man: You...you're from the King's Troupe, aren't you? Here for the "market fair" too?
老人：你们……是国王剧团的人？你们也是来参加“市场集会”的？

Charlie: Oh... Are you... talking to us?
夏利：哇啊……您，您是在跟我们说话吗？

Old Man: Of course, young lady. I've seen the King's Troupe perform, and I recognize a fair few of the faces among you.
老人：当然了，小姑娘。我曾经看过国王剧团的演出，我认出了你们其中的几位演员。

Old Man: Well, whether you were for the "King" or the "Minister" once, those titles have long been lost.
老人：唉，甭管是“国王”还是“大臣”了，它都已经不存在了。

Old Man: With all festivals and theater prohibited. Soon, your troupes may disappear altogether.
老人：而现在，居然连节日与戏剧也不被允许，也要连着一同消失。

Old Man: Festivals and theater!
老人：节日和戏剧！

Old Man: I always thought they were an untouchable part of our lives, but now, they've become unspeakable sins.
老人：我原本以为它们是我们生活理所当然的一部分，但如今，它们却成为了不被容忍的罪恶。

Old Man: No matter how I try to reason with myself, I just can't understand why...
老人：我无论怎么想，也想不明白这是为什么……

The Fool: A tyrant's sin is born of unearned glory. To keep his power and fame, he will abandon all conscience.
弄臣：暴君的光荣来自于罪恶，为了独断与声名，故而抛弃良心。

The Fool: Our existence is a mockery of the tyrant.
弄臣：而我们的存在，则是对暴君的嘲弄。

The Fool: And a tyrant cannot endure mockery.
弄臣：不被允许的嘲弄。

Charlie: Mm... Mr. Fool! You can't speak like that here!
夏利：弄，弄臣先生！这种话是不能在这种场合说的！

Old Man: I watched your performances back then when you were known as the King's Troupe.
老人：我曾经观赏过你们的表演，在你们还叫“国王剧团”的日子里。

Old Man: I saw actors dressed in splendid costumes lined up on either side. Then, the protagonist entered, greeted with thunderous applause.
老人：我看见演员们身着华服，分列两旁，主角缓缓入场，迎接他的是雷鸣般的掌声。

Old Man: Goodness, that was an outstanding play... Something I'll never forget!
老人：天啊，那真是一场杰出的戏剧，我这辈子都无法忘怀！

Old Man: The theater, props, costumes, scenery—everything was so magnificent!
老人：剧场、道具、服装、场景——所有的一切，都是如此的华丽且夺目耀眼！

Charlie: Th-thank you for your praise... I'm sorry to say, I wasn't part of the troupe at that time...
夏利：感，感谢您的称赞……但非常抱歉，我当时还没有加入剧团……

Old Man: What a pity! Those were beautiful times... So different from now.
老人：太可惜了，那可真是一段美好的日子，与现在截然不同的美好日子。

Jeffrey: I understand how you must feel. The more darker the night, the more we yearn for the daylight long past.
杰弗里：我能够理解您的想法。现状越是窘迫，我们越是会回忆起往日。

Jeffrey: I fear we might never witness such a scene again.
杰弗里：但很遗憾，我们如今恐怕是难以再见到这幅景象了。

Old Man: Oh... You're right. Bringing it up only adds to the regret.
老人：哎……你说得对，现在说这些，只是徒增遗憾罢了。

Old Man: The king's troupe has disbanded, the old theaters will be demolished, and soon... all these splendid plays will be forgotten. With none left to remember their brilliant performances.
老人：剧团遭到解散，剧院将被拆除……恐怕要不了多久，这些曾经光彩耀人的戏剧也会遭到遗忘，到时候，又有谁会想起那些精彩的戏剧呢？

Charlie: ...
夏利：……

Charlie: T-that's not true.
夏利：……不会的。

Charlie: They will live on. Through generations, in writings and in memories.
夏利：它们不会遭到遗忘，它们将一代代地延续于纸面与记忆之中。

Charlie: They won't vanish... Not as long as people have hope in their hearts. No, they won't disappear!
夏利：它们同样也不会消失，只要人们对它们有所希冀，它们便不会消失。

Charlie: Whether it's dazzling costumes or crude rags, whether in crowded theaters or in the dark of the woods
夏利：无论是夺目的华服抑或者是粗糙的布衣，无论是在剧场还是在荒野——

Charlie: As long as people have hope, they'll hold true to their hearts' desires.
夏利：只要人们予以希冀，它们依旧能够回应人们的希冀。

Charlie: So, we'll deliver a performance just as outstanding as those of the past.
夏利：因此，我们将会拿出与过往同等杰出的表演。

Charlie: No, more! I vow to you that we'll deliver a performance that surpasses all expectations.
夏利：——不，我向您许诺，我们将会拿出足以超越以往的表演。

The Fool: I vouch for this with all the skill and artistry I have left to give.
弄臣：我同样可以向您保证这一点，以我如今还有的一切起誓。

Old Man: ...!
老人：……！

Old Man: I see now...
老人：原来如此……

Old Man: The theater isn't about splendid costumes, but each actor's performance, the heart they bring to their role—A simple truth, long forgotten.
老人：戏剧并非是因为华服道具而生彩的，而是因为演员们的演出——我居然忘记了这种常识。

Old Man: I underestimated your determination... I will look forward to your performance tonight, young lady.
老人：是我小瞧了你们的决心……我非常期待你今晚的演出，小姑娘。

The old man's eyes sparkled as if a star was reborn in his eyes, and he smiled as he left the square.
老人的眼睛里重新有了光彩，他露出了笑容，离开了广场。

The Fool: A splendid monologue, Troupe Master Charlie.
弄臣：相当精彩的对白，夏利团长。

Charlie: ...
夏利：……

Charlie: ...
夏利：…………

Charlie: I-I just... What was I even saying?!
夏利：我刚刚，我刚刚到底在说什么啊！

Charlie: Bwaahhh... I'm so embarrassed...
夏利：呜呜呜……好羞耻啊……

Jeffrey: You shouldn't be. I'm sure of it now your troupe's performance will win over everyone.
杰弗里：不，您说得很对，我相信剧团的演出足以征服所有人。

Jeffrey: As the master of ceremonies, I should apologize, for putting you in the limelight but—
杰弗里：作为主办者，我要为自己的轻视而道歉。

The Fool: It is plain to see everyone has high hopes for us, Troupe Master Charlie.
弄臣：所有人都对我们寄予厚望，夏利团长。

Charlie: Waaahhh... The pressure— it's too much! My stomach is turning...
夏利：呜呜，压力好大……我的肚子要疼起来了……

The Fool: And yet the show must go on, my dear Troupe Master!

骑兵露出了得意的笑容，他深信自己已经看清了对手的软弱。

钟声的尾音的震颤逐渐平息，而未曾平歇的部分，最终化为了另一种声音。

大量的脚步声从四面八方传来，很快地，星星点点的火把出现在了街道的每一条小巷中。

骑兵：市议会？！你们知道我们是谁吗？！

Resident: Leave! Leave now!

愤怒的人群聚集在夏利的身后，而街道两旁的火焰也越发旺盛。

温度逐渐上升，汗水爬上了军士们的额头。

为首的骑兵咬了咬牙，冲进了一旁街道燃烧的火焰之中。

骑兵：烫死我了！该死该死！这怎么会是真实的火焰!

它不足以对厚重的铠甲造成任何实质性的伤害，但它带来的耻辱，却足以贯穿甲胄。

Charlie: Still think this is just a trick?
夏利：你还认为，这些愤怒仅仅只是幻象吗？

钟声——

城市上空的钟声愈发密集，一声接着一声。

兵士首领的心理防线终于崩溃。

仿佛被抽走了身上所有的力气一般，夏利倒退了两步。

伴随着她的动作，她身旁的人群与街道两旁正在燃烧着的房屋也开始摇曳。

最后，景象变为了破碎的泡沫，露出了“街道”的真实容貌。

——象征着节日的火堆依旧在广场上熊熊燃烧，火光将周围变为了白昼。

但消失的人群仅仅只是一小部分，更多的人，依旧站在她的身后。

——是的，这并非幻影，而是真实之物。

沉寂了片刻，人群之中爆发出了巨大的欢呼声。

夏利：不，不要再提了！
