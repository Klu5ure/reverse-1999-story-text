That was a night in 1971, a night with pouring rain.
那是在1971年的一个夜晚，雨水从天上降下来。

In that rain, the communication center of Zeno lost contact with the Green Lake Campsite.
在那一夜的雨中，芝诺的联络中心失去了绿湖营地的通讯信号。

But ... wasn't it a story?
可……可那不是一个故事吗？

I am kind of attracted, indeed.
我确实有些兴趣。

Staying here is really not a wise choice for you.
留下对你们来说，不是一个明智的选择。

It's our right to stay here. You can't just expel us!
我们有留在这里的权利，你们不能赶我们走！

Someone is passing messages to us through these notes.
有人在用这种方法对我们传递信息。

We have been with a "Butcher" whose identity is unknown to all of us ...
我们和一个忽然混进我们之中的、没人知道是谁的“屠夫”……

Like the story of Zeno Youth Force,we are in real danger now.
和芝诺少年军的故事一样，我们遇上真家伙了。

Blonney ... Blonney ...
金蜜儿——金蜜儿——

Stop playing hide-and-seek! This is not funny!
别藏了，快出来，这不好玩！

The critters are multiplying. Things are getting worse.
魔精还在不断地增加，这里的情况越来越难处理了。
