01  【3.5】绿松石蛇俱乐部08.2

The radiance awakens a blinding light that radiates outward from the Sun Stone.
辉光成功唤醒了面前的太阳石，它闪烁着夺目的光芒。

Añil: The teleportation ritual is about to begin.
野木蓝：传送要开始了。

Añil: Be prepared, miss. You may experience some side effects from the process.
野木蓝：请做好准备，女士。传送的过程中可能会伴随着一些神秘术的副作用。

Lorentz Butterfly: Time to see what this auction's all about.
洛伦兹蝴蝶：我已经迫不及待要看到这个拍卖会的真面目了。

Past the witching hour, in the dark of the cemetery, a candle is lit. A pair of still eyes gaze deeply into the little flame.
夜色昏暗。墓地中，彩蜡烛被点燃，一双沉静的眼睛凝视着烛光。

Ramona: The auction should've started a while ago.
拉莫纳：据我所知，拍卖会已经开始很久了。

Ramona: So why isn't anything happening? This guy isn't exactly the type to joke about this kind of stuff.
拉莫纳：但为什么蜡烛还没起效？那个家伙不至于在这种地方开玩笑。

Her patience wears thin; a crackle of frustration ripples in her voice.
她罕见地感到焦躁，眉头皱起。

Loggerhead: The candle's almost burnt out.
空脑袋：蜡烛快要烧光了……

Loggerhead: What's it supposed to do?
空脑袋：这根彩蜡烛，它有什么作用？

Loggerhead cups her hands around the candle's flame, shielding it from the wind so it doesn't burn too fast.
机械女孩小心地伸手护住彩蜡烛的焰火，防止风让它燃得更快。

Ramona: It isn't just a candle; it's an arcane tool. See the carvings on the side? It's an ancient ritual. The Codex Mendoza says it was once used in sacred offerings.
拉莫纳：这根彩蜡烛的雕刻工艺，是一种古老的复刻术式。在《门多萨手抄本》中有记载，这种有特殊功效的神秘学工具，曾被用作神圣的贡品。

Ramona: It has a synchronizing effect. When a specific ritual starts elsewhere, lighting this candle will replicate it here.
拉莫纳：当某处的仪式开启时，只要引燃蜡烛，它就会在这里重现相同的效用。

Ramona: But we've only got one chance.
拉莫纳：我们只有一次机会。

The candle has nearly melted away. The flame falters. Hope seems lost. Until the dying flame gives out, and in its place an arcane array appears as a gale-force wind sweeps down around them.
蜡烛已接近完全融化，烛火将熄，忽明忽暗。
突然，以虚弱的烛火为中心，阵法显现，一阵狂风凭空而起。

Ramona: Brace yourself! It's happening!
拉莫纳：准备好——是传送法阵！

The wind howls, swallowing her body and voice.
她的身体与声音也几乎被风声淹没。

Loggerhead: AHHHHHHH!!!
空脑袋：啊啊啊啊啊——！！！

Loggerhead: The Foundation really needs to start selling their teleport disks in these new eras!
空脑袋：圣洛夫基金会……应该在所有的年代推广传送软盘！！！
