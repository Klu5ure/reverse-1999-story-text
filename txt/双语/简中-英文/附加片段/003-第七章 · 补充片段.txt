01  第七章7-12.2

Thirty-seven: First is the circle, second is the square.
37：第一个来的是圆，第二个来的是正方形——

Vertin: 37? Is that you?
维尔汀：……37？是你在那里吗？

The little girl does not seem to be listening; she's completely absorbed in building her castle.
年幼的孩子并未听见，只是专心致志于堆砌眼前的沙堡。

Thirty-seven: Put the triangle on the triangle, and the rectangle on the rectangle.
37：……把三角贴在三角上，矩形放在矩形上——

Thirty-seven: Perfect! As things should be!
37：这样就完美了！嗯！

77: The "Emanation" ... Disorder and chaos ...
77：“流溢”……紊乱……

77: This can't be. This isn't right!
77：……不对，这不对！

77: How ugly, how inelegant! How could this be the revelation from the patterns of the transcendental Law above? How could such a terrible answer fall on my ears?!
77：这太丑陋了！一点都不优美！这怎么会是至高规律的启示——我怎么会听到这样的答案？！

77: I can't tell the others about this. There must be a mistake! I need proof. I have to go out there.
77：不能把这个结果告诉他们。一定是哪里出错了，我得求证，我得去到外面——

Vertin: Uhm!
维尔汀：……唔！

Arcana: Vertin. My child.
阿尔卡纳：……喔，维尔汀，我的孩子。

Arcana: Are you here to partake in sand play, as well? No matter how oft we rearrange them, the sight of them never grows stale.
阿尔卡纳：你也想来玩堆沙堡的游戏，是吗？将它们组合又打乱，看多久都不会厌倦。

Arcana: Alas.
阿尔卡纳：可惜——

Arcana: I shall destroy it.
阿尔卡纳：现在我要把它毁掉了。

Vertin: ...!!!
维尔汀：……！！！

Thirty-seven: Vertin? Are you awake?
37：……维尔汀，你还没醒来吗？

Consciousness is snapped back to reality.
意识猛然惊醒。

The surroundings have reverted to the rocky walls, still enclosed within the cave.
四周已回到了熟悉的岩壁。

37 stands with her head tilted, looking curious.
37站在眼前，正不解地歪着头。

Thirty-seven: I, I think I had a terrible dream.
37：唔，我刚刚好像做了一个糟糕的梦。

Vertin: What did you dream?
维尔汀：……你梦见了什么，37？

Thirty-seven: Regulus claimed 6's throne and declared this the rock 'n' roll empire of irrational numbers. She took out a jar of coffee beans and stuffed one into my mouth.
37：星锑接管了6的职位，宣布这里以后就是无理数的摇滚王国。她狞笑着拿了一罐咖啡，逼迫我吃下豆子……

37 shakes her head hard, as if trying to rid her mind of the horrific scene.
37摇了摇头，似乎极力想把这荒唐的一幕从脑海里甩走。

Thirty-seven: I'm relieved that I woke up in time. And you as well!
37：幸好我已经醒来了，你也一样！

Thirty-seven: Let's go, Vertin! We need to tell everyone the numbers.
37：走吧，维尔汀，我们得赶快把无限定者分享的数字真理告诉大家——

She chirps, her face lighting up with delight, finally free from the long nightmare.
她雀跃地说着，脸上洋溢着摆脱噩梦的真切喜悦。

A beam of daylight appears before them.
洞穴的前方透出了狭窄的天光。

The lengthy trial has finally come to an end.
这趟漫长无比的试炼之旅终于迎来了应有的终点。

Thirty-seven: Do you hear that? So many people out there!
37：听，维尔汀，外面听起来真热闹！

Thirty-seven: Must be Sophia here to pick me up! I told her she can wait for me outside.
37：一定是苏菲亚来接我了！我告诉她在外面等我的。

Thirty-seven: Sophia!
37：——苏菲亚！

The girl runs happily towards the light.
女孩欢欣地向光亮奔去。

Like a young swallow swooping out of sight.
像一只灵巧的雨燕，在视线中一晃而过。

Vertin: Wait, 37!
维尔汀：等等，37！

Got to catch up.
得追上她。

Got to leave this place at once.
得离开这里。

Must share the findings here with the others.
得把地底的发现告诉其他人。

Finally, the light of day comes into view.
——终于，那道刺眼的天光就在眼前了。

A gust of wind suddenly rushes by, sending pebbles scattering across the ground.
气流毫无征兆地在眼前卷起，洞穴出口的几何石块在眼前飞出。

The all too familiar stench of sulfur fills the air.
一股似曾相识的味道刺激着鼻腔。

It is unmistakably the smoke of war.
——那是属于战争的硝烟味。

Vertin: ...
维尔汀：……

Vertin: What?
维尔汀：什……么……？
