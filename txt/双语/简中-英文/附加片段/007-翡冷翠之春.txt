01  【S01】翡冷翠之春05.2

APPLe: Hmm ...
APPLe：嗯……

APPLe: This is most confusing.
APPLe：这可真叫人困惑。

APPLe: Almost every fresco has one of these humanoid symbols, each adopting a different pose.
APPLe：几乎所有的壁画上都画了至少一个小人，姿势也各不相同……

Sotheby: I once read something similar in a detective story. It could be a secret code used between close friends to encrypt their conversation. Like Typhon and Orthrus!
苏芙比：哦，苏芙比曾在一本推理小说中见过类似的东西，它是好朋友之间发明的悄悄话，就像提丰和欧特鲁斯对彼此说的那样！

Leonardo: Orthrus? You mean this might be an encrypted version of Ancient Greek? The grammar doesn't seem to match.
莱昂纳多：如果你是指古希腊语，我似乎看不出它的语法结构和这些小人有任何相关性？

Leonardo: Hmm?
莱昂纳多：咦……

Leonardo suddenly turns the pages, slamming his finger on his new discovery.
像是发现了什么，莱昂纳多飞速翻完了剩下的内容。

Sotheby: Mister Leonardo! We haven't finished! Do you mind?
苏芙比：莱昂纳多先生，我们还没看完呢，请注意一下团队精神！

Leonardo: Uccello ...
莱昂纳多：乌切洛……

Leonardo: It's Paolo Uccello!
莱昂纳多：是保罗·乌切洛！

Leonardo: The copies cannot recreate every detail of the originals, but still the style makes it self-evident: the perspective is perfect, and the geometric figures are so precise that it almost starts to get dull ...
莱昂纳多：虽说临摹无法完全复原真迹，可这刻板的几何形、冰冷到近乎无情的线条，以及无懈可击的透视法运用——

Leonardo: There's no doubt this is Paolo Uccello's work!
莱昂纳多：这绝对是保罗·乌切洛的手笔！

Ezio: ...
艾吉奥：……

Vertin: What's wrong, Ezio?
维尔汀：艾吉奥先生，怎么了？

Ezio: I know the name. My mother used to be a patron of his.
艾吉奥：保罗·乌切洛曾是我母亲赞助过的一位画家。

All: What?!
众人：？！

Ezio: I was young when we met. All I remember is that he was a shy man who kept his head low as he worked.
艾吉奥：那时我还很小，只记得他是个内向害羞的人，作画时总是低着头。

Ezio: He left before what happened to my family, then we lost touch ...
艾吉奥：后来他离开了，然后我家发生……变故，我带着母亲和妹妹一路逃离佛罗伦萨。

The assassin lets his eyes rest on the flickering candlelight in the corner.
刺客看着角落昏黄的烛火，思绪似乎飘到很远很远的地方。

Ezio: I haven't seen him since.
艾吉奥：我们已经很多年没见了。

Leonardo: Ezio's mother, La Signora Maria Auditore, is a kindhearted patrona. She has sponsored many artists, myself included.
莱昂纳多：艾吉奥的母亲玛利亚·奥迪托雷女士是一个了不起的人，她赞助了包括我在内的许多艺术家。

Leonardo: I never expected Uccello to be involved. Though, I've heard he was a strange man.
莱昂纳多：想不到竟然和乌切洛有关系，我听说他是个怪人。

Leonardo: He became obsessed with his studies on perspective; then eventually he disappeared. Not even his family knew where he went.
莱昂纳多：他晚年一直沉迷研究透视法，最后竟然发了疯，抛下妻女便消失了。

Leonardo: It was declared that he died sometime ago—1475, I believe. But some claim that he is still alive, still painting, scratching out a living somewhere far from civilization.
莱昂纳多：官方记录说他死于1475年，可很多人坚称他还在为一些偏僻的教堂画画，收取极低的酬金。

Ezio: Hold on. Take a look at the lines in the other frescoes. This bird, its lines are rougher than Uccello's. These were drawn by someone else—an imitator.
艾吉奥：也许有人在模仿他的风格。注意看，后面这几幅画的线条明显潦草很多，比如这只鸟的刻画。

Leonardo: You're right ... This is a counterfeit!
莱昂纳多：的确……你说得没错，这是拙劣的仿冒品！

APPLe: This APPLe hadn't expected an assassin to be so cultured, Ezio.
APPLe：想不到艾吉奥先生在艺术领域也颇有造诣，我对贵组织的人才筛选与培养愈发好奇了。

Leonardo pats Ezio on the shoulder, smiling.
莱昂纳多笑眯眯地拍了拍艾吉奥的肩膀。

Leonardo: Don't be fooled by his rough exterior, sir! The Auditores were once one of the great families of Firenze. Not so powerful as the Medicis, but still ...
莱昂纳多：可别小瞧了你眼前的这位刺客。虽说比不上美第奇，但奥迪托雷家也曾是佛罗伦萨有名的贵族大户……

Ezio: Ahem.
艾吉奥：咳咳。

Ezio only responds with a few coughs, indicating the topic should shift.
艾吉奥轻咳了两声，似乎不想再继续这个话题。

Ezio: As Leonardo said, my mother has been a patron of many artists, including Uccello. I've seen his work many times on the walls of our home in Monteriggioni.
艾吉奥：奥迪托雷家在蒙特里久尼收藏了许多艺术品，其中就有乌切洛的真迹。

Leonardo: Hmm? This is ...
莱昂纳多：嗯，结合刚才翻到的那些东西……

Leonardo jumps up, looking particularly serious.
莱昂纳多“噌”地站了起来，神情十分严肃。

Leonardo: Ladies and gentlemen, I believe I may have discovered the location of the hidden chamber.
莱昂纳多：女士先生们，我似乎知道密室在什么地方了！

Sotheby: Goodness me ... You figured that out before I could even finish the manuscript.
苏芙比：呜……苏芙比只是草草过了一遍手抄本，脑子里什么印象都还没有呢！

Leonardo: I'm sorry, my lady. Once I begin, it is hard to stop the mechanisms of my mind.
莱昂纳多：抱歉，我光顾着自己看了。

Leonardo straightens out his hair, looking somewhat bashful.
莱昂纳多有些无奈地揉了揉头发。

Leonardo: Take a look.
莱昂纳多：稍等，先给你们看个东西。

Leonardo: It's a copy of the dome's blueprint! I've never seen anything so perfect in its design ...
莱昂纳多：这是画师临摹的穹顶设计图！这样细致的结构我也是第一次见到……

Leonardo: The design was inspired by the Pantheon, and Brunelleschi invented the double-layer dome on that basis.
莱昂纳多：当年设计师布鲁内莱斯基参考了罗马万神殿的制式，并在它的基础上发明了伟大的双层穹顶。

APPLe: What a genius idea! The two shells serve as a buttress to each other so that the dome could avoid from collapsing even at this size.
APPLe：哦，真是杰出的天才构想！两个穹顶既能覆盖教堂巨大的圆顶空间，又能互相支撑，避免使用传统的木质拱架……

Leonardo: Precisely! But see here the tunnel between the two shells is scarcely wide enough for a man to fit through. That is except for this space below the ribs.
莱昂纳多：没错！两个穹顶的间距非常狭窄，中间的通道仅能容纳一人通过，唯有肋拱正下方的空间会大一些。

Vertin: Which means the secret chamber has to be there.
维尔汀：因此密室只可能位于肋拱下方。

Leonardo: Exactly! But it's no more than an inference for now. We must visit Il Duomo to confirm it for ourselves.
莱昂纳多：没错。为了验证这个猜想，我们可以去大教堂确认一番。

Leonardo: Ezio, you're familiar with the structure, aren't you? You've been beneath its catacombs.
莱昂纳多：艾吉奥，我想你对大教堂的地形应该很熟悉，毕竟你曾在那里找过刺客墓穴。

Ezio: I am. I have.
艾吉奥：曾经是。

Ezio: But the Cathedral will be under careful watch by the Templars. I doubt we can slip in undetected.
艾吉奥：金苹果的传言发酵以后，大教堂被圣殿骑士严防死守，我已经被他们全城通缉了。

Ezio: We need another way in.
艾吉奥：现在很难像过去一样从侧门溜进去。

Leonardo: Don't worry. I have already devised a way.
莱昂纳多：没关系，我有办法！

Leonardo winks and smiles, which suggests a surprise.
莱昂纳多眨了眨眼，露出神秘的微笑。

Leonardo: Have you heard of my work there, the Assumption of Mary?
莱昂纳多：还记得那幅《圣母升天》吗？

Sotheby: Two vanishing points, huh ...? I wonder if a painting can have three, four, or an infinite number of vanishing points!
苏芙比：两个消失点，听上去真有意思。按照您的理论，一幅画里是不是可以有三个、四个……无数个消失点！

Sotheby: Wait, I guess they would get all tangled and messed up that way. What was I thinking?
苏芙比：可这样一来，它们不会互相打架吗？

Leonardo: Multiple vanishing points ... waning sanctuaries ... EUREKA!
莱昂纳多：消失点、消隐的终点……有了！

Leonardo: I can't believe I didn't think of it sooner! If the whole thing is connected to Paolo Uccello, then of course linear perspective would be crucial for solving the riddle!
莱昂纳多：我可真是个木头脑袋！如果整件事都和保罗·乌切洛有关的话，那么透视学当然是个合理的切入点。

He turns around, excitement gleaming in his eyes as he looks at his two bewildered companions.
他转过身，兴奋地看着眼前两位云里雾里的同伴。

Leonardo: Do you still remember the riddle? "Under the waning glow, the waning sanctuaries unite."
莱昂纳多：还记得那句拉丁文吗？
消隐的终点啊！它们在夕光中交融。

Vertin: Could "waning sanctuaries" be his way of saying "vanishing points"?
维尔汀：你认为“消失点”就是“消隐的终点”。

Leonardo: Yes, I believe so! Following this logic, then for them to "unite" must refer to the intersection of the lines extending from the vanishing points, and "waning glow" naturally must refer to the setting sun!
莱昂纳多：没错。按照这个思路的话……“交融”指的是消失点延伸出的线条的交点，“夕光”就是黄昏了。

He raises his brush, pointing toward the window.
画家长臂一挥，举起画笔指向窗外。

Leonardo: In other words, we need to find the intersection of the vanishing points before the sun sets!
莱昂纳多：也就是说，要在黄昏的时候，找到消失点交汇的地方！

Vertin: But the riddle didn't specify which painting, did it?
维尔汀：可它并没有指明是哪幅画的消失点。

Leonardo: It must be a fresco right outside the secret chamber. Rumors say the secret chamber's switch is hidden within a mysterious fresco.
莱昂纳多：我猜是密室外的那一幅。人们都说，密室的开关隐藏在谁也解不开的湿壁画里。

Leonardo: A fresco often consists of multiple sections. If all of them employ linear perspective ...
莱昂纳多：湿壁画通常由好几幅画组成，如果它们拥有不同的透视关系，那么——

Sotheby: Then there will be a lot of vanishing points to follow!
苏芙比：就会有好多的消失点！

The sound of the bells tolling reverberates through the building, mingling with the rising smoke from a nearby censer.
连绵的牛铃声从窗外飘来，与香炉袅袅升腾的白色烟雾交织在一起。

Leonardo: By the bells, it must be five already. We must hurry and inform Ezio of our discovery.
莱昂纳多：已经是下午5点了，我们需要在日落前找到艾吉奥他们。

Leonardo: We won't be able to solve the riddle once night falls. Time is of the essence.
莱昂纳多：错过的话，今天就没办法解开谜题了。

Sotheby: Uh-huh, then it's time we take action!
苏芙比：嗯哼，看来现在是紧急作战时刻！

Sotheby: Mr. Leonardo, please stay here and keep Father Alberto busy while Vertin and I inform Mr. APPLe about ...
苏芙比：那么莱昂纳多先生待在这里继续应付神父，我们要想办法告诉APPLe先生……

The fog from the censer thickens.
烟雾渐渐浓稠。

Sotheby: Hmm? Where have these clouds ... come from? I feel like ... I could use some shut-eye right now ...
苏芙比：咦，好多云朵……飘过来了。苏芙比忽然好困好困……让我睡一会儿……

Leonardo: No, this isn't time to ... sleep ... We must ... find ...
莱昂纳多：不行……不能睡，你们得找到……找到……

Leonardo: Wait, what are we ... trying to find ...?
莱昂纳多：咦，找到什么……

Sotheby: I-It's sleeping gas ... Cover your nose ... hurry ...
苏芙比：有毒，大家快把鼻子……捂上……

The two lean on each other but soon collapse to the floor together.
两人互相搀扶着，又一齐栽倒在地上。

Vertin: Sotheby! Leonardo!
维尔汀：苏芙比小姐！莱昂纳多先生！

Vertin: Not good. This fog is getting thicker by the second ...
维尔汀：糟糕，烟雾太浓了……

She scrambles to get away but soon falls to the floor with the others.
身体沉沉倒在地上。

After an unknown amount of time, soft footsteps echo in the darkness.
不知过了多久，黑暗中传来略显迟疑的脚步声。

Alberto: Leonardo? What's going on here? Have you fallen asleep?
阿尔贝托：莱昂纳多先生，发生什么了？您睡着了吗？

Their silence is a silent affirmation.
熟睡的人们毫无动静，回应他的唯有沉默。

Alberto: Keep on sleeping, poor lambs ... and may you have sweet dreams.
阿尔贝托：可怜的羔羊们，睡吧……祝你们有个好梦。

Alberto: May the Father of Understanding guide us.
阿尔贝托：愿洞察之父指引我们。

03  【S01】翡冷翠之春11.2

The vanishing points, hidden beneath layers of lime plaster and pigments, finally merge in the waning sunlight.
被掩埋在层层石灰浆、朱砂与胭脂红酸下的消失点，此刻终于在夕光中交融。

The only thing left to do is lift APPLe up and wait—
接下来，只需要举起苹果，然后——

For the perfect ray of light.
等一束光。

The light, here it comes.
光终于来了。

Outside, the bells ring out, startling a flock of rock doves.
钟楼的牛铃响起，惊飞一群岩鸽。

As twilight streams down over the fresco, the once drab surface begins to change color ...
暮光倾泻而下，暗淡的墙面开始扭曲、变色……

Uccello: Let's leave something behind for our own sake! Birds with grayish feathers, bells that ring at dusk, or the Arno River running free ...
乌切洛：让我们留下点什么吧！灰羽的鸟、日落敲响的晚钟和亚诺河……

Uccello: Let's do it! With all that we have left!
乌切洛：总之留下点什么吧！用我们仅剩的全部——

Uccello: Our brushes and pigments!
乌切洛：笔和颜料！

Ezio: ... So that is what all of this is.
艾吉奥：……所以这才是故事的结局。

Just a group of artists who cast aside all constraints to finish a masterpiece in secret.
没有透视，也无所谓造型。

No linear perspective, no intricate designs—yet a masterpiece nonetheless.
一群无名画师抛开所有的条条框框，完成了这件了不起的杰作。

They left behind a magnificent spring for Florence.
他们将春天留给了佛罗伦萨。
