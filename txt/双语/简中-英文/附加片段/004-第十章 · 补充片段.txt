01  第十章10-22.3

Crater, Sanctuary
火山神殿·山巅

Moldir: We've reached the target area. The sanctuary is directly below us.
莫莉德尔：这里就是神殿的正上方了，乌尔里希组长！

Ulrich: This will be a race against the clock.
乌尔里希：海平面上升的速度很快，我们的时间不多了。

Before the helicopter can even stabilize, Moldir leaps out, guiding Ulrich from the cabin.
未等直升机停稳，中尉便率先跃下，接应着意识唤醒者走出了机舱。

Ulrich: Now's the time. Give me a hand with the record player while I generate the magnetic field.
乌尔里希：事不宜迟，立刻开始设置唱片机，生成稳定性磁场……

Ulrich: Damn it! The field is totally unstable. I can't accurately calibrate the record player under these conditions.
乌尔里希：该死！这里的地磁情况在不停变动，唱片机无法校准至理想调校状态！

Ulrich: The asymmetrical nuclide R in the air is far denser than anything we've ever had in the lab.
乌尔里希：这与我之前的两千三百次实验的情况都不一样，大气中非对称核素R的浓度超乎想象！

Ulrich: There are too many variables. There's no way to be sure that "Regulus" will function as expected.
乌尔里希：变量太多，在这种情况下，我甚至无法确定“星锑”能否如我们所期望的那般奏效……

The ferrofluid fractures into scattered dots, then coalesces.
磁流体混乱地将自己分散成无数小点，而后又猛烈聚拢。

Ulrich: Calm down, Ulrich. First, get the latest asymmetrical nuclide R reading and make the necessary adjustments. Then we'll deal with the signal interference. If I can find the pattern of the geomagnetic change—
乌尔里希：总之先检测环境中非对称核素R的浓度，对应调整实验数值；接下来改进信号处理算法，设置防护设施；要是能推导地磁场变化规律……

Ulrich: For Flux's sake! The device is frozen. Somebody help me out here.
乌尔里希：**的[拉普拉斯脏话]——这个装置冻坏了，有人能搭把手——

Another quake in ice and earth all but topples Ulrich. Above, Olitiau's screech pierces the air.
自脚下的又一次震动险些将磁流体震倒。与此同时，头顶传来了奥利图欧的啸叫。

Moldir: Be careful, Ulrich.
莫莉德尔：小心，乌尔里希组长。

Moldir steadies Ulrich just in time.
中尉眼疾手快地扶住了研究员。

Moldir: Leave the monitoring device to me. I can fix it. Buddy, keep those creatures off of us.
莫莉德尔：只是被冻坏了的话，这个环境监测装置交由我修理。嚼嚼松果，排除那些干扰实验的魔物！

Buddy Fairchild: Ain't gotta tell me twice. But, Lieutenant, that thing don't look like no Olitiau I ever seen.
嚼嚼松果：中尉，那似乎不是普通的奥利图欧，那中间的是……

A great boney creature spreads its wings and dives toward them, followed by a swarm of Olitiaus, eager to feast.
白骨构成的庞大生物遮蔽天空，领着疯狂的奥利图欧向山巅的众人袭来。

Buddy Fairchild: Heavens to Betsy! Now this'll be a tough nut to crack.
嚼嚼松果：哇！好一块硬骨头！

Moldir: Prepare to engage. Hold them off while Ulrich works on the record. There is no room for error.
莫莉德尔：……各位，做好战斗准备，保护组长完成实验！

02  第十章10-25

Mountainside, Sanctuary
火山神殿·山坡

Guard: *pant* I knew this wouldn't exactly be a Sunday stroll. Urgh!
护卫兵：呼……这和国家公园里那些登山步道可不一样。

The guard pushes another obstructing boulder aside with effort.
护卫兵费劲地推开又一块拦路的巨石。

Guard: But I didn't think we'd literally be carving our own trail through the wilderness.
护卫兵：这可是未经开发的野生登山路线——！

Guard: Boss, at this rate, the world'll be in absolute bedlam by the time we reach the top.
护卫兵：老板，照这个速度，等我们爬到山顶，整个世界说不定都已经变成餐桌上的黏鹰嘴豆粥了……

Ulrich: Hold on.
乌尔里希：稍等。

Ulrich: Let me check the map Lieutenant gave me.
乌尔里希：让我再研究一下莫莉德尔中尉的地图……

Guard: Lieutenant Moldir ... As grateful as I am that she set us free—which I absolutely am—I have to say, this isn't exactly the type of freedom I imagined.
护卫兵：好心的中尉女士——说实话，如果不是她好意相救，我们恐怕也早成为重塑之手的阶下囚了。

There's a rustling sound.
身侧响起难辨出处的窸窣声。

Guard: Who's there?
护卫兵：什么东西？！

The guard fumbles for her gun, only to smash her elbow against the very boulder she just moved.
生物的求生本能让护卫兵跳开了两步，想要抽出枪，手肘却撞在了刚刚被她推开的巨石上。

Guard: Tch! Ouch!
护卫兵：嘶——好痛！

Several critter-like creatures burst forth from the snow.
几只“类魔精生物”从雪地里蹿了出来。

Penguins: Gaaahh!
企鹅：嘎——嘎嘎嘎！嘎——！

Ulrich: Penguins? Here?
乌尔里希：——企鹅？

The penguins charge toward the climbers, their beaks already pecking at the coats.
极地恶兽争先恐后地冲向登山者，粗短的喙已经啄上了他们的衣服。

Guard: Hey! Give that grenade back!
护卫兵：嘿！别叼我的手榴弹！

Guard: Uhh. "No interference," right, boss? I mean, you're not going to make me fight a penguin, are you? Look at their little faces!
护卫兵：天哪老板……我不想对野生动物使用武力，我、我做不到——

???: All members of the Sentinel Unit Antarctic Advance Party, attention!
？？？：近卫团南极特勤先遣分队——立正！

Ulrich: ...?
乌尔里希：……？

The penguins wobble into a perfectly aligned row, like well-trained circus performers. Or perhaps even soldiers.
企鹅们如马戏团里训练有素的演员一般，摇摇晃晃地列成一个横排。

???: Alrighty, let's see what we got here. Rookie guard, check.
？？？：菜鸟护卫兵，确认。

???: Awakened researcher with a fish tank for a head, check.
？？？：鱼缸头意识唤醒者，确认。

Buddy Fairchild: Mr. Ulrich! Allow me to apologize for the shock my jits have given ya.
嚼嚼松果：十分抱歉，乌尔里希组长！我的队员给您带来了一点不便。

Buddy Fairchild: They're jumpier 'n a spring cricket at the moment. Lot of 'em just lost some dear friends o' theirs after gettin' back from Florida—brutal, really.
嚼嚼松果：大伙仍处于创伤后的精神恢复期，他们不久前才遭受了一场空前绝后的劫难，从佛罗里达归乡的企鹅族群几乎遭到了灭顶之灾。

The little rodent standing before Ulrich snaps off a salute.
站在面前身材矮小的啮齿目哺乳动物向磁流体敬礼。

Ulrich: Penguins in Florida?
乌尔里希：……佛罗里达？

Guard: And a squirrel in Antarctica?
护卫兵：……松鼠？

Buddy Fairchild: Not just any squirrel—yer lookin' at a sergeant, missy! Sergeant Buddy Fairchild, reportin' for duty!
嚼嚼松果：军衔是中士，请叫我嚼嚼松果中士，组长！

Buddy Fairchild: We been waitin' here on account o' the lieutenant's order.
嚼嚼松果：我受中尉所托，于此等候您的光临。

Buddy Fairchild: I been tasked with bein' yer Official Antarctic guide, and it's a good thing too, 'cause ya sure ain't gon' find no one else to guide ya 'round these parts.
嚼嚼松果：您可以将我看作是南极这片冰洲上最专业的向导——毕竟除我以外，你在此刻的南极找不到第二名向导了。

Guard: The lieutenant ... You mean Moldir?
护卫兵：中尉……是莫莉德尔中尉！

Buddy Fairchild: Sure as shootin'. Her message came in earlier today—told me yer fixin' to scale that there mountain and gave me the job o' gettin' ya there.
嚼嚼松果：是的，早些时候我收到了她的通讯讯息，派遣我前来帮助二位攀登这座火山。

Penguins: Ga-gah!
企鹅：嘎！嘎嘎！

In unison, the entire penguin squad delivers a textbook Zeno salute.
全体企鹅一起敬了个标准的芝诺军礼。

Guard: Ah, Moldir, I could kiss you!
护卫兵：好心的中尉女士，感谢您……

Ulrich: So, em, Sergeant Fairchild, you and these, uh, troops, are under Moldir's command?
乌尔里希：你……呃，嚼嚼松果中士，你们也是隶属于她的小队吗？

Ulrich struggles to process the surreal scenario.
磁流体明显还无法消化眼前的现实。

Buddy Fairchild: Let's skip the pleasantries, shall we? The lieutenant's orders were clear as the day is long.
嚼嚼松果：嚼嚼松果中士不允许在如此关键的时刻说上再多一句废话！中尉已经跟我沟通过了。

Buddy Fairchild: If ya really wanna help yer friends and save the world, then we better get yer bee-hinds up that mountain quicker 'n a cat on a hot tin roof and get that record playin'.
嚼嚼松果：你们需要尝试帮助闯进遗址里的朋友拯救世界，并实验那个搭载了了不起元素的唱片。

Buddy Fairchild: Now let's get movin' 'fore the Manus get their ritual goin'!
嚼嚼松果：而这一切的关键在于——赶在仪式开始前登顶这座山！

Guard: "Save the world"? I had no idea she thought so highly of us.
护卫兵：“拯救世界”……？原来那位女士对我们有这么高的评价——

Buddy Fairchild clasps his hands behind his back, tapping the ground with his feet.
松鼠中士麻溜地背起手，他的脚板不耐烦地拍打着地面。

Buddy Fairchild: Hmm. Now, let's see here.
嚼嚼松果：嗯……

Circling Ulrich with paws still firmly behind his back, Buddy Fairchild sizes him up with a critical eye.
松鼠中士背着手绕组长一圈，带着审视的目光上下打量了它一番。

Buddy Fairchild: Mountain ain't no Everest, but it's sure as a storm in July to be a tough climb for ya'll big-n-tall'uns.
嚼嚼松果：我们脚下虽然不是南极洲最高的火山，但接下来的路对于你们这些高个子而言，依然有一定攀登难度。

Buddy Fairchild: Well, can't never could. Buckle up, folks! I don't wanna see no bodies tumblin' down the mountain, ya hear?
嚼嚼松果：做好心理准备，伙计们！可别像颗松果一样滚下山了。
