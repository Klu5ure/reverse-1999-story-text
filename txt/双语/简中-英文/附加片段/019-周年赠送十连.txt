01  【3.7】周年赠送十连

One Day
某日

Merel: Timekeeper?
梅蕾尔：司辰？

Hallway, St. Pavlov Foundation
基金会走廊

Vertin: Hm? You are ...?
维尔汀：嗯？你是……？

Merel: I'm Merel. We met before during the Don River campaign. I was also part of the Antarctica operation.
梅蕾尔：梅蕾尔，我们之前在顿河见过。我也参加了之前南极的行动。

Vertin: Well, then it's good to see you again.
维尔汀：你好，梅蕾尔。

Merel: I heard about what happened there. I wish we could have done more to help you.
梅蕾尔：听说之前你也在南极，非常可惜我们没有遇到。

Vertin: It was a difficult time for all of us.
维尔汀：大家当时都遇到了很多麻烦。

Merel: It was, but it didn't make me feel any less useless at the time. But now I've been reassigned to the SPDM. I'll be assisting Matilda.
梅蕾尔：是的，只是我觉得我没有帮上什么忙……噢，我现在被调去了第一防线学校，协助玛蒂尔达。

Vertin: Is there something I can help you with, Merel?
维尔汀：请问有什么需要我帮忙吗？

Merel: I, oh, no. I just ... I prepared some gifts before leaving.
梅蕾尔：我，噢，不，我只是……在临行前准备了一些礼物。

Merel: Anyway, these are for you. Best of luck with your work!
梅蕾尔：总之，这些给你，希望你工作顺利！

Vertin: Thank you, Merel. You as well.
维尔汀：谢谢你，梅蕾尔。你也一样，工作顺利。

Merel: I'll leave you to it, then. Goodbye!
梅蕾尔：那我就先走了，不打扰你了，再见！

Vertin: This is ... But why?
维尔汀：这是……但是怎么？

She tilts her head, puzzled, and walks away.
不明所以歪了歪头，离开了。

Merel: Vertin must think I'm so odd. If only I'd tried a little harder, it wouldn't have been so awkward.
梅蕾尔：维尔汀一定觉得古怪极了，要是我刚刚再努力一些就不会这么尴尬了。

Merel: Thank you, Vertin, for everything you've done for us through the "Storm."
梅蕾尔：但还是谢谢你，维尔汀，谢谢你在这“暴雨”下为我们所做的一切。
