01  【1.9】去往新世界【槲寄生】

The druid is taking a stroll through the war-torn woods.
一名德鲁伊正在战火之后的树林中漫步。

She rests her hand on the scarred trunk of an olive tree, intently listening to its quiet whispers in the wind.
她将手抚上一棵躲过炮火的橄榄树，倾听着树木的声音。

Druvis III: A beautiful morning to you, Vertin.
槲寄生：早安，维尔汀。

Druvis III: As you described, this is a forest worth looking forward to.
槲寄生：确实如你所说，这里有着一片值得期待的森林。

Druvis III: The branches of the trees are arranged in a perfect pattern, showing a sense of harmony and unity. I can see the gentle and caring nature of the guardian of the forest.
槲寄生：枝丫与主干的规律，独木和树林的统一，曾经维护这些树木的人有着一颗细腻的心。

Vertin: That is a high praise.
维尔汀：我想这是相当高的评价。

Druvis III: Indeed. All branches follow the same pattern and grow in the same way.
槲寄生：是的，不论新枝还是旧芽，所有的分支都按照同一种结构向外扩张，再化作新的分支。

Druvis III: The edges of squares give rise to triangles, and the edges of triangles give rise to squares.
槲寄生：方块的边缘生出三角，三角的边缘又生出方块。

Druvis III: I may not be familiar with this trimming style, but I can hear the trees humming with joy.
槲寄生：虽然我不太了解这种修剪手法，但林木们都为此感到高兴。

Vertin: That pattern is called the Pythagoras tree.
维尔汀：这被叫作毕达哥拉斯树。

Druvis III: I have heard of this Pythagoras. He was a mathematician.
槲寄生：我听说过，那是一名数学家的名字。

Druvis III: Little did I know that his theorem could be expressed on trees.
槲寄生：但没想过他的理念就算在树木上也会得到体现。

Druvis III: My respects to the sagacity of the ancients.
槲寄生：向先贤的智慧表示敬意。
