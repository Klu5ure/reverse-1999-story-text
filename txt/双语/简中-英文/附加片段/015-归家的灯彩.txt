Regulus: And ... done! Phew! That was a long take!

X: There are so many of us in the suitcase, after all. It just takes a long time to record everyone's blessing. Now, let's hang it somewhere.
X：毕竟是我们所有人的新春祝词。好了，让我找个合适的位置把它挂起来。

Regulus: Hang it for what? You're not going to make it another "Goldburger" machine that hits its victims from above, are you? Though I enjoy playing pranks, I'll hate you if I have to stay alert even in the suitcase!
星锑：挂起来？你不会又要做那个“咯噔”装置吧？事先说好，虽然本海盗一向支持惊喜，但我是绝对不会让你把它砸到维尔汀脑袋上的！

Sonetto: No ... It's a tradition from the east. Madam Z said people hung lanterns during Dùshuò Festival to express their blessings for their friends and family.
十四行诗：我想……这更像是古老的神秘学传统。Z女士说，在度朔节期间挂起灯笼，远行的人就会平安归家，与亲朋团聚。

Sotheby: Oh, I understand that! My family used to hang ornaments too, like mistletoe and the Quiet Ravenheart!
苏芙比：哦！就像苏芙比家会悬挂槲寄生和“安静的雷文哈特”！

Druvis III: Heh heh ... Yes, there are similar traditions all over the world. The ornaments people hang may differ, but they have the same meaning—to express the anticipation for reunion.
槲寄生：呵……或许有所不同。但其间的心意应当如一，都是迎接归家之人，昭示期待、温暖与欣喜。

Druvis III: I found a good spot. Let's hang it here.
槲寄生：——悬挂在这里吧，很合适。

Sotheby: Perfect! Vertin will notice it as soon as she comes back!
苏芙比：好耶！这样维尔汀一回箱子就可以看见我们的礼物了！

An-an Lee: The suitcase is like a giftbox laa!
泥鯭的士：这就叫“开门见喜”啦～

Sotheby: I can't wait to see her reaction!
苏芙比：希望维尔汀快点回到箱子看看！

Sonetto: I hope Timekeeper likes it.
十四行诗：希望司辰喜欢我们的礼物。

Regulus: Me too ... so she may give us a few more days off!
星锑：然后……她就会帮我们多申请几天假期！
