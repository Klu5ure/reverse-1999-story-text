Somewhere in the Suitcase
箱内一隅

The Left: Dr. X, making the Chirper user-friendly is one of its key design goals.
左伶：X博士，让每个人都能轻松使用是啁啾表的设计理念之一。

X: Of course. I was merely pondering how a Chirper transformed from a hydrogen cell might differ from its peers.
X：当然，只是我在好奇，氢电池变成的啁啾表会有哪些不同。

X: Do its functions perfectly correspond with its physical form? Or does it retain some characteristics of its original composition?
X：它具有和形式完全对应的功能吗？它残存了最初的物质特征吗？

The Right: Empirically, an entity's physical form is a manifestation of its essence.
右伶：作为客观实在，物质的形态是其本质的显现。

X: Therefore, if two entities share the same form, can we claim their essences are one and the same?
X：也就是说，具有相同形态的物质，可以理解为其具有相同的本质？

X: In that case, the line between man-made machines and the Awakened truly becomes blurred.
X：那么人造机器和意识唤醒者的概念真的要混淆了。

The Left: Dr. Vertin introduced us to the concept of the Awakened.
左伶：意识唤醒者，维尔汀博士向我们介绍了一些。

The Left: These Awakened evoke a feeling of ... kindred connection within me. A sensation I've just discovered.
左伶：这些意识唤醒者让我感到……亲切。一种新学习到的感受。

X: Ms. Radio would be overjoyed to hear that. She might even bloom another flower.
X：无线电小姐听到一定很高兴，说不定会再开出一朵花来。

The Right: Radio ... bloom.
右伶：无线电……开花。

X: The creation of life is always a miracle, don't you think? The gap between inorganic matter and life isn't that wide.
X：生命的诞生总是奇迹不是吗？无机物与生命之间的距离并不遥远。

The Left: Inorganic matter ... life.
左伶：无机物……生命。

X: Hey, I just thought of something! What if this Chirper someday gains consciousness too?
X：嘿，我突然想到，或许某一天这块表也会产生意识！

X: All right then, future Ms. or Mr. Chirper, let's keep this recording short so you have more space for your own thoughts.
X：那么好吧，未来的啁啾表女士，或者先生，让我们少录一些，为你多留出更多自我思考的空间。

X: This on-off mechanism is remarkably accessible. We ought to adopt it for our future designs here in Laplace.
X：确实是能够轻松使用的开关机设计，建议在拉普拉斯推广。

The Left: According to previous data, Dr. X usually prefers unnecessarily complicated designs.
左伶：根据既有数据，X博士应该更喜欢复杂无用的设计才对。

X: But they need to be interesting. No matter how complex a Rube Goldberg machine is, it shouldn't contain any unimaginative, redundant components.
X：但要有趣才行，无论多复杂的戈德堡机械都容纳不下空洞苍白的冗余。

The Left: Understood. Data has been updated.
左伶：理解了，数据已更新。
