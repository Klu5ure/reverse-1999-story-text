02  第一章1-1.3（新手引导湖泊场景）

Vertin: This is where Vertin has never been.
维尔汀：这是从未涉足过的地方。

Vertin: Regulus! Regulus!
维尔汀：星锑！星锑——！

Vertin: I can't see her at all ... The fog is getting thicker …
维尔汀：完全看不到她……雾越来越大了……

Vertin: Vertin goes closer and finds half a spinning wheel in the middle of the lake, with strange threads on it.
维尔汀：走近了才发现，湖中央的半架纺车上，缠绕着奇怪的线。

Vertin: ... The line is two-dimensional?
维尔汀：……这条线是平面的？

The water rises.
水漫了上来。

Vertin: More and more water rises.
维尔汀：水，更多的水漫了上来。

Vertin: The ground is covered with rainwater from the "Storm." Incredibly, the water has no weight, or humidity. It just flows slowly and quietly, like time.
维尔汀：地上浸满了暴雨时的水。奇怪的是，它们没有水的重量，也没有水的湿度。只是缓缓地在流动着——就像时间。

Vertin: Time ...
维尔汀：时间……

Vertin: I seem to have seen this ... It's ... I …
维尔汀：我好像见过这个场景……就在……我——

Vertin: The line in her hand, technically the line-like thing in her hand which cannot be felt in any way, has gradually formed a path in her sight.
维尔汀：手中握着的线，不，这个无法带给人任何感知的线状物，在视线中渐现成一条明路。

Vertin: And someone is going to be invited over.
维尔汀：而有人将应邀前来。
