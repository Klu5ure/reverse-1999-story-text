01  新馆长 / The New Curator

馆长助手：各位宾客，上午好！我谨代表拉普拉斯博物馆欢迎各位莅临本次“雪山生态”展！！
Assistant Curator: Good morning, everyone! On behalf of the Laplace Museum, I’d like to welcome you to the "Snowy Mountain Ecology" exhibition!

馆长助手：请各位宾客十分钟后前往主入口的展馆大厅，我们将在那里举行本次展览的开幕式。
Assistant Curator: Make sure to head over to the main entrance hall in ten minutes for our opening ceremony.

馆长助手：届时，新任馆长爱兹拉先生将为展览开幕致辞。
Assistant Curator: As part of the ceremony, our new museum director, Mr. Ezra, will have a speech.

人群开始向着展厅入口处流动。
The crowd shuffled toward the entrance of the exhibition hall.

X：真巧，我从没想到会在这里遇到你们。
X: Funny running into you here.

这是一场预料之外的相遇。
Quite the unexpected meeting.

兔毛手袋：啊，你是……那个字母小子——你叫什么来着?
Medicine Pocket: Oh, you're... that guy with the initial for a name—what was it again?

小梅斯梅尔：这是X。你应该学着认识自己的同事了，兔毛手袋。
Mesmer Jr.: This is X. You ought to get to know your colleagues, Medicine Pocket.

兔毛手袋：这可不是我工作的义务，他是个未知数，未知的，等待被破解的，而不是需要被铭记的——
Medicine Pocket: It's not my job to socialize; X is an unknown, something to be unraveled, not remembered—

X：……
X: ......

X：我想我们应该更专注于目前的展览上，这是我们新任同僚的首次工作成果。
X: Let's just focus on the exhibition. It's our new colleague's first project.

X：不依靠拉普拉斯外勤小队的支援，孤身一人攀登雪山，收集了所有展品，并且为它们做好注释。
X: He climbed a mountain solo, gathered all the exhibit items, and provided his own annotations, all without help from the Laplace Field Team.

X：这并不是一项轻松的工作，他一定费了大力气。
X: That's no easy feat... He must have put in serious effort.

小梅斯梅尔：我劝告过他，他可以多带些人与他一同登山。
Mesmer Jr.: I recommended he bring more people.

小梅斯梅尔：但是……他并没有那么做。
Mesmer Jr.: But... still he went alone.

小梅斯梅尔：除了来自同僚的工具，他向外界寻求的资源支持十分有限。
Mesmer Jr.: Aside from our tools and equipment, he had no other resources.

X：这么说，爱兹拉也向你寻求过帮助——嗯，也是，不然以你的性格，恐怕很难主动出现在这种“神秘学家”聚集的场合。
X: So, Ezra reached out to you—I suppose you wouldn't have shown up at such an "arcanist" gathering otherwise.

兔毛手袋：好吧，看来我们的小蘑菇头还是得到了不少助力的，他并非“孤身一人”。
Medicine Pocket: Well, well, seems our little mushroom got some help; he wasn't "alone" after all.

兔毛手袋：他找你们都是为了什么？
Medicine Pocket: What did he ask you for?

X：他需要一把武器，一把迂回而柔和，带有一定幽默性的武器。
X: He needed a weapon, something subtle, flexible, and with a bit of humor.

X：而我，恰好有这种武器。
X: And I happened to have such a weapon.

兔毛手袋：有话直说。
Medicine Pocket: Get to the point.

X：我给了他一把“枪”。
X: I gave him a "gun" ... of sorts.

兔毛手袋：哈！我由衷地希望那不是一把枪管会拐弯的傻枪！
Medicine Pocket: Ha! I suppose it's some kind of wacky gun with a twisted barrel!

骚乱打断了对谈，年轻的研究员们将目光转向讲台之上。
The conversation was cut off by a sudden commotion, and the young researchers turned to the podium.

馆长助手：你说……爱兹拉馆长他……
Assistant Curator: Did you say... Director Ezra, he ...

刚刚宣布了馆长演讲信息的助手脸色发白，小声地对着对讲机说了些什么。
The assistant who announced the director's speech grew pale and whispered into the intercom.

紧接着，她维持好冷静的面貌，转向所有观众。
Then, maintaining composure, she turned to the audience.

馆长助手：……非常抱歉，请各位参展者稍作等待，爱兹拉馆长的演讲将推迟至三小时后进行。
Assistant Curator: I apologize for the inconvenience; Director Ezra's speech has unfortunately been postponed until three hours from now.

馆长助手：在此期间，展览照旧举行，请各位自由地进行观赏。
Assistant Curator: However, the exhibition opening will continue as planned. Feel free to explore.

这本该是他亮相的最好时机。
This was supposed to be his moment.

小梅斯梅尔：他迟到了？
Mesmer Jr.: He is late?

小梅斯梅尔皱起眉头，馆长助手急匆匆地退下讲台，回到幕后去了。
Mesmer Jr. frowned as the assistant rushed off backstage.

X：这可不太妙。第一印象人际交往的那套。据我观察，他还挺在乎这些的。
X: This isn't good. First impressions matter in social interactions. And from what I know of Ezra, he cares about these things.

兔毛手袋：呵，要我说，这也算因祸得福。
Medicine Pocket: Well, I think it could all turn out to be a blessing in disguise.

兔毛手袋：从没有人会记得那些墨守成规的家伙，一个迟到的馆长总比准时准点的馆长更具有记忆点。
Medicine Pocket: Who remembers a speech delivered on time? A late director will be much more memorable than a punctual one.

X：他不需要耍花招。
X: He doesn't need any gimmicks.

X：他的展品很优秀，足以让所有人深深记住这个新任拉普拉斯博物馆馆长。你们看那边。
X: His exhibits are all excellent, enough to make everyone remember the new director of the Laplace Museum. Look over there!

X指向展柜的另一端，两名身着灰白制服的职员站在玻璃展柜前。
X pointed to the other end of the display case, where two staff members in gray-white uniforms stood in front of the glass showcase.

其中一位趴在玻璃上，鼻尖几乎贴上了玻璃。
One of them was pressed against the glass, nose almost touching it.

拉普拉斯职员Ⅰ：我还从没见过尺寸这样大的金红石。
Laplace Staff I: I've never seen a Rutile nugget this big.

拉普拉斯职员Ⅰ：老瑞恩只会展出些植物园里的常规物种来做科普——而这个，这个才是真家伙！
Laplace Staff I: Mr. Ryan usually exhibits specimens from the botanical garden—but this... this is the real deal!

拉普拉斯职员Ⅱ：这位新馆长确实有些新想法，你见过他吗？
Laplace Staff II: This new director seems to have some fresh ideas. Have you met him?

拉普拉斯职员Ⅰ：没有。他从澳洲分部过来之后就一头扎进了雪山，见过他的人不多。
Laplace Staff I: No, nearly as soon as he came from the Australia branch, he set off to the snowy mountains. Not many people have seen him.

拉普拉斯职员Ⅰ：但实际上，大家都很疑惑他为什么要选择这个主题，他过去的研究方向是澳大利亚本地菌菇，这两者根本毫无关联……
Laplace Staff I: But everyone's curious why he chose this theme. His previous research was on endemic fungi in Australia, this seems completely unrelated ...

拉普拉斯职员Ⅱ：好吧，这下我倒是要开始敬佩他了。
Laplace Staff II: Well, now he's starting to impress me.

拉普拉斯职员Ⅱ：一个非专业研究者，不借助派遣小队的力量，独自在雪山上开采矿物，这几乎是天方夜谭。
Laplace Staff II: An amateur researcher in search of minerals, on a solo mission to a snow-capped mountain—sounds like the beginning of a pulp adventure.

收回目光，少年研究员满意地点头。
Shifting their gaze, the young researcher nodded in satisfaction.

X：瞧。所有人都对他充满了好奇。
X: See? Everyone's enthralled by him!

小梅斯梅尔：……
Mesmer Jr.: ......

X：怎么了，小梅斯梅尔？
X: What's wrong, Mesmer?

小梅斯梅尔：没什么，我只是在想——他真的做到了。
Mesmer Jr.: Oh, it's nothing. I was just thinking—he really did it.

小梅斯梅尔：我以为他不会成功的。
Mesmer Jr.: I never thought he would succeed.

02  旧线圈 / Change of the Coils

爱兹拉：您好，我是来报到的，前两日在电话上预约过。
Ezra: Hello, I'm here to check in. I made a reservation over the phone a couple of days ago.

馆长助手：西奥多先生，对吗？
Assistant Curator: Mr. Theodore, right?

爱兹拉：是的，叫我爱兹拉就好。
Ezra: Call me Ezra.

馆长助手：请向前，走廊尽头右手那间，馆长已经等候多时了。
Assistant Curator: Go straight down the corridor, and you'll find the room at the end on your right. The director has been waiting for quite some time.

拉普拉斯办公室
Office, Laplace

瑞恩馆长：你好，年轻的孩子。
Director Ryan: Hello, young man.

爱兹拉：您好，瑞恩博士。
Ezra: Hello, Dr. Ryan.

瑞恩馆长：我从报纸上看到了你的丰功伟绩，关于“轮回者”，关于乌卢鲁运动会。
Director Ryan: I've read of the impressive things you've accomplished in the papers, from the "Reincarnator" to the Uluru Games.

瑞恩馆长：你完成了一个许多人类都无法完成的壮举，我也相信你可以承担起拉普拉斯博物馆馆长的职位。
Director Ryan: This is something most humans cannot achieve. I believe you're qualified to be the curator of the Laplace Museum.

爱兹拉：多谢您的信任，博士。
Ezra: Thank you for your confidence, doctor.

爱兹拉：但……我认为自己还有许多不足，过去，我只是一个普通的研究员，并没有担任过任何管理类型的职位……
Ezra: But... I'm not sure I'm ready. I'm only a researcher; I don't have any experience in management…

瑞恩馆长：别担心，孩子。
Director Ryan: Don't worry, my boy.

瑞恩从桌后走出，带领着爱兹拉迈步向一侧的书柜。
Dr. Ryan stepped out from behind the desk and led Ezra to a bookshelf.

瑞恩馆长：你瞧，从这里的第一列，到书柜末尾的最后一册，这些都是你可以获取的经验。
Director Ryan: From the first row here to the last volume at the end of the shelf—these will be all the experience you need.

爱兹拉：这些是……管理笔记？
Ezra: ... Notes on management?

瑞恩馆长：没错，拉普拉斯博物馆从第一天成立起直到今日的所有管理笔记都在这里。
Director Ryan: Yes, precisely. Every note taken from the very first day of the Laplace Museum until this very day is kept here.

瑞恩馆长：你有很漫长的时间研读并且熟悉它们，从中获得经验——但不是现在。
Director Ryan: You'll have plenty of time to dive into them, get familiar, and gain that experience—but not right now.

瑞恩馆长：现在，你必须亲手去实践它。
Director Ryan: Now, you must put into practice on your own.

爱兹拉：……我要举办一场，由我负责的展览会，对吗？我从拉普拉斯的对接人员那里听过这个消息。
Ezra: ... So, I'm to take charge of holding an exhibition, right? I caught wind of it through the Laplace liaison.

瑞恩馆长：没错，孩子。
Director Ryan: Exactly that, my boy.

瑞恩馆长：你可以举办一场以澳大利亚菌菇为主题的展览，也可以把你研究许久的“轮回者”故事搬上台面，它们都是值得展出的、充满魅力的故事。
Director Ryan: You might organize an exhibition focused on your Australian fungi or perhaps bring that long-researched story on the "Reincarnator" into the spotlight. Both very worthy of showcasing.

爱兹拉：不，瑞恩博士，不是这些。
Ezra: No, Dr. Ryan, not those.

年轻人缓慢而坚定地摇了摇头，而后端出了自己的想法。
The young man shook his head slowly but firmly. Then set about expressing his own idea.

爱兹拉：我想举办一场关于“阿尔卑斯雪山生态”的展览。
Ezra: I want to put together an exhibition centered around "The Ecology of Snowy Alpine Mountains."

瑞恩馆长：雪山？
Director Ryan: Snow mountain?

一个意料之外的词。
That's somewhat unexpected.

爱兹拉：是的，雪山。
Ezra: Snowy mountains, yes.

爱兹拉：我计划从北面登上阿尔卑斯山脉的马特洪峰，收集目标植株与矿物，将它们带回展馆，进行展览。
Ezra: I intend to climb the Alps—the Matterhorn, to be precise—from the north, collecting plants and minerals to bring back to the museum for display.

爱兹拉拿出一份清单，他有备而来。
Ezra had made a checklist; he came prepared.

爱兹拉：我对比了往年当月阿尔卑斯山脉的气候、行程路线以及目标展品的分布情况，请您过目。
Ezra: I've got here an itinerary, a distribution of exhibit items to be found in the Alps, and a climate comparison for this month with previous years. Take a look.

瑞恩馆长：唔……
Director Ryan: Hmm...

瑞恩馆长：这的确是一份完备的方案，你为可能存在的危险做出了预案，也避开了最难攀登的峭壁，它确实是具有可行性的。但……为什么呢？
Director Ryan: This is a worthwhile plan. You've thought through the potential dangers and avoided the trickiest approaches; it all seems feasible. But... why?

爱兹拉：我不明白您的意思，什么“为什么”？
Ezra: I don't understand. What do you mean by "why?"

瑞恩馆长：你为什么会选择“阿尔卑斯雪山生态”作为展览主题呢？
Director Ryan: Why pick "The Ecology of Snow-Capped Alpine Mountains" as your exhibition's theme?

瑞恩馆长：你有研究数年的澳大利亚菌菇生态主题，也有你了解深入、具有热忱的“轮回者”主题，你为什么在它们之间选择了这个主题呢？
Director Ryan: You've spent years on Australian fungi and a gripping story in the "Reincarnator.". Why would you propose this instead?

爱兹拉：瑞恩馆长，我明白，在学术期刊与书面资料中，我是如何被勾勒出来并进行分类的——一位澳洲出身的菌类研究学者。
Ezra: Director Ryan, I know how I've been portrayed in academic journals and other written sources—the mycologist from Australia.

爱兹拉：我也很乐意为同僚们带来一场菌类学研究的专题分享，将我过去数年间的成果展示出来。
Ezra: I'm happy to share my mycology research with my colleagues to show off the results of my years of work.

爱兹拉：但这次并不一样，这次展览的选题于我而言有别样的意义。
Ezra: But this time is different. My choice of exhibition theme holds a special meaning to me.

爱兹拉：请相信我，“雪山”是我深思熟虑后的选择。
Ezra: Trust me, my decision to go with the "Snowy Mountains" theme was carefully considered.

瑞恩馆长：……
Director Ryan: ......

瑞恩馆长：作为拉普拉斯博物馆的馆长，我并不建议你在如此稚嫩的时刻挑战如此困难的任务。
Director Ryan: As director of the Laplace Museum, I wouldn't recommend taking on such a challenge for your first step.

瑞恩馆长：可是……那已经是过去式了。
Director Ryan: But... my time in that role has already passed.

瑞恩馆长：选题的决定权归馆长所有，也就是你。
Director Ryan: The decision-making power for your exhibit lies in your hands now.

瑞恩馆长：年轻人善于冒险，也乐于冒险，这是一件好事。
Director Ryan: The young take risks, revel in adventures, and we need that boldness.

瑞恩馆长：像我这样的老头子，也是时候选择清闲的生活，好好陪伴我的小孙女了。
Director Ryan: As for an old gentleman like me, risks lie in my past. Ahead, a peaceful life and quality time with my granddaughter.

小梅斯梅尔：……
Mesmer Jr.: ......

小梅斯梅尔站在办公室门前，手掌相互揉搓，深呼吸，再深呼吸。
Mesmer Jr. stood outside the office door, clasping her hands together, taking deep breaths as if trying to calm herself.

小梅斯梅尔：别担心，已经结束了……现在可以休——
Mesmer Jr.: Don't worry, it's all done now... You can finally relax.

小梅斯梅尔：……！
Mesmer Jr.: ......！

这是一位出乎意料的访客。
An unexpected visitor entered.

爱兹拉：抱歉，我是不是……吓到你了？
Ezra: Oh, sorry. Did I startle you?

小梅斯梅尔：你为什么……会在这里？是谁带你来的？
Mesmer Jr.: Why are you here? And who brought you?

爱兹拉：是拉普拉斯的研究员，他们说我可以在这里等你结束手头的工作……
Ezra: Another Laplace researcher. They told me I could wait for you to finish your work here...

小梅斯梅尔：如果你想找我进行人工梦游诊疗，你应该通过正常的登记、备案，然后在规定的时候前往诊疗室……而不是一声不吭地跑来我的办公室。
Mesmer Jr.: If you wanted a consultation on Artificial Somnambulism, you should have gone through the proper registration process and waited for your appointment ... rather than slinking into my office without a word.

年轻的姑娘像是一只冒着消毒水味儿的豪猪那样充满了锐利的尖刺，她站在房间的角落，一步也不向前进。
She stood in the corner, not budging an inch. Around her hung an air of sharpness accented by disinfectant.

爱兹拉很熟悉这种紧绷的氛围，他常常在这种时候充当调和剂。
But Ezra was accustomed to diffusing tense situations.

爱兹拉：我为我的唐突道歉，小梅斯梅尔小姐。
Ezra: Apologies for my abruptness, Miss Mesmer Jr.

爱兹拉：我是爱兹拉，近期刚刚被调派来到拉普拉斯总部，接手了博物馆馆长的工作。我在这里等待你，是为了向你寻求帮助。
Ezra: I'm Ezra, the new director of the Laplace Museum. I've been waiting for the chance to ask for a favor.

小梅斯梅尔：爱……兹拉？我记得我听说过这个名字……
Mesmer Jr.: Ezra...? I think I've heard that name before...

小梅斯梅尔：是……“乌卢鲁运动会”的爱兹拉，我从报道中读到过你的名字。你是一位菌类研究学者，还是……一位纯血人类。
Mesmer Jr.: Yeah... "Ezra of the Uluru Games." I've read about you in the reports. A mycology researcher, and... a pure-blooded human?

爱兹拉：是的，那就是我。
Ezra: You've got me.

年轻的孩子点头应允，而后小心地站起身来。
The girl nodded cautiously where she stood.

爱兹拉：很抱歉我似乎打扰到了你，如果可以的话，我可以为你进行必要的情感安抚与疏导。
Ezra: Sorry, did I disturb you? If you would like, I can provide emotional reassurance and counseling.

爱兹拉：我在澳大利亚分部时常常做这样的事，我有一定的经验，如果你愿意的话，我愿意为你提供帮助。
Ezra: I did this sort of thing back at the Australian branch. I'm here to help, if you're open to it.

小梅斯梅尔：不……我没事，你不用在意我。
Mesmer Jr.: No, no ... I'm fine. Don't worry about me.

年轻姑娘放松了不少，她走向沙发，坐在边角。
Her stance relaxed a bit, and she moved to the sofa before taking a seat in the corner.

小梅斯梅尔：你想要我帮你什么忙？
Mesmer Jr.: So, what's this favor?

小梅斯梅尔：我对博物馆的管理一无所知，对展出也并不热心……我不确定我是否能帮上你的忙。
Mesmer Jr.: I'm afraid I don't know much about managing museums, nor planning exhibitions... I'm not sure how I could be of much use to you.

爱兹拉：不用担心，小梅斯梅尔小姐。我想问的是关于“磁性”的事，更准确地说，是“磁场”。
Ezra: Ah, no ... Miss Mesmer Jr., I'm actually seeking your expertise in "magnetism," or more specifically, the "magnetic field".

爱兹拉摘下背包，掏出寻水杖。
Ezra took off his backpack and pulled out a divining rod.

爱兹拉：你看，这是我的寻水杖。仰赖拉普拉斯的技术，它能感应到附近区域的生物磁场，为我指引生物与菌群的活动痕迹。
Ezra: This is my divining rod. Thanks to Laplace's tech, it can sense the magnetic fields of organisms and fungi nearby and guide me to traces of their activity.

爱兹拉：无论是在雨林、在堡礁，还是在盆地，它都是我最忠实的助力。
Ezra: It's been my trusty sidekick through rainforests, reefs, and valleys.

爱兹拉：但当我的旅途进行到了高原地区时，它的反应精度便开始大幅度下滑，为我的工作带来了困难。
