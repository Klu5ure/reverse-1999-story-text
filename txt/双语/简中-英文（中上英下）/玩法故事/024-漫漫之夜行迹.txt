01  挑战者 / The Challenger

挑战者
The Challenger

210：“天上的声音如是说，有件大事将要发生在你身上。”
210: "A voice came from above and spoke to the man, 'An important thing will happen to you.'"

高谈阔论者尚且稚嫩年轻，他像是一枝新生的葡萄藤，挺拔向上。
The lecturer is young and proud, standing like a newly sprouted grapevine growing upright.

210：“那人便开口问了，那是什么样的事？”
210: "What will it be? The man asked."

210：“天上的声音回答，你将变得富足美满，拥有智慧与声名，迎娶美丽的妻子。”
210: "You will gain wealth, you will gain fame. You will walk in the garden of wisdom and happiness. The voice answered."

210：“于是，他终其一生地等待一个奇迹，直至死亡到来，一切仍未发生。”
210: "Thus, until he died, he spent his life waiting for his fortune, and it never arrived."

他手中持着记载文字的书卷，由广场的这一侧，步行向那一侧。
The lecturer walks from one side of the square to the other, an unfurled scroll in his hands.

210：天上的声音带来许诺，换而言之，那人窥见了自己将要来的命运。
210: The voice from above brought promises—a glimpse of his forthcoming fate.

210：那人便相信了，顺从，由此盲目，从而铸成了自己可悲的结局。
210: He believed in the promise, and, in doing so, forged his own tragic end.

37：……
Thirty-seven: ...

这是平缓的、安宁的呼吸声。
A soft, contented cooing comes from the audience.

37：……呼。
Thirty-seven: ... Mhmm ...

苏菲亚：37……37……
Sophia: 37 ... 37 ...

低声的呼唤难以叫醒沉睡的姑娘。
The whisper fails to awaken the sleeping girl.

踟蹰片刻，苏菲亚伸出手，轻而缓地晃动了两下身旁的37。
After hesitating for a moment, Sophia reaches out and gently shakes 37.

37：……嗯……嗯？怎么了……？
Thirty-seven: ... Hmm ... Huh? What's happening ... ?

小姑娘揉着眼睛，将散乱的头发撩起，有些恍惚地四下查看。
37 rubs her eyes, lifts her disheveled hair, and looks around in a daze.

37：……210的演讲结束了吗？
Thirty-seven: ... Has 210 finished his speech yet?

苏菲亚：还没有，只是你睡得太熟了，有些打鼾，我担心他们听见……
Sophia: Not yet, but you were sleeping so soundly just now that you were snoring a bit. I was worried they might hear you ...

苏菲亚：而且……在别人演讲的时候睡觉，好像也不太好……
Sophia: And ... it doesn't seem polite to sleep during someone else's speech ...

苏菲亚：尤其是210，他一向喜欢说话，也在乎人们是不是真的有在认真地听他说话……
Sophia: Especially for 210, you know how much he enjoys delivering these lectures. He's always so concerned about whether he has the audience's attention ...

37：嗯……可是，难道耽误他人获取知识不是更不好的事吗？
Thirty-seven: Surely though it's worse to hinder others from acquiring knowledge like this?

37：真理不该仰赖雄辩，也不该含混不清。我看不出这种华而不实的修辞里会有什么真正的知识。
Thirty-seven: Truth should neither rely on such rhetoric nor be so opaque in presentation. I fail to see how there could be any real knowledge in all this flamboyance.

37：啊哈，我已经在尽量减少他的罪孽了……至少他谈话的韵律很适合入睡……
Thirty-seven: *Yawn*, so you see, I'm simply lessening his sins ... At least the rhythm of his speech makes for a fine sleeping aid ...

37小小地打了一个哈欠，靠在苏菲亚的身上。
37 yawns softly and leans against Sophia.

可关于命运、道路、自我选择的辉煌一类的高谈阔论并没有绕过她们。
Yet the rhetoric about fate, path, and glory of self-selection continues to pour into their ears.

37：……
Thirty-seven: ...

37睁开眼睛，看见红发之下，苏菲亚认真向前注视的目光。
37 opens her eyes once more to see Sophia gazing forward intently beneath her red hair.

37：你对这个话题很感兴趣吗？
Thirty-seven: Are you truly so rapt by this topic?

苏菲亚：……我也不太清楚。
Sophia: ... I suppose I'm not sure yet.

苏菲亚：但人总会在乎自己的命运，在乎明天会发生什么。
Sophia: People have always cared about their fate, about what will happen tomorrow and the next day.

苏菲亚：我还没有自己的数字，还未了解自己的本质，更不清楚自己的命运……
Sophia: I don't know my own number yet. I don't understand my essence, let alone fate ...

苏菲亚的视线下垂，看着自己放在膝盖上并拢的双手。
Sophia's gaze drops to her clasped hands resting in her lap.

苏菲亚：他说的事情有道理，努力地向前走未尝不是一件好事。
Sophia: His lecture does make sense: working hard and striving forward is obviously a good thing.

苏菲亚：但是，我还未找到属于我的方向。
Sophia: But I feel like I still haven't found my way.

苏菲亚：我刚到达这里不久，我要学习的事情还有许多……
Sophia: I only just arrived here, there's still so much for me to learn ...

年轻姑娘的声音渐渐地、渐渐地弱下去，沉闷下去，最终到达了一处低谷。
Sophia's voice is subdued, fading until finally reaching a whisper.

紧接着，她吸了一口气，像是重新振奋了精神，抬起眼睛望向身旁的朋友。
She takes a deep breath, as if to revitalize her spirit, and lifts her eyes to look at 37.

苏菲亚：你呢？37，你不好奇你的命运……
Sophia: What about you, 37? Aren't you curious at all about your fate?

苏菲亚：啊……
Sophia: Ah ...

37：……呼。
Thirty-seven: ... Mhmm ...

呼吸平稳。
37 breathes out a steady huff.

37：○……不是最大的素数……
Thirty-seven: ○ ... is not the greatest prime number ...

显而易见，年轻孩子需要睡眠，尤其擅长酣然入睡。
Obviously, she needs sleep, and she is especially adept at falling into deep slumbers.

友人的激昂论调对她来说如此乏味，以至于难以维持片刻清醒。
210's impassioned rhetoric is apparently so tedious to her that she can't hold her eyes open even another moment.

210：“那人缠绵病榻时，又对天上的声音询问，那件大事在何时发生？”
210: "When the man lay sick in bed, he cried up to the voice from above, asking, 'When will all that you have promised finally arrive?'"

210：“天上的声音回答他：一切早已发生，只是你未参与其中。”
210: "The voice from above scorned him, saying, 'It has already come and gone, only you never sought it out.'"

210：“于是那人悔恨，悔恨自己的胆怯，悔恨自己错失的命运。”
210: "So the man lamented his cowardice, his idleness, and all the fortunes he had lost."

210：所以我们应该警醒，我们要保持目光的清明，倾听自己的声音，而非怠惰地停滞。
210: That's why we should be vigilant about our own fortunes. We must keep our vision clear, listen to our own voice, and not stagnate waiting for the promises of others.

210：如若恐惧、胆怯、故步自封，即使是命运中所注定的馈赠，也会轻易流逝。
210: If we succumb to cowardice, or complacency, even those gifts promised to us by fate can slip away.

210：而人的变革与新知，来源于勇气、求知的探索欲与源源不断的好奇之心。
210: Human innovation and knowledge come from courage, a thirst for truth, and constant curiosity.

210：正是因此，我们才得以开拓道路，掌握自己的命运——
210: It is for this reason that we must forge our own paths and control our own fates—

高举起手，未来修辞学家的模样已初现端倪。
He raises his hand high, and the future rhetorician begins to emerge.

一片，两片，慷慨陈词之下，少年人面前支持的海贝已堆起一座小丘。
As he makes his eloquent speech, a pile of seashells amasses before him, one by one, representing the audience's support.

他对此满意，却不够满意。
He is satisfied, but not completely.

210：命运——是的，我们既然探讨命运，就不能回避我们之中坐着的人。
210: Fate—yes, and as we're discussing fate, we cannot ignore the fate of those sitting among us.

210：正如我们的赫尔墨斯之星，她出生便降落在命运之上，带来了启明的希望。
210: Our star of Hermes, fell upon fate at birth, bringing us hope for enlightenment.

210：我们可以让她来发表一番言论，因为她的光芒不曾消减，只是现在，睡梦拖住了她，让她无法闪耀。
210: We could let her speak, to show her light hasn't diminished, but it seems sleep has taken hold of her, preventing her from shining.

210调转方向，突兀地指向另一侧。
210 turns around and points abruptly to the other side.

210：但还好，我们之中仍有人足以代表命运。
210: But fortunately, there are still those among us who can represent the inevitability of fate.

他伸出手，掌心向上，指尖向前，做出邀请的姿态。
He extends his hand, palm up, fingers forward, in an inviting gesture.

人群如受惊的游鱼一般散开几米，露出了淹没在其中的少年。
The crowd disperses like startled fish, revealing a young man.

210：来吧，亚齐，我的朋友——将受“启示”之人。
210: Come, Atticus, my friend—he who will receive the "revelation."

210：你注定成为最完满的数字，将获得一切的智慧以及所有当有的应许——我们阿派朗未来的领导者。
210: You are destined to become the perfect number, to receive all the wisdom and all the promises due—our future leader of Apeiron.

210：不要让沉默吞没那些光。请阐述那些有关命运的观点，请与我们分享你的智慧。
210: Don't let silence swallow that light. Please articulate to us your views about fate and share your wisdom.

被赋予颇长头衔的男孩在人群中并不突出。他的目光在邀请的手掌之上滑过，停留在它旁边的一处空气上。
The young man, given a prestigious title, does not stand out in the crowd. His gaze slides over the inviting hand and rests on an empty spot in the air beside it.

亚齐：这并非我的演讲，210。
Atticus: This is your speech, not mine, 210.

210：可没有人比你更了解命运，你每时每刻都浸泡在完美的“6”之中等待着它的到来。
210: But surely no one understands fate better than you, our perfect "6", awaiting the arrival of your fate every moment, every day.

年轻的雄辩者等待着——是的，对方理应有着可以与自己媲美的智慧。
The young orator holds the moment with anticipation—yes, if anyone deserves to show wisdom comparable to his own it would be his present target.

可以预见，这应是一场令人喜悦的、势均力敌的酣畅辩论。
He greedily seeks the arrival of what he imagines to be an evenly-matched debate.

亚齐：……我……
Atticus: ... I ...

周围的目光显然令这位惯于隐没在人群中的少年感到不适，他甚至微微侧身，试图以动作辅助自己表达抗拒。
All eyes move to surround Atticus. Accustomed to being invisible in a crowd, he attempts to turn slightly as if to escape the challenge and the weight of their gaze.

苏菲亚：唔，亚齐看起来不太高兴……210说的“启示”是什么？
Sophia: Hmm, Atticus doesn't seem too happy. What is this "revelation" that 210 is talking about?

37：没什么大不了的，只是一个故弄玄虚的词。
Thirty-seven: Oh nothing important. Just a lot of nonsense.

37：在每一任学派领袖的灵魂数字出现时，他们会得到某个不传的“指示”。这是他们继任的征兆……
Thirty-seven: All leaders are supposed to receive a special "revelation" when their soul numbers are revealed. It's the sign of a 6's succession to leadership.

37：就像是人们以前在德尔斐的神庙里聆听阿波罗的神谕。
Thirty-seven: Like the oracle of Apollo in Delphi.

喧闹让女孩儿睡得并不踏实，也或许是她对那样张扬似孔雀的演讲者有所不满。
The commotion must have disturbed her sleep, or perhaps it is the peacocking of the orator that awakens her.

苏菲亚：这，这听起来很了不起。你是说他们能听到神的声音么……？还是数字之类的……？
Sophia: It all sounds incredible. Are you saying they can hear the voice of the gods ... ? Or is this something about numbers ... ?

初来岛上不久的红发女孩发出惊叹的低呼。
The redhead, still new to the island, seems in awe.

她的同伴对此却不以为意。
However, 37 thinks it's no big deal.

37：可每一任学派领袖的数字都是6，所以这指示没有什么新奇的——我们早就知道结果了。
Thirty-seven: But the leader's number has always been 6, so there's nothing new about this "revelation." We already know the outcome.

37：210用了太多无意义的修辞。何不直接称他为6呢？
Thirty-seven: 210 uses far too many meaningless words. Why not just call him 6?

苏菲亚：你是说亚齐？可6……6不是那位……？
Sophia: You mean, call Atticus 6? But isn't the current 6 "that person" ...?

37：噢，是的，他还没有接受“启示”。
Thirty-seven: Oh, yes, Atticus hasn't received the "revelation" yet.

37：但他早晚都会是6的，因为这是他的本质。
Thirty-seven: But he will be 6 eventually. It's in his destiny.

亚齐：——我没有任何看法。
Atticus: —I don't really know about that.

男孩的声音变得高了些，打断了远处的窃窃私语。
The young man's voice grows louder, interrupting the distant whispers.

苏菲亚：……！
Sophia: ... !

210：怎么会？
210: How can that be?

210：你是数字6，我们都知道。你将成为数字6，拥有一个6应当拥有的一切——无穷的智慧、无上的视界、敏锐的思维与领导者的气魄。
210: You are number 6, we all know. You will become number 6 and possess everything a 6 should have—infinite wisdom, supreme vision, a sharp mind, and the poise of a leader.

210：因为你继承了预言中的血脉，“启示”自你降生时已注定，甚至比天上的声音更加明晰。
210: Since you inherited the bloodline of the prophecy, your "revelation" has been predestined since your birth. It's even more clear than a voice from above.

210：瞧瞧吧——你与故事中的人何其相似。你们都听到了预言，也都已知晓了自身的命运。
210: See how similar you are to the person in the story. You both heard the prophecy, and you both know your own fates.

210：你从中得到了什么，又听到了什么？
210: What did you gain from it? And what did you hear?

这是一场冲锋。
It is a bold challenge.

由骄傲的、羽翼渐丰的雄鸟向这座岛上预备的领导者发起的诘问。
A verbal challenge from the proud peacock to the island's future leader.

210：你并未投掷你的海贝，我想知道其中的原因，也想了解与我不同的想法。
210: You didn't throw your seashell; I want to know why, perhaps you can illuminate some of your ever hidden thoughts.

210：朋友，思想在辩论中交锋，我们能从中相互获得更多，我希望你能够分享——
210: My friend, it is through the clashing of minds in a debate that we can gain a deeper understanding of one another. I pray you will share—

“当啷。”
"Clack."

210：……
210: ...

话头被清脆的声响打断。他低下头，看见高耸的海贝顶端迎来了新的加入者——对方将他的“战书”同这枚海贝一起轻轻抛掷在他的脚尖。
His speech is interrupted by a crisp clattering sound. He looks down to see a new addition to the seashells at his feet. His hoped-for opponent gently tossed his "challenge" aside along with the shell.

——210读不出这枚海贝除“羞辱”以外的其他含义。
210 cannot read anything other than "humiliation" in the shell.

亚齐：你理解错了。对于命运，我没有想法……
Atticus: You misunderstand, 210. I have no thoughts on fate ...

亚齐终于应向对方的目光。
Atticus finally meets his gaze.

亚齐：我对此也无话可说。
Atticus: I don't have an answer to your question.

02  玻璃瓶 / A Bottle

玻璃瓶
A Bottle

那次，我们不欢而散。
That time, we parted on poor terms.

许久之后，我长高了个子，裙边从脚踝上升到小腿，我与亚齐才再次提起了那场被回避了的辩论。
After a long while, I grew taller, the hem of my dress had risen from my ankles to my calves, and Atticus and I brought up that avoided debate again.

苏菲亚：亚齐……亚齐！
Sophia: Atticus, Atticus!

我发现亚齐的时候，他与往常一样，独自在沙滩上游荡。
When I found Atticus, he was, as usual, wandering alone on the beach.

他总在清晨来这里，所以当我需要寻找他的时候，我总会在清晨来这里。
I always found him there early in the morning, so if I needed to speak with him, I would come here at the same time.

亚齐：早上好，苏菲亚。
Atticus: Good morning, Sophia.

他将一只搁浅的鱼儿丢入海中，向我露出一个微笑。
He was bringing a stranded fish back into the sea, as it flashed from his hands he gave me a smile.

苏菲亚：早上好。
Sophia: Good morning.

亚齐是这座岛上与我交流得最顺畅的人。
Of all the people on the Island, Atticus was the one I could communicate with the easiest.

但这并非是其他人的问题——阿派朗有着一套独属于自己的规律，他们因此而平稳运转。外来者想要融入如此严丝合缝的逻辑之间，显然需要一定的时间与努力。
It wasn't anyone else's fault—Apeiron operated on its own set of rules to maintain stability. It was always going to be difficult for an outsider to break into their tight-knit community.

但在那段时间里，我前往沙滩，并不总是为了找他。
But my visits to the beach were not just to see him.

亚齐：你今天仍旧是来装细沙的？
Atticus: Are you here to collect your sand again today?

苏菲亚：是的，细沙子可以很好地打磨镜片，那副眼镜就快要做好了。
Sophia: Yes. The fine sand here is ideal for polishing lenses. My father's eyeglasses are just about ready.

苏菲亚：我希望它能让爸爸的眼睛看清楚些，他的视力一直在下降。
Sophia: His eyesight has been deteriorating more and more. I hope these will help.

苏菲亚：……
Sophia: ...

也许是因为父亲身体的缘故，我比往日更加忧郁、感性。
Maybe it's because of my father's health, I feel more upset and sensitive than usual.

这不是一个倾诉的好时机。
This is not a good time to confide.

苏菲亚：他今天早晨醒来，然后问我，为什么现在还是黑夜？难道白昼没有到来？
Sophia: This morning, he woke up and asked me why it was still dark. Hadn't the day arrived yet?

苏菲亚：我担心，担心这副眼镜赶不上爸爸眼睛坏掉的速度，或者说，它从来就不能帮助他……
Sophia: I worry, worry that the glasses won't keep up with his decline, or perhaps that they were never going to help him ...

亚齐：嗯……
Atticus: Hmm ...

亚齐：但你还是如此做了。
Atticus: And yet, you still made them.

苏菲亚：……？
Sophia: ... ?

我不甚确定，那应当是一句毫无波澜的陈述句。但我却在其中感受到了他的困惑……比任何一句疑问句之中蕴含的疑惑都要丰富。
I'm pretty sure it is a declarative sentence, yet I sense his confusion inside—more confusion than in any question.

但我不总是对的，尤其是在这座岛上。所以这很可能只是一个错觉。
But I'm not always right, especially on this island. So, perhaps I'm imagining things.

苏菲亚：……
Sophia: ...

亚齐：……
Atticus: ...

我止住了话头，这份对于父亲的担心很难从分享中获得减轻。海风从我们身侧吹过，海鸥的鸣叫在此刻分外清晰。
I stop speaking, knowing that sharing my worries over my father will do nothing to lessen them. The sea breeze blows over us. Each seagull's cry is distinct and loud.

苏菲亚：……
Sophia: ...

在学派里，我常因这样异常的安静而感到心口泛酸——那是一种名为焦虑的情绪。
In the school, such unusual quietness often causes an uneasy feeling in my heart—anxiety.

但此刻，我的心跳平静，呼吸平和。这份安宁，令人如此舒适。
But now, despite the silence between us, my breathing is even. This peace of the moment feels comforting.

亚齐：……
Atticus: ...

不知过了多久，日光逐渐移到了头顶。亚齐站起身来。
Time passes in hours as the sunlight reaches overhead near its zenith. At last, Atticus stands up.

苏菲亚：你要走了吗？
Sophia: Are you leaving?

亚齐：是的，已近午时，快到用餐的时间了。
Atticus: Yes, it's nearly lunchtime.

苏菲亚：……呃？好像……还有一段时间？你饿了吗？
Sophia: ... Huh? But it's still early yet? Are you hungry?

亚齐：……不。
Atticus: ... No.

They surge so tumultuously and vastly that entire lifetimes of achievements and thoughts from mortals are like bottles of water compared to the mighty ocean.

命运的巨石已然落下，新的6诞生了。
The boulder of fate has rolled down from its peak, and a new 6 is born.

礁岩仍未改变分毫，它早已在长夜的磨砺中变得足够坚硬。
The reef remains unchanged. It has been hardened by the erosive force during the long night.

停驻的手轻轻落下，将那页揭过。
The paused hand gently falls and turns the page.

12  穷举法 / Method of Exhaustion

亚齐：你今天仍旧是来装细沙的？
Atticus: Are you here to collect your sand again today?

苏菲亚：是的，细沙子可以很好地打磨镜片，那副眼镜就快要做好了。
Sophia: Yes. The fine sand here is ideal for polishing lenses. My father's eyeglasses are just about ready.

苏菲亚是一个努力的姑娘。她无时无刻不准备着改变自己，成为更好的自己。
Sophia is a hard-working girl. She is always ready to change, to become a better version of herself.

她对自己未来的命运一无所知，因此也没有任何惧怕。内核如此坚固，行得踏实，令人艳羡。
She knows nothing of her future fate and, therefore, has no fear. Her core is so firm, her steps so sure. It's enviable.

这也许是我对她提出那个问题的原因之一。
Perhaps that's one of the reasons I asked her that question.

亚齐：……你为什么会选择在命运的道路上如此挣扎？
Atticus: ... Why do you choose to struggle so much against the path of fate?

我并没有奢求过她的回答，这只是出于偶发的好奇。
I didn’t really expect an answer from her; it was just spontaneous curiosity.

我们是完全不同的人。即使她给出了自己的理由，那也不会对我有所启示。
We are entirely different people. Even if she gave her reasons, they wouldn't help me, not really.

因为我已经穷尽了每一种可能，求索了每一条道理。
Because I have exhausted every possibility and sought every line of reasoning.

这个问题是无解的。
My question is unsolvable.

苏菲亚沉默着，她想说些什么，但又有所犹豫，手指拧着卷发的末端，眼神闪避。
Sophia remains silent, wanting to say something but hesitating. She twists the ends of her hair, avoiding my eyes.

苏菲亚：……我总得做点什么，不论什么都好。
Sophia: ... I have to do something, anything.

这是一个相当奇怪的回答。
That’s a peculiar answer.

它没有任何理由，任何道理，仅仅是要“做点什么”。
Without any reason, any logic, just "doing something."

于是我再度疑惑了。
So, I am left puzzled again.

苏菲亚：……面对即将要发生的事，做些准备，总比不做准备要来得更好。
Sophia: ... But preparing for what must come is better than doing nothing at all.

苏菲亚：对了，水瓶——就像是桌子上的水瓶……
Sophia: Right, the glass bottle—like the one on the table ...

她手足无措地试图举例，她虚构出一只必然破碎的水瓶，而后在其周围铺满软垫。
She flusteredly tries to give an example, inventing a glass bottle that is bound to break, then surrounding it with a cloth.

这样，即使水瓶仍旧破碎，我们也能更方便地捡拾它的碎片。
This way, even if the bottle still breaks, we can pick up the pieces more easily.

这是个实用的好解释——务实勤勉，没有任何抽象。这是属于她命运的解答，与我截然相反。
It's a practical explanation—pragmatic and devoid of abstraction. This is her answer to fate, the total opposite of mine.

亚齐：……谢谢你对我说这么多，苏菲亚。
Atticus: Thank you for the long talk, Sophia.

一条新的道路从海水之间被开辟出来。
A new path is carved out between the waters.
