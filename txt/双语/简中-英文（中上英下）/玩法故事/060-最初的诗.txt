01  开场 / [English text unavailable]

昌迪加尔的街头，人群聚集于一个小摊旁。
The crowds in Chandigarh are gathered around a small roadside stall.

坎吉拉：客人，哪一张是黑桃王后？
Kanjira: My dear guest, which of these cards is the queen of spades?

人群中央，坎吉拉与阿贾尔之间的纸箱上摆着三张纸牌，背面向上。
Three playing cards are laid out face down on a cardboard box between Kanjira and Ajar.

阿贾尔：就是……中间这张！！
Ajar: It's ... the one in the middle!

坎吉拉：哎，又被你逮住了！确实是黑桃王后。
Kanjira: Argh, you've got me again! This is indeed the queen of spades.

阿贾尔：哈哈，我可一直盯着呢！
Ajar: Haha, I've been watching like a hawk!

坎吉拉：30卢比，你应得的。
Kanjira: Here are the 30 rupees, well-deserved it is, too!

阿贾尔：再来一局？
Ajar: How about another round?

坎吉拉：你已经赢我三把啦，就饶了我吧。
Kanjira: Have mercy. You've already bested me three times.

坎吉拉：我可不跟你赌了。不是所有人都像你这样……这样机敏。
Kanjira: I will not gamble with you again. Not everyone is so clever like you.

二人装作互不相识，等着鱼儿上钩。
The two pretend to be mere strangers, waiting for a victim.

阿贾尔：好吧。也许你能从别人身上把钱赚回来？
Ajar: Alright then. Maybe you'll make your money back from someone else?

坎吉拉：不靠运气，只靠双眼。哪位客人有兴趣来玩玩纸牌？
Kanjira: Trust not luck, but your eyes. Anyone interested play cards with me?

报童：算我一个？
Newsboy: Count me in, then?

坎吉拉：当然欢迎，请上前来，客人。
Kanjira: Welcome, welcome. Please come forward, my dear guest.

坎吉拉：规则很简单。三张牌，一张黑桃王后，两张方块。
Kanjira: The rules are simple. Three cards, one is the queen of spades, and the other two are diamonds.

坎吉拉：盯牢王后，别被这两张士兵绕晕了。
Kanjira: Keep your eyes glued to the queen. Don't be distracted by the two "little guards."

报童：我早就看明白了，赶紧开始吧。
Newsboy: I've got it all. Just begin.

坎吉拉：你真自信！烛星夜庆快要开始了，不如……我们来赌一次大的？
Kanjira: I like your confidence! The celebration for Dīpa Festival's Eve coming. What do you say ... we make a larger bet?

报童：多少？
Newsboy: How large?

坎吉拉：100卢比一局。
Kanjira: 100 rupees a round.

报童：赌就赌，快挪牌吧。
Newsboy: You're on. Hurry up and shuffle the cards.

坎吉拉：迫不及待要大赚一笔了？看仔细咯。
Kanjira: Can't wait to win big? Then, keep your eyes peeled.

坎吉拉：跟紧王后，财富相随！！
Kanjira: Watch the queen, and fortune shall follow!

喷吉：嘶——
Punji: *Hiss*

坎吉拉灵活地交换着三张纸牌的位置，双手飞快，像是带着残影。
Kanjira's nimble fingers move the three cards so fast that they almost leave images of themselves in their wake.

坎吉拉：好啦，哪一张是黑桃王后呢？
Kanjira: Well then, which card is the queen of spades?

报童：……
Newsboy: …

坎吉拉：你好像一直盯着左边的两张牌。
Kanjira: Looks like you always stares two cards on the left.

坎吉拉：中间这张，还是左边这张？
Kanjira: Is it the one in the middle, or the one on the left?

话语的诱导远比手法更关键，无论在街头还是王宫。
Beguiling the audience with words is much more important than sleight of hand, whether it be on the streets or in the palace.

报童：都不是。
Newsboy: Neither.

坎吉拉：呃，怎么会呢？左边这两张才是我交换最频繁的。
Kanjira: Eh, how can that be? These two cards on the left, I moved them most often.

报童：这两张只是士兵，整日巡逻，混淆视听。
Newsboy: They are just guards. They patrol here and there to confuse me.

报童：而右边这张才是——帷幕之后的王后。
Newsboy: This card on the right is the big one—the queen behind the scenes.

坎吉拉双手将右边这张牌盖住，做出即将翻开的动作。
Kanjira covers the card with her hands, as if ready to flip it over.

坎吉拉：你这么确定？给你一次反悔的机会。确定了吗？
Kanjira: Are you so sure? This is your last chance. Are you sure?

报童：确定，开牌吧。
Newsboy: Yes, flip it over.

坎吉拉：呃，咳咳——好吧，你赢了。
Kanjira: Ahem! Er, alright then, you win.

坎吉拉并未将牌面掀开，因为败局已然注定。
Kanjira doesn't flip the card over, as the loss is in the cards.

坎吉拉：愿赌服输……喏，100卢比。
Kanjira: You won fair and square. Here, 100 rupees.

坎吉拉：哎呀，真是倒大霉啦！今天就到这里吧～
Kanjira: Ah, what a bad luck day! That's it for the day, folks.

坎吉拉：散伙啦——各位。我可不想今天就破产……
Kanjira: The show's over, everyone. I don't planning to go broke today.

人群嬉笑着散开，只留下阿贾尔和坎吉拉在原地。
The crowd laughs and disperses, leaving Ajar and Kanjira behind.

阿贾尔：嘿，嘿！坎吉拉大姐……这和我们排练得不一样啊！
Ajar: Hey, hey! Boss, that wasn't what we rehearsed!

阿贾尔：你明明已经用“三公术”的手法把王后给换掉了呀？
Ajar: Didn't you already use the Three-Card Monte to swap the queen out?

坎吉拉：哼哼，小子，你的耐心还需要多加训练……
Kanjira: Hmph, kid. Your patience could use a lot of improvement.

喷吉：嘶——
Punji: *Hiss*

坎吉拉：喷吉，干得不错！！
Kanjira: Great job, Punji!

坎吉拉：那小子才没找到真正的王后呢。
Kanjira: That kid didn't find the queen.

阿贾尔：那最右边这张牌是？
Ajar: Then what card was it on the far right?

坎吉拉：士兵整日巡逻，混淆视听，王宫之中，王后早就带着国王跑了。
Kanjira: The guards walk walk to confuse him. The queen has long fled the palace with her king.

阿贾尔翻开了最右边的纸牌，一张方块7，底下竟叠放着另一张牌，一张褐色卡背的纸牌。
Ajar flips over the card on the far right to reveal seven of diamonds. Beneath it is another card with a brown back.

坎吉拉：咦？带人像的小卡片，就像是插画书附带的限量卡片。
Kanjira: Hm? A small card with some pattern? This looks like a limit edition card, from a picture book.

坎吉拉：U、T、T、U……这是个什么单词？夏尔贾没教过这个……
Kanjira: U-T-T-U ... Is this a real word? Ms. Sherjah did never teach me this.

阿贾尔：这东西能值100卢比？
Ajar: Is this really worth 100 rupees?

坎吉拉：哼……你呀，目光短浅！那只是饵，我们得“放长线，钓大鱼”。
Kanjira: Hmph! You is so short-sighted! This is just bait to reel a big catch in.

坎吉拉：走，跟上去！我倒要看看这个“UTTU”在搞些什么名堂……
Kanjira: Come on, keep up! I want to see what this "UTTU" is up to.

02  《至林中去》 / To the Forest

坎吉拉与阿贾尔躲在街道的拐角处，远处的报童身旁站着几位熟悉的人影。
Kanjira and Ajar hide themselves behind the corner of the street, observing a few familiar figures standing by the newsboy.

阿贾尔：伽菈波那、玛蒂尔达，还有鬃毛大叔！！他们怎么在——
Ajar: Kaalaa Baunaa, Matilda, Shamane! What are they ...

阿贾尔：唔，呜！！
Ajar: Ugh, mmph!

坎吉拉：嘘！！小点声！！他们要走过来了，别被发现。
Kanjira: Shh! Keep it down!! They're walking our way. Don't let them discover us.

坎吉拉转身捂住了阿贾尔的嘴。
Kanjira turns to silence Ajar by putting a hand over his mouth.

坎吉拉：嗯？人去哪儿了？
Kanjira: Hm? Where did they go?

鬃毛沙砾：嘿！你们两个小家伙，在这儿鬼鬼祟祟的，做什么呢？
Shamane: Hey! Kids, what are you doing sneaking about here?

阿贾尔：哇！吓我一跳……
Ajar: Whoa! You scared the hell out of me!

坎吉拉：我们……呃……
Kanjira: We ... Uh ...

女孩转了转眼珠，昂起头来。
The girl thinks for a split second before looking up.

坎吉拉：明，明明说好一块来政府广场逛……你们却把我们支开了！
Kanjira: W-we agreed to walk Government Square together, but you sent us away!

阿贾尔：嗯？不是我们偷偷溜……
Ajar: Hm? Didn't we tail th- ...

坎吉拉：咳咳——！！
Kanjira: Ahem!

坎吉拉一通挤眉弄眼，阿贾尔不明所以，迷迷糊糊地闭上了嘴。
Ajar clams up and looks rather bemused, having not understood any of Kanjira's pointed looks.

坎吉拉：唔，你们都是来参加这个什么乱七八糟的……集会的？
Kanjira: Uh, are you also here to join this topsy-turvy gathering?

玛蒂尔达：这才不是什么乱七八糟的集会呢！UTTU闪烁集会可是神秘学界有名的盛会～
Matilda: This isn't a "topsy-turvy" gathering! UTTU Flash Gathering's a famous event amongst arcanists!

坎吉拉：闪烁集会？听着像——
Kanjira: Flash Gathering? Sounds like ...

阿贾尔：听着像是很值钱的玩意儿！
Ajar: Sounds like something that'd fetch a lot of money!

玛蒂尔达：是读书会！不过……倒也没错。知识可是最值钱的东西啦！
Matilda: It's a reading event! But you're not wrong. Knowledge is the most valuable thing of all!

鬃毛沙砾：这次是——《罗摩衍那》，唔，该怎么说，非常——经典。
Shamane: The theme of this one is the Ramayana. Uh, how should I put it? An absolute classic.

坎吉拉：罗摩？噢，他长什么样？我也许见过他。
Kanjira: Rama? Oh, what is he look like? I may saw him before.

鬃毛沙砾：哦？哈哈哈哈……你这人脉真是让我刮目相看了。
Shamane: Oh? Hahahaha. What a lot of people you must know.

玛蒂尔达：……罗摩是神话里的人物！你究竟去哪里才能认识他啊？
Matilda: Rama is a great character worshipped in the legend! How could you possibly know him?

坎吉拉：呃……哼，我是说我肯定在书上见过他，如果……如果，他真有你说的这般有名。
Kanjira: Uh ... Hmph, I mean, I must saw picture of him in books, if ... if ... he's as famous as you say.

伽菈波那：坎吉拉说得倒不错，这片土地上的每个人都曾见过罗摩……在书本上，在寺庙里，在梦中。
Kaalaa Baunaa: Kanjira's right. Everyone in this land has seen Rama, in books, in temples, and in their dreams.

伽菈波那：现在，来自其他土地的人们来到这场读书会，共同聆听《罗摩衍那》，聆听罗摩的故事。
Kaalaa Baunaa: Now, people from other lands are here for the event to listen to the Ramayana, to hear about Rama's story.

玛蒂尔达：我们刚读完了一本侧刊。瞧，还有附赠的卡片呢。
Matilda: We've just finished FLASH: FAME. Look, it even comes with a card.

阿贾尔：啊！这个卡片……
Ajar: Oh! This card ...

玛蒂尔达：记录神秘学家信息的名人卡！它们可是“硬通货”，其中稀有的名人卡可是有市无价呢！
Matilda: This is a FAME Card with information on an arcanist! FAME Cards are a "hard currency" of sorts, and the rare ones are priceless!

鬃毛沙砾：还有全新的解读视角……我是说，瞧瞧这些游客吧！
Shamane: That's a fresh perspective. I mean, look at these tourists!

坎吉拉：哦……嘿，这不正好？带我们也一块儿去吧～
Kanjira: Oh! Hey, that's it! Take us with you!

玛蒂尔达：才没那么容易呢！这个主会场神出鬼没，只能从侧刊的线索中推测出大概位置。
Matilda: Not so fast! The venue is too elusive. We can only find it according to the hints in FLASH: FAME.

坎吉拉：哼哼，这种小事～交给聪明的坎吉拉吧！
Kanjira: Heh, not a big deal. Leave it to clever Kanjira here!

03  《猴国之旅》 / The Monkey Kingdom

坎吉拉：这些字，嗯……长得像天书。
Kanjira: These words ... Um ... They look like nothing I seen before.

女孩点了点头，做出了中肯的评价。
The girl nods and makes a fair comment.

坎吉拉：真不敢相信……伽菈波那，你能读懂这些文字？
Kanjira: I can't believe ... Kaalaa Baunaa, can you really read these words?

伽菈波那：唔，读懂梵文并非一件易事……谁能想到天文学科研路上还有这样的小径呢。
Kaalaa Baunaa: It's no easy task to read Sanskrit. I didn't imagine that my study of astronomy would come in useful like this.

伽菈波那：好在它有用武之地……就像现在。总刊中关于金鹿的桥段十分有趣。
Kaalaa Baunaa: Luckily, it has its uses, like now. The part about the Golden Deer in UTTU: FLASH is really interesting.

坎吉拉：哦，那个可恶的魔王！调虎离山，还用诡计抢走了悉多……
Kanjira: Oh, that bad bad Ravana the Demon King! He lured them away, then he used a dirty ploy to kidnap Rama's wife, Sita …

阿贾尔：就是，跟坎吉拉大姐一样狡猾——
Ajar: Yeah, just as cunning as Boss!

坎吉拉：你说什么？
Kanjira: What did you just say?

阿贾尔：诶呦！错了，错了，别掐我！！
Ajar: Ow! My bad, my bad, don't pinch me!

阿贾尔：不过，我还是更喜欢神猴哈奴曼！
Ajar: But I prefer the Divine Vanara, Hanuman!

坎吉拉：噢噢！他太酷了！简直无所不能！
Kanjira: Oh yeah! He's really cool! There's nothing he can't do!

伽菈波那：孩子们总是更喜欢他。
Kaalaa Baunaa: That's why all children love him!

坎吉拉：魔王以为能抓住他，结果自讨苦吃，他一溜烟就回去报信了。
Kanjira: The Demon King wanted to catch Hanuman, but he was asking for trouble! Hanuman ran away, then, told this to Rama!

坎吉拉：罗摩得到了消息，他那么厉害，肯定不会放过魔王罗波那。
Kanjira: The great Rama got the news. He will never forgive the Demon King.

阿贾尔：那么接下来会发生什么呢？
Ajar: Then what happened next?

鬃毛沙砾：当然是去搬救兵咯，去找一位神通广大的——
Shamane: Rama went to seek the aid of an all-powerful ...

坎吉拉：打住！！不许剧透。
Kanjira: Stop! No spoilers.

鬃毛沙砾：嘿！你又读不懂梵文。由伽菈波那转述给你，或是我直接告诉你，又有什么分别嘛！
Shamane: Heh! You can't even read Sanskrit. What difference does it make whether it's Kaalaa Baunaa or I telling you?

坎吉拉：读来的和听来的，当然不一样咯～
Kanjira: Reading and hearing are different!

坎吉拉：这么说，罗摩肯定会横渡大海，去找魔王复仇吧？
Kanjira: Then, Rama must go cross the sea and revenge on the Demon King?

鬃毛沙砾：呜——！我的嘴被缝上了，什么话都说不出来咯～
Shamane: Uh ... My lips are sealed. Can't say a word!

坎吉拉：你……！算了，当我什么也没问。
Kanjira: You ...! Never mind. Forget I asked.

男人大笑着拍了拍女孩的肩膀，将目光投向一旁的建筑。
The man roars with laughter and pats the girl on her shoulder. Then, he looks at the adjacent building.

鬃毛沙砾：瞧瞧这些举办集会的场馆……就像罗摩的神迹一样神奇。
Shamane: Look at these event venues. They're as amazing as Rama's palaces.

坎吉拉：对哦，摩卢旁卡就没有这样的建筑。
Kanjira: You is right. Mor Pankh don't have buildings like this.

伽菈波那：这里被称为“梦想之城”。
Kaalaa Baunaa: This place is called the City of Dreams.

伽菈波那：来自世界各地的建筑师接到邀请，来到昌迪加尔，与这里的人们共同兴建起了这样一座颇具实验性的城市。
Kaalaa Baunaa: Architects from all over the world were invited to Chandigarh and worked with the locals to construct this extraordinary city.

伽菈波那：但我想，它之所以被称为“梦想之城”，或许并不仅仅是因为这些独特的城市规划……
Kaalaa Baunaa: But I think the reason why it's called the City of Dreams lies beyond its unique planning.

伽菈波那的手触上一旁的墙壁。
Kaalaa Baunaa runs a hand over the wall beside her.

伽菈波那：……而是因为，这是一座承载了人们对美好生活的向往的城市。
Kaalaa Baunaa: It's because this is a city that reflects the longing of its people for a good life.

伽菈波那：人们都收下过去，面向着未来——摩卢旁卡的烛星节没被忘却，这里仍是这样热闹。
Kaalaa Baunaa: Everyone's embraced their past and turned their gaze to the future. Dīpa Festival has not been forgotten in Mor Pankh. This place is as lively as ever.

伽菈波那：新旧两个时代在这里相遇，就像是行星彼此擦肩相会。
Kaalaa Baunaa: The old and new meet here, like two planets that brush shoulders with each other.

坎吉拉：这样的集会……更多的建筑，更多的故事，唔……
Kanjira: A gathering like this ... More buildings, more stories, um ...

坎吉拉：快！我等不及要看下一章了！
Kanjira: Come on! I can't wait to read next chapter!

04  《不死甘露》 / The Nectar of Immortality

坎吉拉：罗波那也太厉害了吧，长了整整十颗头。
Kanjira: The Demon King is unbelievable. He's got ten heads.

伽菈波那：不死甘露让魔王近乎永生，而这十颗头正是他的十种恶习。
Kaalaa Baunaa: The Nectar of Immortality has made the Demon King almost impossible to kill, and these ten heads represent his ten vices.

鬃毛沙砾：我的师父曾告诉我，每个人都有十颗头。
Shamane: My master once told me that everyone has ten heads.

坎吉拉：真的假的……？！
Kanjira: For real ...!?

鬃毛沙砾：咳咳，提问！罗摩斩断魔王罗波那的头颅后，为何要去忏悔呢？
Shamane: Ahem! Question! Why did Rama seek penance after cutting off the Demon King's heads?

玛蒂尔达：我知道！！嗯，文章里说：“罗摩忏悔，因为自己杀了一个人。”
Matilda: I know! Uh, the text says, "Rama sought penance, for he had taken a life."

坎吉拉：他明明杀死的是魔王呀？
Kanjira: But he killed the Demon King, right?

伽菈波那：当罗摩杀死魔王的时候，他也杀死了一个人。
Kaalaa Baunaa: When Rama killed the Demon King, he also killed a "mortal."

鬃毛沙砾：一个国王、一位学者、一位虔诚的信徒、一个人。
Shamane: Because the Demon King himself was also a king, a scholar, a faithful devotee, and a mortal.

坎吉拉：你的意思是……？
Kanjira: What do you mean?

鬃毛沙砾：“善妒”、“贪婪”、“自我”，那是人们的恶习。
Shamane: His ten heads represented jealousy, greed, selfishness … All of these are the vices of mortals.

鬃毛沙砾：魔王也正因忽视凡人而被罗摩击败。
Shamane: He despised mortals, so he did not seek divine protection against mortals' harm.

伽菈波那：——正是人克服了人的恶习。
Kaalaa Baunaa: But eventually, it was Rama, a mortal, who overcame his vices and killed him—the mortal killed the mortal.

鬃毛沙砾：不错，这也是我对《罗摩衍那》的理解。
Shamane: Not bad. This is also my understanding of the Ramayana.

伽菈波那：相传……《罗摩衍那》的作者蚁垤曾以偷窃为生。
Kaalaa Baunaa: It's said that Valmiki, the author of the Ramayana, once made a living as a pickpocket.

坎吉拉：……！
Kanjira: …!

伽菈波那：但在某一日，他开悟了。他开始收集罗摩的故事，编撰成集。
Kaalaa Baunaa: But one day, realization struck. He began to gather tales about Rama and edited them into a collection.
