01  【3.5】蝴蝶玩法剧情01 / [English text unavailable]

拉普拉斯科算中心
Laplace Scientific Computing Center

拉普拉斯研究员Ⅰ：欢迎来到拉普拉斯科算中心总部。
Laplace Researcher I: Welcome to the LSCC headquarters.

拉普拉斯研究员Ⅰ：我记得你是从外部的科研工作室转过来的，很快你就会发现这里和外头不太一样。
Laplace Researcher I: I see you were brought in from one of our partner labs. It won't take long for you to notice we do things differently around here.

拉普拉斯研究员Ⅰ：拉普拉斯的第一要义是：别惹事；
第二要义是：要惹事就惹个大的。
Laplace Researcher I: Rule one at Laplace is don't make trouble. The second rule is if you really have to make trouble, make it spectacular.

新人研究员：谢谢你，无需替我担心。
New Researcher: Thank you for the warning, but I'm sure you needn't worry.

拉普拉斯研究员Ⅰ：显然你还没有明白……噢，第三要义是：千万别惹怒兔毛手袋研究员！你的目的地到了。
Laplace Researcher I: I don't think you understand just what you're in for yet. Ah, third rule: never, under any circumstances, get on the wrong side of Medicine Pocket. Here we are.

两人在某扇饱受牙印摧残的门前停下。
一张稿纸歪斜地贴在门上，彩色马克笔着重勾勒了标题。
The two of them stand before a door ravaged by bite marks. A crooked sheet of paper is taped to it, the title aggressively outlined in colored marker.

新人研究员：“人员招募！‘暴雨’前地球大气环流与海洋环流观测小组。”
New Researcher: "Join us! The Pre-Storm Atmospheric and Oceanic Circulation Survey Group is now recruiting new members!"

新人研究员：蛮有意思的课题。如果不是写在一张充满童趣的传单上。
New Researcher: It's a compelling research topic. Though it's a little strange to see it written out in such a childish way.

拉普拉斯研究员Ⅰ：自从上次兔毛手袋研究员因为经费延迟的问题摧毁了这条走廊上所有的门框后，我们已经找不到任何一间完好的办公室了。重建工作排在了178个工作日之后……
Laplace Researcher I: Oh, don't mind the door. It's only the unfortunate aftermath of a disgruntled researcher. Allow me to remind you of Rule Three. Repairs will be ordered as soon as the budget permits; should be around 178 business days, give or take.

拉普拉斯研究员Ⅰ：啊！该死，谁给门把手通上电了！就算这层楼所有人都被电死了，你也不可能继承我们的经费的，兔毛手袋研究员！
Laplace Researcher I: Ah! Damn it! What kind of monster electrifies a doorknob?! Electrocuting someone doesn't mean you inherit their funding, Medicine Pocket!

拉普拉斯研究员Ⅰ：总之，这里就是你接下来要待的地方。
Laplace Researcher I: Anyway, this is your new workspace.

话说到这里，研究员的声音顿了顿。
At that, the researcher's voice pauses for a moment.

拉普拉斯研究员Ⅰ：奇怪，我怎么隐约听到了希曼女士的声音？
Laplace Researcher I: That's weird. I could've sworn I heard Ms. Simone's voice just now.

拉普拉斯研究员Ⅰ：难道她是来宣布环流小组的课题彻底终止的？
Laplace Researcher I: Don't tell me she's come to shut the circulation survey group down once and for all.

环流观测室
Observation Room

观测室内，总负责人助理站在环流观测小组仅有的两名成员面前。
Inside the observation room, the assistant stands before the circulation survey group's only two members.

蕾切尔：是好消息？坏消息？还是不坏不好的消息？
Rachel: Which is it? Good news, bad news, or something in between?

玛格丽特：蕾切尔，让希曼女士说话吧……
Marguerite: Tous les mêmes. Just let Ms. Simone speak.

玛格丽特：她一定是来传达什么重要消息的。
Marguerite: I'm sure she'll tell us whatever the case may be.

温柔而怯懦的抚慰，但无法打断研究员执拗的自言自语。
It's a tepid attempt at calming her colleague, and it predictably falls flat.

蕾切尔：天大的好消息，天大的坏消息，我觉得只有这两种可能——
Rachel: I know. It's either spectacularly good news or catastrophically bad news. There's no middle ground.

助理希曼：蕾切尔，玛格丽特，我是来告诉你们一个好消息的。
Assistant Simone: Rachel, Marguerite, I have good news.

助理希曼：“暴雨”前地球大气环流与海洋环流观测小组的外出申请，正式通过了。
Assistant Simone: The Pre-Storm Atmospheric and Oceanic Circulation Survey Group's field research proposal has been approved.

玛格丽特：太好了，希曼女士……
Marguerite: That is excellent.

玛格丽特：蕾切尔，我们终于可以准备第一次野外考察了。
Marguerite: We can finally begin preparations for our first field study.

本该爆发出喜悦的研究员，却率先把注意力放在了别的地方。
But her colleague seems hung up on something entirely different.

蕾切尔：希曼女士是第三个记得我们小组全名的人。您一定对加入环流观测小组有极大的兴趣吧！
Rachel: You know, you're the third person to ever say our project's entire name correctly. Does that mean Laplace is finally going to give us the attention we deserve? Dare I dream that you might even increase our budget?

助理希曼：没这必要。
Assistant Simone: Uhm, I'm afraid not.

蕾切尔：终于！终于！不枉费我每周发一次申请，每天发一次传单。我甚至把传单邮寄到了各地协作的科研工作室……
Rachel: Fine, fine, but at least tell me that all those pamphlets I sent out paid off! I even mailed copies to all our partner labs.

助理希曼：蕾切尔研究员，还差一点儿，你的行为就会触犯“暴雨”保密协议。希望你牢记，仅有部分科研工作室能够知晓“暴雨”的存在。
Assistant Simone: Putting you a hair's breadth away from violating the "Storm" confidentiality agreement. You should know better than to reveal that kind of sensitive information without approval.

助理希曼：但发传单的确有效。
Assistant Simone: Nevertheless, they did achieve their intended effect.

蕾切尔：嗯？什么意思？
Rachel: Huh? Really, what do you mean?

助理希曼：有一位来自南威尔士气象中心的研究员希望能转来总部，加入环流观测小组。
Assistant Simone: We've received a transfer application from a researcher at the South Wales Meteorological Centre. She's expressed interest in joining the circulation survey group.

助理希曼：玛格丽特，你们是校友，她也毕业于剑桥大学。
Assistant Simone: Marguerite, you might be familiar with her. She attended Cambridge around the same time you did.

玛格丽特：校友吗……？
Marguerite: Is that so?

玛格丽特：我……很高兴能在这里遇到故人。
Marguerite: Well, it's a big university, but who knows, it could be nice to see a familiar face.

温柔的研究员一如既往地微笑着，面目显得有些模糊。
The mild-mannered researcher wears her usual smile, though her actual emotions beneath are harder to read.

玛格丽特：请进。
Marguerite: Come in.

新人研究员：“暴雨”前地球大气环流与海洋环流观测小组……是这里对吗？
New Researcher: The Pre-Storm Atmospheric and Oceanic Circulation Survey Group. Is this the right place?

蕾切尔：第四个念出小组全名的人！
Rachel: Absolutely! And you're now the fourth person to ever get our full name right!

玛格丽特：欢迎您的加入，女士。这位是组长蕾切尔，而我是——
Marguerite: Welcome to the team. The enthusiastic one is our group leader, Rachel, and I'm—

来人将目光停留在面前灰金发研究员的身上。
The newcomer's eyes swivel to the ash-blond researcher and then hold a long stare.

短暂的沉默，空气仿若无察，一切继续流动。
A brief silence follows.

新人研究员：玛格丽特，好久不见。
New Researcher: Marguerite, it's been a long time.

黑墙：各位好，我是黑墙。
Coalwall: You might not remember my name. Call me Coalwall.

助理希曼：请坐，黑墙研究员。我正要说明具体情况。
Assistant Simone: Please, have a seat. I'll outline the specifics.

助理希曼：虽然小组的外出考察申请已经获批，但是，安全条例还需完善。
Assistant Simone: Although your field expedition has been approved, we believe your safety protocols require further revision.

助理希曼：比如说，把“暴雨”倒计时前半小时撤离改成前八小时撤离。
Assistant Simone: For example, your evacuation has to be set eight hours before the "Storm" countdown ends; we can't justify leaving it any closer.

蕾切尔：但司辰的“暴雨”预警只有二十四小时。届时，留给我们行动的时间就不多了。
Rachel: But the Timekeeper's "Storm" warning only gives us 24 hours. That won't give us much time to observe the "Storm's" effects.

蕾切尔：调整成一个小时如何？或者……两个小时？
Rachel: Could do one hour instead. Two maybe?

助理希曼：如果不把研究员的生命安全放在第一位，我们会立刻撤回批准。这是人事委员会的意思：我们不能因为“暴雨”失去更多的成员了。
Assistant Simone: Researcher safety must be placed above all else. This new requirement comes directly from the Staff Committee. We cannot afford to lose any more of our people to the "Storm."

玛格丽特：人事委员会的担忧是有道理的。目前的行动方案确实十分危险……
Marguerite: The Staff Committee's concerns are justified. The current evacuation plan does have us leaving with very little time to spare.

助理希曼：虽然蕾切尔研究员的科研能力出众，但显然不擅长处理这些。
Assistant Simone: I understand your desire to have as much observational data as possible, Researcher Rachel. But we can't afford to put you in danger.

助理希曼：玛格丽特研究员，你比她有耐心许多。如果你可以多多帮助她，那我们双方都可以省去很多麻烦。
Assistant Simone: Marguerite, I trust you to show more patience in this matter. I hope you might be able to guide our colleague here to a more reasonable conclusion.

玛格丽特：我会尽力协助的。
Marguerite: I'll do my best.

玛格丽特：虽然我没有办法像同事们那样，时常有些灵光一现的天才点子……但处理好文书工作，全力支持蕾切尔的研究，是我能尽的最大努力。
Marguerite: I may not have the same sudden flashes of brilliance my colleagues do, but you know my documentation is always meticulous.

她的面上恰到好处地浮现出一丝尴尬。
A perfectly measured hint of embarrassment crosses her face.

助理希曼：如今理论物理组只剩两个人了，除了蕾切尔，另一个从第一次“暴雨”之后就把自己锁在研究室里，每天和酒精过日子。
Assistant Simone: There aren't many researchers left at the LSCC approaching the "Storm," from the perspective of natural phenomena. One of them has been locked up in his laboratory since the first "Storm," with only a bottle for company.

助理希曼：我知道你是害怕蕾切尔会变成阿德勒研究员那样，但你也不能事事迁就她，尤其是安全方面。
Assistant Simone: While I'm sure neither of you wants to end up in the same position, that doesn't mean you can or should expect accommodation in every matter, especially when it comes to safety.

物理天才的世界因“暴雨”而坍塌，不算意外的故事。
A brilliant physicist's entire world undone by the "Storm" is hardly an unusual tale.

玛格丽特：我明白了，希曼女士。
Marguerite: I understand, Ms. Simone.

黑墙：我有一套《三分钟通过审查》的书——我也会帮忙的。
Coalwall: Perhaps I might be able to assist? I wrote more than a few safety manuals for my old laboratory.

助理希曼：感谢各位。
Assistant Simone: Thank you, everyone.

助理希曼：现在，蕾切尔研究员，作为组长，你需要和我去申领手册和文件，你忙吗？
Assistant Simone: Now, Rachel, I need you to accompany me to collect some manuals and documentation for your new colleague. Are you free right now?

蕾切尔：我有组观测数据还没……
Rachel: I still have some meteorological data to—

玛格丽特：我和黑墙研究员来替你整理数据吧，蕾切尔。正好作为她入职的过渡工作。
Marguerite: We can organize that data for you. I'm sure Ms. Coalwall could use a good introductory assignment.

助理希曼：那就走吧。
Assistant Simone: In that case, let's get going.

蕾切尔：我会尽快回来的！
Rachel: I won't be long!

玛格丽特：没关系，不用着急。
Marguerite: No need to rush back.

一扇门，隔开了嘈杂与寂静。
A single door separates noise from silence.

玛格丽特平静地整理起桌上的草稿，另一人则对她投以注视。
Marguerite gathers the scattered drafts on the desk while the new researcher watches.

黑墙：你变了。
Coalwall: You've changed.

玛格丽特：是吗？
Marguerite: Have I?

她看了看面前沉默的新人，这是又轻又快的一瞥。
She casts a light and fleeting glance toward the quiet newcomer before her.

黑墙：在来的路上，带路的研究员告诉我：“玛格丽特的科研水平不过尔尔，没什么有意义的成果，很多人经常忘记她的存在。”
Coalwall: "Unremarkable at best. No significant achievements. We sometimes forget she's even there." That's what they say about you, you know?

黑墙：温柔、怯懦、无能的老好人……
Coalwall: Gentle. Meek. Harmlessly ineffectual.

黑墙：和剑桥里那位沉默寡言却堪称恃才傲物的玛格丽特，很难想象是同一个人。
Coalwall: Sounds nothing like the woman I knew from Cambridge.

在没有第三个人的房间里，那张面孔上的笑容依旧保持着一致的弧度，如同焊上去的面具，让黑墙忽生烦躁。
Marguerite turns. She holds a smile in an exact curve, like a mask welded in place, provoking a sharp reaction from Coalwall.

玛格丽特：嗯，不算糟糕的形容。
Marguerite: I see. Well, it isn't the worst assessment.

玛格丽特：没想到你还记得我，黑墙小姐。
Marguerite: I'm surprised you still remember me.

玛格丽特：很高兴在拉普拉斯科算中心见到你，发现有一位校友也跨过了“暴雨”——
Marguerite: But I'm glad to see you here all the same. Nice to know a fellow alumna also made it through the "Storm."

黑墙：够了！省下这些恭维的话，我们不算熟人，也上演不了同窗好友多年未见的戏码。
Coalwall: Spare me the pleasantries. We weren't close, and this isn't some long-awaited reunion.

玛格丽特：抱歉，如果不是为了寒暄，那你想与我说些什么呢？
Marguerite: I apologize. If you're not in the mood for small talk, then what would you like to discuss?

她依旧无懈可击地微笑着。
Her frozen smile remains unwavering.

黑墙：你还记得安妮吗？
Coalwall: You remember Annie, don't you?

黑墙：那个你在路德维希教授的课上认识的安妮，总是像一条小尾巴一样跟着你的安妮，与你一起在高空站完成气象推测课题的安妮……
Coalwall: From Professor Ludwig's class, you remember, don't you? She followed you around like a shadow for a long while. She worked with you on that meteorological projection study.

黑墙：1987年，安妮死在一条小道上，被发现的时候，她的衣襟上凝结着露水。
Coalwall: And then one day, they found her dead on a footpath. Her collar was covered with dew.

玛格丽特：我有些意外，你会和我提起这件事。
Marguerite: Why bring up something awful like that?

玛格丽特：印象里，你和安妮并不算是熟人或者好友。
Marguerite: As I recall, you and Annie were not particularly close.

黑墙：是啊，我和她并不算熟稔……
Coalwall: No. We weren't close.

研究员的面上流露出一丝隐藏的哀伤。
A faint, concealed sorrow passes across the researcher's face.

黑墙：但……玛格丽特，我必须问你，你与安妮的死，真的毫无关系吗？
Coalwall: But, Marguerite, I need to know: what really happened to Annie?

剑桥大学
Cambridge University

1987年，剑桥大学。
Cambridge University, 1987.

阴冷潮湿的风拂过树梢，讲台上，渡鸦教授短暂地被簌簌声吸引了注意力。
A cold, damp wind brushes the treetops. At the lectern, Professor Ludwig pauses, momentarily distracted by the rustling.

路德维希：……总而言之，上述系统虽然完全由确定的方程支配，但依然可以表现得充满混乱。
Ludwig: In short, even a system ruled by fixed equations can produce outcomes that appear entirely chaotic.

路德维希：这个感觉就像是在阅读《精神现象学》——在缜密的推论中头晕目眩。
Ludwig: It feels like reading The Phenomenology of Spirit, precision so dense it leaves you lightheaded.

路德维希：注意，我没有推荐你们去阅读任何哲学著作。今天的课就到这里。
Ludwig: To be clear, I am not assigning you any additional philosophy reading. That will be all for today.

学生们纷纷道别。他将讲义收拾完毕，留意到仍有三两学生并未离去。
The class disperses. As he collects his papers, he observes a small cluster of students lingering.

路德维希：玛格丽特。
Ludwig: Marguerite.

玛格丽特：托特教授。
Marguerite: Professor Thoth.

路德维希：我看起来是渡鸦，并不像朱鹭。
Ludwig: Thoth? What an absurdity. I don't even look like an ibis.

路德维希：距离你入学已有一段时日，这段时间适应得如何？
Ludwig: Humorous nicknames aside. I'm curious: It's been a while since you transferred to my class. How are you adjusting?

玛格丽特：托您的福，一切都好。我想，课业相关的内容，您可以从课题报告上看到，无需来问我。
Marguerite: Everything's going well, thanks to you. As for my coursework, I believe my research reports speak for themselves.

面前的学生漫不经心地解释着。
The student before him explains with an air of nonchalance.

路德维希：是的，《大气中微子反常现象对振荡模型的启示》，非常出色的文章。
Ludwig: Yes. On the Atmospheric Neutrino Anomaly and Its Implications for Oscillation Models. An excellent paper, indeed, indeed.

路德维希：客观来说，它有些超出当前时代的进度，但物理学不该随着时代而倒退。
Ludwig: Objectively speaking, it is somewhat ahead of its time. Still, physics ought not regress simply because the era lags behind.

路德维希：看来不仅是课业，你对这个年代也适应得很好。是我多虑了。
Ludwig: It appears you've adapted not only academically but to this era as well. My concern was unnecessary.

玛格丽特：“这个年代”。
Marguerite: "This era" ...

她咀嚼着这突如其来的字眼。
She turns the unexpected phrase over in her mind.

玛格丽特：噢，您并没有忘记发生在1999年的事情。
Marguerite: Oh, you haven't forgotten what happened in 1999.

玛格丽特：“哲学教授是世界上最荒唐的职业”？
Marguerite: "To teach philosophy is the most absurd profession in the world?"

路德维希：这正是我如今站在这里的原因，以物理学客座教授的身份。
Ludwig: That is precisely why I stand here today, in the capacity of a visiting physics lecturer.

路德维希：剑桥大学对于科算中心普列谢茨克分部正在进行的研究非常感兴趣，邀请我在闲暇时过来授课。
Ludwig: Cambridge has taken an interest in the research underway at Laplace's Plesetsk Branch and extended an invitation for me to teach when time permits.

路德维希：1999年，因为当时的时代剧变，我无法再延续教职，而错过了举荐你的机会。
Ludwig: The events of 1999 cut short my appointment, and with it, my chance to sponsor you.

路德维希：如今，很高兴看到你也跨越了“暴雨”。
Ludwig: I am pleased to see you also survived the "Storm."

玛格丽特：您不关心我是如何做到的吗？
Marguerite: You're not curious to know how I survived?

路德维希：这会影响你的课题报告吗？还是说会影响你的生活？
Ludwig: Would such knowledge alter your academic progress?

玛格丽特：哈，当然不会。
Marguerite: Hah, of course not.

路德维希：既然如此，它就是属于个人的隐私。
Ludwig: Then it's none of my concern.

路德维希：下周太空站的航空实验讲座，如果你有兴趣的话，也可以来旁听。
Ludwig: There's a lecture on aerospace experiments at the orbital station next week. You're welcome to sit in.

玛格丽特：感谢您的邀请，托特教授。
