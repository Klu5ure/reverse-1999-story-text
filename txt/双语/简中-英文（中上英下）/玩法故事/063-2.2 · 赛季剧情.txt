基金会职员：本录像为实验项目——趋光性研究的操作规范示例。
Foundation Staff Member: You are about to watch a series of videos demonstrating the operational procedures of our trial project, "Phototaxis in Study."

基金会职员：请在实验前完整观看本录像，以免发生事故。
Foundation Staff Member: Please watch each video in its entirety to ensure your safety during the experiment.

基金会职员：首先，请牢记您的个人身份，您是受圣洛夫基金会委派，负责进行实验的专员。
Foundation Staff Member: First, confirm and remember your personal identity: You are a staff member of the Foundation, assigned to participate in these trials.

基金会职员：房间内的诱蛾装置会吸引蛾子来。
Foundation Staff Member: The lighting system in the experimentation room has been specifically designed to attract moths.

基金会职员：那些蛾子是我们的特工的神秘术表现，它们中有些携带着关键情报。
Foundation Staff Member: These moths are arcane creations, conjured by our staff to deliver vital intel.

基金会职员：而您的职责是亲身体验飞蛾带来的情报，排除飞蛾中的无用信息，归纳出有阅读价值的情报档案。
Foundation Staff Member: Your role is to review each piece of intel and extract any valuable information.

基金会职员：过程中您可能会产生身份认同危机，比如想不起自己的名字，或者觉得自己是某个不认识的人。
Foundation Staff Member: During this process, you may experience disruptions to your sense of identity, including symptoms such as amnesia and dissociation.

基金会职员：届时请远离仪器，立即联络医务人员并进行人格稳定性检测。
Foundation Staff Member: Should you notice any of these symptoms, please leave the lab immediately and contact the medical team for a personality and identity assessment.

基金会职员：请时刻记住您的个人身份——您是受圣洛夫基金会委派，负责进行实验的专员。
Foundation Staff Member: Always remember: You are a staff member of the Foundation, assigned to participate in these trials.

基金会职员：接下来开始播放实验录像，我们会为您示范一遍规范操作，请反复阅读该录像直至您完全掌握操作流程。
Foundation Staff Member: The first video is about to begin. Please pay close attention and review the content as many times as necessary to fully understand the operational procedures.
