一项任务
A Mission

在世界尽头最古老的严酷冰原，风雪会阻隔所有窥探的视线。
At the world's end, upon the oldest, most merciless icefield, a blizzard blinds all onlookers.

即便是军人如鹰般锐利的目光也无法穿透南极的风雪，他不得不转向身后。
Even the soldier's eagle eyes can make out nothing through the Antarctic storm, and so he turns back.

前芝诺近卫团某驻地
Former Zeno Sentinel Unit Station

白手套队长：中尉，我们还有多少时间？
Sergeant White Gloves: Lieutenant, how much time do we have?

铅笔在笔记本上写画的声音和雪粒敲击帐篷声混杂在一起，但很快就只剩雪粒的声音。
Scribbles on paper resonate amid the whipping snow.

莫莉德尔：二十七个小时。
Moldir: 27 hours.

莫莉德尔：为了平衡负重、尽可能扩大侦察范围，安德烈带领的侦察队并未携带太多补给。
Moldir: Andrea's team traveled light to cover more ground. They will have minimal supplies.

莫莉德尔：鉴于失温、复杂地形以及潜在未侦测到的战斗威胁，安德烈队伍的黄金救援时限保守估计为三十六小时。
Moldir: Factoring in exposure, terrain, and unconfirmed hostiles, Andrea's team has a golden window of 36 hours for rescue at best.

莫莉德尔：现在距离他们的预定返回时间已经过去了九个小时。
Moldir: It's been nine hours since they were expected back.

莫莉德尔结束计算，合上了手里的笔记本。
She finishes her calculations and shuts the notebook.

莫莉德尔：安德烈的能力毋庸置疑，但我很担心戴维德……他太年轻了，还没来得及积累足够的经验。
Moldir: Andrea is more than capable, but David ... He's still too young and raw for this.

莫莉德尔：将军不会愿意见到任何一个孩子掉队。
Moldir: The admiral would never allow even a single child to be left behind.

莫莉德尔：三人三组的搜救小队是目前能做到最合理的分配，我们必须抓紧时间。
Moldir: Three teams of three. That's all we can spare right now. Let's not waste another minute.

莫莉德尔：请协助我做好相应准备，队长。
Moldir: Please assist me with the preparations, Sergeant.

白手套队长：是，中尉。
Sergeant White Gloves: Yes, Lieutenant!

他看向外面。雪粒还在不依不饶地拍击帐篷，只要有足够的时间，它们会磨平一切。
He glances outside. Pellets of blistering snow are still hammering the tent. Given enough time, they'll bury any trace of it.

白手套队长：我们什么时候出发？
Sergeant White Gloves: When do we move out?

莫莉德尔：考虑到往返救援的时长，我们最多还有三个小时。
Moldir: Factoring in the round-trip time for the rescue, we will have to leave within three hours.

莫莉德尔：三个小时内，只要暴风雪停下，我们就立刻出发。
Moldir: If the storm lets up in that window, we head out immediately.

中尉同样看向外面，郑重地审视着这个看起来无法战胜的敌人。
Moldir also turns to look outside, eyes steady as she stares down her enemy. There is no strategy to defeat it; the only option is to withstand the onslaught.

莫莉德尔：如果天气没有站在我们这边，就在这三个小时内做好更多的准备和预案。
Moldir: If the weather stays against us, we'll use these three hours to prepare and adjust our strategy.

莫莉德尔：三个小时后，无论如何都必须出发。
Moldir: But in three hours, we move. No matter the circumstances.

他们的运气不坏，一小时二十七分钟后，敌人主动收缩了防线。
Luck is on their side. 1 hour and 27 minutes later, their enemy at last relents.

暴风雪停了。
The blizzard has stopped.

莫莉德尔：十分钟内做好所有出发准备，按原定分配计划拆分需携带的必要物资。
Moldir: You've got ten minutes to gear up. Stick to the assigned supply splits—no changes.

士兵Ⅰ：是，中尉！
Soldier I: Yes, Lieutenant!

莫莉德尔：请记住，无论能否找到安德烈小队，都必须严格按照你们收到的命令进行行动。
Moldir: Listen carefully. Whether or not we find Andrea's team, you follow your orders to the letter.

莫莉德尔：严禁超过规定探索时间，严禁偏离本队既定探索方向。
Moldir: No exceeding your assigned search time. No straying from your team's designated path.

莫莉德尔：我会在集合点等你们。
Moldir: I will be waiting at the rendezvous point.

中尉的目光逐一扫过他们的脸。
She scans her small force, her eyes sweeping over each soldier.

莫莉德尔：每一个人。
Moldir: Every single one of you.

距离安德烈小队失联二十个小时。
20 hours after Andrea's team went missing.

莫莉德尔：……不能再前进了，我们现在就调转方向，前往集合点。
Moldir: We can't go any farther. Turn back and head for the rendezvous point.

士兵Ⅰ：可是中尉，我们还没有抵达原定的距离！
Soldier I: But Lieutenant, we haven't reached our target location yet.

莫莉德尔：如果遭遇恶劣天气，探索范围必须缩减，这也是出发前的约定之一。
Moldir: If weather conditions turn, we scale back. That was in the mission briefing. We all agreed.

士兵Ⅱ：报告中尉，我们还可以坚持！
Soldier II: Lieutenant, with respect, we can keep going!

中尉对他们的勇气予以认可，但对他们的提议进行了否决。
She admits a slight grin, admiring the grit of her soldiers, but shakes her head.

莫莉德尔：我们必须严格按照约定方针行动，停止前进，转向集合点执行汇合命令。
Moldir: Stick to the plan. End of discussion. Turn back and head for the rendezvous point.

士兵Ⅰ：可是……
Soldier I: But ...

莫莉德尔：在这样严酷的环境下，选择后勤物资分散负重的方式出动，任何失约都是对战友生命的不负责任。
Moldir: Out here, with supplies split across the team, breaking protocol puts lives at risk. That is not a gamble I'll allow.

中尉的语气严厉，认真地将行动守则向部下强调。
Her tone sharpens, each word carrying weight.

莫莉德尔：服从命令，士兵。
Moldir: Follow your orders, soldier.

士兵Ⅰ：……是，中尉！
Soldier I: Yes, Lieutenant!

莫莉德尔深深看了一眼雪原深处，带头调转了方向。
Moldir surveys the deepening snow once again, then leads the team back.

她面无表情，但南极的严寒出卖了那一声霜白色的叹息。
Her face is cold and still, but the Antarctic wind steals a quiet, frost-white sigh.

She ends the call with High Command and lowers the radio hesitantly. A small gesture signals to the sergeant that he can begin his report.

白手套队长：根据原定作战计划，我们将进行六个小时的修整，然后继续下一轮搜救活动。
Sergeant White Gloves: According to the original briefing, we are to rest here for six hours, then begin our search operation again radially from this location.

白手套队长：中尉，六个小时会不会太久了？我们的时间已经不多了。
Sergeant White Gloves: Lieutenant, speaking freely, that seems too long. Aren't we running out of time?

莫莉德尔低头看了一眼手表。
Moldir checks her watch.

莫莉德尔：保证大家的体力和找到安德烈小队一样重要，我们已经承受不起任何减员了。
Moldir: If we keep going without rest, we'll put our teams at risk. We cannot afford more casualties.

白手套队长：距离安德烈小队失联已经过去了二十四个小时，中尉。
Sergeant White Gloves: It's been 24 hours since they went dark, Lieutenant.

白手套队长：保守的黄金救援时间只有三十六个小时，如果我们现在休息六个小时，那留给安德烈小队的时间还剩多少？
Sergeant White Gloves: Our rescue window is dwindling. How much time does that leave them?

莫莉德尔：极限生还可能时限是七十二个小时，队长。

莫莉德尔：我相信戴维德，他的意志像钢铁一样坚硬，他不会轻易放弃。

白手套队长：我也相信他不会。

白手套队长：是，中尉。
Sergeant White Gloves: Yes, Lieutenant.

莫莉德尔：我会再确认一下明天的路线和时限设置……在保证安全的情况下，尽可能扩大搜索范围。
Moldir: I'll double-check tomorrow's route and timing. If conditions allow, we'll widen the search range as far as we can.

白手套队长：收到。
Sergeant White Gloves: Understood.

白手套队长：请早点休息。

Moldir: Thank you for your concern, Sergeant. I won't go against the protocols I set myself.

距离安德烈小队失联二十五个小时。
It has now been 25 hours since Andrea's team went missing.

在发出命令一小时后，莫莉德尔仍然没有休息。
An hour has passed, and Moldir still hasn't slept.

摊开在木板箱上的军用地图比其他人手中的多了一些标记。在代表路线的箭头前方，漆黑的手掌无声地罩笼着雪原。
The military map spread across a wooden crate bears layers of markings. In front of each hand-drawn arrow is a looming dark hand.

莫莉德尔：……
Moldir: ...

莫莉德尔：必须想办法在保证搜救覆盖范围的同时，避免和他们起冲突。
Moldir: We need full coverage, but we can't afford a direct clash with them.

莫莉德尔：现在是将军和他们合作的关键时期……
Moldir: This is a crucial phase in the admiral's negotiations ...

中尉陷入沉思，她看了看手中的合影，穿着芝诺军装的戴维德笑容灿烂，脸庞却稚气犹存。
She falls into a long silence and drifts her hand toward a photo. David, in his Zeno Corps uniform, grins brightly, looking in every way like a fresh-faced cadet.

指尖压住的边缘隐隐露出一线鲜艳的橘红，中尉的目光聚焦在那里。
Just beneath her fingertip, a sliver of orange peeks out from the photo's edge. Her eyes fix on it.

她把相片翻过去，然后收回到最安全的地方。
She flips the photo over and slips it back into the safest place she can think of.

突如其来的细碎声响吸引了她的注意。
A sharp sound pulls her attention.

莫莉德尔：谁在那里？！
Moldir: Who's there?!

莫莉德尔：一只松鼠？

03  一起意外 / An Encounter

一起意外
An Encounter

莫莉德尔：深夜的军用帐篷中，中尉握紧了自己的武器，与闯入者们对峙着。
In the dead of night, the lieutenant stands inside her tent, weapon in hand, facing off against these bizarre intruders.

莫莉德尔：她并没有因为自己的对手是一只松鼠和三只企鹅就放松警惕。
She can't afford to lower her guard just because her opponents are a squirrel in an odd outfit and three penguins.

嚼嚼松果：听我说，小妞，冷静点儿。别用你手里那玩意儿对着松鼠，这可不太礼貌。
Buddy Fairchild: Whoa now! Easy there, missy. Mind pointin' that popgun o' yers, well, at any other place than at me? Round where I'm from, it ain't seen as good manners.

嚼嚼松果：也不符合动物保护法。
Buddy Fairchild: Also—though I ain't accusin'—the shootin' of these here defenseless penguins would be a vi-o-lation of animal welfare law!

莫莉德尔将枪口略微下移，但没有完全放开对武器的掌握。
Moldir allows the barrel to drop but keeps a firm grip.

莫莉德尔：我能感受到你为放松气氛做出的努力。
Moldir: I appreciate your attempt to defuse the situation.

莫莉德尔：但影响我是否攻击的决定性因素并不包括物种。
Moldir: But I don't take species into consideration when it comes to assessing threats.

莫莉德尔：你有一分钟时间可以表明身份和来意。
Moldir: So you have one minute to explain who you are and what you're doing here.

嚼嚼松果：嘿！这难道还不够明显吗？我们当然没有敌意。

中尉不为所动，视线盯着自己的手表。

企鹅：嘎嘎嘎嘎。
Penguins: Wawk wawk wawk!

嚼嚼松果：绒毛公爵，红色泰坦，杰克老弟。虽然刚从迈阿密回来，但是货真价实的南极土著企鹅。

Moldir tilts her head, trying to make sense of the bizarre situation.

嚼嚼松果：显而易见！所以我们可不是什么可疑的人，赶紧把你的枪放下。

嚼嚼松果：总之，我很抱歉闯入了你的私人领地，但一分钟快到了，你能不能先把那玩意儿放下？

嚼嚼松果：冰层缝隙、积雪空层、火山烟道！每个都畅通无阻，比高速公路还快。
Buddy Fairchild: Cracks in the glaciers, hollow snow pockets, volcano vents ... that sorta thing. Give us a little wiggle room, and we can squeeze in juuust about anywhere.

企鹅：嘎嘎嘎。
Penguins: Wawk wawk!

嚼嚼松果：好吧，抱歉伙计，我的情绪是有点儿激动，毕竟我可不习惯被人用枪指着。
Buddy Fairchild: Yeah, I hear ya, Jake. You'll have to pardon my pal's anxiety, missy. He ain't all that used to havin' a gun trained on him.

嚼嚼松果：而且我的脑袋又开始疼了，真不知道是怎么回事儿！
Buddy Fairchild: Oww, and now this ol' headache is poundin' somethin' fierce. Now where in the heck did that come from?

中尉仔细观察着松鼠和企鹅钻出来的地面，冰雪之下隐约可见幽邃狭小的孔洞证明了它的话。
The lieutenant studies the snow where the creatures had emerged. Faint, narrow holes beneath the surface hint that the squirrel is telling the truth.

她如约收起了武器。
At last, she eases up and stows her weapon.

莫莉德尔：你说的那些“面具怪人”，活动范围离这里很近吗？
Moldir: Those "masked creeps" you mentioned you were evading, do you know if they're operating close to this area?

嚼嚼松果：这可不好说。
Buddy Fairchild: Hard to say.

嚼嚼松果：我们走的都是“捷径”，跟地面上的距离可不太一样。
Buddy Fairchild: What with all the shortcuts we been takin', I couldn't say for certain where we last caught sight of 'em.

企鹅：嘎嘎，嘎嘎嘎。
Penguins: Wawk wawk wawk!

嚼嚼松果：没错。附近有好几个大冰山，从上头翻过去可不容易。但如果你能走火山烟道的话，一下子就能过来，不用半个小时。
Buddy Fairchild: Pree-cisely, Duke. Now, we just came down off them glaciers over yonder, and climbin' 'em was a nightmare and a half. But if you can navigate the volcanic vents, I'd say ya could make it there in under half an hour.

中尉看向一旁打开的军事地图，结合一些简单的计算，快速确定了它所描述的位置。
The lieutenant looks over to the military map beside her. A few quick calculations are enough to pin down the area the squirrel is describing.

莫莉德尔：如果是这里的话……
Moldir: If that's the place you mean ...

嚼嚼松果：嘿，小妞，我得说——
Buddy Fairchild: Hey, missy, I gotta say—

莫莉德尔：中尉。莫莉德尔中尉。
Moldir: Lieutenant. Lieutenant Moldir.

嚼嚼松果：天啊，当然！她有个军衔，显而易见。
Buddy Fairchild: Heavens to Betsy, o' course! Shoulda known a lady o' yer caliber'd be an officer.

09  一位军人 / A Soldier

一位军人
A Soldier

时间定格在四十一小时二十五分钟。
41 hours, 25 minutes.

超出保守黄金救援时限四个小时，但远比极限生还时限从容。
Over four hours past the safe rescue window, but still well within the absolute survival limit.

使用药剂让病床上呻吟着胡言乱语的戴维德安静下来，莫莉德尔和白手套队长默契地退出了临时诊疗室。

队长看着她，流露出了少见的不赞同的神色。

白手套队长：你知道我想说什么，中尉。

莫莉德尔：我相信将军不会放弃我们任何人，队长。

莫莉德尔：我更希望你下次能走正门。
