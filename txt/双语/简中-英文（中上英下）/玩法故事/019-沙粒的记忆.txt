01  37玩法剧情00 / [English text unavailable]

遥远的永恒
Eternity From Long Ago

苏菲亚：37，37！
Sophia: 37, 37!

苏菲亚：快瞧我刚刚打的水漂！
Sophia: Look at the trail I made in the water!

37：嗯，我数过了，最终的结果是数字4。
Thirty-seven: Yes, I was counting, the end result was the number 4.

37走到苏菲亚身边，认真地竖起四根手指。
37 walked over to Sophia, raising four fingers seriously.

37：这说明你的行为非常正确。“4”意味着正义与力量。
Thirty-seven: That means what you did was spot on. "4" signifies justice and strength.

苏菲亚：太好了！
Sophia: Wonderful!

苏菲亚：再来一下，这次我会打出更多水花。
Sophia: Let me try again, I'll try to make more skips this time.

苏菲亚：一，二，三……
Sophia: One, two, three ...

苏菲亚兴致勃勃地捡起石子，却迎来了同伴不解的目光。
Excitedly, Sophia picked up a few more smooth pebbles, only to receive a puzzled look from her companion.

37：你已经得到了正确答案，为什么要再丢一次石头？
Thirty-seven: You got the right answer already. Why throw more?

苏菲亚：……？
Sophia: ... ?

这是一个未曾预想过的问题，她停下动作，困惑地望向身旁的同伴。
The question made Sophia pause, and she matched her puzzled face.

而37显然更为疑惑。
But 37 seemed to be the more confused.

37：数字并不是越大就越好。如果你是想检验投掷技巧，4是最合适的数字。
Thirty-seven: Bigger numbers aren’t always better. If you're testing your throwing technique, 4 is the best possible number.

37：还是说，你想检验的是其他数字？如果你追求热诚和协调，5就会更好！
Thirty-seven: Or do you want to test your passion and coordination? 5 would be better for that!

苏菲亚：不，我并没有想检验什么数字……这，这只是个游戏……
Sophia: No, I’m not trying to test any number ...  It's a game ...

红发的女孩儿陷入了短促的窘境。
The red-haired girl felt caught in the awkwardness of the moment.

她踏上这座岛屿的时间还太短暂，这些数字在运算之外的概念也时常让她费解。
She hadn’t been on the island for very long, and the seriousness with which they took arithmetic still left her feeling alien.

好在她有个擅长于此的岛上伙伴。
Thankfully, she had a companion who was well-versed in these matters.

37：游戏……哦，游戏！
Thirty-seven: A game ...  Oh, a game!

37：你是说像“科塔博斯”那样，在餐后酒会上用双耳杯泼掷葡萄酒液的游戏吗？
Thirty-seven: Like kottabos, where you fling wine dregs from two-handled cups at dinner parties?

苏菲亚：……我想，我想是的？这听起来和打水漂有些相像……
Sophia: ... I guess so. I mean it almost sounds like skipping stones ...

女孩小心地表示着赞同。她并不知道这游戏的具体所指，只是不想让话题僵住。
She had agreed without really understanding the specifics, but not wanting the conversation to stall.

——也不让她的玩伴失望。
... or to disappoint her new friend.

37：噢……好吧。
Thirty-seven: Ah ... alright.

37：我完全不明白这些有什么意义。
Thirty-seven: I don't get the point of all this.

苏菲亚：哎……？
Sophia: Huh ... ?

37耷拉下了眉毛——她现在的表情可称大失所望。
37’s brow furrowed—as if let down by her companion.

37：投掷葡萄酒液或是鹅卵石片，击中浮碟或是水面，让碟子沉下，又或者让石片在水面上弹射数下——
Thirty-seven: Throwing wine or pebbles, hitting a target, sinking a plate, or skipping stones on water—

37：这种行为究竟有什么意义呢？人们到底想从中得到什么呢？
Thirty-seven: What's the point of these actions? What are we trying to achieve?

37：如果是为了在数字上分出高低，我能明白。就像210，他总是致力于成为科塔博斯的第一得分手。
Thirty-seven: If it's about being the highest-ranked, I get it. Like 210, he's always trying to get the top score in kottabos.

37：小一点的那个6也是，他总是在尽可能地追逐数列的末端。
Thirty-seven: So is 6. He's always chasing the end of each series.

37：可有时候人们好像没有任何目的，只是在重复这一行为。
Thirty-seven: But sometimes it seems people do things without any purpose at all.

苏菲亚：唔，他们应该只是在玩吧？
Sophia: Well, they're just playing.

苏菲亚：因为游戏就是这样，只是和朋友们待在一起，单纯地度过一段愉快的时间……并没有什么特定的目的，我想。
Sophia: That's what most games are about—spending time with friends and having a fun time ... without any greater purpose, I guess.

37：……玩？
Thirty-seven: ... Fun?

37：也就是说，他们没有任何超越的目的吗？
Thirty-seven: You mean ... these things don't have any transcendent purpose?

37：他们没有提升自己的序列，也没有追求到什么知识；没有净化自己的灵魂，更没有醉心于真理的求验。
Thirty-seven: They don't climb up the hierarchy, seek knowledge, purify their souls, or seek out the truth?

37：只是在感官世界里毫无目的地耽误了一段时间……是这样吗？
Thirty-seven: So all they're doing is idling in the sensory world.

女孩的语气变得冷淡而充满疑惑，这是苏菲亚始料未及的。
The young girl's tone grew much more distant, even cynical, surprising Sophia.

苏菲亚：不，我想，我想也不全是……
Sophia: No, I think ... well, it's not entirely like that ...

苏菲亚：抱歉，37，我刚来这里，还有很多事不明白，如果我说错了——
Sophia: Sorry, 37 ...  I'm new here, and there's a lot I don't understand. If I'm wrong—

蓝发女孩只是托住下巴，一门心思地琢磨着。
The blue-haired girl was resting her chin in her hands,she had already drifted away in thought.

37：唔，抱歉，你刚刚说什么？
Thirty-seven: Oh, sorry, what were you saying?

37：没关系，这都是些琐事！我们应该有更重要的事要做。
Thirty-seven: Never mind these trivial matters! We have more important things to do!

37：妈妈让我来带你熟悉岛屿。那么，你想在沙滩上验证的数字就是4了吗？
Thirty-seven: Mother asked me to familiarize you with the island. So, is 4 the number you wanted to validate on the beach?

苏菲亚：……
Sophia: ...

苏菲亚没来得及完成道歉，因为37已经迅速地将之抛到脑后。
Sophia tried to finish her apology, but 37 dismissed it with a wave, she was already caught in another train of thought.

这样的事以后还会反复发生多次——直到她终于习惯为止。
This kind of thing would happen several times until she got used to it.

但现在，幼鸟炽热的心还未被海风与礁石打磨，因此她还会再三发问。
But back then, her still-fledgling heart was unpolished by the sea breeze and sand, and she was full of questions.

苏菲亚：37，我想我还是不太明白……你们“阿派朗”对数字的信仰。
Sophia: 37, I still don't quite understand ... this"Apeiron" belief in numbers.

苏菲亚：在来到岛上前，我也经常和数字打交道，但那只是学习应用一些基本的运算技巧。
Sophia: Before coming here, I dealt with numbers in my studies, but it was just arithmetic.

苏菲亚：在解出题目时，父亲会奖励我一颗糖，我把它们攒在罐子里卖掉，好在去医院前给母亲买一束花……
Sophia: When I solved problems, my dad would reward me with a candy, and I’d save them in a jar to buy flowers for Mom before going to the hospital ...

37：……？
Thirty-seven: ... ?

苏菲亚意识到自己说得太多了。这绝非适宜的场合，这样的谈话也不适用于她的这位新朋友。
Sophia realized she was saying too much. It wasn’t the right context, nor did it concern her new friend.

苏菲亚：……总之，我对数字的印象还停留在那些课本上的练习题。它们远没有这些抽象的，超验的，信仰上的意义。
Sophia: ... Anyway, my impression of numbers is all caught up in those textbook exercises. Far removed from these abstract, transcendent meanings.

苏菲亚：为什么你们会相信它们是永恒的呢？
Sophia: Like I don't understand, why you believe that numbers are eternal?

37：唔？因为它们就是永恒的啊！
Thirty-seven: Because they are eternal!

37那些令人费解的话语越发增多，令苏菲亚几乎后悔问出这个可称冒犯的问题。
37’s answer, more a declaration, was yet more puzzling, making Sophia regret asking at all, and risking offense.

好在，质数是机灵的——从来都是。
Thankfully, prime numbers like 37 are nimble-minded—at least that is what she would say.

37：我不知道该如何回答你……但我想到一个非常合适的例子！
Thirty-seven: I don't know how to explain it with words ...  But I have the perfect example!

37：是欧几里得曾做出的，关于存在无穷多个质数的证明。
Thirty-seven: It was Euclid who once proved that there are an infinite number of prime numbers.

苏菲亚：质数……那是，你的数字？
Sophia: Prime numbers ...  Like how 37 is a prime number?

37：是的，13是质数，23也是质数，37也是质数，337也是质数！它们的因数只有1和它们自己，无法被其它自然数整除。
Thirty-seven: Yes, 13 is prime, 23 is prime, 37 is prime, and 337 is also prime! Their factors are only 1 and themselves. They cannot be divided by any other natural number.

37：质数可以无限地罗列下去。可是，我们怎么证明这一点呢？
Thirty-seven: Prime numbers can go on infinitely. But how can we prove this?

她兴致勃勃地在沙滩上写下一串数列，并在末端画上一个圆。
37 enthusiastically wrote a series of numbers in the sand, finishing with a circle at the end.

37：欧几里得的方法是，先设想存在一个最大的质数——就是这个○。
Thirty-seven: Euclid's method is to imagine the existence of the largest prime number—this is ○.

37：那么我们就得到了完整的质数序列：从最小的2开始，一直写到最大的○。
Thirty-seven: So, we make a complete series of prime numbers: starting from the smallest 2, all the way to the largest ○.

37：在这基础上，我们设想存在一个更大的数，△。
Thirty-seven: Based on this understanding, let's imagine there's a larger number, △.

37：△代表所有序列里的质数相乘，再加上1。
Thirty-seven: △ represents the multiplication of all prime numbers in the queue, plus 1.

37：很明显，它不能被所有列举出来的质数整除，用2、3、5……○里的任何一个数去除，都会余1。
Thirty-seven: Clearly, it cannot be divided by any of the listed prime numbers. The numbers 2, 3, 5 ...  or any number represented by ○ will result in a remainder of 1.

37：如果△不是一个质数，那么根据定义，它理应被一个比○更大的质数整除。
Thirty-seven: If △ is not a prime number, it should be divisible by a prime number larger than ○.

37：如果△是一个质数，那它自身就是一个比○更大的质数。
Thirty-seven: If △ is a prime number, then naturally it's larger than ○.

37：这二者都违反○是最大质数的假设，矛盾！
Thirty-seven: Both scenarios contradict the assumption that ○ is the largest prime number.

37：所以，结论就是——不存在这样一个最大的质数○。质数是无限的。
Thirty-seven: Therefore, the conclusion is—there's no such thing as the largest prime number ○. Prime numbers are infinite.

37结束了书写，她拍拍手掌，抖落沙粒，从沙滩上站起来。
37 finished writing, clapped her hands, shook off the sand, and stood up beaming.

苏菲亚：我……在学校听过这个定理。可是，我还是不明白……
Sophia: I ...  heard about this theorem at school. But I still don't understand what it means, really ...

她仍不明白这样具体的数学定理中存在什么超验的含义。
She couldn't grasp the transcendental meaning behind such specific mathematical theorems.

对她而言，它们严格、正确，就像讲台上粉笔的硫酸钙颗粒，单调，质地鲜明，难溶于水。
To her, they were strict, correct, but nothing more than chalk particles on a blackboard, monochromatic, sharply defined, and barely soluble in water.

——也仅仅如此。
—And that was all.

37：……妈妈告诉我，欧几里得曾在两千多年前写下了这个证明。
Thirty-seven: ... Mother told me Euclid wrote down this proof over two thousand years ago.

37：写下这个证明的人会死亡，记录这个证明的草纸会被腐蚀，读到这个证明的我们也会消失不见。
Thirty-seven: She said, "The person who wrote this proof will die, the paper that recorded this proof will decompose, and we, who read this proof, will also one day disappear."

37：一切都会像海边的砂砾一样被冲刷殆尽。
Thirty-seven: Everything will be washed away like sand on the seashore.

37：可是这个证明却依然正确、优美、精巧，令每一个后来者驻足欣赏。
Thirty-seven: But this proof will endure, always correct, beautiful, and intricate, to be admired by every future generation.

37：——我想，这就是永恒。
Thirty-seven: —I suppose that's eternal.

苏菲亚：……！
Sophia: ... !

37：岛上的每个人都告诉我，我是特殊的。
Thirty-seven: Everyone on the island tells me I'm special.

37：因为质数就像是算术大厦里的基石，一切整数都是由质数相乘得来的。
Thirty-seven: Prime numbers are like the foundation stones of arithmetic. All integers are derived from prime numbers.

37：但我只是无穷多个质数的其中之一。永远有更多、更新的数字，我并不特殊。
Thirty-seven: But I'm one of a set of infinite prime numbers. There will always be more, newer numbers; so I'm not truly special.

37：可即使我仅是37，沙滩上可数的37个点，我也依然被包含在了这个无穷的序列里。
Thirty-seven: But even if I am just 37 ...  37 grains of sand on the beach ...  I'm still encompassed within this infinite queue.

37：——而这样的数列还有无穷个！整数的序列，分数的序列，无理数的序列……它们都是无穷的！
Thirty-seven: —And there are an infinite number of such sequences! Sequences of integers, sequences of fractions, sequences of irrational numbers ...  Each and every one infinite!

37转过头来，现在她的眼睛和阳光下的砂砾一样闪闪发光了。
37 turned around, and now her eyes sparkled with the grains of sand in the sunlight.

37：我们每个人都是无穷的一部分，我为此感到骄傲。
Thirty-seven: We're all a part of infinity, and I take pride in that.

37：——我想，苏菲亚，这就是无限。
Thirty-seven: —I think, Sophia, that is what eternity is.

日光映照之下，砂砾宛如黄金。与37对视时，她感到一阵炫目与刺痛。
Under the bright sunlight, the grains of sand of the beach gleamed like gold. As she locked eyes with 37, it was blinding yet poignant.

37：别闭上眼睛，教典说：“面对任何美丽的事物都不应回避。”
Thirty-seven: "Don't close your eyes", as the scripture says, "Do not shy away from anything beautiful."

苏菲亚：……啊……
Sophia: Oh ...

苏菲亚：很久以后，我仍会想起37告诉我的这个定理。
Sophia: As time passed, I often remembered the theorem 37 told me.

苏菲亚：我从未真正领略过她的世界，那是一个奇妙而抽象的数学王国，所有数字都是她的朋友。
Sophia: I never quite understood her world— this fascinating, abstract kingdom of mathematics where every number is her friend.

苏菲亚：可那时在她的眼里，我的确短暂见到了“永恒”那美丽的光辉。
Sophia: But in that moment, I caught a fleeting glimpse of the beautiful brilliance of "eternity" in her eyes.

苏菲亚：如此迷人……如此令人向往和歆羡。
Sophia: It was enchanting ...  inspiring and admirable.

苏菲亚：它成为了我吸纳学派信仰的火种。
Sophia: It became the seed that led me to embrace the school.

苏菲亚：沙滩也的确在往后的日子变得不同。
Sophia: The beach changed in the days that followed.

苏菲亚：船只驶近岸边，“流溢”在眼前发生，欢呼变为悲叹和啜泣。
Sophia: The ship approached the shore, the "Emanation" unfolded before our eyes, and cheers turned into sorrowful cries.

苏菲亚：最终，只留下了这些冷冰冰的几何体。
Sophia: Eventually, all that remained of what we lost were cold, lifeless geometric shapes.

苏菲亚：但37从未因此动摇。
Sophia: Yet, through it all 37 stood unwavering.

苏菲亚：她带着惊人的专注投入她母亲留下的研究，不曾露出任何悲伤和怯意。
Sophia: She poured incredible focus into her mother's research, never displaying any sadness or hesitation.

苏菲亚：就像她那天在沙滩上提及的证明一样，永恒，坚决，不被时光冲刷。
Sophia: Just like the theorem she spoke of that day on the beach—unchanging, resolute, immune to the passage of time.

苏菲亚：——为何37可以这样独特，从不被外界改变？
Sophia: —How could 37 stay so uniquely unaffected by the world around her?

苏菲亚：……
Sophia: ...

苏菲亚：我无法解答出这个问题，只能为她提供微不足道的帮助。
Sophia: I couldn't find an answer to that question; all I could do was offer her my help, meager as it was.

岛屿 · 研究室
Laboratory, Island

爬满凌乱笔迹的草稿纸，堆放在各处的厚重书籍，魔精掉落的毛发……
Scattered scribbled notes, piles of thick books, and stray strands of critter hair ...

仿佛有一群阿其翁曾光顾此地，或是这间研究室已在洋流中无休止地打转了数百年。
It looked as if a flock of Abraxas had visited, or the lab had been caught in a whirlwind for centuries on end.

任谁见到这副景象，都会由衷地感到疲惫。
Anyone would have been exhausted by such a sight.

而研究室的主人正趴在桌前，安心地沉入美梦。
And amidst the panoply the lab director herself lay sprawled across a table, lost in her own head.

苏菲亚：唉，37。你就从没想过试着自己打扫房间吗？
Sophia: Umm ... 37 ...  Have you considered tidying up this room?

她放轻动作，跨过地面的书本小山，逐步靠近那张长桌。
She moved nimbly, stepping over scattered books, as she approached the long table.

放在37手边的笔记本看上去格外眼熟。
The notebook which lay beside 37 looked oddly familiar.

苏菲亚：这是……
Sophia: Is this ...

曾经的使用者十分珍惜这本笔记，其中的图画与注解工整清晰。
Someone had treasured this notebook, adorning it with illustrations and notes.
