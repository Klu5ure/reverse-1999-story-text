01  燃火处 / Ignition

和平乌鲁：黑暗像一袭柔软的床褥，它将我拖入它之中，将我包裹，向我迫近。
Ulu: The darkness is like a soft mattress. Sinking under me, folds enveloping me, drawing me ever deeper.

和平乌鲁：暗处唯有持续的、有节奏的响动陪伴我，像是鼓点，又如同红土地上的欢呼。
Ulu: In the darkness, I find myself accompanied by unceasing, rhythmic sounds, like the beating of drums and wild cheers, echoing a time when I blazed over red earth.

和平乌鲁：于是我尝试寻找那阵隐约的声响。它搏动着，如此熟悉与亲切，令我的火焰随之摇曳。
Ulu: And so I try to find it, that faint sound. It beats with a familiarity and intimacy that entices my flames to sway along with it.

和平乌鲁：而后，远处的光亮扑入视野。我重新遇见了她。
Ulu: And then, a distant light flickers into view. There, I meet her once more.

和平乌鲁：在追逐那光亮的途中，我能瞧见她正经历的一切。
Ulu: As I follow that light, I see all she experiences.

和平乌鲁：她还尚是一粒火星儿，光芒微弱，却满心好奇。
Ulu: She is only a spark, her glow faint, but burgeoning with curiosity.

和平乌鲁：一天，又一天。以周围的事物为燃料，所见，所触碰，所听闻的一切都使她燃烧得更旺。
Ulu: Day in, day out. She consumes more fuel—everything she sees, touches, and hears makes her burn brighter.

和平乌鲁：你还好吗？你能听见我吗？我们还能再聊起上次未讲完的话儿吗？
Ulu: How are you? Can you hear me? Shall we continue our unfinished conversation?

和平乌鲁：没有回音……
我仍然呼唤着——向我最最亲密的旧朋友。
Ulu: No response...
Still, I call out, I must call out... to my dearest friend.

卧室
Bedroom

可燃点：……咦？好像有谁叫我？

理所当然，她一无所获。

Spathodea: *sigh* ...

She refocused her energy back to the meticulously collected items before her.

可燃点：再瞧瞧这张……唔，和其他地方记载的乌卢鲁运动会项目不太一样，但……

——例如此刻。

小女孩儿三两步跑到门前，转动把手。

可燃点母亲：正好有东西要给你，我就一块带过来了，在这儿。

可燃点母亲：她知道你很宝贝这些，特地用塑胶袋装好放在抽屉的最上层，你瞧，封面上的涂鸦一点儿都没被磨掉。

Spathodea: I just... I just keep thinking about the photos, and...

可燃点母亲：没关系，我们慢慢说说。
Spathodea’s Mother: It's okay, we’ve got all the time in the world.

母亲的手掌绕过小女孩儿的肩膀，将她搂进怀里，头碰着头在床沿坐下。

Spathodea: It was when... Ms. Jones was talking to us about after-school sports and asking what our favorite events were... I remembered the Uluru Games.

可燃点：然后我抽出册子，打算找些项目出来回答。
Spathodea: I took out my album to find some events, so I’d have a good answer.

可燃点：我可以说飞跃树梢，水怪障碍赛……它们都很有趣，但琼斯女士却说，我们只能选择一个“最”喜欢的项目……
Spathodea: I could’ve said the branch vault, the water beast steeplechase... They’re all so fun, but Ms. Jones said we could only choose our single most favorite event...

可燃点母亲：这对你来说不是个难题，孩子。你最喜欢的运动，你总是把它挂在嘴边……
Spathodea’s Mother: That shouldn't have been a problem for you, my little love. You’re always talking about your favorite sport...

可燃点：是的！是的，妈咪！
Spathodea: Yeah! That’s right, Mummy!

可燃点：那是和传说中的石怪有关的——马林吉的收获！
Spathodea: It's the one with the stone monster from the stories—Malingee's Harvest!

她用力点了两下头，辫子梢像两簇小火苗般在身后跃动。

但愉快的火焰只扑闪了一小会，就重又垂下头去。
But these happy flames flicker for only an instant before she lowers her head again.

可燃点：我的脑子里挤满了玩马林吉的画面，它们……就像真的一样，像是……我在梦里看到的那些一样……
Spathodea: My head got so filled with thoughts of playing with Malingee. It was like... like it was all real... Like... the things I see in my dreams...

可燃点：运动场的红土踩起来坚实、柔韧，我在空气里闻得见木头燃烧的味道……
Spathodea: The red earth of the sports field felt so solid and spongy when I stepped on it... and I could smell the wood burning in the air...

可燃点：人群吵嚷着围住被点燃的火堆，火烧得又旺盛，又美丽，就像一只活泼的动物……我伸手想要摸摸它的皮毛……
Spathodea: The crowd gathered around the fire—burning wildly and beautifully, like a wild animal... I wanted to reach out to touch its fur...

可燃点：……
Spathodea: ...

可燃点：……然后琼斯女士就握住了我的手。
Spathodea: ...Then Ms. Jones took my hand.

Spathodea opens her eyes, returning from her memories with a pang of regret, her voice timid as a whimpering puppy.

可燃点：不过，我确实不应该在她的课堂上开小差……
Spathodea: But, I know... I really shouldn't have dozed off in her class...

可燃点：可是这些事……他们说我都是在做白日梦，说……我只是因为太喜欢乌卢鲁运动会，而变成呆瓜了……

可燃点母亲：你是一个好孩子，我的小饼干。
Spathodea’s Mother: You’re a good girl, Cookie.

母亲轻声叹息，在怀抱中加上了细微的摇动。

可燃点母亲：你的喜好与你的爱从不是错。无论你喜欢的是绿皮鳄鱼，还是一场消失在历史中的运动会，那都是你宝贵的爱。
Spathodea’s Mother: There’s nothing wrong with the things you like and love. Whether it's green crocodiles or even a long gone sports event, they’re all your precious treasures.

可燃点母亲：说不定呢，说不定未来的某天，你所爱的乌卢鲁运动会就会回来呢？就像是它忽然消失时一样——忽如其来地再次出现。

和平乌鲁：比草原还要辽远，比红土地更加宽阔，足够她尽情奔跑。
Ulu: Even more distant than the grassland, more vast than the fields of red earth—vast enough for her to run freely.

和平乌鲁：但在途中我又一度与她失散。
Ulu: But along the way, I lost touch with her once again.

和平乌鲁：我短暂地醒来，见到她与家人或朋友聊天，欢笑。
Ulu: I would awaken briefly to see her talking and laughing with her family or friends.

和平乌鲁：见到她在训练时咬紧牙关挥出的拳头。
Ulu: To see her clenching her teeth and swinging her fists during training.

和平乌鲁：以及她失落的、沮丧的眼泪，浇在火焰之上，腾起一阵白色的烟。
Ulu: And I saw her lost and downhearted, tears pouring over flames and fizzling them into a puff of white smoke.

和平乌鲁：我多庆幸还能再见到她！
Ulu: I’m so lucky to see you again!

和平乌鲁：在乌卢鲁运动会的赛场上，看见她举起火把，颈项上是她期待的奖牌。
Ulu: On the field of the Uluru Games, I saw her holding a torch with the medal, the very one she had so long looked forward to, hanging from her neck.

和平乌鲁：我停下漫长的奔跑，回到她的身边。
Ulu: I stopped my endless running and returned to her.

和平乌鲁：她跌跌撞撞地抬起步子，朝我奔来。
Ulu: And she staggered onward, picking up her pace—running toward me.

和平乌鲁：——就像火扑入另一团火。
Ulu: Like two flames merging into one.

岩石与红土之间重又回荡着激动的呼喊。
Excited cries echo between the rocks and the red earth.

神秘学家踏足此地，在马吉林的收获中点燃炬火。
This is where arcanists tread—where they light their torches for Malingee's Harvest.

人类亦在此停留，举起球拍，让小球弹跳着飞往高空。
Mankind also ventures here, raising rackets and bouncing balls into the air.

和平乌鲁：瞧，你与我又一次归为了两朵独立的火星儿。
Ulu: Look. You and I are independent sparks, standing apart from each other again.

可燃点：我明白你要讲的话，和平乌鲁。
Spathodea: I know what you’re trying to say, Ulu.

可燃点：我们可以朝着同样的方向并肩行走，两朵火焰能照亮的地方比一朵要多。
Spathodea: But we can head to the same destination. Two is always better than one.

可燃点：——这一点和过去一样。
Spathodea: It’s the same as it was in the past.

可燃点：来吧。
Spathodea: Come on.

女孩儿摘下拳套，摊开手掌。
The girl takes off her gloves and spreads her palm wide.

和平乌鲁：好吧，但我得提醒几句，我现在可是老人家，难免讲话啰嗦。
Ulu: Okay, but don’t forget—I'm old now, so don't blame me if I nag at you.

和平乌鲁：可别再摔倒了，绕着草丛走，注意脚下……
Ulu: Just don't fall again—walk carefully around the grass and watch your feet...

和平乌鲁：我想你也知道的……
Ulu: I think you already know...

火焰跃到掌心之中，如一只猫儿轻巧落地。
The flame leaps into the palm of her hand and lands as lightly as a cat.

她包裹着她的五指与手腕，大小正正合适，就像她最珍惜的拳击手套。
She wraps around her five fingers, spreading up to her wrists. It’s a perfect fit, well-worn and familiar like her most cherished boxing gloves.

——向前迈步。
Take another step forward.

踏入乌卢鲁运动会的场地，回到来处，回到火中。
Step into the grounds of the Uluru Games. Go back to where you came from. Back to the fire...

回到熟悉的，应在的地方。
Return to that familiar place. The place you belong.
