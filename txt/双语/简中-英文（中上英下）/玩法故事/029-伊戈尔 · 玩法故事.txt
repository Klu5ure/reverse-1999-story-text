01  【3.3】伊戈尔玩法剧情01 / [English text unavailable]

用马蹄来翻耕
Furrowed by Hooves

伊戈尔：不要回头。
Igor: Don't look back.

伊戈尔：同志们，向前跑，不要回头。
Igor: Keep running. Don't look back.

士兵，你的任务就是站在这里，如果我们中有人转身逃跑……
Soldier, you're to hold position here. 
And if you see any of our own turn to run ...

你就用这把枪，直接打死他，就像打野兔子一样。
Take this rifle and shoot. Don't think. 
Just shoot, like hunting a hare.

伊戈尔：是的，长官。
Igor: Yes, sir.

我相信我们的队伍，没有一个战士会退缩。
I believe in our men. Not a single soldier will step back.

伊戈尔：没有一个战士会退缩。
Igor: Not a single soldier will step back.

他的手一直很稳，不管是对着野兔、大雁、敌军，还是自己的战友。
His hands were always steady. It didn't matter if his sights were on a hare, a wild goose, the enemy, or his own comrades.

但雪太大了，雪花落在了枪管上，压得他的手微微颤抖起来。
But the snow comes down in thick blankets. The icy flakes melt over his hands. He stifles a shiver.

伊戈尔，别担心，我们没有人会往回跑。
Do not worry, Igor. They won't turn back.

没有人跑回来。
No one turned back.

士兵把食指稍微从扳机上移开，以防金属黏住手指——那里冻得像一块冰。
The soldier eases his finger off the trigger, wary of his skin sticking to the frozen metal.

伊戈尔：……！
Igor: ...!

伊戈尔再次按住那块冰——瞄准镜里出现了一个人影。
Igor's finger twitches back to the cold metal as a figure enters his scope.

草原树林
Forested Steppe

伊戈尔早就看见了那个士兵。
He had already seen that soldier.

士兵和战马都沉默地藏在树林的阴影里，但月光还是照亮了军帽上的徽章。
That soldier had cloaked himself and his horse in the forest shadow, yet the moonlight reveals the glint of a badge on his cap.

伊戈尔拍了拍自己的战马，红马优雅地踏了几步，低下头喝水。
Igor gives his own mount a firm pat. The roan red warhorse dips toward the water's edge.

伊戈尔：放心，这条小河的水足够两匹马喝，士兵。
Igor: At ease, soldier. This river's got enough for two horses.

士兵：是，将军！
Soldier: Yes, sir!

士兵迈着正步将马牵到小河旁，在伊戈尔面前站定，敬了个军礼。
The soldier leads his horse to the river, halts before Igor, and salutes.

士兵：报道！骑兵团一营……
Soldier: Reporting! First Battalion, Cavalry Regiment.

伊戈尔：格里高利·安德烈耶维奇，你是骑兵团的侦察兵，我知道。
Igor: Grigory Andreyevich. Cavalry Regiment Scout. I know.

士兵的脸涨红了，这让他并不端正的高鼻子看起来更歪了一点。
A flush creeps up the soldier's face, making his crooked, high-bridged nose seem to curve like a saber.

格里高利：我不知道您记得我的名字。
Grigory: I didn't expect you'd know my name.

伊戈尔：我记得你们每个人的名字。我还知道，你父亲是顿河的哥萨克。
Igor: I remember. Your father is a Cossack from the Don.

战马：咴——咴——
War Horse: *neigh*

一匹漂亮的白马，刷得干干净净，马鞍上没有一点磨损，四角银饰闪闪发亮。
It's a beautiful white horse, groomed to perfection. The saddle shows no wear, silver trinkets glistening at the corners.

三色编织的鞍袋装得鼓鼓囊囊。像要去过节，或者远行。
His colorful saddlebags seem more fit for celebration than the long road ahead.

他们确实要远行——别列格勒之战后，伊戈尔军休整了几日，就要拔营。
And a long road it will be. After their brief rest following the Battle of Bereggrad, Igor's army must soon break camp.

但拔营的前夜，牵着战马出行的士兵……这不符合任何一条军规。
But on the eve of departure, a soldier is leading his horse out. It goes against protocol.

格里高利：将军，我知道士兵不该擅自离营，但是……
Grigory: Sir, I know I shouldn't leave camp alone, but ...

伊戈尔：我只是来这条小河饮马，中士。
Igor: I only came to water my horse, soldier.

他打断了士兵接下来的话。
He cuts the soldier off.

伊戈尔：看看这条河，看看这片草原。
Igor: Take a good look at this river and the steppe.

金色的草原即将入冬，空气里是腐烂的橡树皮辛辣的气味。
Winter is closing in, and the air is thick with the decay of leaves and oak bark.

羽茅草挺直身子，闪着幽微的银光，这银光又倏忽向下伏去，勾勒出风的形状。
Feather grass stands tall in the steppes beyond the woody copse, glimmering with a soft silver light that suddenly bows low, tracing the wind's path.

伊戈尔：你们可能已经不记得它的样子了，但你父亲一定记得。
Igor: You may have forgotten, but your father wouldn't.

伊戈尔：这里也是我的故乡，在离开之前，我想回来看看。
Igor: This was my home once. I intend to see it for myself again before we leave.

河水像一块深绿的翡翠，它不慌不忙地流着，知道自己迟早会到达大海。
Steady and patient, the river flows like deep green jade toward the distant Azov Sea.

伊戈尔：像你们这个年纪的哥萨克，恐怕都没有在顿河里抓过鱼。
Igor: A young Cossack like you probably wouldn't remember fishing here.

格里高利：我记得的，将军。
Grigory: I remember, sir.

士兵突然说。他的喉咙变得不那么紧了，声音里自然地带上一丝稚气。
The soldier speaks up. His tone is wistful, almost childlike, as if he's speaking to a brother, not his commanding officer.

格里高利：我记得小时候父亲带我来顿河钓鱼，在月亮很圆的晚上。
Grigory: My father would take me fishing when I was small, always on nights with a full moon.

格里高利：我太小了，还不能划船，父亲也不划，就等河水把我们的船带到顿河中央。
Grigory: I was too young to row, but Father didn't row much either. We just let the current take our boat wherever it willed.

格里高利：然后父亲往水里撒一把黑麦粒，放下鱼线。月亮升到头顶上的时候，钓竿头突然往下弯……
Grigory: Father would toss a handful of rye into the water and cast his line out, then we'd sit under the moonlight for the rod to dip.

格里高利：那时候河里的鱼力气大得像公牛，父亲在水里把它遛乏了，才抓上来。
Grigory: The fish back then seemed to be as strong as bulls. We'd have to tire them out before bringing them in.

格里高利：是一条金色的鲤鱼，还张着嘴喘气哩！
Grigory: I remember one catch, a good-sized catfish, with its puffing lips gasping as we pulled it out.

格里高利：父亲把鲤鱼扔到篓子里让我背回去，说他明天要上战场了。
Grigory: He tossed it into my basket to carry home and said he'd be marching to the front tomorrow.

河水泛起一圈涟漪，鱼儿听到说话声，不敢露出自己闪光的鳞片。
A ripple spreads on the water. Hearing their voices, the fish hide their shining scales.

伊戈尔：今天在顿河抓鱼，明天就上战场，这就是哥萨克，孩子。
Igor: Fish in the Don today, fight in the battlefield tomorrow. This is how we Cossacks live, child.

伊戈尔的目光没有在河上停留太久，他大步向白色的战马走去。
Igor's eyes don't linger on the water. Instead, he strides up to the white warhorse.

白马发出一声活泼的嘶鸣，好奇地朝红色战马走了几步，又折返回来。
The horse lets out a spirited whinny, making a playful trot toward Igor's own horse, then turns back.

伊戈尔：你父亲不仅教会了你钓鱼，还教会了你养马。这是一匹漂亮的儿马。
Igor: Your father taught you more than fishing, I see. This is a fine steed.

格里高利：嗯，我自己刷马，给它喂燕麦和豆饼，这孩子不喜欢黑麦。
Grigory: I groom him daily and feed him oats and soymeal that I grind myself. He hates rye.

伊戈尔：是一匹好战马。是要去冲锋的，不是去拉大炮的。
Igor: A true warhorse. Bred for the charge, not for hauling guns.

伊戈尔拉住马的辔头，白马晃了晃脑袋，拿头去蹭将军的手臂。
Igor takes the bridle. The white horse shakes its head and nudges his arm.

伊戈尔：嘿……好孩子。
Igor: Hey. Good boy.

伊戈尔看着白马黑亮又湿润的眼睛。
Igor meets the horse's black, glassy eyes.

伊戈尔：但它还没上过战场，害怕炮声吗？
Igor: He hasn't seen war yet. Do you think he can handle the battlefield?

格里高利：将军，您怎么看出它没上过战场？
Grigory: Sir, how can you tell he hasn't fought?

伊戈尔：和人一样。上过战场的人，和没上过战场的人，眼神是不一样的。
Igor: There's a look in his eyes. Horses and men are alike in that way.

伊戈尔：它让我想起以前的佩伦。
Igor: He reminds me of the stories of Perun.

格里高利：佩伦？
Grigory: Perun?

伊戈尔吹了声口哨，高大的红马甩了甩头，来到他身边。
Igor whistles. The tall, red horse tosses its mane and comes to him.

伊戈尔：我战友起的名字，因为它跑起来势如雷霆。但这孩子以前连炮声都害怕。
Igor: Perun, the thunder god. A comrade named him that, because he runs like thunder. But even Perun once feared the sound of gunfire.

格里高利：是吗？
Grigory: Did he?

格里高利：你现在看起来真威风，伙计。
Grigory: Not so much anymore, eh, boy?

格里高利用艳羡的目光看着高大的战马。
Grigory gazes up at the tall warhorse with clear admiration.

格里高利：我在马厩里见过它很多次，您一直带着它。
Grigory: I've seen him often in the stables. You always keep him close.

士兵轻轻拍了拍佩伦的脖子，战马没有躲开。
He pats Perun's neck. The horse doesn't shy away.

格里高利：它几岁了？
Grigory: How old is he?

伊戈尔：快二十岁，要退役的年纪了。
Igor: Nearing 20. Almost time to retire.

格里高利：但它很漂亮。它什么时候开始上的战场？
Grigory: But he's splendid. When did he first see war?

伊戈尔：已经是十五年前的事了。
Igor: That was 15 years ago.

十五年前，在伊戈尔成为现在的伊戈尔将军之前。
Fifteen years ago, before Igor became Admiral Igor.

在“暴雨”之前。
Before the "Storm."

草原原野
Field, Steppe

马儿喝饱了水，北斗七星矮矮地卧在地平线上。
The horses have finished drinking. The Big Dipper hangs just above the horizon.

伊戈尔跨上战马。他打量着整装待发的白马，和握着缰绳的士兵。
Igor mounts. He looks over the ready white horse and the soldier holding its reins.

伊戈尔：中士，咱们是同路吗？
Igor: Are we going the same way?

格里高利将军帽压下来一点，帽檐的阴影遮住了他的眼睛。
Grigory pulls his cap lower. The brim shadows his eyes.

格里高利：或许是的，将军。
Grigory: Perhaps we are, sir.

02  【3.3】伊戈尔玩法剧情02 / [English text unavailable]

可爱的牺牲
Righteous Sacrifices

阿尔蒙塔尔·指挥部
Command Post, Al Muntar

1995年，阿尔蒙塔尔，芝诺第九师临时指挥部的院子里坐着一圈军官。
1995, Al Muntar. Zeno's 9th Division. Officers sit in a ring in the yard of the temporary command post.

没有椅子，军官们坐在草垛上或者地上，他们没法讲究这些。
军大衣上凝着干枯的血和灰尘，比地更脏。
There are no chairs. They sit on straw or hard earth, past concern for comfort. Their coats are stiff with dried blood and dirt.

军官Ⅰ：你没看见我把那个炮兵从装甲车里拖出来的时候，他的眼睛还在朝外冒石油……
Officer I: You didn't see what I saw. That gunner I pulled out of the car—his eyes were leaking oil.

军官Ⅱ：真不知道我们在打什么东西，防空系统瘫痪了，侦察机也派不出去。
Officer II: Who knows what we're fighting? Air defense is dead. Can't get a scout plane up.

军官Ⅲ：不知道的以为回到了五十年前，还在打卫国战争呢。
Officer III: It seems almost like how things were in the Great Patriotic War.

军官Ⅱ：五十年前打的是什么？德国人的坦克，800毫米的大炮，三个人大的棕熊……
Officer II: And what did we fight then? German tanks, 800 mm guns, and bears the size of three men.

军官Ⅰ：总好过对付这些怪物。
Officer I: Better than dealing with these monsters.

伊戈尔：阿尔谢尼，你那边什么情况？
Igor: Arseny, what's the situation?

阿尔谢尼：我们跑得快，减员不到百分之三十。
Arseny: We fell back in good order. We minimized casualties to below expectations. Less than thirty percent.

阿尔谢尼：坦克全留下了，一开出去就动不了，装甲兵跑回来一半。
Arseny: But the armored corps is gone. The tanks are all stopped dead out there. Only half the crews made it back.

阿尔谢尼：最可怕的不是对方的装甲水平比我们高……他们用来压制我方火力的不是任何科技，是神秘学术式。
Arseny: They've outclassed our armor entirely. But what's pinning us down isn't their tech. It's their arcane skills.

伊戈尔：我知道。
Igor: I know.

伊戈尔：先把剩下的装甲兵编入步兵连，让他们去领新的枪和子弹。
Igor: Have whatever's left of the armored corps placed into the infantry companies. Give them whatever rifles and ammunition we can spare.

伊戈尔：夜里我会让近卫队把牺牲士兵的遗体都运回来。我们需要一块更大的墓地。
Igor: Tonight, the guards will bring in all our dead. We will need more space for graves.

阿尔谢尼点了点头。
Arseny nods.

沉默轻轻地落在院子里，像一层又一层灰。
Silence settles over the yard, layer upon layer like dust.

马厩里传来水声。清水冲刷着战马明亮的枣红色皮毛，使它成为这个院子里唯一干净的色彩。
The sound of spraying water comes from the stables. A stream of mud, blood, and oil flows down from the bright chestnut coat of a horse.

军官Ⅰ：……安德烈，什么时候了，你还在伺候你的“佩伦”。
Officer I: Andrea. How can you still be in the mood to fuss over a horse at a time like this?

军官Ⅱ：只有歪鼻子还宝贝他的马，骑兵早就派不上用场了。
Officer II: Old Crooked Nose babying his precious Perun again, what's new?

军官Ⅲ：对啊，吃草的马怎么扛得住吃油的坦克！
Officer III: What good's a beast of flesh and bone against tungsten and steel?

安德烈围着佩伦转了一圈，确保战马身上没有增添伤痕，才回头反驳。
Andrea circles around Perun, checking him diligently for wounds, before wheeling to face his jeering comrades.

安德烈：哥萨克不骑马叫什么哥萨克！我骑上这匹战马，哪儿都能去。
Andrea: You'd have a Cossack go without his horse? You forget there are places a tank is not fit to go.

安德烈：高山、树林、沼泽、大街……坦克能去的地方我能去，坦克不能去的地方我也能去。
Andrea: Mountains, forests, swamps, city streets ... Where tanks will go, I take a tank. But where they cannot, Perun will go.

阿尔谢尼：安德烈，你是不是又在白天喝酒了？
Arseny: Day drinking again, Andrea?

安德烈没有理会，他轻轻撑住围栏，魁梧的身子灵巧地翻过马厩。
Andrea ignores him. He places his hands on the fence, his burly frame clearing it smoothly.

将要落地的时候，他将手中的马鞭迅疾地向空中一指。
Mid-descent, he thrusts his riding whip toward the sky.

安德烈：我们的战术只有一条——骑着马往前跑，用马刀砍断所有挡在前面的东西！
Andrea: Our plan is simple! We ride forward and cut down everyone in our path with our shashkas!

安德烈：但要注意，别被子弹追上！
Andrea: All we need to do is ride faster than their bullets!

军官们笑了起来，笑声落在院子里，像一片水流。
The gathered officers laugh. It's almost enough to break the tension in the air.

伊戈尔也笑出了声。
Igor laughs, too.

安德烈：伊戈尔……我的老长官，你不能笑我，这可是你的战术！
Andrea: Igor, sir, not you too. It was your plan!

伊戈尔：这是我的战术，可你的马不够快，还是被子弹追上了。
Igor: It was my plan. But your horse was too slow. The bullets caught you.

军官Ⅰ：安德烈，你的鼻子就是被子弹打歪的吧！
Officer I: That's how that nose of yours got bent out of shape, wasn't it!

歪鼻子的安德烈举着马鞭，发出赶马的嘘声。
The crooked-nosed man raises his whip, making a herding call.

安德烈：去——去——我的鼻子是天生的，我父亲的父亲就长这样。
Andrea: Shoo, shoo! I was born with this proud feature. As was my grandfather and his before him.

伊戈尔：他的鼻子确实不是被子弹打歪的。
Igor: It wasn't a bullet that caused that damage anyways.

伊戈尔：打仗的时候这家伙不知道从哪儿找来的工兵铲，举在头顶，子弹就从铁铲上滑过去了。
Igor: In the fight, this one pulled a shovel out of nowhere, held it overhead, and the bullet just bounced off.

军官们更加放肆地笑起来。笑的水流成为浪潮，冲散了一层层尘土。
The laughter grows louder, becoming like a steady ripple, at last piercing through the air.

伊戈尔的副官来到他身边，在笑声的掩护下压低了声音。
Igor's adjutant comes close, voice low beneath the laughter.

副官：师长，鸽子屋。
Adjutant: Sir, the Pax House.

鸽子屋的通讯器和芝诺的红色电话不太一样。
The Pax House's communicator is different from Zeno's red phone.

它是白色的，一尘不染，像春天顿河里的鹅卵石。大多数时候，它也像石头一样沉默。
It is white, clean, like a pebble from the Don. Normally, it is just as quiet.

伊戈尔：……我方雷达还处于失灵状态，防空系统和装甲系统全面瘫痪，无法找到原因。
Igor: Our radar remains nonfunctional. Air defense and armor systems are paralyzed. Cause unknown.

伊戈尔：初步怀疑是神秘术的影响。不是单个神秘学家士兵的能力，是一种复杂的术式。
Igor: Initial assessment points to arcanist activity. Not a single arcane soldier's power, but an elaborate ritual.

伊戈尔：我们需要更强大的火力支援，同时，需要专业的神秘学队伍。
Igor: We need heavier fire support. We will also need a dedicated arcanist detachment.

通讯器里只有极细微的沙沙声。少将伊戈尔笔直地站在通讯器前，等待着。
The request meets a long static hum. Vice Admiral Igor stands straight, holding stiffly by the communicator, waiting with perfect detachment.

通讯器里传来一个年轻的，和大理石一样冰冷又严丝合缝的声音。
A young voice comes through, cool and even.

通讯器：“鸽子屋赞许您的忠诚，为了您在这种极端情况下，没有私自撤退，少将。”
Communicator: "The Pax House acknowledges your loyalty for not withdrawing without orders under these conditions."

伊戈尔：在收到命令之前，我的军队会一直前进。
Igor: My army will keep marching until the order comes.

通讯器：“鸽子屋向您的军队致敬。您请求的支援已经在路上。”
Communicator: "We salute your efforts. Reinforcements are on the way."

伊戈尔：我需要知道支援到达的时间。
Igor: I need an ETA.

瓦西里：你要畏惧死亡，孩子。
Vasily: You must fear death, boy.

瓦西里：不仅畏惧自己的死亡，你要畏惧每一个人的死亡。
Vasily: Not just your own. You must fear the death of every man.

伊戈尔：他们的死亡就在我的肩膀上。
Igor: Their deaths are on my shoulders.

伊戈尔：我会一直和战士们站在一起，不管是生者，还是死者。
Igor: My soldiers will be with me, no matter whether they live or die.

伊戈尔：我会一直和我的罪孽站在一起，直到冥界的黑暗将我彻底吞噬。
Igor: My sins will be with me until the darkness of the underworld devours all that I am.

老兵不置可否地笑了笑，他把干枯的手伸进军衣兜里，却没有掏到酒瓶。
The old soldier smiles faintly. His calloused hand dives into his coat but finds no flask.

瓦西里：啧，小伊戈尔，还记得怎么用枪吗？
Vasily: Tch. Hey, little Igor. Do you remember how to hold a rifle?

伊戈尔：记得，父亲。
Igor: Yes, Father.

伊戈尔：先把枪托紧紧地抵在肩上，身体微微向后。
Igor: First, plant the stock firmly against your shoulder. Lean back a little.

伊戈尔把右手食指放在扳机上，他看见雪花落在枪管，扳机冷得像一块冰。
Igor's right index finger finds the trigger. He sees flakes settle on the barrel. The trigger feels cold as ice.

伊戈尔：保持身体稳定。
Igor: Steady ...

伊戈尔：慢慢地……
Igor: Slowly ...

伊戈尔：均匀地……
Igor: Steady.

伊戈尔：吸气，呼气，这口气的尽头，就是“时机”。
Igor: Breathe in, breathe out. At your breath's end, that is the moment.

他闭上眼，再睁开眼，瞄准镜里出现一个人影。
He closes his eyes, then opens them. A figure enters his scope.

伊戈尔：不要回头，同志。
Igor: Don't look back, son.

伊戈尔：向前跑，不要回头。
Igor: Keep running. Don't look back.

人影越来越清晰，停在了草原和雪地的交界，没有前进，也没有后退。
The figure grows clear, halted between the steppe and snow, neither advancing nor retreating.

他看了很久，才看清了眼前的人。
He looks a long time before he knows the man before him.

伊戈尔：你不能回头，你只能前进，战士。
Igor: Don't look back. You must move forward, soldier.

伊戈尔：我不会回头，也不会遵循任何人的命令前进。
Igor: I will not look back, nor will I follow another order.

伊戈尔：你背叛了曾经相信你的人，你背叛了你自己。
Igor: You betrayed those who trusted you. You betrayed yourself.

伊戈尔：我已经走上另一条路。
Igor: I've taken another path.

伊戈尔：那是一条正确的新路，还是重蹈覆辙？
Igor: Is it the right path?

士兵和将军一起站在风雪里，他们思考了很久。
The soldier and the admiral hold their distance together against the wind and snow, as Igor lets himself think the next moment through.

命令一直只有一条。
There has only ever been one order.

伊戈尔：是的，长官。
Igor: Yes, sir.

伊戈尔：服从。
Igor: Obedient.

伊戈尔：坚定。
Igor: Loyal.

伊戈尔：从不退怯。
Igor: Unwavering.

他的手指一直按在扳机上。
His finger stays upon the trigger.

砰——
Bang!
