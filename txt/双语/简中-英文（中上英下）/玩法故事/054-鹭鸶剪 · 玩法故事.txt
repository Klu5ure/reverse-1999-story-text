01  【3.4】鹭鸶剪玩法01.1 / [English text unavailable]

起初，是令人心安的静谧。
The morning began with soothing silence,

接着，有柔和、清凉的风。
punctuated only by a soft, cool breeze.

星星点点的阳光。
And soon after, the first rays of sunlight broke through in full,

——春日的清晨。
announcing the first gasp of spring.

年轻道人躺在枝叶繁茂的树上。春风吹拂、树影摇曳，她几乎要如此沉沉睡去。
A young Daoist rests atop a leafy tree, feathered by the spring breeze and shaded by the leaves, slowly drifting toward sleep.

鹭鸶剪：……嗯？
Paper Heron: Hm?

树下响起极轻的交谈声。
Faint voices from below rouse her.

鹭鸶剪：……
Paper Heron: ...

道人细细辨认。
She leans in to listen carefully.

鹭鸶剪：师父？师姐？
Paper Heron: Master? Finch?

她懒洋洋地侧过身去，想要听得更清楚些——
Though drowsy from her rest, she leans closer still, trying to make out what the voices are saying.

又一次，她坠入无边的黑暗。
Only to lose her balance and fall into endless darkness.

淳镇·客栈小院
Inn, Chun Town

天色是萧瑟的青灰。才出正月，冰消雪融，温度反而降了不少。
The sky above is a dull, pale gray. The first lunar month has just ended, and despite the melting of the ice and snow, the air feels colder than it should.

掌柜在院中背手巡视一圈，捡了张无人的椅子坐下。
The innkeeper slowly paces about the courtyard with her hands behind her back before deciding to rest in an empty chair.

客栈掌柜：这么冷的天，也不知道添柴……嗯？那小子人呢？
Innkeeper: It's freezing! Why hasn't that boy thrown more wood on the fire? Hm? Where is that lout, anyway?

“嘭！”
*bang*

？？？：——外面的规矩，没人教过你吗？
???: Didn't anyone teach you that there are rules out here?

？？？：我不讲你的规矩，只讲我的道理！
???: I don't care about your rules. I follow my own principles!

客栈掌柜：好端端地，又有人动刀动枪……
Innkeeper: Is it too much to ask for a single day where no one draws their weapon in my inn?

掌柜叹了口气，离开还没坐热的椅子，熟练地绕到回廊后躲了起来。
The innkeeper sighs and slips away from her barely warmed chair, moving deftly around the corridor to hide herself.

？？？：咳咳，那个，东家……
???: *cough* Madam ...

客栈掌柜：好啊——我说怎么找不到你人，原来在这里偷懒呢。
Innkeeper: Ah! I was wondering where you'd gone off to. Avoiding your work, no doubt!

伙计：不能怪我啊！今天本来也没几桩生意……
Waiter: This is hardly my fault! Besides, it's not as though we have any real customers today.

客栈掌柜：嘘，小声些，你怕那边听不见啊？
Innkeeper: Shh, be quiet! They might hear you!

伙计：对对对，东家说得是。我瞟一下现在是个什么情况。哟，男的可提着刀呢！小姑娘倒斯斯文文……
Waiter: Alright, alright! I'll just take a quick look to see what's happening. Oh my, that man has a knife in his hand, but the girl he's facing actually looks calm, relaxed even.

伙计：咦，她那头花里胡哨的骡子很眼熟啊……
Waiter: Wait, that peculiar mule of hers seems familiar.

“锵——”
*clang*

掌柜一把拉回探头探脑的伙计，瞪他一眼。
The innkeeper pulls the curious waiter back down and glares at him.

客栈掌柜：看什么看，命不要了？
Innkeeper: What are you doing?! Are you trying to get the both of us killed?!

客栈掌柜：唉，我这榆木桌椅可是冬天刚换的。都快打烊了，偏来这么一出……
Innkeeper: *groan* Please don't break my elm tables and chairs. I only bought them this winter. Why must they start a fight just as we're closing for the night?

小院被遗忘的角落里，传来一声突兀的哈欠。
A sudden yawn comes from a forgotten corner.

？？？：……店家，要打烊了吗？
???: Closing time already?

穿红衣的青年道人似是刚被吵醒，从臂弯中抬头，脸上还带着一道衣褶压出的印子。
The young Daoist in red lifts her head from her arms. A crease from her sleeve has left a faint mark on her cheek.

她茫然地站起来，拍拍衣摆，面露迟疑之色。
She stands up and brushes off her clothes but looks reluctant to leave.

道人：可我的绿豆糕还没吃完呢……
Daoist: But I haven't finished my mung bean cakes.

客栈掌柜：这位道长，先别管你那绿豆糕了，赶紧躲一躲。前边打起来啦！
Innkeeper: Forget about the cakes. You'd better find somewhere to hide. There's going to be a fight!

客栈掌柜：万一没刹住，伤着碰着可不是闹着玩的！
Innkeeper: Should it get out of hand, some innocent bystander may get hurt. And that's the last thing I need on my conscience!

道人：前边吗？我看看。
Daoist: A fight? Where? I don't see any—Oh!

道人：……唔。
Daoist: Hmm.

伙计：哎哎，你怎么又坐下了？
Waiter: What are you—Why are you sitting back down?

道人：问题不大，小姑娘手底有分寸，不会伤人。
Daoist: Because there's nothing to worry about. That girl knows her limits. She'll be careful.

伙计：还有那凶神恶煞的男的呢！
Waiter: What about the angry-looking man?

道人：他啊，他赢不了的。
Daoist: The man? Oh, he's certain to lose.

道人：呵……好困。这架还有得打呢……店家，等打烊了再叫我……
Daoist: *yawn* So sleepy. This will take a while. Wake me when you close.

客栈掌柜：道长，休息也看看时候吧！
Innkeeper: What? This is no time for a nap!

道人对掌柜随意挥了挥手，重新趴回桌上。
The Daoist gives a casual wave of her hand, her head already resting on the table once more.

院内的客人睡得正好，门口却剑拔弩张。
While the Daoist sleeps soundly, tensions run high near the gate.

牵骡子的少女：我们修道之人，就算不想着行侠仗义，也不该滥用术法骗人钱财！
Girl with a Mule: Even if they don't aspire to be a hero, a Daoist should never use their skills to cheat people!

持刀男子：——行个屁！
Man with a Knife: Ha! Who spoke any words of heroes?

持刀男子：不长眼的东西，管到爷爷头上来了？我倒要看看你有几两本事！
Man with a Knife: I won't let some irritating little bug lecture me! Come on, let's see what you're made of.

牵骡子的少女：我也没多少本事，只是你做得不对，我既见了，就不能袖手旁观！
Girl with a Mule: It takes no strength to know right from wrong, and this is a wrong I cannot walk away from!

持刀男子：好大口气，来！
Man with a Knife: Big talk, little bug. Let's go!

持刀男子：……呃！
Man with a Knife: Urgh!

男子被逼退一步，踏入小院，稳了稳身形。
The man, pushed back a step into the courtyard, nearly topples before steadying himself.

牵骡子的少女：再来！
Girl with a Mule: Bug's waiting. Come on!

回廊后，伙计忽然一拍柱子。
From his hiding spot, the waiter slaps a nearby wall in realization.

伙计：我想起来了！就是她，上个月在对面当铺也闹过这么一回。
Waiter: I remember her! She was involved in that trouble at the pawnshop last month.

伙计：当时王大官人非要客人把家传的物件死当，她路过，上去替人讲理，三两句就动起手来，场面还不小！
Waiter: Apparently, the pawnbroker Wáng was pressuring a customer to sell their family heirloom. That girl happened to pass by, so she intervened to try and reason with him. After exchanging only a few words, it escalated into a full-blown fight!

客栈掌柜：——是她啊！小姑娘是不是年前就来镇上了？
Innkeeper: Oh, that girl! She showed up just before the Dushuo Festival, if I'm not mistaken.

客栈掌柜：她到现在还没找到那个……那个什么，气脉？
Innkeeper: I heard she was looking for the, the, what was it again, Qi flows?

伙计：这我哪知道？反正事是没少找。开当铺的王大官人、卖符箓的李天师、走镖的赵把头……都没在她手上讨到好。
Waiter: Whether she found it or not, she's certainly causing a world of trouble. Neither pawnbroker Wáng, talisman-seller Lǐ, nor the Head of the Armed Escort, Mr. Zhào, could stand against her.

伙计掰着手指挨个数过去，掌柜摇了摇头。
The waiter counts out the names on his fingers, while the innkeeper shakes her head disapprovingly.

客栈掌柜：唉，还是年轻啊……这下碰到硬点子了。
Innkeeper: She's certainly bitten off more than she can chew this time.

客栈掌柜：……
Innkeeper: ...

客栈掌柜：不好，他们冲这边过来了！快快快，咱们走！
Innkeeper: Oh no, they're coming this way! Come, let's get out of here!

客栈掌柜：道长你走不走？
Innkeeper: Um, you there, are you coming?

道人：唔……
Daoist: Hmm.

睡梦中的道人只是换了个姿势。
The sleeping Daoist only shifts place in response, clearly more interested in staying where she is.

打斗声越来越近，掌柜长叹一声，猛地把还在愣神的伙计往后一拽，自己也跟着钻进后厨。
As the fight draws closer, the innkeeper lets out a weary sigh, grabs her bumbling waiter by the collar, and rushes into the kitchen.

持刀男子：呼……有两下子……
Man with a Knife: *sigh* So, you've got a few tricks up your sleeve after all. Not bad.

牵骡子的少女：把骗来的钱财还回去，立誓不再作恶，我就放你一马！
Girl with a Mule: If you return the money you swindled and swear to clean up your act, I'll let you go!

持刀男子：立誓有个屁用？
Man with a Knife: Ha! And what makes you think I'll stay true to my word?

持刀男子：钱嘛，爷爷我早就花完了。你也别得意得太早……
Man with a Knife: That money is long gone. And I wouldn't be so confident if I were you.

男子瞥了一眼不远处趴在桌上小憩的红衣道人，忽地手腕一翻。
With a glance at the napping Daoist, the man's sneer twists into a malicious grin. He flicks his wrist, sending his knife flying toward her.

牵骡子的少女：小心！
Girl with a Mule: Watch out!

她咬咬牙，抬手掷出横在身前的长剑，剑身破空而去，隔开刀锋。
Gritting her teeth, she pivots and throws her longsword, sending it flying through the air and knocking the knife aside. She breathes a subtle sigh of relief.

“锵。”
*clang*

长剑落地。男子冷笑一声，挽了个刀花回转锋芒，沉重的一记向少女凌空劈下——
The longsword clatters to the ground, the sound almost lost in the man's bellowing laughter. With another sneer, he draws a second blade and brings it down in a heavy slash.

道人：——这就过分了吧？
Daoist: Now that's a little much, don't you think?

一只手止住了刀锋。
The blade stops mid-descent, its edge caught between a pair of fingers.

道人指尖萦绕着莹白的剑气。她回头对还有些恍惚的少女笑了笑。
White Qi coats the Daoist's fingertips. She turns and smiles at the stunned girl.

道人：其实你自己也能打得过他，我就是路见不平……
Daoist: Apologies for interrupting. I'm sure you can handle him yourself, but I just hate it when people don't play fair.

道人：——我的绿豆糕！
Daoist: I should've—No, my cake!

她迅速并拢双指捏了个新诀，在空中一划，被刀锋震开的糕点便顺着手指的方向归拢回桌上。
She swiftly brings her index and middle fingers together, tracing a sigil in the air. At once, the blade-knocked cake sails back onto the table.

道人：呼……还好还好。
Daoist: Phew! That was close.

持刀男子：……哪来的道士？
Man with a Knife: A Daoist?

男子狐疑地上下打量，看到她身侧的白玉长剑，不由冷笑一声。
The man looks her over and notices the white jade sword at her side.

持刀男子：好啊，还带着家伙……原来是找了高人在这里埋伏爷爷呢。
Man with a Knife: So you had a powerful master waiting for me, did you?

道人：高人？我吗？
Daoist: Powerful master? Who? Where?

持刀男子：小小年纪，如此卑鄙！
Man with a Knife: You're nothing but a fraud! Despicable little bug!

牵骡子的少女：我没有！
Girl with a Mule: I know nothing of her!

道人：我真是来吃绿豆糕的，路见不平而已，你怎么就不信呢？
Daoist: Uh, I really only came for the cakes. Trouble showed up, so I stepped in. Nothing more, nothing less.

道人：不过既然停手了，我们不妨有话好说……
Daoist: Now that you've stopped, perhaps we can talk this out like reasonable people.

又一道刀光闪过。
The man lashes out with another strike, harmlessly cutting through the air above the Daoist's head.

道人：唉。看来你是不打算有话好说了。
Daoist: *sigh* Not a talker, I see.

道人：——贯炁明素，宸洞开微！
Daoist: With   the   flow   of   Qi,   the   mind   opens   wide!

红色的剪纸在空中飘飘悠悠转了两圈，忽地落下去。
A red paper swallow flutters in the air, spins twice, then comes to rest on the tip of the man's knife.

持刀男子：哼，雕虫小技。
Man with a Knife: Hmph. Is that all you've got?

燕子落在男子的佩刀上，打了个趔趄。
The paper swallow teeters on the spot.

道人：抱歉抱歉，我很久没和人动手了……着！
Daoist: Ahem! Sorry. Haven't done this for a while. Now!

持刀男子：……怎么回事？！
Man with a Knife: Wh-What's happening?!

那燕子啄了啄刀柄，奋力拍拍翅膀，叼起佩刀向门外飞去。
The man only stares, dumbfounded, as the swallow snatches the knife by the hilt and flies off with it toward the gate.

持刀男子：——我的刀！
Man with a Knife: My knife!

持刀男子：你……你们……
Man with a Knife: You, you two ...

持刀男子：好啊，今日便算了，以后别让我在淳镇看到你！
Man with a Knife: Hrgh! Fine. You and your master win this round, little bug. But don't let me see you in Chun Town again!

道人：别担心，我倒也没做久留的打算……
Daoist: What a coincidence. I was planning on moving on anyway.

道人：咦，他走得好快，不知听见没有？
Daoist: Oh, he's gone. I wonder if he heard me.

道人摇摇头，懒洋洋地坐下，对着骡子的方向歪了歪头。
The Daoist sits back with a yawn and a lazy shake of her head, her eyes turning to the girl's mule.

道人：你这骡子，不用拴起来吗？
Daoist: Your mule ... Perhaps you'd like to tie it up?

牵骡子的少女：哦……哦，对！
Girl with a Mule: Huh? Oh, right!

她恍然地应了一声，摸了摸骡子的耳朵，将它牵到廊柱边拴上。
Gathering herself, the girl affectionately pats the mule's ear and ties it to a pillar.

牵骡子的少女：刚才真是好险，多谢前辈相助！在下阿措，还没请教前辈如何称呼？
Girl with a Mule: That was rather hairy. Thank you for dealing with that brute. My name is Pip, by the way. Would my rescuer be so kind as to give me hers?

鹭鸶剪：不必客气，叫我鹭鸶剪就好。
Paper Heron: You're very welcome, Pip. They call me Paper Heron.

阿措：鹭鸶剪前辈……
Pip: Lady Paper Heron!

鹭鸶剪：鹭鸶剪。
Paper Heron: But just Heron will do.

阿措：好，鹭鸶剪。
Pip: Alright, Heron.

阿措：鹭鸶剪，我打扰了你休息，该请你吃顿饭当作赔礼的。
Pip: So sorry to have disturbed your rest. Please, allow me to repay you with a meal.

鹭鸶剪：那倒不必，我还剩了半碟绿豆糕呢……
Paper Heron: Oh, there's no need for that. I still have my cakes to work through.

阿措：那便算……放下！！！
Pip: It would be remiss of me not to ... *gasp* Drop that right this instant!!

屋檐上的积雪簌簌落下。
A sheet of snow slides down from the inn's eaves.

阿措的惊叫声中，鹭鸶剪顺着她的目光转过头去。
Paper Heron turns her gaze toward Pip's outburst.

不知何时挣脱了缰绳的骡子慢悠悠看她一眼，伸出舌头，将最后一块绿豆糕卷进嘴里。
The mule, somehow freed, eyes her slowly. Then, with a deft flick of its tongue, it swipes the last cake.

骡子：咴？
Mule: Whinny?

鹭鸶剪：你……
Paper Heron: Did your mule just ...

阿措：我并未指使！
Pip: I swear to you, I didn't tell him to do that!

鹭鸶剪：咳咳，别紧张。
Paper Heron: Right, right. Not to worry.

鹭鸶剪：我就问问，你刚才是不是说要请我吃饭？
Paper Heron: So, you said something about buying me a meal?

？？？·白马派
White Horse School, ???

？？？：鹭鸶剪？
???: Heron?

？？？：……鹭鸶剪！
???: Heron!

鹭鸶剪：师姐？你不是明天才回来吗？
Paper Heron: Finch? I thought you were due to be back tomorrow?

师姐：我不在的这几日，你又做了什么？
Pearl Finch: So, what did you do with yourself while I was away?

鹭鸶剪：我在山上还能做什么？不外乎早晚功课，依时诵经……
Paper Heron: The only thing there is to do: recite texts, morning and evening, over and over.

师姐：我不是问这些。来时我看到师父在中庭不住叹气，难道跟你没关系？
Pearl Finch: And Master sighing in the courtyard has nothing to do with you?

鹭鸶剪：咳咳……师父有事问我，我只是如实相告嘛。
Paper Heron: Well, he asked me a question, and I answered honestly.

师姐叹了一口气。
Pearl Finch lets out a resigned, knowing sigh.

师姐：唉，如实相告。你是怎么如实相告的？
Pearl Finch: "Answered honestly." And what might that answer have been, exactly?

鹭鸶剪：昨日早课前，师父如往常一般教诲了大家好久好久，又突然挨个问我们往后有何打算。
Paper Heron: Yesterday, before the morning class, Master went into his long lecture as always, and then asked each of us about our plans for the future.

鹭鸶剪：二师兄的志向还是那样。他喜欢剑术，立志要苦练一番，把咱们白马派的剑发扬光大。
Paper Heron: Brother Liǎng's aspirations are the same as always. He dreams of mastering the art of the blade, wants to carry the White Horse School's name far and wide.

鹭鸶剪：你是知道师父的——“我派法剑，并非好勇斗狠的凶器！”
Paper Heron: You can guess what Master said to that, "Our school's sword is not a tool for violence."

鹭鸶剪：喏，我学得像不像？
Paper Heron: Not a bad impression, eh?

师姐：……自然，没人比你惹他发火的次数更多。
Pearl Finch: Well, you would know how to do it. You are the one who puts that look on his face most often.

鹭鸶剪：三师兄立刻站出来，说修道之人最要紧是无为，要修己心，自吕祖以来就是如此，所谓人能常清静，天地悉皆归嘛。
Paper Heron: Then Brother Sān stepped forward and said all who practice Daoism ought not to disrupt the natural flow of things but rather tend to their own minds—the very idea passed down from Ancestor Lǚ. You know the words: "When one's mind is tranquil and free of distracting thoughts, all things between heaven and earth will become one with it."

鹭鸶剪：——气得师父背着手在原地转来转去。
Paper Heron: Master got so angry he did that thing where he paced back and forth with his hands clasped behind his back.

师姐：唉，你们真是不教人省心。
Pearl Finch: Oh, what am I to do with you all?

鹭鸶剪：师姐，你真了解师父，他就是这么说的！
Paper Heron: That's exactly what Master said!

鹭鸶剪清了清嗓子，两手背在背后。

Pip: Legend of the Getian, The White Horse Immortal: A Tale of Medicine, and Three Years of Strange Dreams: A Collection.

鹭鸶剪：看来两百年过去，《葛天氏外传》卖得依旧很好嘛。我记得里面有个凡人向葛天氏求道，下山后发现人间已经过了千年的故事，是不是？
Paper Heron: So Legend of the Getian is still popular, even after two hundred years, eh? I remember a story about a mortal seeking the Way from the Getian clan. He descends the mountain, only to discover that a thousand years have passed.

鹭鸶剪：那些受人欢迎的长生故事，或是仙人眷红尘，或是凡客慕玉京，说到底，讲的都是有所不能。
Paper Heron: Those stories talk of immortals desiring the mortal world or mortals yearning for the immortal realm. At their core, they describe what one cannot attain.

Paper Heron: My senior sister once said that, as a person ages, time seems to speed up.

鹭鸶剪：我当时没有反驳，但心里很不以为然。
Paper Heron: I didn't argue then, but deep down, I disagreed.

鹭鸶剪：十多岁的时候，花开花落是为一季；等到十几年后、几十年后，一季的时间，不也同样是从花开等到花谢吗？
Paper Heron: When you're young, the blooming and fading of flowers marks a season. That time doesn't change, even decades later. Flowers still bloom. Seasons still pass. Everything feels just as it did.

鹭鸶剪：但现在看来，她说得对。

Paper Heron: The first ten years feel slow. But the next ten are easier to endure.

Pip: He's a slow thing, but it's not as though you're in a hurry.
