01  周期性挣扎 / Periodical Suffering

周期性挣扎
Periodical Suffering

“我似乎伫立了很久”

“思绪万千，却一言未发”

“南岛上的三位水手仍然活着”

“那些本应早已逝去的人们”
"I seem to have stood still for an eternity,"

"Engulfed by a sea of thoughts, kept silent as a cemetery."

"The three lighthouse keepers on the isles still remain,"

"Where they should have long passed into memory."

马库斯：……
Marcus: …

马库斯：弗兰南群岛上发生了太多太多的事，其中的每一幕都令我印象深刻，难以割舍……
Marcus: So much has transpired here upon the Flannan Isles, each and every event etching itself into my consciousness ...

马库斯：尽管《弗兰南群岛史》已经连载了好几期，但我所记录的甚至不到十分之一……
Marcus: Despite all my installments so far on the History of the Flannan Isles, what I've documented barely scratches the surface ...

马库斯：读者们一定都像我一样，迫不及待地等着下一期的《白日梦邮报》……
Marcus: The readers, I believe, share my eagerness for the next issue of the Daydream Post ...

马库斯：呼……我不能辜负他们的期待。
Marcus: *deep breath* I cannot let them down.

马库斯：……
Marcus: …

马库斯：年轻的姑娘揉了揉眼睛，将思绪重新浸入脖颈与帽檐之间的领地。
Marcus: The young lady rubs her eyes and refocuses back to her writing.

马库斯：留给《群岛史》的版面大概还有三分之一张报纸……可这座岛上还有太多我没有提及的内容，它们说不定正是读者尚未了解的……
Marcus: The History of the Flannan Isles column has to fit in roughly a third of a page ... But there are so many more stories about the archipelago I've yet to tell—stories the readers still haven't heard ...

马库斯：丹尼希女士曾说，连编辑们都对弗兰南群岛的谜团相当好奇……如果内容足够丰富，兴许她会允许我再补充一节！
Marcus: Ms. Dennehy mentioned that even the editors have been following the mysteries of the Flannan Isles with intrigue ... If I can keep up that interest, she might allow me an additional section!

马库斯：这样，人们就能完整地了解弗兰南群岛发生的一切了。
Marcus: That way, I can present our readers with a more comprehensive look at their stories.

她打起精神，又伏在了书案上。
Livened up, she dives back into her writing.

马库斯：“弗兰南群岛，位于苏格兰的外赫布里底群岛之中。关于它的讨论已持续了数年。直到今天，我们仍能记起关于它的传闻……”
Marcus: "The Flannan Isles, a small island chain in the Outer Hebrides of Scotland, have been the subject of rumor for years. Even up to today, many mysteries still remain here ..."

马库斯：“据可靠消息称，不论是在灯塔内，抑或是岛上的任何一处角落，一切似乎都无比正常。”
Marcus: "Of course, to hear it from the credible authorities, the lighthouse and indeed, the whole archipelago, were perfectly normal, all except for ..."

马库斯：“除了那三位离奇消失的灯塔管理员……”
Marcus: "... its three vanished lighthouse keepers."

马库斯：“人们纷纷猜测其中的可能性。来自官方的调查员先生坚持认为，某场意外发生的海上风暴吞没了他们……”
Marcus: "Speculation over their disappearance drove the public wild, yet the authorities claimed they were simply swept away in a deadly storm."

马库斯：“但这显然无法说服大多数人……”
Marcus: "This, of course, did nothing to convince them."

马库斯：“有水手声称，他在弗兰南群岛的上空目睹了来自太空的飞碟，它们通体黝黑，散发着幽深而寒冷的光束……”
Marcus: "Meanwhile, a sailor claimed to have seen coal-black UFOs over the islands, casting eerie, cold beams of light ..."

马库斯：“当然，关于巨型鸟类、无底深渊的猜测同样不少……”
Marcus: "Other theories included gargantuan man-eating birds, a bottomless abyss, and so on ..."

马库斯：“人们众说纷纭，但始终没能得出答案……”
Marcus: "Rumors continue to swirl, but the truth remains elusive ..."

马库斯：“警察先生们出具的调查报告，守塔员们所遗留的日志记录，以及迟迟未能找到的尸体……它们提醒着我，这绝非一起简单的失踪事件……”
Marcus: "The police report, the log entries, and the lack of any recovered bodies ... They all scream one thing—this is no simple disappearance ..."

马库斯：“尽管已过去了十二年，但怀着对真相的好奇，我仍然决定来到这座岛上……也见到了那份始终没有公开的记录。”
Marcus: "Twelve years have passed, yet driven by curiosity, I decided that I must visit the islands myself ... And there I discovered a never-before-seen log book."

马库斯：“上面的落款是，1900年12月15日 记录人：杜卡特。”
Marcus: "The signature on it reads 'James, recorded on December 15, 1900.'"

马库斯：……
Marcus: …

马库斯：握笔的手顿在了半空，如同无风的帆船，迟迟未能驶入下一行。
Marcus: Her pen hangs in mid-air, like a sail on still water, hesitating to embark on the next line.

马库斯：这起事件已经过去了这么多年，关于岛上的谜团并没有减少，反而越来越多了……
Marcus: So much time has passed since the incident, yet the mysteries only seem to multiply ...

马库斯：我……该如何拣选这些素材呢？
Marcus: Hmm ... How can I sift through all these story threads?

马库斯：当我尚未踏上这座岛时，我便听说了水手们的传闻。途径这儿的船只大多会无故抛锚，甲板上甚至会渗出古怪的液体……以致于这儿的航线远比其他岛更少。
Marcus: Before setting foot on the islands, I heard other unsettling rumors from the sailors. Ships passing too near often find themselves caught motionless on the sea, with eerie and unnatural ooze forming on their decks ... These frightful tales have meant that most give this archipelago a wide berth.

马库斯：这是个值得一写的故事……
Marcus: This is a story worth telling ...

马库斯：可……十二年都不曾中断的灯塔同样是个重大的问题。
Marcus: Meanwhile ... there is the matter of the island's lighthouse, which has, through unknown means, been kept in operation for twelve years despite being abandoned.

马库斯：如果没能将它记入《弗兰南群岛史》，我一定会抱憾终身……！
Marcus: If I failed to include that sort of mystery in my History of the Flannan Isles, I'd surely regret it ...!

马库斯：当然，还有最重要的守塔员失踪事件……
Marcus: Which, of course, leads to the central mystery of them all. The disappearance of the original lighthouse keepers in the first place ...

她陷入了迟疑。
Spoiled for choices, Marcus is, nonetheless, in a very briny pickle.

残酷的、不留情面的选择题横亘在这个可怜姑娘的眼前。
An arduous choice lies before the poor girl.

马库斯：……
Marcus: …

马库斯：《弗兰南群岛史》属于弗兰南群岛全体对象。出于公平起见，我也应该平等地使用它们……
Marcus: History of the Flannan Isles belongs to the islands themselves. It's only fair that I treat their stories impartially ...

马库斯：无论放弃哪一个，都是对弗兰南群岛的背弃，是对这本书的误读！
Marcus: To turn down the opportunity to tell any of these stories feels like betraying the truth itself!

她的目光游离，落在更多、更多的稿纸之上。
Her gaze wanders, shifting from one page of notes to another.

一条足以容纳所有内容的路显现了出来。
There it is, a narrow path that she could follow to capture the essence of all these stories.

马库斯：“我放下了那本记录本，注意到身前这个仍然发着光亮的灯塔。”
Marcus: "I set the log book aside, noticing the lighthouse just as its light washes past me."

马库斯：“在那段离奇的失踪案之后，再也没有新的管理员愿意驻扎在弗兰南群岛……艾琳·莫尔灯塔早已无人维护，至少本应如此。”
Marcus: "After the disappearances, no one proved willing to station on the Flannan Isles ... The Eilean Mor lighthouse was left unmanned, or so it should have been."

马库斯：“我站在灯塔的底下，逐级向上攀爬。出乎我意料的是，脚下的石阶，严丝合缝的绳结与螺丝，以及从塔顶向四周洒下暖黄色光亮的灯泡……”
Marcus: "From the base of the lighthouse, I walk step by step upward. Curiously, I notice, the wooden stairway beneath my feet appears to be meticulously well-maintained, and stranger still is the warm yellow light spilling from the apex of the tower ..."

马库斯：“它们无声无息，却又诚实地向我传达着一个震撼了我许久的讯息：使那座灯塔得以正常运转的一切，都仍保存着十二年前的余光！”
Marcus: "They all silently, yet truthfully, convey an astonishing revelation: that this lighthouse has been running just as well as it had since it was abandoned twelve years ago!"

马库斯：“我的脚步虚浮，几乎惊讶地向后仰翻……可我要怎么相信呢？”
Marcus: "My footsteps falter, nearly tumbling backward at the realization ... Yet, how could I believe all that I am seeing?"

马库斯：“那些使我双脚打滑的苔藓与斑驳的划痕，毫不留情地向我证明着，这儿确确实实已经历经了整整十二年的时光。”
Marcus: "Nevertheless, the slippery moss and weathered scratches adorning the grounds prove with cold logic that twelve years must indeed have passed here."

马库斯：“在短暂地平复心情后，我决定开始探索这儿。”
Marcus: "After composing myself, I decide to begin my exploration."

马库斯：“在此之前，我鸣响了船上的汽笛，以便途径这儿的船只能够注意到这儿。随后，我拿起了仅剩三支的信号弹……”
Marcus: "Just before I alighted, I sounded the ship's steam whistle to signal to passing vessels, if there were any. Then, I took the three remaining flares ..."

马库斯：“倘若我真的不幸遭遇了意外，凭借这个，海上警事至少能在一周之内来到这儿，收殓我的遗体，也能够将这本群岛史出版，以向世界证明我的贡献。”
Marcus: "If misfortune befalls me, at least the maritime police can collect my body here within a week and have my story publicized, if that must be my last humble contribution to the world."

马库斯：“再次踏上岛屿后，我的心情早已没有最初的惬意与闲适，些许恐惧与愁苦掩住了它们。但值得庆幸的是，我的心中仍然怀着为人类进步而努力的勇气……”
Marcus: "Stepping back onto the island, I no longer feel as relaxed as before; a touch of fear and sorrow occupies me. But thankfully, my heart still harbors the courage to strive for knowledge and human progress ..."

马库斯：“以及揭开那片令人陌生的、神秘世界的真相。”
Marcus: "And to unveil the truth of this mysterious place."

马库斯：“紧锁的房间无法阻挡我的脚步，我灵活地翻过了橱窗，爬进了这个理应早已无人问津的逼仄空间里。”
Marcus: "Door locks won't bar my way; I deftly climb through a window, entering into a cramped space that, by all rights, should have been long forgotten and moldering."

马库斯沉浸在自己唯一的世界里，在白纸上肆意漫步，在墨色的文字间徜徉。
Marcus has fully immersed herself in the world she is recreating, wandering freely along the blank page, then back to the ink-stained words.

马库斯：“厨房的门敞开着，里面空无一物，既无发霉的苹果或柑橘，也缺乏必要的蜘蛛网布景。”
Marcus: "The kitchen door is open, though there is naught inside. Not even a moldy apple, a rotten orange, nor even a cobweb."

马库斯：“我的大脑不由自主地放松了下来，一个更加显而易见，也更加令人轻松的想法从我的脑海中升起。”
Marcus: "My mind, as yet stiff with anticipation, suddenly and involuntarily relaxes as it stumbles over an idle thought."

马库斯：“这也许是一场失败的恶作剧，并且稍显拙劣。”
Marcus: "Perhaps this was nothing more than a prank, if a poorly executed one."

马库斯：“兴许只是某个心血来潮的善良海员路过了这里，为了全体航船的安全，也为了自身的荣誉，他自发地更换了灯塔的灯源，处理了锈迹斑斑的老旧螺丝……”
Marcus: "Or perhaps a kind-hearted soul, for the safety of all the ships in the area, made a habit of replacing the lighthouse's lamp and repairing its rusty screws, but bowed by some secret honor, swore to never tell any of his work ..."

马库斯：“我如此想着。”
Marcus: "These are, for now, my best theories on the nature of this mystery."

马库斯奋笔疾书，周遭的一切声响似乎都远远地消失了。
Marcus wields her pen with renewed fire, drowning out the sounds around her.

马库斯：“出于必要的环节考虑，我决定在离开之前做最后一次无关紧要的确认。”
Marcus: "Out of obligation, I settled on making a final cursory check before leaving."

马库斯：“这是一场新悲剧的开始。”
Marcus: "To think it would mark the beginning of a whole new chapter of this unfolding mystery."

马库斯：“我鬼使神差地敲响了卧室的门……当然，我很快便意识到了自己的愚蠢。”
Marcus: "Inexplicably, I found myself knocking on the bedroom door ... Stifling a chuckle, as I soon realized my folly."

“咚咚——！”
"Knock-knock-knock!"

马库斯：“而当我正准备用力推开那扇尘封的门时，它毫无征兆地打开了，挟着刺耳的嘎吱声。”
Marcus: "But as I brace to push open the door, it swings open in spite of me with a grating creak."

马库斯：“突如其来的脱力使我踉跄地跌了进去，摔到了壁炉旁。”
Marcus: "The sudden movement causes me to stagger and stumble, falling beside the fireplace inside the room."

马库斯：“不慎擦在手中的煤灰并没有如预想般的冷硬，它们有着恰到好处的余温，似乎理所当然温暖着房内的所有人。”
Marcus: "To my surprise, the coal dust under my palms holds a comforting warmth, as if it had come from a dying flame, one that could again alight."

马库斯：“两道由透明而逐渐变得清晰的身影映入我的眼帘，同时被映出的还有我强烈的恐惧。”
Marcus: "Two figures form just beyond my vision, at first pale and translucent but gaining substance, as if to match my palpable fear."

马库斯：“你们是……幽灵？！”
Marcus: "Are you ... ghosts?!"

马库斯：“而这两位幽灵先生的惶恐并不亚于我，‘你能看见我们——？！’他们发出了尖锐的呐喊。”
Marcus: "The pair of specters suddenly shriek, as if in terror, 'You can see us?!'"

马库斯：“当我们面面相觑时，一阵不紧不慢的敲门声响了起来，来自门外——”
Marcus: "And we face each other dumbfounded, before a series of knocks come from outside—steady and deliberate."

马库斯：“咚咚——！”
Marcus: "Knock-knock-knock!"

“咚咚咚——！咚咚——！”
"Knock-knock-knock ..."

马库斯：……？！
Marcus: ...?!

突兀响起的敲门声似乎并不只是作者颅内的情绪渲染。
The knocking is no longer just a figment of the author's recollection.

？？？：马库斯女士……马库斯？
???: Ms. Marcus ...?

？？？：你在里面吗——？
???: Anyone in there?

马库斯：啊……是的，我在这儿。
Marcus: Ah ... Yes. A moment please.

她急急忙忙地收拢那些在桌面上躺得四仰八合的稿纸，将它们整齐地码在一旁。
She hurriedly gathers the papers now scattered across the desk, stacking them aside neatly.

在外面的声音变得更加不耐烦之前，门终于小心翼翼地被拉开一条缝。
Lest the voice outside had grown impatient, she cautiously pulls the door open.

Flannan Isles

调查员：你好，马库斯女士。
Investigator: Greetings, Ms. Marcus.

调查员：眼前这抹冷硬的灰白色与错落的格子礼貌地朝她点了点头。
Investigator: The man before her, with his stern gray and neatly patterned tie, nods courteously.

马库斯：……您好。
Marcus: ... Greetings.

调查员：如你所见，我是圣洛夫基金会的调查员，负责埃利安锡尔区域的神秘学家登记与注册。
Investigator: You'll see here I've been sent by the St. Pavlov Foundation. I am the investigator responsible for registering all arcanists in the Eilean Siar region.

他的语气淡漠，毫不遮掩地显露着他的骄傲，理所当然。
His tone is flat and official, but his bearing exudes an air of entitlement and pride.

马库斯：基、基金会……？
Marcus: The ... Foundation?

遗憾的是，对方似乎并不能理解这个名字所代表的重要意义。
The significance behind that name has clearly been lost on the young lady.

02  孤鸟或灰白犬 / A Lone Bird, or a Gray Dog

孤鸟或灰白犬
A Lone Bird, or a Gray Dog

门前的两人面面相觑，惊疑不定的情绪迅速地泛滥开来。
The two arcanists at the door exchange identical puzzled glances.

这场面显然令深入简出的专栏作者无法应付。
The growing tension of the moment proves too much for the columnist to handle.

马库斯：糟糕……我说错了什么吗？
Marcus: Oh no ... Have I misspoken?

马库斯：一定是因为我太过沉浸在自己的世界里，怠慢了这位远道而来的先生。
Marcus: Was I so immersed in my writing that I neglected this gentleman's summons.

马库斯：我甚至想不起来敲门声响起了多少次，二、三、五……不，也许更早。
Marcus: I don't recall how many times the door was knocked. Was it two? Three? Or five? No ... perhaps more than that, indeed.

马库斯：这扇门的质地并不好，声音也比普通的木门沉闷得多，或许我应该写信给丹尼希女士，请她取用一部分稿费，替我购置一盏门铃。
Marcus: The sound of the door seems more muffled than usual. I should write Ms. Dennehy to see if she might buy me a doorbell, to send along with my fee.

马库斯：可是，到岛上来的路程并不方便，除了邮差先生，再没有人乐意到这儿来了……
Marcus: But to travel all this way is nowhere near convenient. I doubt anyone besides the post would deliver here ...

她的思绪越飘越远，一声刻意的咳嗽将她扯了回来。
Her thoughts wander further and further, only to be brought back by a deliberate and expectant cough.

调查员：直入正题吧，马库斯小姐。
Investigator: Allow me to get straight to the point, Ms. Marcus.

调查员：根据我们的调查，你已经很久没有离开弗兰南群岛了。
Investigator: Our records show you've not left the Flannan Isles in some time.

马库斯：是……是的。
Marcus: Y-Yes.

调查员：坦白说，你的行动路径为我们的登记工作造成了不少困扰。
Investigator: To be candid, this choice of accommodation has caused a bit of an inconvenience for our registrations.

调查员：尚未登记的神秘学家大多出没于皇家博览会，或是爱丁堡艺术节之类的场所……
Investigator: Most arcanists, at the very least, will frequent a bare minimum of social events like the grand exhibitions or the festivals in Edinburgh ...

调查员：内莉·梅尔巴爵士美妙的歌喉响彻在欧澳之间的大小剧院，高地运动会更是每年都能吸引数以万计的观众与运动员们投身其中。
Investigator: Lady Nellie Melba's divine voice echoes in theaters from Europe to Australia, and if nothing else, the Highland Games attract thousands of spectators and participants every year.

调查员：哪怕是我再愚钝的同僚，也愿意花上几天的时间为之庆祝。
Investigator: Somehow even the dreariest of my colleagues find time to celebrate these occasions, at least ... on occasion.

调查员：无数美好的象征都在这片土壤中发生着——恕我直言，马库斯小姐，你应该去更大的地方看看。
Investigator: I mean to say, the world is filled with all kinds of wonders. And forgive my directness, Ms. Marcus, but you ought to consider seeing more of it. And I hesitate to mention ...

马库斯：我……
Marcus: I …

接连不断的名词从夸夸其谈的口中涌出，令马库斯一阵头昏耳热。
The man's barrage of verbiage overwhelms the reclusive lady at the door.

马库斯：或许这位先生不慎感染了热病？还是我在岛上呆了太久，外面的人们都是如此的……健谈吗？
Marcus: Perhaps this gentleman has caught some kind of mania, or ... have I really been isolated on this island too long and forgotten that people are all ... talkative like this?

马库斯：冷静点，马库斯，你能应付好这一切的。
Marcus: Steady on, Marcus, you can handle this.

她握紧拳头，小声地为自己打气。

Marcus: Human science, progressive ideologies, arcanists' sports, various newly discovered critters, and many, many more ...

马库斯：它们理应被更多人知道——不论是人类，或是神秘学家。
Marcus: I wanted to share those things, the pleasant ones, they deserve to be known by more people—whether humans or arcanists.

马库斯：她的声音微微发颤，却又无比坚定。
Marcus: Her voice is trembling yet grows more resolute on each word.

马库斯：只要我一直阅读下去，我就能从中找到所有问题的答案，能为神秘学家们介绍人类社会的艺术与出色的工艺，能向人类解释困扰他们的种种神秘传闻与传说……
Marcus: If I keep reading, I'll eventually find the answers to everything. I can introduce human arts and crafts to the arcanists, and demystify arcanist fables and legends for the humans ...

马库斯：能够令人类与神秘学家相互理解，甚至能……成为两个种族之间的桥梁。
Marcus: I'll be the bridge between humans and arcanists, helping them understand one another.

马库斯：……
Marcus: …

马库斯：天、天呐……我、我竟然说了……把什么都说出来了……
Marcus: Oh dear ... D-Did I say that all out loud ...?

马库斯：她的脸颊涨得通红，大口地汲取着新鲜的、裹着湿润海风的空气。
Marcus: She awkwardly gasps trying to take in a great puff of the fresh sea air, her face flushing red from ear to ear.

略显突兀的掌声轻轻响起。
Still, a gentle but intentional clap of applause follows.

霍夫曼：马库斯小姐，这是个伟大的理想。
Hofmann: That is a great aspiration, Ms. Marcus.

霍夫曼：基金会同样在为之努力。
Hofmann: The Foundation has been working toward the very same goal.

马库斯：谢谢您的鼓励，霍夫曼女士……
Marcus: Thank you for your kind words, Madam Hofmann ...

先前的种种事故走马灯般地浮现在她的脑海，冷酷地敲打着她。
Yet, the "catastrophe" flashes through her mind once more, plunging her back into cold reality.

马库斯：事实上，我的写作反倒令它们离我越来越远了……我不仅没有向读者们普及神秘学知识，还连累了出版社……
Marcus: Only, it seems my writing has done nothing but push me further from my goal ... Not only have I failed to demystify arcanum, I failed my publisher's expectation and my readers ...

马库斯：我甚至连最基本的故事都没有写好……
Marcus: I couldn't even write a serviceable story ...

霍夫曼：你说的是这一篇吗？
Hofmann: Ah, do you mean this edition?

霍夫曼掏出了一份折了几折的报纸，弗兰南群岛的照片像是幽灵般缠上了马库斯。
Hofmann produces a folded magazine. The images of the Flannan Isles on it coldly clutch at Marcus's heart.

马库斯：您……您居然还留着它……
Marcus: Y-You didn't throw it away ...?

霍夫曼：你低估了自己，马库斯小姐。这并非一个无可救药的故事。
Hofmann: You underestimate yourself, Ms. Marcus. The story isn't as hopeless as you think.

马库斯：……我不太明白您的意思。
Marcus: ... I don't quite understand what you mean.

她的呼吸急促了起来。
Her breath quickens.

马库斯：霍夫曼女士，难道您能拯救它吗……？！
Marcus: Madam Hofmann, a-are you saying that I might still be able to turn this story around?

霍夫曼：人类的阅读习惯并不同于神秘学家。那些神秘学家们视作理所当然的细枝末节，恰恰是人类所无法理解的。
Hofmann: Humans often read things differently from arcanists. What arcanists consider perspicuous may, on the contrary, read as unfathomable to humans.

霍夫曼：相比于依托灵感的肆意发散，我们更倾向于确凿的结论。
Hofmann: Compared to the wild, divergent way of thinking of arcanists, humans are more inclined toward concrete conclusions backed by hard evidence.

霍夫曼：当我抵达岛上后，我理解了《弗兰南群岛史》的作者与读者们在思维上的隔阂。
Hofmann: After arriving here, I quickly grasped the gap in thinking between yourself and the human readers of History of the Flannan Isles.

霍夫曼：你认为有多少人了解幽灵的存在？
Hofmann: To begin with, just how many people do you think are aware of the existence of ghosts?

认真做着笔记的手停下了，它在纸上无意识地轻点。
Marcus stops in place as she goes over Hofmann's unexpectedly stunning question.

马库斯：布朗克尔公爵夫人的故事流传很广，应该有不少人试图找到她……格拉姆夫庄园的故事就要小众一些了，也许只有苏格兰的人们听说过……
Marcus: Well, I understand the story of the Brown Lady of Raynham Hall is widespread. Many people have tried to find her ... Meanwhile, the ghosts of the Graham Mansion are probably only known to the Scottish people ...

马库斯：白女士的故事在德国相当有名，但它大多被用在恐吓孩子的话术里……
Marcus: The White Lady is quite famous in Germany, but people mostly tell her stories to scare children ...

霍夫曼：很好，马库斯小姐。你理解了这一点。
Hofmann: Ah, so now you see it, don't you, Ms. Marcus?

霍夫曼：《弗兰南群岛史》刊登于《白日梦邮报》的历史版面。对它的大部分受众而言，他们并不能轻易理解幽灵的存在。
Hofmann: History of the Flannan Isles is published in the Daydream Post historical column. For most of its audience, the existence of ghosts is beyond far-fetched.

霍夫曼：你采用了三位幽灵的故事，却从未向读者们解释过它们。
Hofmann: For you to adapt the stories of three ghosts, without ever taking the time to explain the ghosts themselves to your readers. Well, is it any wonder so many doubted you?

霍夫曼：读者们看不见你的幽灵朋友，不明白幽灵先生们绝不是无所不能，不理解他们不过是如我们一般的普通人，同样茫然于弗兰南群岛的真相。
Hofmann: Your readers couldn't see and understand your ghost friends as you do. Nor could they have realized these ghosts were nearly as equally about the truths of the Flannan Isles as anyone else.

霍夫曼：人们只能跟随这些脚不着地的幽灵们窜上窜下，却从没有一次真正追上过他们。那些属于神秘学的故事如泡沫般在他们眼前破碎，却令他们浑然不觉，一无所获。
Hofmann: The readers followed the ghosts throughout the story, but never got to know them. They also couldn't fully understand the parts on arcanum. The readers didn't feel engaged.

霍夫曼收起了报纸。
Hofmann puts away the magazine.

马库斯：……
Marcus: …

马库斯：…………
Marcus: ...

马库斯：您是对的……我口口声声说着要让人类与神秘学家互相理解，盼望着能给读者带来更深层的体验，却疏漏了如此简单的问题……
Marcus: You're right ... I kept saying that I wanted humans and arcanists to understand one another, that I wanted to bring readers a more profound experience, but in doing so, I overlooked the obvious ...

马库斯：这、这实在是……
Marcus: H-How did I let this happen …

马库斯：懊悔从脚尖开始翻涌，逐渐涨了上来，几乎要将她吞没。
Marcus: A strong sense of regret surges up to her throat involuntarily.

霍夫曼：人类囿于理性的思维定式，难以触及情绪中细微的褶皱。但这同样困住了神秘学家，他们为情绪所俘获，却往往忽略最浅显的信息。
Hofmann: Humans are often unable to sense the subtle folds in emotions. They're confined by their rational senses. On the other hand, arcanists can just as easily be trapped by their emotions, overlooking the obvious, as you say. So ...

霍夫曼：这并非你个人的缺陷。
Hofmann: Don't blame yourself for this.

一阵温和的触感抵达了肩头。
A touch of warmth reaches Marcus's shoulder.

马库斯茫然地抬起头来，那支掉在地上的笔不知何时已到了对方的手中。
Marcus lifts her head blankly, not yet realizing that Hofmann picked up her pen from the ground.

霍夫曼：你能够心无旁骛地沉浸在阅读的世界，轻易地了解他人所忽略的一切。它来自你的天赋，也成了这个故事的症结。
Hofmann: As an arcanist, you can "read" the intricacies of the world that others would easily miss, but that is also the problem.

霍夫曼：而你的读者则更擅于捕捉线索，将其置入故事，置入至高的逻辑王国，以使繁复的归于简洁，使抽象的归于具象，使所有的谜都能得到唯一的结论。
Hofmann: Human readers are more used to sifting through clues and details to make logical sense of the story. They also like to simplify things and draw a single conclusion.

霍夫曼：针对《弗兰南群岛史》目前的内容，我有一些修改建议。
Hofmann: If you would permit me to offer some editorial advice for your History of the Flannan Isles, I have a suggestion.

马库斯：可……我该怎么做呢？
Marcus: P-Please tell me.

霍夫曼：你需要找到关乎自己的答案。
Hofmann: You need to find some answers of your own.

马库斯：答案……？
Marcus: Conclusions ... of my own?

霍夫曼：你是否全然理解了弗兰南群岛的一切？你能够解开关于这座岛的全部真相吗？
Hofmann: I think, you yourself do not yet know all the mysteries of the Flannan Isles? But do you believe you can unravel all the truths about this place?

马库斯：我……我做不到……
Marcus: No ... I-I don't think I can ...

马库斯：难道……我从一开始便不应该写下这篇专栏吗……？
Marcus: Perhaps ... creating this column was a mistake, after all?

霍夫曼：不，马库斯小姐。对真相一无所知并非什么不可原谅的罪行。
Hofmann: No, Ms. Marcus. Not knowing the truth is nothing at all to be ashamed of.

霍夫曼：理性推动着我们记录所见的真实，好奇心则帮助我们写下尚未了然的谜团，而两者都能成为写作的燃料。
Hofmann: Rationality drives us to understand reality as we see it. Curiosity aids us in searching for the puzzles yet to be solved. Both of them must be the fuels that power your writing.

马库斯：我以为我们笔下的一切，都应该都是确信的……
Marcus: I thought a writer should be absolutely certain about everything they write ...

马库斯：……没有真相的谜团，读者们会接受吗？
Marcus: If my readers want conclusions, how could they accept that there are puzzles that even I cannot solve ...?

霍夫曼：我想会的。
Hofmann: I think they will. If you offer them the chance.

霍夫曼：读者们能够理解你的迷茫与好奇，理解你希望揭开谜团的热情。
Hofmann: They will understand your confusion and curiosity, as well as your passion for trying to unravel these mysteries.

霍夫曼：他们因此发觉一种来自神秘学家的新奇视角，收获一段足够引人入胜的经历。
Hofmann: Whether you find the truth or not, it is the journey that keeps us engaged. What they need is an exhilarating experience from the perspective of an arcanist.

马库斯：以一个未解的谜来吸引读者，启发读者的好奇心……我的故事能做到吗？
Marcus: Spark their curiosity with puzzles I've still left unsolved ... Do you think my story could really do that?

马库斯：我能鼓动他们……去寻找真相吗？
Marcus: Can I really inspire them to seek the truth themselves ...?

霍夫曼：也许，这会是段漫长的旅程。但无论如何……
Hofmann: Perhaps. To seek the truth is a long journey, but still ...

霍夫曼：坦然承认“一段缺乏真相的现实”，本就是一种可敬的真相。
Hofmann: When we bravely admit that truth doesn't always exist, we find another truth. And this courage deserves respect.

当天的太阳并不比往常的任何一天更明亮。
The sun isn't any brighter than usual, nestled under the blanket of cloudy sea air.

但那股温暖与刺目却真实地灼痛了她。
But its dazzling warmth still seems to scorch her for the first time in a long time.

马库斯：霍夫曼女士并未在这儿停留太久。
Marcus: Madam Hofmann didn't stay long.

马库斯：她留下了那张写满了批注与建议的报纸，以及一个如她一样简洁的地址。
Marcus: But she left behind a journal filled with her comments and suggestions, and an address as concise as she was.

马库斯：我记住了它，一座位于弗兰南群岛旁的无名小岛。霍夫曼女士会在那儿继续她的工作，也许会在几周后彻底离开这儿。
Marcus: To a nameless small island near the Flannan Isles, where Madam Hofmann said she will continue her work for a few weeks before returning to the Foundation.

马库斯：临走前，她又一次提到了基金会……我同样记了下来。
Marcus: She also spoke with me about joining the Foundation before she left ...

马库斯：但眼下，我还有更重要的事需要完成。
Marcus: But for the time being, I still felt I had more pressing matters to attend to.

马库斯：我再次拿起了笔，重新面对这份糟糕的作品。
Marcus: I picked up my pen and courage again to face up to my catastrophe and overcome it.

马库斯：我不再全盘将我阅读到的故事与所有的细节都塞进那小小的方格纸中……
Marcus: I took Madam Hoffman's advice to heart. My writing was no longer crammed with every detail until I began to scribble in the margins and between the rows ...

马库斯：尽管向杜卡特先生、马歇尔先生、唐纳德先生表达了歉意，但我的删改仍然遭到了他们的不满与严正抗议……
Marcus: This revision was, quite frankly, an extensive overhaul. The gentlemen ghosts voiced some over-eager protests despite my repeated apologies ...

马库斯：霍夫曼女士的批注给予了我巨大的勇气。
Marcus: But Madam Hofmann's comments and suggestions instilled great courage in me.

马库斯：终于……
Marcus: And finally ...

马库斯：我完成了它。
Marcus: I completed it.

07  颁奖辞 / Award Speech

颁奖辞
Award Speech

灯塔忠实地朝着四周播洒光亮，海浪一丝不苟地冲洗着沿岸的礁石，闹作一团、其乐融融的幽灵先生们。
The lighthouse illuminates its surroundings, faithful and steady as always. The waves lash against the rocky shore, and the three ghostly gentlemen are in the midst of a merry uproar.

一阵海风久违地拂过了马库斯。
A soothing sea breeze brushes past Marcus, like a long-lost friend.

眼前的一切都令她熟悉而恍惚。
Everything before her is familiar yet surreal.

马库斯：先生们，你们……不再争吵了吗？
Marcus: So, gentlemen, have you stopped arguing ...?

马歇尔：嘿，朋友们，瞧瞧谁来了——！
Thomas: Look who's here, mates, and the lass a sight for dead eyes!

三人迅速地将马库斯围在中间，像是簇拥着一束终于盛开的花。
The three surround Marcus with a delicate kindness, as if encircling a flower that has finally bloomed.

唐纳德：我……我还以为你不再愿意和我们当、当朋友了……
Donald: We ... we thought you might not want to be friends with us after all our arguing ...

马库斯：抱歉，先生们……整理新的几期《弗兰南群岛史》耗去的时间令我始料未及……
Marcus: No-no, I'm sorry ... It was only that it took a little extra time for me to prepare the new edition for my History of the Flannan Isles ...

马库斯：但我总算将这几期稿件都寄给了丹尼希女士，希望这没有让她等待太久。
Marcus: But at last, I've finally completed my final draft and sent it along to Ms. Dennehy. Hopefully, I haven't made her wait too long as well.

一双略微透明的手轻拍了拍马库斯的肩膀。
A pair of transparent hands gently pat Marcus's shoulder.

杜卡特：我们都能理解你，小马库斯。创作可不是件轻松的事，否则，你也没法重新赢回读者们的心……
James: We know what you've been through, lassie. Without your perseverance and talent, winning back those readers would have been impossible.

杜卡特：这可比在蒙特卡罗一掷千金要有勇气得多！
James: And a spot more courage than a sailor betting the last of his pay in Monte Carlo!

杜卡特：可你做到了，你取得了巨大的成功——！
James: But you did it, lass. Aye, and some fine work, if I do say so myself.

马库斯：杜卡特先生，您的话太夸张了……
Marcus: Stop it now, Captain, you're just flattering me ...

马库斯紧张地缩了缩脖子。
She shrinks her neck with embarrassment.

马歇尔：我可以作证，老杜卡特说的半点儿也不夸张——噢，你一定没看最近的报纸，你的读者们又一次给你寄来了许多信！
Thomas: No, not at all, Ms. Marcus. It's just as the blowhard said, for once. You dinnae gone to collect the post yet, have you? Seems your edition made the papers already, and the postmen brought the replies.

唐纳德：但、但……这次，它们不可怕，它们很好……
Donald: And, and they're g-good letters this time ... N-Nothing terrible about them ... We checked!

一封封眼熟的信件又一次被递到了马库斯的手中。
Marcus opens one of the readers' letters.

马库斯：“很荣幸向您写下这样一封信，《弗兰南群岛史》、噢现在应该说是《到岛上来》的作者。”
Marcus: "I feel so honored to write you this letter, as a now humbled fan of your History of the Flannan Isles—oh, should I call it To the Archipelago now?"

马库斯：“我敬佩您的勇气，敬佩您敢于对自己所认可的故事进行大刀阔斧地修改……”
Marcus: "Your courage is more than admirable. Who could have thought you'd be brave enough to make such an extensive revision ...?"

马库斯：“同样，我希望能向您表达祝贺。您写下了一个如此精彩的故事，尽管它的结局尚未发表，但它仍然在我心中留下难以忘怀的印象……”
Marcus: "Let me express my warmest congratulations, for now that I see your work for what it truly is, I am so pleased to have experienced such an unforgettable fantasy tale."

马库斯：故事……？
Marcus: ... Fantasy?

马库斯：她压下了短暂的疑惑，又接着读了下去。
Marcus: Suppressing her fleeting doubts, she continues reading the letter.

马库斯：“正因为有它们、有您的存在，我们才得以了解这令人陌生、而又充满惊喜与趣味的另一个世界。”
Marcus: "Thanks to you and these ghost gentlemen you've invented, all of us following along get to experience a little piece of their world, just as you imagined it."

马库斯：“——一位苦苦等待下一节的热切读者”。
Marcus: "Yours faithfully, a reader eagerly awaiting the next edition."

马库斯：……！
Marcus: ...!

马库斯读完了信，小心翼翼地收起了不知从何而来的失落。
Marcus feels at once a sense of loss, something she tries to suppress rather than let it eat at her.

唐纳德：不、不止这些……《白日梦邮报》也、也给你写了信……
Donald: Th-There are a mess more of these letters ... oh, oh including one from the Daydream Post.

唐纳德：“尊敬的马库斯小姐，我们很高兴地向您宣布，因为您的《到岛上来》，《白日梦邮报》的销售额获得了巨大的成功。”
Donald: "Dear Ms. Marcus, we are more than delighted to share with you that, thanks to your newly updated To the Archipelago series, the Daydream Post has seen a tremendous rise in sales."

唐纳德：“读者们积极地向我们提议，他们希望能修改《到岛上来》的连载计划，建议我们每周出版两节以上的新内容。为此，出版社决定，为您的作品设立新的版面——”
Donald: "What's more, we've even received requests to change the serialization schedule—they want at least two editions per week. After discussion, the publishing house has decided to provide you with an entire new section."

唐纳德：“——《灯塔故事集》！”
Donald: "We call it, The Tall Lighthouse!"

唐纳德：“向您与您的幽灵朋友们致以最诚挚的问候。”
Donald: "Our sincerest regards to you and your ghostly friends."

纷繁的褒赞之词从不同的信封中涌出，争先恐后地挤入了马库斯的眼中。
Praise pours out from one letter after another, overwhelming Marcus's humility until she feels faint.

她踉跄着退后，又缩回了那所小小的房间。
Staggering, she retreats into the sanctuary of her room.

来自于出版社编辑的热心提议始终映在她的脑海中。
The excitement conveyed in the editor's letter still lingers as a vivid image in Marcus's mind, yet she remains more than a little perplexed.

马库斯：《到岛上来》不再发表在“历史纪实”版面了吗……？
Marcus: Did they choose to move To the Archipelago from the Documentary Literature section ...?

她难以理解其中的深意，只好继续写了下去。
Unsure about the publisher's decision, she continues writing.

一页页新的稿纸寄出，一封封热情的信件寄回了这座岛。
As new manuscripts are sent out, enthusiastic readers' letters are delivered to the island one after another.

马库斯：“一个完美的故事，调查员小姐与三位幽灵的友谊令人动容！”
Marcus: "This is what I call a perfect representation of literary fiction. The tale of this investigative columnist's friendship with these three ghosts brought tears to my eyes!"

08  小小调查员 / The Young Investigator

小小调查员
The Young Investigator

一本微茫的书在无边无际的黑夜中漂流着。
Marcus is like a book swept out to sea, floating directionless through the dead of night.

过往的每一页在马库斯的脑海中迅速掠过。
Familiar pages flash rapidly through her mind.

如在昨日，如在眼前。
Echoes of the past ringing yet again in the present.

马库斯：我想起来了……我希望能做出一个属于自己的选择……
Marcus: I remember now ... I wanted to make a choice of my own ...

突如其来的剧痛几乎要把整本书都撕裂开来。

Marcus: C-Could it be that I ...

The active cells within her body finally find an outlet amidst the surging pain.

Marcus: But ... I didn't do anything helpful for you ...

某种熟悉的温和触感又一次抵达了肩头。

Marcus: ...!

Marcus finally lifts her head.

Marcus: Madam Hofmann ... She understood that lesson all along and tried to pass it on to me.
