01  一场恶梦 / A Nightmare

黄昏的音序
A Series of Dusks

隐蔽小屋
Secret Cabin

队员Ⅰ：报告，二楼未发现目标！
Team Member I: Target not in Building 2!

队员Ⅱ：报告，房屋周边未发现目标！
Team Member II: Confirmed, target not in vicinity of the house!

塞梅尔维斯：所有地方都没有找到那家伙。
Semmelweis: They're not here.

塞梅尔维斯：情报有误，我们被摆了一道，“剃刀”。
Semmelweis: Our intel was wrong. We were set up.

剃刀：见鬼！
"Razor": A fenébe!

尽管早有预期，但在听到确切的消息时，“剃刀”还是忍不住一拳砸在了木质书柜的侧面，将书柜击出了一个大洞。
"Razor" swings his hand in frustration knocking a hole clean through a wooden bookshelf.

躺在木板上的笔记在颠簸中倒向地面，散成一座纸张堆砌的小山。
The notes lying on the shelf fall and scatter.

塞梅尔维斯：冷静点，“剃刀”。任务已经失败了，这么做也无济于事。
Semmelweis: Calm yourself, "Razor." Tearing up this place won't get us anywhere closer to our target.

塞梅尔维斯：对方是真真切切地活了两百年以上的超自然者，活生生的传说，不是那么好对付的对手。
Semmelweis: This "Beyond" has been alive for over two hundred years. It's a living legend, we knew it couldn't be this easy.

“剃刀”将自己的手臂从木质板材的破洞之中抽出。
"Razor" pulls his arm out, breathing out steadily.

剃刀：塞梅尔维斯调查员，你说得有道理，我有些失态了。
"Razor": You're right. I lost my composure.

塞梅尔维斯：虽然有些可惜，但我们必须放弃收容这只血食怪的计划了。
Semmelweis: I am not one to admit defeat, but we are out of time to contain this vampire.

塞梅尔维斯：我们必须尽快返回基金会，谁也不知道“暴雨”会在何时落下。
Semmelweis: We need to go back to the Foundation; we can't afford to take risks with the "Storm."

剃刀：我们对于血食怪的神秘术能力所知甚少，这在一定程度上阻碍了我们对它的追踪。
"Razor": We know so little about this vampire's arcane skill. It has made it next to impossible to track.

塞梅尔维斯：这也是我们需要收容它的原因。
Semmelweis: Which is all the more reason to hunt it.

塞梅尔维斯：更何况，这次的行动也不算一无所获。
Semmelweis: All that aside, our operation wasn't entirely fruitless.

塞梅尔维斯俯下身，自地面捡起了“剃刀”打落的笔记。
Semmelweis bends down to pick up the papers now lying on the ground before the shelf.

她随意地翻阅着这份笔记，若有所思。
She scans the page, absorbed in thought.

剃刀：这份笔记上写了什么？
"Razor": What does it say?

塞梅尔维斯：尽管年代有些久远，但这本笔记看样子是这只血食怪的账单。
Semmelweis: It's an old bill.

塞梅尔维斯：拉普拉斯科算中心的研究者们或许会对此有兴趣的。起码，这份账单能够帮助我们研究血食怪的日常饮食结构。
Semmelweis: The Laplace researchers might be interested in this. At the very least, it could help us study the dietary habits of vampires.

塞梅尔维斯：“出售唱片”、“购买面包”、“购买红酒”、“购买鲜花”——
Semmelweis: "Record of Delivery," a "baguette," "bottle of red wine," "bouquet of flowers"...

塞梅尔维斯：你看，这说明了血食怪并非像是我们传统认知中的那样单纯以血液为食。
Semmelweis: This suggests that the vampire doesn't exclusively feed on blood as we thought.

塞梅尔维斯：它也会吃一些别的东西。比方说，面包、红酒，还有鲜花。
Semmelweis: It apparently also dines on bread, wine, and flowers. Perhaps they consume pollen for energy.

剃刀：……
"Razor": ...

塞梅尔维斯：别当真，这只是一个玩笑，血食怪应该没有抢走蜜蜂与蝴蝶的工作的兴趣。
Semmelweis: Just a little levity, "Razor." I am not suggesting that vampires are the nocturnal answer to bees.

塞梅尔维斯：“致意亚琛”，不得不说，这位血食怪对于花卉的选择颇有品位。
Semmelweis: "Gruss an Aachen." I must say, this vampire has exquisite taste in flowers.

剃刀：嗯？这是什么？
"Razor": Hm? What's this?

就在塞梅尔维斯低头翻阅资料的时候，“剃刀”似乎注意到了角落的某个闪光的物件。
While Semmelweis is bent over the documents, "Razor" catches sight of something shiny in the corner.

剃刀：看上去似乎是一颗水晶球……不好！
"Razor": It looks like an Orbuculum ... uh-oh!

水晶球的表面浮现出了一道红色的光芒。
The Orbuculum flares up with a red glow.

剃刀：……！
"Razor": ...!

剃刀：趴下……！
"Razor": Get down!

“簌簌——”
Woosh!

数道暗红色的术法朝着房间的众人射去。
Dark red incantations shoot towards them.

折叠的长刀快速展开。“剃刀”挥动长刀，将它们一一击落。
A folded blade swings open. "Razor" swipes down on one spell after the other.

剃刀：塞梅尔维斯调查员，退到房屋的外面！
"Razor": Semmelweis, get out!

已经太迟了。
It's too late.

一道身影自塞梅尔维斯身后的阴影之中出现。
A shadow appears from behind Semmelweis.

？？？：……
???: ...

塞梅尔维斯：……！
Semmelweis: ...!

下一个瞬间，脖颈处传来剧烈的疼痛，那阵疼痛，仿佛要将身体撕扯为两半。
A sharpness pierces her neck, pain radiates through her body as if tearing her in two.

黑色的阴影逐渐覆盖上塞梅尔维斯的身体，将她压得喘不过气。
The shadow covers Semmelweis entirely, crushing her beneath it.

塞梅尔维斯：不——
Semmelweis: No—

塞梅尔维斯：不要！
Semmelweis: No!

旅馆
Hotel

塞梅尔维斯：……
Semmelweis: ...

塞梅尔维斯猛地从床上坐起，她伸手放在自己的胸口，试图安抚剧烈跳动的心脏。
Semmelweis lurches up from the bed. She reaches out to place her hand on her chest, trying to calm the erratic beating of her heart.

她剧烈地喘着粗气，仿佛要将脖颈处如同实质一般的疼痛送出身体。
She gasps for air, then puffs it out again as if trying to expel the memory.

塞梅尔维斯：……是梦吗？不，是幻觉吗？
Semmelweis: Was that a nightmare? Or perhaps a hallucination?

一个过分真实的梦境，令人难以分辨真伪。
It's difficult to tell the difference, nothing feels real now.

塞梅尔维斯深吸一口气，伸出手轻轻地触摸着脖颈之上的伤痕。
She takes a deep breath and touches the wound on her neck.

血食怪留下的伤痕已经几乎消失殆尽，若是不仔细触摸，很容易便会忽视掉这两个浅浅的坑洞。
The scars have nearly faded now, she traces her fingers over the twin puncture marks.

塞梅尔维斯：……
Semmelweis: ...

塞梅尔维斯走下柔软的床铺，来到窗边。维也纳的街道熙熙攘攘，人声鼎沸。
Semmelweis rises from her bed to look out from the window. The streets of Vienna are bustling with activity.

马车与电车并排行进在宽敞的街道上，佩戴着礼帽的绅士于路边的咖啡馆内高谈阔论，政局的紧张并未影响这座巨大城市的活力。
Carriages and trams roll side by side down the wide street, gentlemen engage in animated discussions at the cafe. It has an odd tranquility about it, like the calm before a storm.

与高山截然不同的景象让她一时间有些恍惚，足足花了三次呼吸的时间，塞梅尔维斯才终于复归平静。
It is a scene far removed from the mountains she remembers. She takes in three deep breaths.

房间门外的一阵敲门声打断了塞梅尔维斯的思绪。
A knock on the door interrupts her thoughts.

埃米尔：队长，你醒了吗？
Emil: Captain, are you awake?

塞梅尔维斯：是埃米尔吗？进来吧。
Semmelweis: What is it, Emil? Come in.

埃米尔：我按照队长你的指示，收集了维也纳最近的报纸。
Emil: As per your instructions, I collected the latest newspapers from across Vienna.

埃米尔将一叠分类整理完毕的报纸放在了桌面上。
Emil places the neatly sorted stack on the table.

塞梅尔维斯自窗边离开，随手捡起了一份报纸观看。
Semmelweis leaves the view behind as she goes to pick up a newspaper.

塞梅尔维斯：“女演员伊索尔德即将举办纪念亡兄的艺术沙龙，届时著名艺术家西奥菲尔的遗作也将第一次得到展示……”
Semmelweis: "The actress Isolde is about to hold an art exhibit in memory of her late brother, the famous artist Theophil's posthumous works will be displayed for the first time ..."

塞梅尔维斯：伊索尔德……我还记得她，在我们离开维也纳之前，她还是维也纳最受瞩目的歌剧演员。
Semmelweis: Isolde ... I remember her. She was the star of the city's opera, before I left.

埃米尔：在如今的维也纳，她依旧还是最为热门的话题。
Emil: It seems she's still the hottest topic in Vienna today.

埃米尔：不过，议论的话题早已转移到了她即将举办的艺术沙龙之上了。
Emil: But the talk has shifted to this upcoming exhibit of hers.

埃米尔：“——主办方希望通过这次艺术沙龙唤起人们对神秘学家境遇的思考与关注。”
Emil: "... The organizers hope to use it to provoke thought about and attention toward the Arcanists' plight."

埃米尔：呵，毫无疑问，这是我们这个时代最严重的顽疾之一！但一场艺术沙龙又能起到什么作用呢？
Emil: Certainly it's one of the greatest issues of our time. But what good will an art exhibit be for that?

埃米尔：它什么都改变不了！
Emil: It won't change anything!

埃米尔指了指报纸上所标注的日期。
Emil points to the date on the newspaper.

埃米尔：不过，我们回来得正好。艺术沙龙举办的时间刚好就是今天……更准确来说，是在三个小时之后。
Emil: But we are just in time. The exhibit is today ... Or even more precisely, in three hours.

塞梅尔维斯：当然了，没有什么比自杀的画家的绝笔之作更有噱头了。
Semmelweis: Of course, what could be more tantalizing to the Viennese public than a glimpse at the final works of a dead artist.

塞梅尔维斯一连翻阅了几份报纸，头版头条无一例外都印刷着伊索尔德的身影与西奥菲尔的遗照。
Semmelweis leafs through several more newspapers. Without exception, the front pages feature Isolde and Theophil's mourning portraits.

埃米尔：就连“自杀率急速攀升”、“博物馆失窃”、“政治谋杀”、“游行示威频发”这些大事，也只能在二版占据一个不大不小的方块。
Emil: "Rising suicide rates," "museum thefts," "assassinations," and "major demonstrations" are all playing second fiddle to this event.

塞梅尔维斯：这不奇怪，无论是什么年代，一般的自杀新闻都只会引起社会评论家的关注，但画家的自杀却是一场亘古不变的盛事。
Semmelweis: I'm afraid it's hardly novel. There's something about when an artist takes his own life—that potent mix of tragedy and brilliance—No matter the era, it has always grabbed the headlines.

塞梅尔维斯：搭配上“古老贵族世家最后的男性继承人”这一点……不难想象，漫天的花边新闻很快便会将这些时政新闻挤压到最不起眼的位置。
Semmelweis: All the more so that he was "the last male scion of an ancient noble house," the exact sort of gossip the Viennese gobble up. Politics and revolutions will have to wait their turn.

埃米尔：这个国家的每一个人都只关心那些没有任何用途的艺术家，没有一个人在思考应该如何解决这些民族冲突与政治问题！
Emil: I guess I shouldn't expect any difference. This city has always found a way to distract itself with sensationalism, never mind that the Empire is falling apart at the seams.

埃米尔：即便是“神秘学家”这种最容易解决的问题，他们也都不愿意花费精力去研究如何解决，只会采用粗暴到了极点的政策。
Emil: They can't even put effort into addressing the "Arcanist" problem, which I'd say is the easiest to solve.

埃米尔：他们根本不清楚，他们所谓的那些登记政策，只会让那些安分守己的神秘学家在臃肿的官僚机构之中疲于奔命。
Emil: All their registration policies do is force law-abiding Arcanists to be weighed down by unwieldy bureaucracy.

埃米尔：而对于那些本就目无法纪的神秘学疯子来说——他们从一开始就根本不在乎这个**的登记政策！
Emil: Never mind that the lawless fanatics couldn't care less about the damned registration!

埃米尔：若是不及时处理，这些“神秘学疯子”一定会把普通的神秘学家也……呃，抱歉，我不是有意的——
Emil: Mark my words, captain, these lunatics will drag the normal Arcanists down right along with them ... Uh, sorry, I didn't mean to ...

埃米尔突然想起了面前上司的神秘学家身份，尴尬地停止了原先的高谈阔论。
Emil catches himself, remembering perhaps too late that his supervisor too is an Arcanist. Embarrassed, he stops his tirade.

塞梅尔维斯：若是不及时处理，这些“神秘学疯子”一定会把普通的神秘学家也变为新的神秘学疯子，从而掀起动乱的。
Semmelweis: If left unaddressed, these "Arcanist lunatics" are certain to turn many normal Arcanists into lunatics themselves. Unrest breeds further unrest, and crackdowns.

塞梅尔维斯：但是，所有人都必须承认，在波西米亚人、克罗地亚人、罗马尼亚人——在这些更为严峻的问题面前，神秘学家还不足以让帝国为之投入过多的精力。
Semmelweis: However, I spare a little sympathy for the Empire, between the Bohemians, Croats, and Romanians, they're verging on total collapse. It is little wonder the Arcanist problem has been left to fester.

民族主义的浪潮席卷了这个时代。它就像一枚深入骨髓的生锈螺丝钉，用创口和破伤风将这个垂垂老矣的帝国折腾得死去活来。
A wave of nationalism is sweeping through this era. With each side pulling apart, the tapestry of this ancient empire seems destined to be frayed and torn.

似乎是注意到了埃米尔的坐立不安，塞梅尔维斯笑了笑。
Noticing Emil's agitation, Semmelweis smiles disarmingly.

塞梅尔维斯：噢，别在意。我和神秘学家们相处得往往不太愉快，我本人对于神秘学家同样很难称得上有什么好感。
Semmelweis: Oh, never mind that, Emil. It's often unpleasant to deal with Arcanists. I admit, I am myself, not very fond of Arcanists.

尤其是超自然者和血食怪。
Particularly when it comes to The Beyond.

塞梅尔维斯：请继续你的汇报工作，埃米尔。
Semmelweis: Please continue your report, Emil.

埃米尔：神秘学家……队长，这也是我要报告的第二件事情。
Emil: Arcanists ... Captain, that is to say, it relates to Arcanists.

埃米尔：我对维也纳的神秘学家难民群体展开了简单的调查，获取了更多关于“莱茵河的罗蕾莱”的信息。
Emil: I conducted a cursory investigation into the Arcanist refugee community here in Vienna and gathered more information about this so-called "Lorelei from the Rhine."

“莱茵河的罗蕾莱”。
"Lorelei from the Rhine."

即便早有心理准备，在听到这个名字时，塞梅尔维斯的呼吸仍旧在一瞬间变得急促了起来。
Even though she has been expecting it, Semmelweis catches her breath as she hears the name.

她下意识地想起了那封信件。那封她并未向基金会的任何人透露过内容的信件。
She instinctively recalls that letter—the one whose contents she has not disclosed to anyone in the Foundation.

埃米尔：罗蕾莱在这些社会底层的神秘学家中的声望，比我们想象中的还要高。
Emil: Lorelei's reputation among the lower-class Arcanists is beyond what we had imagined.

埃米尔：依靠着“预言”，她成功地将众多神秘学家团体凝聚在了一起，让这些神秘学家团体一直没有与帝国发生直接的冲突。
Emil: Using her gift of "prophecy," she has united many Arcanist groups, so far preventing them from directly clashing with the Empire.

塞梅尔维斯：“预言”？
Semmelweis: "Prophecy?"

埃米尔：我更愿意称它为，“神秘学家的胡言乱语”。
Emil: I prefer to call it "Arcanist nonsense."

埃米尔：——只有神秘学家会说，只有神秘学家会听，也只有神秘学家会信以为真的胡话。
Emil: It seems to me that only Arcanists could come up with and believe such superstition.

塞梅尔维斯：但神秘学家难民群体都对她的“胡言乱语”深信不疑。
Semmelweis: But nonsense or otherwise the Arcanist refugees are convinced.

埃米尔：罗蕾莱声称，世界是一条流淌着河流的河谷，而我们是在河流两侧生活的蚁群。
Emil: According to this prophecy of hers the world is a valley with a flowing river and we are colonies of ants living on either side of it.

埃米尔：在过去的时日中，我们一直在这条河流旁边繁衍、生活，享受着河流带来的恩惠，就如同我们的祖祖辈辈一样。
Emil: In times past, we lived and died beside the river, enjoying its bounty, just as our ancestors did.

埃米尔：然而，如今的河流却陷入了前所未有的混乱之中——洪水将会从上游奔腾而下，吞噬沿途的一切事物。
Emil: Today, however, the river is facing a cataclysm. The floodwaters will soon come rushing down to devour everything in their path.

埃米尔：我们所处的这条河谷同样不会例外，它也同样将会被洪灾所吞没。
Emil: And our valley will be no exception.

埃米尔：所有人都对这场无法逆转，不可战胜的灾害无能为力。我们唯一能做的，也只有想方设法地寻求帮助，以求逃离这场滔天的洪水。
Emil: In her words, we are entirely helpless against this irreversible reckoning. All we can do is seek help to escape it.

埃米尔：而罗蕾莱则宣称，自己会成为那个帮助众人的救助者。
Emil: But Lorelei claims to be the savior who will rescue everyone.

塞梅尔维斯：……！
Semmelweis: ...!

埃米尔：相当俗套的故事！我至少听过八个版本的这类传说了。
Emil: It's all a bit cliche, isn't it? She may as well just call herself "Noah."

在塞梅尔维斯耳中，这个故事却有着截然不同的含义。
But to Semmelweis, this story meant something entirely different.

塞梅尔维斯：罗蕾莱口中的“洪水”……莫非指的是“暴雨”？

女孩的歌声戛然而止，周遭散落的追随者和她同样不知所措，等待着下一个旋律出现。
Her singing halts. The believers scattered around her freeze, waiting for her to continue.

塞梅尔维斯：你终于清醒了。
Semmelweis: You're finally back.

塞梅尔维斯：现在叫上你的人跟我走，我们还来得及去基金会。
Semmelweis: I need you to get these people moving. We can still make it to the Foundation.

时间稍许有些紧迫，不过结果还好，只要抓起罗蕾莱的手，她的追随者和她就都会被带回基金会。
Time is tight, but it'll work. As long as she holds Lorelei's hand, the others will follow.

这次维也纳之旅不但保住了自己的性命，还救下了恩人的未来。
She can save her own life—and the one who gave her hope.

活着，而且心情舒畅，还有什么比这更好的？
Alive and in good spirits—what could be better?

罗蕾莱：小……塞梅尔维斯？慢一些，你走得太快了，小心路边的小石子。
Lorelei: Little—Semmelweis? Slow down, you're walking too fast. There are stones in the path ahead.

战斗之后的激情仍未褪去么？还是达成新生后的兴奋所致？
Is she still riding the rush of battle? Or is this some kind of delayed exhilaration from being reborn?

塞梅尔维斯屡次调整自己的脚步以配合罗蕾莱等人的步伐，慢些……慢些……
Semmelweis keeps adjusting her pace to match Lorelei and the others. Slower … slower ...

慢些……慢些，慢些，慢些慢些，慢——
Slower, slower, slower, slower, slow—

最终她的意识化为一团黑雾。
Her consciousness fades into black mist.

瓦伦缇娜：我说了，我的预感可是特别的准。
Valentina: You really should learn to trust me. My intuitions are always on point.

瓦伦缇娜：别担心，小罗蕾莱，她只是转化之后有些兴奋过度，过一会儿就好了。
Valentina: Don't worry, little Lorelei. Our pet's just a little overexcited from her transformation. She'll be right as rain, soon enough.

瓦伦缇娜：不，不，你们没法把一团雾气怎么样，把她给我吧，我有办法。
Valentina: No, no—there's nothing you can do with that cloud of mist. It's better you leave her to me.

瓦伦缇娜：而你们最好不要辜负她的好意，否则这会，嗯，“有损她的心情”。
Valentina: And you'd best not waste her goodwill. Otherwise, well … I suspect it would "really ruin her mood."

瓦伦缇娜：基金会就在那个方向，现在还来得及靠双脚赶去。
Valentina: The Foundation's that way. Keep going, and you might just make it.

等到塞梅尔维斯恢复意识时，她正平躺着接受瓦伦缇娜的俯视，脑袋后方僵硬而冰冷。
When Semmelweis wakes, she's lying flat with Valentina peering down. The back of her head feels stiff and cold.

瓦伦缇娜：你总算醒了，睡得还好么？原谅我找不到柔软又温暖的枕头。
Valentina: Finally awake? Sleep well? A thousand pardons—I couldn't find a fluffy pillow for you.

塞梅尔维斯：你早知道会变成这样。
Semmelweis: You knew this would happen.

瓦伦缇娜：很可惜我没有为你的转化帮上什么忙，对不对？
Valentina: Perhaps I did, but then again, I didn't hold up my end of the bargain, did I?

没时间再与瓦伦缇娜进行无谓的争吵，塞梅尔维斯迅速起身，朝基金会的方向奔去。
There's no time for another pointless debate. Semmelweis scrambles up to her feet, intent on running to the Foundation.

瓦伦缇娜：你不可能赶得上了，现在恐怕只有我能帮你回避“暴雨”，要不要跟我来？
Valentina: You won't make it, I'm afraid. Now, only I can shelter you from the "Storm." Will you come with me?

一句简单的话语拦下了塞梅尔维斯的脚步，她转过身去，试图用视线杀死眼前仍保持着笑容的老血食怪。
One simple sentence stops her cold. She turns, trying to pierce the smiling old vampire with her glare.

塞梅尔维斯：不戴面具。
Semmelweis: No mask.

瓦伦缇娜：放心吧，我自始至终就没有带着面具。
Valentina: Do you see me wearing one?

塞梅尔维斯：哎……
Semmelweis: *sigh*

瓦伦缇娜：你为了生存而挣扎时的表情要更有趣些，塞梅尔维斯。
Valentina: You were much more entertaining when you were desperate to survive, Semmelweis.

瓦伦缇娜：这里的看守已经都被我请出去了，放松些如何？
Valentina: I've already sent the guards away. Will you please just relax?

塞梅尔维斯：我在和基金会彻底失联后，与一名重塑之手的血食怪一起，在重塑之手的秘密安全屋里度过了“暴雨”……
Semmelweis: I've completely lost contact with the Foundation, sitting in a secret Manus Vindictae safehouse, riding out the "Storm" with one of their vampires …

塞梅尔维斯：我该怎么样汇报这些信息？汇报多少？如果恳求琳赛包庇我，她能做到哪一步？
Semmelweis: Could I still report back to the Foundation? What would I tell them—how much would I tell them? Even if I begged Madam Lindsay to cover for me … Would she?

塞梅尔维斯：哎……
Semmelweis: *sigh*

瓦伦缇娜：我的邀请仍然有效，塞梅尔维斯。
Valentina: The offer still stands, Semmelweis.

塞梅尔维斯：我拒绝，回去接受基金会审查带来的坏处要远小于加入重塑之手的代价。
Semmelweis: And I still refuse it. Whatever punishment awaits me with the Foundation will still be worth it compared to the cost of joining Manus Vindictae.

瓦伦缇娜：忘了重塑之手吧，海因里希不在任何一条小巷子里，也没有其他人来找我，我想我们之间的契约被单方面撕毁了。
Valentina: Then forget Manus Vindictae. Whether he's lying dead in some alleyway or simply ditched us, Heinrich won't be coming here. Nor will anyone else for that matter. I'd say our deal has been broken—unilaterally.

瓦伦缇娜：先是你，然后是海因里希，我的维也纳之行真是充满了欺诈和毁约。
Valentina: First, you, then, Heinrich. This Vienna trip seems to be one betrayal after another.

瓦伦缇娜：我现在想邀请你加入的，是一场血食怪下午茶话会，目前与会者只有我和你。
Valentina: But enough of that; I'm inviting you into something else entirely—consider it an afternoon tea party for vampires only. So far, it's just the two of us.

塞梅尔维斯：请问成为你的伙伴对我能有什么好处？
Semmelweis: And what do I stand to gain from being your partner?

瓦伦缇娜：你在帮助小罗蕾莱时，有索取任何好处么？你冒着巨大的风险去为她的安危奋斗时，难道不是全凭着善意？
Valentina: Did you expect something in return when you helped our little Lorelei? You risked everything for her safety, purely out of goodwill.

瓦伦缇娜：不能把这份善意的十分之一分给我，和我做个朋友么？
Valentina: Won't you spare just a tenth of that goodwill for me? Be my friend?

塞梅尔维斯：帮助罗蕾莱那样的人令我心情愉悦，这就是我得到的好处。但与你这样的人做朋友，我一点都不会开心。
Semmelweis: Helping someone like Lorelei makes me happy. That's what I get out of it. Being friends with you … I'm not sure if that would bring me anything close to joy.

瓦伦缇娜：你对我真是充满恨意，那如果我现在离开这儿，你会好受些么？
Valentina: So full of hate. What if I were to disappear right now—would that make you feel better?

塞梅尔维斯：有人当着我的面消失在“暴雨”中一样会令我心情不悦，所以请不要这么做。
Semmelweis: Watching someone vanish into the "Storm" before my eyes would do nothing to improve my mood. So no, please don't.

瓦伦缇娜：那又有什么关系呢？不论“暴雨”回到十年前，还是百年前，那里可能都有一个我，只不过忘掉了几年琐事而已。
Valentina: And what does it matter? Whether the "Storm" takes us back ten years or a hundred, there's likely to be a version of me there—only short a few years.

瓦伦缇娜：于世界于我，这都算不上多大的损失。
Valentina: For the world, and for me … it isn't so much of a loss.

塞梅尔维斯：这可真是非常新颖的猜想，我相信拉普拉斯的人听到了一定会赞扬你，并以你的名字来命名这个新假设。
Semmelweis: What a wonderfully novel hypothesis. I'm sure Laplace would name it after you in their next report.

瓦伦缇娜：回到茶话会的话题，你真的不加入我么？
Valentina: So—back to our tea party. Are you sure you don't want to stay and join me?

塞梅尔维斯：既然这是个邀请，我想我应该拥有拒绝的权利？
Semmelweis: You say it's an invitation; I assume I have the right to decline?

瓦伦缇娜：当然，你当然有。而距离“暴雨”过去还有一段时间，所以我也有很多时间来说服你。
Valentina: Naturally, my dear. But there's still some time yet before the "Storm" ends—so I hope you won't mind my trying to convince you.

塞梅尔维斯：……我想这就是活下去的代价，必须在这儿忍受一个血食怪的骚扰。
Semmelweis: So this is the price of survival—enduring the nagging of a vampire in some dusty old place.
