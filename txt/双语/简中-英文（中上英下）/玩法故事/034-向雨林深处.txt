追捕行动
Manhunt

少校办公室
Major's Office

洛佩拉：不，这次的行动我不会参加。
Lopera: Sorry, Sir, but I respectfully decline this operation.

卡西扬少校：但你在卡洛斯将军的部署名单里，少尉。
Major Kasyan: You're already on General Carlos's deployment list, Lieutenant.

对话才刚开始不久，年轻少校的喉头就已经有些干涩，想必他已经一百次地预见过说服洛佩拉的工作会有多么艰苦。
They haven't been speaking long, but the young major's throat is already drying up. He's run through this conversation a hundred times in his head.

他有气无力地拍了拍手中的一沓名单与简报。
Wearily, he straightens the stack of lists and briefings in his hand.

卡西扬少校：“……索科罗居民与天使兄弟会暗中勾结，私运武器，阴谋杀害芝诺士兵，袭击营地……”总之，线报是这么说的。
Major Kasyan: "... These people are working with the Apostles Brotherhood, smuggling weapons, plotting to kill Zeno soldiers and attack our outposts ..." The intel is crystal clear.

洛佩拉：你看不出来吗？名单上的嫌疑人只是普通的渔民和船夫，他们和兄弟会可闹不到一块去。
Lopera: Am I the only one around here who isn't completely loco? These "suspects" are all just ordinary people—fishers and boatmen. They've got nothing to do with the Brotherhood.

卡西扬少校：我提醒过卡洛斯将军，但他确实相当信任他的线人提供的情报。
Major Kasyan: Believe me, I've spoken to General Carlos about your concerns, but he's adamant that our informant's intel is correct.

洛佩拉：卡洛斯脑壳里长的其实是巴拉纳松果，这下我的话应验了吧？
Lopera: Then Carlos has a Paraná pinecone where his brain should be. When have I been wrong? Tell me—when?

卡西扬少校：对长官放尊重些，少尉！
Major Kasyan: Lieutenant, watch your tone when speaking about a superior officer!

少校的嘴角轻微抽动。他强压住不敬的笑意，压低了声音。
The edges of the major's mouth twitch. He suppresses a disrespectful smile and lowers his voice.

卡西扬少校：……把它当作治安任务就好，抓几个人审一审，没有问题就放走。
Major Kasyan: It's just like any other security operation. We snatch up a few suspects, do some interrogations, then let them go if there's nothing fishy.

洛佩拉：治安任务？你是第一天在圣保罗当兵吗？只要卡洛斯敢带人去市区，当地人保管叫他吃枪子。
Lopera: A security operation? This your first day in São Paulo or something? If Carlos sends our people into that city, then the locals will be feeding us all lead by the day's end.

洛佩拉：然后因为他们开了枪，卡洛斯就会扯着嗓子叫：暴乱！果真是蓄谋已久的暴乱！
Lopera: And you wait, 'cause when they fire first, there'll be "Generalissimo Carlos" screaming—"It's a riot! It's a riot!"

卡西扬少校：……
Major Kasyan: ...

卡西扬少校：洛佩拉，下命令的人不是我……但我会尽量和平地推进行动。
Major Kasyan: Lieutenant, orders are orders. I don't have any leeway with our objectives, but I'll do what I can to make sure this plays out as peacefully as possible.

卡西扬少校：你不需要直接参与战斗，你的任务是去圣保罗捞一个人，名叫桑德罗。
Major Kasyan: Besides, you shouldn't need to get your hands dirty. You have your own mission in the city: to retrieve someone named Sandro.

洛佩拉：桑德罗？他又是什么人？
Lopera: What? Sandro? Who's that?

卡西扬少校：背景信息中只提到桑德罗和兄弟会的人闹过一些不愉快，芝诺要派人找到他并带回芝诺保护，免得他遭到报复。
Major Kasyan: They're keeping the details on a need-to-know basis. I guess Sandro had some unpleasant run-ins with the Brotherhood. Zeno wants to find him and bring him back for his own protection—to prevent any retaliation.

洛佩拉：我懂了，卡洛斯要找人替他干脏活——这我可不干。
Lopera: Need-to-know? Yeah, I know exactly what I need to know; I know that Carlos needs someone to do his dirty work—and I'm not doing it.

洛佩拉：这事你扔给别的什么人都行，我呢——哦，我今天还要去圣保罗买一些绿蜂胶。
Lopera: Let some other grunt bite that bullet. I'll be in São Paulo, picking up some própolis verde.

洛佩拉在随身携带的坩埚上轻轻一拍，像一只鲜艳夺目的鸟儿那样扑腾着离开了。
Lopera taps the cauldron in her arms as she flutters off without any hesitation.

卡西扬少校：……唉。
Major Kasyan: *sigh*

少校没有刻意阻拦，他回到文件堆里，思考着如何兑现自己“和平推进行动”的承诺。
The major doesn't stop her; he knows he can't. So he returns to his operation notes, while rubbing his temples with one hand.

街角旅馆房间
Hotel Room

从芝诺基地到圣保罗市区的旅途她早已驾轻就熟，找到一个安全隐蔽的落脚处更是轻而易举。
The journey from the base to São Paulo is a well-worn path to her. As are the familiar streets that lead to her safe and hidden spot in town.

她拨通记忆中的号码，在沙哑的信号忙音中平复一路奔波后的喘息。
She calms her breathing as she dials and is met with a raspy, busy signal.

洛佩拉：……
Lopera: ...

圣保罗街头扬起的灼热尘埃从窗缝中钻进来——躁动而浓烈，多半是因为其中混着年事已高的大排量汽车尾气。
Hot, sticky dust rises up from São Paulo's streets and seeps through the cracks of her window blinds, mixed fragrantly with engine exhaust and the smell of old fruit.

莫莉德尔：……你好？
Moldir: Hello?

洛佩拉：姐姐！
Lopera: Molly!

莫莉德尔：……洛佩拉？你为什么要用普通电话线路？
Moldir: Pera? Why are you calling from an unsecured phone line?

洛佩拉：这你就别管了，出发之前我得和你说说圣保罗的事。
Lopera: Never mind that. I need to tell you something about São Paulo before I set off.

莫莉德尔：出发之前……你在干什么？碰上什么麻烦了？
Moldir: Before you set off? What is it? Pera, you're not causing trouble again, are you?

洛佩拉：卡洛斯那个白痴就是最大的麻烦。
Lopera: That idiota Carlos is the only cause of trouble here.

莫莉德尔：卡洛斯？你已经说过他很多次了，父亲认为没有必要——
Moldir: General Carlos? You've mentioned him in your reports. Father thinks ...

一阵模糊而沉重的声音中断了姐妹二人的谈话。
A vague, heavy sound from the other end interrupts the sisters' conversation.

莫莉德尔：——嗯？对，是洛佩拉……嗯，好的。
Moldir: Hmm? Yes, it's Lopera. Understood, sir.

洛佩拉：……姐姐？
Lopera: Molly?

伊戈尔将军：——是我。
Admiral Igor: It's me.

熟悉而遥远的声音饱含着克制的力量，如同地平线之上滚落的雷鸣。
The voice that comes next is deep yet distant and filled with restrained power, like thunder rolling over the horizon.

洛佩拉：父亲！我本来还想让姐姐转告你——
Lopera: Father! I was going to have Molly tell you ...

伊戈尔将军：如果你想说的是卡洛斯的抓捕行动，那么，我已知情，圣保罗基地提交过一份报告。
Admiral Igor: This concerns General Carlos's operation? Yes, I'm aware. The São Paulo base already submitted their plans.

洛佩拉：没错，卡洛斯的妄想症又发作了，你肯定能看出来的，对吧？
Lopera: It's Carlos' paranoia on the rampage again. You can see that, can't you?

洛佩拉：我得阻止他，父亲，这么弄下去圣保罗肯定会乱套的。
Lopera: I have to stop him, Father. If this continues, he'll have the whole city plunged into chaos.

伊戈尔将军：……
Admiral Igor: ...

洛佩拉：……父亲？
Lopera: Father ...?

将军的沉默有些出乎意料——洛佩拉本以为父亲对此事的态度会更加果决。
The admiral's silence is unexpected. Lopera thought he would be more decisive.

毕竟在她看来，阻止卡洛斯的愚行是第一要务，这件事没有多少考虑的空间。
In her eyes, stopping Carlos had become their highest priority.

伊戈尔将军：你在部署名单内吗？
Admiral Igor: You are on the deployment list, are you not?

洛佩拉：当然，我……
Lopera: Of course, I ...

不安的预感像蜘蛛一样爬上心头，这样的对话并不是第一次发生。
A sense of unease crawls up her spine like a spider. She knows where this conversation is headed.

洛佩拉：……我在。
Lopera: ... Yes.

伊戈尔将军：为什么拒绝执行命令？
Admiral Igor: Then, you are refusing your orders?

洛佩拉咬住嘴唇，无法作答。
Lopera bites her lip, unable to answer.

伊戈尔将军：卡洛斯将军是你的长官，就算他确实是个白痴，这一点也不会改变。
Admiral Igor: General Carlos is your superior officer. This does not change even if he is a fool.

伊戈尔将军：长官下令，士兵执行，无论是要冲上滩头还是放弃阵地。
Admiral Igor: It's a soldier's duty to follow orders. An officer gives orders, a soldier executes them—whether it is charging a beachhead or abandoning a position.

洛佩拉：可是……我可以弄清楚到底发生了什么，父亲，我能证明卡洛斯的情报是错误的。
Lopera: But, I can find out what's really happening. Father, I'll prove that his intel is wrong.

伊戈尔将军：以士兵的身份挫败上级的作战行动？
Admiral Igor: Are you a soldier, Lopera? Or a saboteur?

伊戈尔将军：洛佩拉，再往前进一步，这就可以被视作叛乱。
Admiral Igor: Lopera, if you cross that line, it could be considered treason.

洛佩拉：——不！我绝没有这么想过！
Lopera: What?! No, I didn't even think of that!

叛乱——这个词像钟锤一样敲上她的太阳穴，在颅内引起阵阵嗡鸣。
Treason. The word strikes at her like a bell hammer, ringing through her skull.

手指紧紧扒住桌角，指节逐渐失去血色。
Her fingers grip the edge of the table tightly, knuckles losing color.

洛佩拉：不是这样的……
Lopera: It can't be ...

伊戈尔将军：你的任务是什么？
Admiral Igor: What is the mission?

她吸了吸鼻子，尽力重拾讲述的勇气。
She sniffs, mustering the courage to continue.

洛佩拉：是……去找一个叫做桑德罗的人。
Lopera: Sandro. I-I'm to find someone ... named Sandro.

伊戈尔将军：那就去找到桑德罗，严格遵守指令，不要做多余的事，完成任务后向你的长官汇报。
Admiral Igor: Then find this Sandro as instructed. Complete your mission and report for duty. Do nothing else.

“桑德罗”，这个名字化作一根纤细的救命稻草，正在远处摇晃着向她招手。
The name becomes a slender lifeline, a path back into his graces.

洛佩拉：明白了，父亲，我一定会找到桑德罗的。
Lopera: Yes, Father. I'll find him.

伊戈尔将军：稍后我会评估你提供的信息。莫莉德尔，你和她还有没说完的话吗？
Admiral Igor: I will look over your reports about the general, after you complete your mission. Moldir, is there anything else?

从听筒中传出的模糊声音判断，电话正在被转移到另一人的手上。
Muffled sounds come from the receiver as the phone is handed over.

莫莉德尔：……父亲没有说错，洛佩拉。
Moldir: You know he's right, Pera.

洛佩拉：我知道……但父亲真的会一直放着卡洛斯不管吗？
Lopera: Hmm. Yes, I know, but how long will we keep turning a blind eye to the damage that Carlos is doing?

莫莉德尔：芝诺人员和物资的补充速度仍然远远没有达到需要的标准，暂时没有人能替换他的位置。
Moldir: Zeno is as slow to replace personnel as it is to restock supplies. There's no one available to take his post.

莫莉德尔：……不过，我答应你会找恰当的时机再与父亲谈论一次圣保罗的事。
Moldir: But I promise I'll discuss the situation in São Paulo with Father again, when it is suitable.

不确定的承诺已经是最高的让步标准。
There's nothing emptier than a promise awaiting suitable conditions.

莫莉德尔：那么……祝你行动顺利，洛佩拉。
Moldir: Good luck with your mission, Pera.

02  骰子游戏 / Dice Game

骰子游戏
A Game of Dice

观光游船
River Cruise

一艘游船在林木茂密、远离人烟的河岸边暂时停靠，肚中满载着的外国游客兴奋地朝外探头探脑。
A sightseeing boat docks along a densely wooded riverbank. From the deck, it could be mistaken for the depths of the jungle. Excited tourists poke their heads out to look around.

如果期待着一场顺流而下的丛林探险，又不愿真的涉足危机四伏的亚马逊雨林，那么圣保罗周边会是个不错的选择。
For those seeking the appearance of adventure without venturing deep into the perilous Amazon, the outer environs of São Paulo are a fine substitute.

？？？：——是啊，拍几张照片回去，我敢保证你的欧洲朋友看不出一丁点破绽。
???: That's right, bring a few photos back home with you. I guarantee your friends back in Europe won't notice a difference.

外国游客：可这说到底并不是亚马逊，就像我前天买到的假鳄鱼皮包一样。
Tourist: But when all's said and done, this ain't the Amazon—it's as fake as the crocodile bag I bought the other day.

外国游客：那店主人还说“荷美斯”的皮匠私下里都在他手上买生皮呢。
Tourist: Can't believe I fell for it, even after he said "Ermees" secretly buys all their hides from him.

老船长用亲切的大手掌拍了拍游客掺了涤纶的西装，自然而然地无视了他不那么典雅的品牌名念法。
The old captain pats the tourist's polyester suit with a friendly hand, ignoring his less-than-elegant pronunciation of the name.

？？？：鳄鱼？我最亲爱的老朋友，你早些来找我就不会被骗了。
???: Crocodiles? My friend, if only you had come to me earlier, I would never have let you be cheated like that.

？？？：我认识一个不要命的家伙，在雨林里偷偷养了好几条货真价实的宽吻鳄，那些怪物咬开乌龟壳也就是一口的事。
???: In fact, I know of a certain daring individual who keeps a few genuine broad-snouted caimans out in the rainforest—The kind that can crush a turtle shell with a single bite.

？？？：我可以介绍你去看看——对了，朋友，你带着美元的，对吧？
???: I'd be happy to make some introductions. By the way, my friend, you're carrying American dollars, aren't you?

外国游客：……
Tourist: ...

外国游客：我早该猜到克鲁塞罗根本不顶用。
Tourist: So what, is all this cruzeiro just completely useless?

？？？：哈哈，趁着你手里的东西变成废纸之前换回美元吧。
???: Haha! Not useless at all. It's great for kindling, confetti, even waste paper for your parrot. Just take my advice, friend; exchange what you have for dollars before it sinks any lower.

？？？：对了，别让银行里的那些小白脸有机会给你下套，我可以带你去见一个熟人，专做兑钱的生意，保管实惠。
???: Ah, but don't let those suits at the bank trick you. A friend of mine specializes in exchanging currencies. I guarantee you it's a better deal.

外国游客：让我想想……
Tourist: Let me think.

他和这位热情的本地人已经是认识了足足三个小时的好友，而后者还向前者传授了双人凯纳斯特牌的技巧。
He and this enthusiastic guide have been friends for a good three hours, and in that time, his host even taught him the rules to double-deck Canasta.

考虑到这些，这份钱币兑换的提议应该相当可信——不过，一团明晃晃的橘色冒出来打断了游客的思考。
Somehow, this currency exchange proposal begins to seem almost credible. However, a face wreathed in bright orange interrupts the tourist's thoughts.

洛佩拉：抱歉，先生，能让我和罗德里戈船长说两句吗？
Lopera: Excuse me, sir. Mind if I speak with Captain Rodrygo for a moment?

外国游客：嘿，你走丢了吗，孩子？
Tourist: Hey, kid, you lost or something?

罗德里戈：洛佩拉？！我都不知道你还有兴趣来我的破船上玩呢。
Rodrygo: Lopera?! I didn't know you were interested in seeing my shabby old boat.

外国游客：你们是亲戚？
Tourist: Are you two related?

洛佩拉：我和他可没有半点关系。
Lopera: What makes you think I'm related to this old picareta?

罗德里戈：我一直以为自己有个不怎么见面的可爱女儿呢，洛佩拉，你这么说可是会让我这个老光棍流眼泪的。
Rodrygo: What a shame! You know, I've always considered you like the violently hot-headed daughter I never had, Lopera. Don't go breaking this old man's heart.

外国游客：孩子，我和罗德里戈先生还有事情要商量，你先去别处玩好吗？等会儿我请你喝船上的可可。
Tourist: Yeah, whatever. Just beat it, kid. Mr. Rodrygo and I have business to discuss. Why don't you go play somewhere else for a while? If you're good, maybe I'll get you some hot cocoa later.

外国游客：那味道可真不错，双份奶油，双份糖，和粥一样挂在你的喉咙里慢慢滑下去——罗德里戈船长推荐给我的，你肯定也会喜欢。
Tourist: It's delicious—double cream, double sugar, the stuff slides down your throat like porridge—the captain was just telling me about it. You'll love it.

洛佩拉：先生，不管罗德里戈和你说过什么，我劝你一个字也别信，包括可可。
Lopera: Senhor, whatever Rodrygo has told you, I advise you not to believe a word of it. Including anything about his cocoa.

罗德里戈翻了个白眼，接着咧嘴一笑。
Rodrygo rolls his eyes, then grins.

洛佩拉：来这儿你就该试试瓜拿纳果汁，这比可可和咖啡带劲多了。
Lopera: But why don't you try the guarana juice while you're here? It'll perk you up more than any cocoa or coffee.

外国游客：是……是吗？
Tourist: I ... did you say guarana?

洛佩拉：对，去岸上看看吧，说不定有人推着车在卖呢。
Lopera: Of course. You should check further down the shore. There's bound to be a stall somewhere selling it.

沿岸确实聚集了不少流动的摊贩，这里离市区还不算太远，很适合小商贩携着稀奇古怪的特产来卖个好价钱。
True to her word, there are many stalls bunched along the shoreline, which, not far from the city, serves as a fine spot for small traders to sell their exotic goods at a good price.

外国游客：太好了！我就该多尝试一些平时见不到的东西。
Tourist: Hot dog! Well, I guess I should try something special to the area.

外国游客：回头见，罗德里戈船长，还有……这位小姐。如果有瓜拿纳果汁的话，我会给你们也带一些回来的。
Tourist: See you later, Captain, and ... Miss, if I find any guarana juice, I'll be sure to bring some back for you.

带着探寻新刺激的期许，游客忙不迭地转身下船去往河岸。
Anticipating the new experience awaiting him, the tourist disembarks in a rush toward the riverbank.

罗德里戈：这下鱼儿落回水里啦，小鸟儿。
Rodrygo: You do realize that you just let a big fish slip away, right? Silly little birdie.

罗德里戈无可奈何地摇晃着脑袋，不过他的语气中听不出有多少遗憾。
Rodrygo shakes his head in resignation, but his tone lacks any hint of anger.

他反倒露出惬意而憨厚的笑容，像一个标准的热带人那样豁达。
Instead, he opens his mouth into a wide and knowing smile.

洛佩拉：只要你能帮我找到一个人，芝诺给你的酬劳一定比这条小鱼要多。

Lopera: Wait! Rodrygo, I think I've figured it out! You're not used to the side effects of these things; they can make you a little ...

洛佩拉打算用一些直接有效的方式加速罗德里戈的清醒——她从脚边的泥水坑里捧起一滩水。
Lopera decides on a method to speed Rodrygo's recovery, scooping up a handful of water from a muddy puddle.

砰——
Bang—

罗德里戈：这不是枪声，当然不是枪声，我们住的地方非常安全。
Rodrygo: No, that couldn't be a gunshot. Of course, it wasn't. This place is very safe, my little birdie.

洛佩拉：……？
Lopera: ...?

女孩的动作忽然僵住，将要泼洒的泥水留在掌心，一点点从指缝溜走。
The girl freezes. The muddy water she was about to splash him with seeps out through her fingers.

罗德里戈：对，你猜得一点没错，外面正在庆祝狂欢节呢，不要再发抖啦。
Rodrygo: Of course. They're only celebrating the carnival outside. There's no need to be afraid.

洛佩拉：罗德里戈？
Lopera: Rodrygo?

罗德里戈：嗯，我会为你买狂欢节礼物的，但是你要答应我一件事。
Rodrygo: Hmm, yes, I'll go get you a gift from the carnival, but you have to promise me one thing.

罗德里戈：永远不要踏出这个房间，永远……不然我只能把你锁在屋子里了。
Rodrygo: Never leave this room, ever, or I'll have to lock you in.

罗德里戈：你决不能去参加狂欢节。
Rodrygo: You can't go to the carnival. No, you must not leave, not tonight.

手中的泥水从手心砸向了脚背。
The remaining mud splashes down onto her foot.

洛佩拉：罗德里戈。
Lopera: Rodrygo.

罗德里戈：嗯……嗯？
Rodrygo: Hmmm.

男人的目光越来越浑浊，直至变为两汪模糊难辨的污水坑。
His eyes have grown increasingly cloudy, until they become like two indistinguishable pools of gray water, and then just as quickly the clouds recede.

罗德里戈：噢，你摔倒了吗？手上为什么全是泥巴？
Rodrygo: Epa, you fell over a bit back there, didn't you? Your hands are all covered in mud.

洛佩拉：没什么，你醒了就好。
Lopera: It's nothing. Welcome back to reality.

洛佩拉走在了罗德里戈前面，留给后者一个无法理解的背影。
Lopera walks ahead of Rodrygo, leaving him staring at her blankly as if in a trance.

洛佩拉：我们去找桑德罗。
Lopera: Come on now. We're not catching up to him in this mud.

10  玛平瓜里 / Mapinguari

玛平瓜里
Mapinguari

一片许久无人问津的湿地躺在草木之中，躁动的巨大蚊虫无休无止地为它奏乐。
The shallow water ahead seems infested with mosquitoes of outrageous proportions. They hover across the surface, producing an audible buzz.

罗德里戈：……没错，就是这片湖。
Rodrygo: This is it. Captain Rodrygo's very own private lake.

洛佩拉：除了杀人蜂和食人鱼之外，这里还有什么特别的？
Lopera: What's so special about this place, pernilongos and piranhas aside?

罗德里戈：看见那条船了吗？它是被我留在这儿的，就在我被汽水厂辞退之后不久。
Rodrygo: See that boat over there? That's the very one I got after I was fired from the soda factory.

罗德里戈：当时我一时冲动，想要模仿那个生活在船上的老兄。结果——你也看得出来，我就是做不到无牵无挂。
Rodrygo: Something came over me. I wanted to be like that old man on the water, to live on that boat. But, as you can see, I couldn't handle that kind of freedom.

罗德里戈：不过，更重要的是，我在湖边一间废弃的小屋里藏了一些东西。
Rodrygo: But, more importantly, I have a cabin nearby, with a few goodies for us both.

罗德里戈：冲锋枪和防弹衣，虽然可能受了点潮，但至少是正儿八经的美国货。万一再碰到敌人可以派上些用场。
Rodrygo: Including some submachine guns and a few bulletproof vests. Not cheap trash either, American-made. They might be a bit damp, but they should still be functional.

洛佩拉：你怎么搞到这些东西的？
Lopera: How'd you get your hands on those?

罗德里戈的面孔上泛起苦涩的涟漪。
A bitter ripple spreads across Rodrygo's face.

罗德里戈：那屋子里藏着老罗德里戈最大的秘密，还是不要搞清楚为好，孩子。
Rodrygo: Kid, that cabin holds Old Rodrygo's biggest secrets. Some things are best left unknown, little birdie.

罗德里戈：另外，你身上还有多少子弹？介意多分给我几颗吗？
Rodrygo: Eh, how many bullets do you have left? Mind sharing a few?

洛佩拉：没剩多少了，你从叛军身上搜到的不够用吗？
Lopera: Not many. Didn't you pick up any from those rebels?

罗德里戈：我的手受过伤，举枪不太稳当。多给我一些用来犯错的子弹吧，等找到了小屋，你想要我还多少都行。
Rodrygo: Yes, but I've injured my hand, so I'm not too steady with a gun. I'll need a few more to account for my shoddy aim—Just for now, they'll be plenty in the cabin.

洛佩拉：啧，好吧。
Lopera: Tch, fine.

洛佩拉不情愿地抓出一把子弹塞进罗德里戈的手里，几乎分出去了三分之二的存量。
Lopera reluctantly grabs a fistful of bullets and shoves them into Rodrygo's hand, not a small number either, at least two-thirds of what she had left.

洛佩拉：我的枪法很准，的确用不着太多。
Lopera: I'm not that bad a shot.

罗德里戈：对我来说，这大概是够了。
Rodrygo: This should be enough for me.

罗德里戈：来吧，我们绕着湖岸走，身子压低点，我可不想被树后面藏着的叛军放冷枪。
Rodrygo: Come on, let's walk around the lake. Stay low—who knows if we might run into more rebels.

洛佩拉：嗯……你带路。
Lopera: Hmm. Lead the way.

罗德里戈弓着背在前方开路，一层层拨开浓密的灌木。洛佩拉尾随在后，像一个跟随长官的士兵。
Rodrygo hunches forward, parting the dense underbrush layer by layer. Lopera follows behind in military fashion, scanning for trouble.

一双精于甄别昆虫与树果的眼睛，正在仔细甄别每一片树叶背后可能藏着的敌意。
A pair of eyes skilled at distinguishing insect and fruit species carefully scrutinizes each leaf for hidden threats.

洛佩拉：这里……不，这只是鸟而已。
Lopera: Here. No, just a bird.

洛佩拉：呵，犰狳，这可是新鲜玩意。
Lopera: Oh. Heh heh, an armadillo. You don't see those every day.

无论嘴上说得多么随意，捏住枪的手却从未放松。
Despite her casual tone, she maintains a firm grip on her weapon.

砰——
Bang—

这次是罗德里戈在开枪。
This time, it is Rodrygo who fires first.

洛佩拉：在哪？！
Lopera: Where are they?!

罗德里戈：看见草丛了吗？朝那里开枪！
Rodrygo: Fire, to the brush!

洛佩拉举枪望去，一丛叶片像在打招呼似的招摇着。
Lopera raises her gun, aiming at the waving leaves.

弹巢内的子弹倾泻而出，又被新的弹药迅速填满。
A shot rings out, quickly followed by another in quick succession.

罗德里戈：继续，孩子，朝我开火的方向射击！
Rodrygo: Keep going, kid. Aim right where I fired!

弹药再次打空，洛佩拉有条不紊地抽出第二把枪，继续倾泻她剩余的子弹。
The hammer clicks empty. Lopera steadily draws a captured pistol, continuing her barrage, magazine after magazine, until it too is spent.

洛佩拉：没子弹了！
Lopera: I'm out of bullets!

罗德里戈仍在自顾自地开火，连续的轰鸣声盖过了洛佩拉的声音。
Rodrygo keeps firing, the roars of his rifle drowning out Lopera's voice.

终于，罗德里戈也停下了扣动扳机的手指。
At last, Rodrygo stops pulling the trigger.

罗德里戈：就算是一只玛平瓜里也活不下来了。
Rodrygo: Not even a mapinguari could survive that.

洛佩拉：你最好真的杀死了一只玛平瓜里……我去检查一下。
Lopera: Let's hope we killed a mapinguari then. I'll check it out.

罗德里戈：不，我们先去取子弹，免得碰上敌人的援军。
Rodrygo: No. There's no time. Let's make our way to the cabin first. We need to stock up in case there's more.

洛佩拉：但是——
Lopera: But—

罗德里戈：这里离我的小屋已经很近了！
Rodrygo: We're close, come on!

罗德里戈向某个方向撒腿狂奔，此刻他毫不在乎是否会被藏在暗处的敌人发现。
Rodrygo sprints off, seemingly uncaring of any would-be enemies spotting him.

洛佩拉：喂——你！
Lopera: Hey! You ...

洛佩拉敲了敲空荡荡的弹巢，不耐烦地追了上去。
Lopera taps her empty cylinder and chases after him impatiently.

罗德里戈的藏身处
Rodrygo's Cabin

室内的陈设看起来就像主人匆匆离去之后再未回头——它不像是个藏匿宝贵枪械和弹药的地方。
The cabin's interior looks as though the owner left in a hurry and never returned. Not at all like the kind of place one would hide valuable weapons and equipment.

罗德里戈在房间四处翻找着，除了纷纷扬起的陈旧灰尘之外，他一无所获。
Rodrygo combs over the room, producing nothing but clouds of dust.

罗德里戈：咳咳，我真该定期回来打扫的。
Rodrygo: *cough* I should've come back once in a while to tidy up.

罗德里戈：这个柜子呢——
Rodrygo: This cabinet ...

哗啦——罗德里戈从杂物堆中抽出一只一人长的油布袋，又赶紧揉皱了塞回去。
Crash! Rodrygo pulls out a long oil-cloth bag from the clutter, then just as quickly crumples it and stuffs it back.

罗德里戈：这个……哦，这可不好……船上用的旧东西了，哈哈，我原本是打算用它来装我的科德尔小说的。
Rodrygo: Haha. This, oh boy, this is no good. Just an old relic from the boat. Haha, I was going to use it to store my Cordell novels.

洛佩拉：放弃吧，罗德里戈，你已经找了快半个小时了。
Lopera: Give it up, Rodrygo. You've been searching for close to half an hour.

洛佩拉警惕地靠在窗边，时不时向外瞄一眼，以应对可能到来的威胁。
Lopera stands by the window, keeping her eyes on the outside, ready for any potential threat.

罗德里戈：……是该放弃了。
Rodrygo: Nothing.

罗德里戈的目光闪烁着，在洛佩拉身上蜻蜓点水般落了半拍，接着迅速背过身去，看着杂物堆出神。
Rodrygo's gaze flickers, briefly landing on Lopera before turning back to the clutter.

罗德里戈：这样可真不好，孩子。
Rodrygo: Kid. This is bad.

洛佩拉：看看你那玛平瓜里做的好事，现在我们弹尽粮绝了。
Lopera: Look at what your mapinguari did. Now we're out of ammo.

女孩停顿了很久，下一句话像从胃里翻上来的硬骨头一样，卡在她的喉咙里。
The girl pauses for a long time. Her words come out like a hard bone rising from her stomach, catching in her throat.

洛佩拉：……我们可能跟丢桑德罗了。
Lopera: We've lost Sandro for certain.

罗德里戈：我们已经在雨林里走了相当长的一段路，就连追着我们咬的蚊子都换了好几茬，三天后要是没有得热病就该感谢上帝了。
Rodrygo: We've been walking through the rainforest for so long that the mosquitoes eating away at us have changed shifts. It'll be a miracle if we don't catch a fever three days from now.

罗德里戈：在这种时候放弃，就连最伟大的伊戈尔将军也没有理由责备你。
Rodrygo: If you were to give up, I don't think even the great Admiral Igor would blame you.

洛佩拉：我才没说过要放弃。我要带桑德罗回去交差，就算是……他的尸体。
Lopera: You're wrong. Who said anything about giving up? I'll find this rat, even if it's just his corpse.

罗德里戈：桑德罗怎么会死呢？
Rodrygo: How could Sandro be dead?

罗德里戈几乎是有些难以置信地轻叹一声。他再度转过身来，罩在阴影下的面孔暗沉而模糊。
Rodrygo sighs with contempt. He turns again, his shadowed face now dark and indistinct.

罗德里戈：桑德罗不去芝诺，我会带他到国外去。
Rodrygo: You can't take him to Zeno. I'll take him across the border.

罗德里戈的这句话几乎不附带任何语气，似乎只是在陈述一件早已达成共识的事。
Rodrygo's words are emotionless, as if stating an agreed-upon fact.

洛佩拉：等会，离开巴西？
Lopera: Wait, you mean you'll leave Brazil??

洛佩拉：你搞清楚——你带我找到桑德罗，我把他带回芝诺，这之后就没有你的事了。
Lopera: Let's get this straight—after you help me find Sandro, I'm taking him back to Zeno. He has no choice in the matter. Whatever happens after that is none of your business.

罗德里戈：本来是这样……本来所有事都应该是这样，但现在……卡洛斯死了！！
Rodrygo: But ... It wasn't supposed to be like this. This was supposed to go down easy. Now, with Carlos dead ...

罗德里戈在柜子上结结实实地砸了一拳，留下一块碎裂的浅坑。
Rodrygo punches the cabinet, leaving a shallow dent.

罗德里戈：……芝诺的人正忙着自相残杀，谁会来兑现承诺？谁会顾虑桑德罗的安危？
Rodrygo: You know that Zeno is tearing itself apart from within? Who will keep their promises? Who can I trust with Sandro's safety?

洛佩拉：圣保罗的叛乱不可能持续太久，伊戈尔将军不会放任不管，他一定能把叛徒收拾干净。
Lopera: This little mutiny won't last long. Admiral Igor will put things right. We'll handle all of these traitors.

罗德里戈：这我可拿不准，我没法像你一样相信一个千里之外的陌生人。
Rodrygo: I can't put my faith in a stranger a thousand miles away.

洛佩拉：他是我的父亲，我知道他是一个怎样的人。
Lopera: He's my father. I know what kind of man he is.

洛佩拉：伊戈尔将军永远会做出最正确的决策，桑德罗在他的保护下一定是最安全的。
Lopera: Admiral Igor knows what's best. He will make the right decision. He always does. Sandro will be safest under his protection.

罗德里戈：谁能永远做出“最正确的决策”？
Rodrygo: The "right decision"? You really think there is such a thing?

罗德里戈耸耸肩。
Rodrygo shrugs, hanging his face.

罗德里戈：我不相信胸前挂满勋章的人，那些小金属片儿太刺眼了。
Rodrygo: I've never trusted anyone with a chest full of medals. Those little things start to blind their eyes.

洛佩拉毫无征兆地逼近到罗德里戈面前，一只手搭在枪柄上，仰头直视男人藏在影子中的双眼。
Lopera suddenly steps up to Rodrygo, hand on her gun, looking up into his shadowed eyes.

洛佩拉：你对他没有任何了解！伊戈尔将军的每一块勋章都是无可争议的荣誉！
Lopera: Idiota! You don't know anything about him! Every medal Admiral Igor wears is a mark of honor. Don't speak about my father that way!

罗德里戈：重点不在于荣誉是否真实，孩子。
Rodrygo: Kid, it's not whether those honors are real that matters.

罗德里戈：你认识他又有多久了？
Rodrygo: He adopted you, right? How long have you known him?

罗德里戈：十五年？十年？还是不超过五年？
Rodrygo: Not fifteen years? Is it ten? Less than five?

洛佩拉：……
Lopera: ...

女孩的沉默为罗德里戈提供了答案。
The girl's silence gives Rodrygo his answer.

罗德里戈：在不到五年的时间里，你是否有机会看一眼伊戈尔眼中那个被赫赫战功与无数权柄堆砌起来的世界？
Rodrygo: Just a few short years—what sort of father is that? Have you ever seen the world through his eyes? That world he built with the power he wields?

洛佩拉：我——
Lopera: I—

罗德里戈：——你没有，孩子，不然你不会认识满身鱼腥和汗臭味的老罗德里戈。
Rodrygo: You haven't, kid, or you wouldn't know Old Rodrygo, and the reek of fish and sweat.

罗德里戈：如果伊戈尔曾将你举过头顶，那么像我这样的人只不过是轻易就能被忽视的一块泥土而已。
Rodrygo: If Igor had ever lifted you above his head, people like me would be nothing but ants to you.

罗德里戈：你依附于伊戈尔，但他的肩膀太高，你永远攀不上去。
Rodrygo: Cling on to his boots all you want, but his shoulders are too high. He won't pull you up there. He never will.

罗德里戈：想想你真正的父亲，孩子，或许你会明白我在说什么。
Rodrygo: Kid, maybe you should think about your real father? Then you'll understand what I mean.

洛佩拉：明白？他就是个王八蛋——这是我唯一明白的事。
Lopera: Understand what? He's a malparido, an evil man! That's the only thing I need to understand.

罗德里戈没有任何动作，如同一面黑暗的墙，等待倾诉者吐出自己的故事。
Rodrygo remains still, like a dark wall, waiting for the speaker to tell her story.

洛佩拉：他出卖了相信他的人——罢工的日期、参与的人员和集会的地点，这些全都被他打包卖给了水果公司。
Lopera: He betrayed everyone who trusted him. He sold them all out to a goddamn fruit company. Handed over their plans, their leaders, like a good little lapdog.

洛佩拉：他们本来可以成功的，只要再添把火，卖香蕉的就会滚出哥伦比亚。
Lopera: They could've done it. Just one more push, and those bastards would've been pushed out of Colombia, bananas and all.

洛佩拉：但他们最后像大豆苗一样倒下，街道上的枪声一整天都没有停下来。
Lopera: But, then, it all fell apart. The shooting echoed through the streets from morning until dark.

洛佩拉：他告诉我这是人们庆祝罢工胜利的礼炮！
Lopera: You know? He told me they were "victory fireworks" for the strikers!

洛佩拉：这话听着耳熟吗，罗德里戈？
Lopera: Sound familiar, Rodrygo?

黑暗的墙并不愿意回答女孩的问题。
The man doesn't answer. He faces the wall, unspeaking.

罗德里戈：……或许，你父亲为此再也犯不着去种植园里卖命了。
Rodrygo: Then I suppose at least your father never had to work on the plantation again.

洛佩拉：对啊，他还像经理人那样穿上了西装，就算热得浑身冒汗也不肯脱下。
Lopera: Never. He dressed like some kind of big boss, sweating through his suit every day but still refusing to take it off.

洛佩拉：但是我们再也不能回家了，只能不停地逃亡，生怕被复仇的人追上。
Lopera: But we could never go home. We were always on the run after that, sleeping with one eye open, always afraid.

洛佩拉：那时候我根本搞不明白，为什么其他人看见我就像见到了毒虫，朝我脸上扔满是泥巴的拖鞋。
Lopera: I never understood why people looked at me like I was some pest, why they threw their muddy chanclas at us.

洛佩拉：我们总是住在离城市很远很远的屋子里，那些地方到了晚上连个鬼影都找不着。
Lopera: He moved us far from the city, always in isolated places.

罗德里戈：最后你又是怎么知道这些事的？
Rodrygo: How did you find out about all this?

女孩的视线向下跌落，凝固在没有子弹的左轮枪上。
The girl's gaze drops to her empty revolver.

洛佩拉：是尼迪亚告诉我的……你不必知道她是谁。
Lopera: Nydia told me. You don't need to know who she is.

洛佩拉：我不知道她是怎么找到我的住处的……她一定花了很多年的时间，走了很远的路。
Lopera: I still don't know how she found me after all those years and so far away.

洛佩拉：直到那晚，她敲响了我的窗户……
Lopera: Until that night, when she knocked on my window.

洛佩拉：我推开窗，差点以为自己遇见了梦中的幽灵。
Lopera: I opened it for her, but she didn't come in. I thought I was seeing a ghost.

洛佩拉：尼迪亚是个褐色皮肤的梅斯蒂索人，但那天晚上她的脸色像月亮一样苍白，如同枯枝的手臂拼命攀在窗檐上。
Lopera: Nydia was a mestizo, with skin as dark as cocoa, but that night her face was pale as the moon. Her skinny arms were clinging to my windowsill.

洛佩拉：她哑着嗓子对我说——
Lopera: She said to me in a hoarse voice.
