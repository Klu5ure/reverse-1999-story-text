如被葡萄酒浸润过的歌声愈发甜润，却被一声不合时宜的干咳打断。
Until a pointed cough interrupts the bard's song.

吟游诗人：嘿——瞧瞧这是谁来了？
Bard: By Zeus! Is that who I think it is?

吟游诗人：永远奔波在路上的驯鹰人，为希腊带来崭新黎明的战争新秀——卡珊德拉～
Bard: If it isn't the rising star of the akropolis, Kassandra, the Eagle Bearer!

卡珊德拉：停停停，艾琳娜。
Kassandra: Stop, Euphonia.

卡珊德拉：我只是个收钱办事的佣兵，可没兴趣竞选什么执政官，你这套吹捧还是留给那些大人物吧。
Kassandra: I'm just a misthios for hire. I've no interest in politics. Save those grandiose words of yours for the Archons.

吟游诗人：我可没在吹捧你，你以一己之力帮助斯巴达人占领墨伽里斯的事迹……呵呵，早就传到城内啦。
Bard: Ah, but I speak only the truth. The tales of your valorous exploits in Megaris precede you ...

吟游诗人：再说了，人们不介意给众神添一名子嗣，雅典便能多飘荡一段传奇……
Bard: They declare that you're a hero throughout Athens and praise you as a demigod ...

正当吟游诗人打算继续夸耀对方的神武时，断矛的佣兵摇了摇头，突然欺身上前。
Kassandra shakes her head as she approaches to interrupt the bard.

卡珊德拉：别卖弄这有的没的了，艾琳娜，你知道我想问什么。
Kassandra: I have a question for you, Euphonia. The crowd will just have to wait for your next performance.

吟游诗人：嗯？卖弄——这词太过分了。要知道，许多人都在“卖弄”着什么，诗人为了颂神而卖弄歌喉，佣兵为了雇主而卖弄暴力……
Bard: What? You wound me, Kassandra! We bards display our art to please the gods not just the vulgar crowd, just as you mercenaries do.

卡珊德拉：都是为了那一点德拉克马，不是吗？
Kassandra: Anything for a Drachma or two, right?

佣兵从兜里抛出一枚闪着光泽的德拉克马，它在半空划出优美的弧线，稳稳落入诗人手心。
Kassandra grabs a shining Drachma from her pouch and tosses it into the bard's hand.

吟游诗人：呵呵，当然……每个人都“卖弄”着自己的某一部分，也包括那两个手持锉刀的家伙。
Bard: We all have our gifts to display, don't we? From the lowliest bard to the greatest of warriors, even those craftsmen are no different.

吟游诗人：我知道，你注意到他们了。
Bard: I suppose you've noticed them too.

远处，两个鬼祟的黑影蹲在卫城的墙角，手中拿着锉刀上下翻飞。
In the distance, two figures crouch in a corner of the akropolis, chiseling the wall with their files.

卡珊德拉：所以卫城的墙砖，平时也需要那样细致的“雕琢”吗？
Kassandra: It seems you take your maintenance a little too seriously here.

吟游诗人：当然不需要，一块破砖，这儿的修补匠费半天的力就能修补好……不过，我倒是打听到点好玩的事情。
Bard: Oh, it's nothing really. Just a few loose bricks. Easily replaced in a matter of hours. Still, these particular artisans have attracted some rumors.

吟游诗人：他们一个自称是受雅典娜恩赐的启迪者，一直念叨着“雅典娜神像”、“金箔”、“战争军费”什么的，另一个则似乎在不停抱怨着什么……
Bard: One of them claimed to be enlightened by Athena. He kept mumbling nonsense about "the statue of Athena," "gold leaves," and "unjust wars." And his companion seemed to be a real grouch.

吟游诗人：大家邀请他们去城外采摘浆果和野花，可他们统统拒绝了。
Bard: I've seen a few people try to speak with them, invite them to pick berries outside the city, but they seemed to have no interest ...

吟游诗人：请别太在意……毕竟在大酒神节的日子，什么怪人都有可能出现的。
Bard: Anyways, what does it matter? The Dionysia is drawing near; is it any wonder that eccentrics like them might show up?

卡珊德拉：原来如此。
Kassandra: I see.

卡珊德拉：谢了，艾琳娜。
Kassandra: Thanks, Euphonia.

几枚金光灿烂的德拉克马落入吟游诗人面前的陶碗，而佣兵的身影早已消失得无影无踪。
She tosses a few more Drachmae into the bard's bowl as she strides away.

吟游诗人：……！
Bard: ...!

矫健的步伐无声无息间靠近了街道的晦暗一角。
Kassandra stealthily approaches a dark corner of the street.

探头探脑的鬼祟身影们正小声地交头接耳，不时张望着四周的环境。
There, two figures have gathered together whispering, and casting vigilant looks around.

蹩脚的“演员”：为了这片大地……决不允许融化金箔用作军费的暴行……我在此执行审判……为了雅典娜……也为了执政官脑袋上的悬赏金……
The Rookie "Actor": For the love of this land, I will not tolerate you raiding the coffers of our people to fund this war. Judgment has been passed, and I shall be its executioner. This is for Athena and for the bounty on Archon's head.

智慧的“剧作家”：台词记得不错，多米蒂乌斯～
The Wise "Playwright": Excellent, Domitius. You've memorized your lines well.

智慧的“剧作家”：不管是否成功，你刺杀的消息一定会人尽皆知，所有人都能顺理成章地从情报贩子那儿拿到悬赏书，我们的计划便能够传递给所有同僚！
The Wise "Playwright": Whether you succeed or not, rumors of your attempt will pass through every corner of the city. All that's left is for the others to receive their messages, then they will know our plans.

蹩脚的“演员”：我早说过我能做好的，伊索妮娅。
The Rookie "Actor": Haven't I told you it's in the bag, Isonia?

蹩脚的“演员”：可是，我们到底是怎么传递组织消息的？明明，“智者”什么也没告诉我们呀……
The Rookie "Actor": So, how are we supposed to read these messages when they come? The Sage explained nothing to us ...

智慧的“剧作家”：……
The Wise "Playwright": ...

智慧的“剧作家”：这不是你现在该考虑的问题，白痴！
The Wise "Playwright": That's none of your concern, fool!

蹩脚的“演员”：哦，看来你也不懂“智者”到底要我们干嘛……
The Rookie "Actor": Seems like you don't know any more about the Sage's plans than I do.

自诩聪慧的伊索妮娅女士显然不能接受如此批评。
The "playwright" scoffs dismissively.

智慧的“剧作家”：你懂什么，老是“智者”来，“智者”去的！
The Wise "Playwright": You're always going on about the "Sage" this and the "Sage" that; I'm tired of it!

智慧的“剧作家”：别以为只有你一个人接受了这些大人的命令！
The Wise "Playwright": Do you think you're special just because you were chosen for this task? You're a veritable Narcissus!

智慧的“剧作家”伊索妮娅怒不可遏，但看到缩头缩脑的“演员”，她心中不禁涌起一丝嘲讽之意。
The subject of her tirade shrinks under her words, causing the "playwright" to press the attack.

智慧的“剧作家”：噢，当然，你自诩为一个艺术家，呵呵。
The Wise "Playwright": If you really think you deserve that praise, heh heh ...

智慧的“剧作家”：那就别忘了这个……
The Wise "Playwright": You'd better not forget this.

智慧的“剧作家”：一位专业的艺术家正需要这样合适的暗杀工具。
The Wise "Playwright": The perfect tool for our trade ... and your mission.

智慧的“剧作家”：记着，“智者”说了，每个成员都只能得到一把锉刀，你可千万别弄丢了。
The Wise "Playwright": We will only receive one file each—that was the Sage's own words. Keep it safe.

蹩脚的“演员”：噢——好的！
The Rookie "Actor": Oh, I will!

蹩脚的“演员”：可它看起来一点儿也不锋利，这样的武器能搞定那些执政官吗？
The Rookie "Actor": Huh? This thing is as dull as a weathered stone. How am I supposed to cut the Archon's throat with this?

智慧的“剧作家”：那你就把它磨光亮些！
The Wise "Playwright": Then sharpen it, fool!

没有理会仍在打量锉刀的拙笨演员，不耐烦的声音迅速远去了。
The "playwright" turns from him with another scoff, leaving the "actor" still studying the file.

蹩脚的“演员”：你要去哪儿，伊索妮娅？
The Rookie "Actor": Where are you going, Isonia?

智慧的“剧作家”：当然是回去复命，白痴。需要指派剧本的可不止你一个。
The Wise "Playwright": You really are deluded, aren't you? I've more work to do before the plan is ready.

蹩脚的“演员”：哦，祝你顺利，女士。
The Rookie "Actor": Oh ... good luck then.

他挥了挥手，握紧手上的锉刀，慢悠悠地挪到了墙边。
He waves goodbye to the "playwright," then clenches the file in his hand and moves to the wall.

蹩脚的“演员”：嗯……趁着天还没黑，我还有不少时间可以把它磨得更锋利些。
The Rookie "Actor": Ah ... I should have enough time to put an edge on this before it gets dark.

蹩脚的“演员”：仅仅依靠蛮力可没法搞定那个虚伪又狡猾的演说家，附近巡逻的士兵也不少……我的刺杀时间非常有限。
The Rookie "Actor": There's no way I can take down that self-important windbag with brute force alone ... Not to mention there's only a small window for me to strike.

喃喃自语间，一柄断矛唐突地刺入了他眼前的另一块砖墙里。
A broken spear is thrust into the brick next to him.

蹩脚的“演员”：——！
The Rookie "Actor": ...!

卡珊德拉：为了某些大人物的安全考虑，我需要排除一些看起来可疑的家伙。
Kassandra: You're a suspicious sort of character, aren't you? For the safety of this city—and the self-important windbags that run it—I keep an eye out for your type.

蹩脚的“演员”：什么……不……？！
The Rookie "Actor": W-What? No!

手忙脚乱之际，一片矫健的阴影迅速袭向了他。
Kassandra pounces on the still dumbfounded "actor."

可怜的狂徒被捆了起来，他瑟瑟发抖地蜷缩在一旁，卡珊德拉干净利落地拍了拍手。
The "actor" curls up his legs in fear, his hands bound up tight behind his back. Kassandra clasps her hands and leans over him.

卡珊德拉：聊聊你的刺杀目标吧。
Kassandra: So, whose throat were you sharpening this for again?

蹩脚的“演员”：……！
The Rookie "Actor": ...!

卡珊德拉：公民大会和五百人委员会的成员众多，但经过这儿的大人物屈指可数。
Kassandra: There are many figures I could imagine, from the Ecclesia or the Boule, perhaps? But how many of them would find their way here?

卡珊德拉：我不想多费拳脚，所以劝你也别耍什么心机。
Kassandra: I've no particular desire to kill you. So, it's in your interest to speak, and to speak only the truth.

蹩脚的“演员”：……
The Rookie "Actor": ...

对方沉默了许久。终于，一个名字从微微张开的唇齿间挤出。
The "actor" hesitates until he reads the woman's hard face and finally stammers out a name.

蹩脚的“演员”：伯……伯里克利。
The Rookie "Actor": Perikles.

卡珊德拉：嚯，雅典城的大麻烦。
Kassandra: Ah, of course. It would be that troublemaking Strategos.

蹩脚的“演员”：我决不会透露更多的细节！
The Rookie "Actor": There's nothing else I can tell you!

卡珊德拉：没问题，你可以不说。
Kassandra: Alright, if that's all you can say.

卡珊德拉礼貌地点了点头。
Kassandra moves closer with a nod.

“啪——”
*thwack*

蹩脚的“演员”：唔……
The Rookie "Actor": Urgh ...

脖颈后传来的剧痛令他几乎晕眩过去。
The sudden strike to his head renders the man limp.

卡珊德拉：但伯里克利不能在这儿倒下。
Kassandra: But I won't let Perikles be taken out by the likes of you.

卡珊德拉：我同样不希望这儿出现什么影响太大的篓子。
Kassandra: Besides, the last thing I want is more trouble here.

卡珊德拉：至于更复杂的问题，就留给伯里克利去头疼吧。
Kassandra: I'll hand this matter over to the man himself.

一个厚实的布袋“温和”地裹住了对方的脑袋。
She stuffs the "actor" into a thick cloth bag.

随后，一阵嘹亮的鹰鸣响彻天空。
As she ties up the bag with a knot, an eagle's cry pierces through the air.

02  悲剧与生活 / Life and Tragedies

议会会场
Assembly

议会会场的气氛相当热闹，民主的辉光照亮了为各自事务而战的所有公民。
A group of men has assembled in the Pnyx. The light of democracy shines upon the Athenians as they engage in a fierce discussion.

执政官Ⅰ：不行！拨给狄俄尼索斯剧场的预算一个子儿也不能少！这可是伟大悲剧上演之处，愚钝的笨脑瓜们——
Archon I: Inconceivable! Not a single Drachma should be cut from the budget for the Theater of Dionysos! The tragedies we perform there are a matter of pride for the whole polis!

执政官Ⅰ：噢——我伟大的狄俄尼索斯啊，倘使你睁开眼睛见到如此的灾难，即便再醇香的美酒也无法消解如此的苦痛！
Archon I: O Dionysos! If only you could open your eyes and witness this calamity befalling us—no wine, not even your finest, could ease such agony!

伯里克利：有理……在大酒神节上演悲剧这一传统，历来是我们的骄傲。
Perikles: I do not dispute your words. Athens prides itself on our tragedies, especially during the Dionysia.

执政官Ⅱ：哼，别再鼓弄你的唇舌了，赫尔米普斯，我们眼下可是面临着更大的危机。
Archon II: Hmph, what nonsense, Hermippos! Don't you think we have greater issues at hand?

执政官Ⅱ：如果不迅速解决对波蒂戴亚的围城战，恐怕过不了多久，在这儿举办你心爱的大酒神节的，只能是那些你瞧不上眼的斯巴达木头了。
Archon II: If the fighting in Potidaia does not go in our favor, I fear it will be the Spartans that host our Dionysia.

伯里克利：说的没错，况且当下的庆典很可能会影响到军队的日常训练和战备状态……
Perikles: You counsel wisely; the money that would go to this festival will come at the expense of our ability to fight this war ...

执政官Ⅲ：那你又打算怎么做，安德里奥斯，所有人都勒紧裤腰带，然后把金钱和人力都投进一场看不到头的战争里？
Archon III: So, what do you suggest, Andrius? Are we to just throw every Drachma and fighting man we have left into this battle? All for a war that seems to have no end?

执政官Ⅲ：大家早就眼巴巴地等着这场盛会了，这可不是什么追求享乐的奢侈宴会，是所有雅典人民的愿望。
Archon III: The polis has been eagerly awaiting the Dionysia. This festival is not some mere indulgence or luxury—it brings hope to the people and the favor of the gods!

伯里克利：因传统而永远长青的城邦啊……
Perikles: Yes. Athens thrives because we honor our traditions ...

执政官Ⅲ：难道你打算摧毁民众们的愿望，推翻我们的传统与信仰吗？
Archon III: Answer me, Andrius. Do we shatter our people's hope? Must we abandon our traditions and the gods for victory?

执政官Ⅱ：这是在偷换概念，该死的——
Archon II: I never said that! Hades take you!

执政官Ⅱ：你就好好地祈祷自己的尖牙利嘴在敌人的长矛面前也能派上用场吧！
Archon II: What good are some honeyed words on a stage against a spear? You want more tragedies, eh? Good, they'll be writing them about our hubris.

执政官众：……
The Archons: ...

众人的辩论声此起彼伏，有的甚至挽起了袖袍。
The words gain venom with each reply until the men are nearly at each other's throats.

伯里克利：噢，塞奥多尔，你的唾沫星子都溅到我的袍子上了——
Perikles: Theodoros. You just spat on my robe.

伯里克利：冷静点，胡须是无辜的，安德里奥斯——
Perikles: Please, Andrius. Let go of his beard!

成熟的执政官有条不紊地平衡着会场的气氛，民主的辉光在此刻显得愈发耀眼。
With calm and commanding presence, Perikles positions himself between the brawling Athenians.

伯里克利：……希罗多德似乎提起过，当你听到一声不寻常的鹰鸣时——
Perikles: Herodotus wrote that whenever you hear an eagle cry—

“哐当——”
"Clang—!"

一团展开翅膀的阴影突兀地划过执政官们的头顶，将叼在口中的锉刀扔在砖地上。
A shadowy, winged figure sweeps over the crowd and hurls a metal tool along the ground.

锉刀滚动了几圈，像是在越发吵闹的气球里轻轻地划破了一个口子。
It rolls a few times before coming to a stop—an ordinary file.

执政官Ⅰ：刺客——有、有刺客！
Archon I: Are we under attack? An assassin!?

执政官Ⅱ：一只叼着匕首的……鹰？
Archon II: Did that eagle ... drop a blade from its mouth?

执政官Ⅲ：卫队、卫队呢？！保护伯里克利大人！
Archon III: Guards! Guards! Defend Perikles!

卡珊德拉：别紧张，执政官先生们，刺客已经被抓住了。
Kassandra: There's no need for all this drama; your assassin has already been captured.

卡珊德拉：就在你们面前的这个布袋里。
Kassandra: He's ... in the bag.

佣兵一把将硕大、破烂的布袋扔在众人面前的地上，布袋内传来一阵闷哼。
Kassandra drops the bag in front of the gathered Archons, prompting a grunt of pain from inside.

执政官Ⅰ：咿……这，这家伙是……
Archon I: Huh ... W-Who are you ...

伯里克利：——果然是你，卡珊德拉。
Perikles: Who else could it be ... Kassandra.

伯里克利：很高兴再见到你，真希望我的事没给你添麻烦……不过，看起来，你似乎已经解决了这个麻烦。
Perikles: I am pleased to see you again. I hope I didn't cause you any trouble. Though you seem to have taken care of it.

卡珊德拉：佣兵永远不嫌麻烦多。再说了，这刺客有点不寻常。
Kassandra: Hah. I'm a misthios, trouble follows in my wake. But this man intended to trouble you.

卡珊德拉：我希望你能看下这个。
Kassandra: And there is something unusual about his weapon.

卡珊德拉捡起老鹰扔在脚边的锉刀，递给对方。伯里克利在手中仔细地打量了起来。
Perikles receives the file from Kassandra and examines it.

伯里克利：每个来找我的人都说有“不寻常”的事，但这个嘛……
