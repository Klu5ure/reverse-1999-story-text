拉普拉斯研究员：77 114 46 32 85 108 114 105 99 104?
Laplace Researcher: 77 114 46 32 85 108 114 105 99 104?

拉普拉斯研究员：84 104 101 114 101 39 115 32 97 32 102 111 120 32 104 101 114 101 32 116 111 32 115 101 101 32 121 111 117。
Laplace Researcher: 84 104 101 114 101 39 115 32 97 32 102 111 120 32 104 101 114 101 32 116 111 32 115 101 101 32 121 111 117.

房间里的人比研究员以为的要多。
The researcher steps into the room, surprised to see so many people inside.

他礼貌地向哑谜、冷周六、兔毛手袋三位同僚，总跟在星锑研究员后头的APPLe先生，以及一位佩戴基金会徽章的小姐点头致意，然后等待乌尔里希组长的回复。
He gives polite nods to them—Enigma, Hissabeth, Medicine Pocket, Mr. APPLe, though not a researcher himself, always at Regulus's side, and a woman wearing a Foundation badge—then waits quietly for Ulrich to respond.

正埋头于一叠报告的磁流体抬起头。
Ulrich looks up from his stack of reports.

乌尔里希：……什么？
Ulrich: ... Pardon?

拉普拉斯研究员：79104！
Laplace Researcher: 79104!

研究员仿佛才想起来什么一般，从口袋里掏出一个机器，对着它将那几串冗长的数字又重复了一遍。
The researcher's eyes widen for a moment, then he quickly pulls a device from his pocket and recites a string of long numbers into it.

乌尔里希：……
Ulrich: ...

所有人等待着。几秒后，那个小小的金属盒子里响起了合成人声。
After a short pause, an artificial voice speaks from the device.

转译器：抱歉，乌尔里希组长，我忘了开转译器——有个奇怪的狐狸头想要找你。
Translator: Sorry, Mr. Ulrich. I forgot to switch the translator on. There's a strange fox-headed character asking for you.

乌尔里希：噢，没事，我本来该反应过来你说的是十进制，但我正在看一篇八进制的报告……
Ulrich: Oh no, it was my mistake. Damn report's got me stuck thinking in octal. I didn't realize you were using decimal.

乌尔里希摆了摆手，像是要把数字们从眼前挥开。
Ulrich gives a wave of his hand, as if brushing the numbers away.

乌尔里希：是缪斯三世来了，我们马上过去。
Ulrich: Must be Musae III. We're on our way.

讲着奇怪编码的研究员离开了。
The code-speaking researcher leaves the room.

房间里的黑发男人在这场对话刚开始时就面露讥讽，而现在显然是一个不必再克制的好时机。
Enigma, who had maintained a mocking expression through the whole scene, now sees no reason to hold back his thoughts any longer.

哑谜：三分钟，真惊人。多亏了你在玩具箱里的人文关怀，现在我们的交流效率堪比猿猴。也许再过一星期我们就能抛弃无意义的喊叫，进化到用壁画记事了。
Enigma: Three minutes. Remarkable. Thanks to your "great compassion" in the toybox, we're now communicating as efficiently as early primates. At this rate, we might graduate from meaningless shouting to cave paintings within the week.

乌尔里希：这和我的人文关怀没有一点关系。
Ulrich: Let's get one thing straight. This has nothing to do with my humanitarian efforts.

磁流体恼怒地闪烁了一下，再次驳回了自它回归以来哑谜对它反复提出的指控。
The ferrofluid flickers with irritation as he is forced once again to brush off the same accusations Enigma has been throwing at him since his return.

乌尔里希：事实上，如果不是我打开了沙滩里的密码锁，我们根本发现不了玩具箱出了问题，更不知道这该死的语言异化症来自哪儿。
Ulrich: In fact, if I hadn't cracked that lock on the beach, we'd still have no clue about the toybox malfunction, let alone where this damn language disorder came from.

本不该在房间里的两位研究员彼此交换了一个眼色。
Two researchers, who clearly have no real reason to be in the room, exchange a glance.

很明显，比起和实验室里满口数字字母的同僚共事，他们更乐意参与到眼前的“项目”中来。
Clearly, they'd rather be involved in this "project" than stuck trying to communicate with colleagues who only speak in numbers and codes.

兔毛手袋：这是第几次了？
Medicine Pocket: Ugh, not this again.

冷周六：算上他俩刚从玩具箱出来时的那回吵架？第八次。嘘。
Hissabeth: If we count that fight right after they came out of the toybox, this makes it the eighth time. Just stay quiet and enjoy.

哑谜：或者，如果你专注你的项目，别管那些棋子发生了什么，就能及时在玩具箱带来乱子前把它还给狐狸。
Enigma: Maybe if you'd focused on the actual project instead of worrying about those pieces,

哑谜：而不是像现在这样，让魔盒里的绝望跑得到处都是，“潘多拉”先生。
Enigma: you could've returned the toybox before it unleashed all its miseries on the world, Mr. Pandora.

乌尔里希：别开玩笑了！语言异化症蔓延的速度快得离谱——
Ulrich: Quit joking around! The language disorder is getting worse by the hour.

乌尔里希：刚刚那个来通知的欧文还能听懂英语，汉娜从昨天下午开始就变得只能理解希尔密码的符号。你如何断言状况不会发生？
Ulrich: Owen—the one who brought the message just now—still understands English, but Hannah's only been able to read Hill cipher symbols since yesterday afternoon. How can you be so sure it's not going to get worse?

哑谜：把你沉迷过家家游戏的时间刨除，完成“暴雨”前演练的总时长可以缩减至少三成。
Enigma: If you'd just stop wasting time on those silly simulations, we could shorten the Pre-Storm Protocol by at least thirty percent.

哑谜：假设你的玻璃脑袋还没被编码语言搞坏——
Enigma: Assuming all the coded language swimming around in that glass tank of yours hasn't completely scrambled your circuits!

乌尔里希：假设你不是只会挖苦，而是有哪怕一点真正的作用——
Ulrich: Assuming YOU'RE capable of being useful for once rather than only throwing out smug comments!

一场再次发生的争论。在升级为更激烈的争吵前，它被一个细弱却不容忽视的声音打断了。
Another argument breaks out, but before it can go any further, a quiet yet steady voice speaks up.

马库斯：先生……先生们……
Marcus: Sir ... Sirs ...

马库斯：抱歉打断您，但是……语言异化症？
Marcus: I hate to interrupt, but did you say language disorder?

磁流体转过身，努力平缓自己的剧烈波动，并无视了一旁蛇发女妖那明显带有遗憾意味的叹息。
Ulrich turns away, struggling to steady his turbulent emotions, pretending he doesn't hear Hissabeth heave a regretful sigh.

乌尔里希：是的，马库斯小姐。如你所见，拉普拉斯从四天前开始陷入了一场语言混乱，二进制编码、摩斯电码、栅栏密码……几乎所有人都开始用奇怪的符号系统讲话。
Ulrich: Correct, Ms. Marcus. For the last four days, communication at Laplace has completely broken down. People are speaking in binary code, Morse code, even rail fence cipher. Almost everyone has switched to encrypted languages.

乌尔里希：我们推测这一切和用于模拟雨前演练的玩具箱有关。
Ulrich: We think it's linked to the toybox we've been using for our Pre-Storm Protocol stimulations.

乌尔里希：一周前缪斯三世检查箱子时提到了有东西失窃，这之后，语言异化症就从箱子所在的楼层开始迅速蔓延……并且仍没有停止的迹象。
Ulrich: During the inspection last week, Musae III mentioned something was stolen. After that, the language disorder started spreading fast from the floor we keep the toybox on, and it hasn't let up since.

乌尔里希：虽然这一情况目前尚无其他异变，除了沟通效率之外也暂未造成其他影响，但……
Ulrich: While we haven't seen any other effects beyond the communication problems so far ...

女孩儿的神情紧张起来。
Marcus looks nervous.

马库斯：您担心……它可能会引起时代的混乱……？
Marcus: Are you concerned this could ... cause time disturbances?

冷周六：不不不，放松些，没那么严重，应该不会引起一次室内“暴雨”。
Hissabeth: Oh please, don't panic. It's not going to trigger a "Storm" inside Laplace or anything.

冷周六：但它绝对会引起拉普拉斯每周例会、实验室经费申请、以及员工食堂自助选餐的混乱。
Hissabeth: But it will absolutely wreck our weekly briefings, lab funding applications, and the canteen's ordering system.

冷周六：换言之，引起拉普拉斯全部日程的混乱。
Hissabeth: In other words, it'll throw off Laplace's whole schedule.

马库斯：嗯……可是，我怎样才能帮上您的忙？我不懂密码，也不擅长数学……
Marcus: I'm just wondering ... how can I help? Codes and math aren't really my strengths ...

哑谜：上一次，你从那个“奇迹的魔圈”里听写出了那份咒文，对吧？
Enigma: Didn't you manage to write down that incantation from "The Circle"?

哑谜：突破了状态与形式的隔阂，直接对目标进行解读……这是你的神秘术？
Enigma: You were able to push past physical barriers and understand the real meaning directly. That's your arcane skill, isn't it?

乌尔里希：这次也是和语言有关的谜题。我认为，既然普通的信息沟通出现障碍，你也许能从书页上阅读出额外的信息。
Ulrich: Our current issue is also about language, mathematical as it may be. Your skills in picking out hidden messages might just be exactly what we need.

马库斯：我明白了，我会尽我所能——
Marcus: Understood. I'll do the best I can.

或许是阿德勒研究员的衬托，如此平和流畅的交流让乌尔里希赞许地点点头。
Ulrich gives a small nod, pleased to see even a brief moment of calm, focused discussion.

乌尔里希：缪斯三世已经在确认相关情况了，走吧，我们得去敲定后续的行动方案。
Ulrich: Musae III is already looking into it. Let's go; we need to confirm our next steps.

缪斯三世：我会持续维护玩具箱，尽力抑制语言污染的扩散，与此同时——真抱歉，恐怕得请你们再进到玩具箱里去。
Musae III: I'll keep working to fix the toybox and try my best to slow down the language corruption. But ... I'm afraid I'll have to ask you to go back inside it again.

哑谜：还来？
Enigma: You can't be serious.

黑头发研究员狠狠瞪着双头狐狸玩偶，语气里充满了不可置信。
Enigma glares at the two-headed fox plushie, his voice brimming with disbelief.

缪斯三世：这次不太一样，朋友们。
Musae III: This time it's different, my friends.

缪斯三世：玩具箱的核心不翼而飞，但并没有“飞”远。我确信它还在箱子里，只是不在它原本该在的地方。
Musae III: The core of the toybox is gone, yes, but it hasn't left the system. I'm certain it's still in there somewhere, just not where it should be.

缪斯三世：90%以上的可能性，核心位于玩具箱程序层——也就是玩具箱的更下层。
Musae III: There's a high chance—higher than ninety percent—that it's moved to the program layer, and that means it's deeper inside the toybox.

哑谜：先等会儿，狐狸，你最好说清楚，我们到底要找什么？一个棋子，一枚零件？
Enigma: Wait a second, whiskers. Be clear—what exactly are we supposed to find? A piece? A component?

缪斯三世：喔……这正是难点所在。我很难给你描述它的样子。
Musae III: Well ... that's the tricky part. I don't really know how to describe what it looks like.

缪斯三世：根据我检查的结果来看，箱内的认知系统已经紊乱，也就是说，它或许没有能被稳定识别的形态……
Musae III: From what I can tell, the toybox's cognitive systems aren't functioning as they should. Simply put, it might not have a fixed or familiar shape now.

缪斯三世：但别沮丧，您瞧，就像被污染的语言仍发挥语言功能，这枚核心也必然还保留着它维持认知的特性，也许您会发现，它的形态变化有其规律……
Musae III: But not to worry! Just as a corrupted language still functions as a language, the core should still hold its basic cognitive properties. Perhaps you'll notice its shifting forms follow some kind of logic ...

尽管缪斯三世以轻缓的口吻和委婉的语调，发挥了它作为一位商人与一只毛绒玩偶所能发挥的全部安抚能力，乌尔里希和哑谜的脸色此刻仍然不太好看。
Even though Musae III speaks with all the soothing skills of both a businessman and a stuffed animal, neither Ulrich nor Enigma seems any more relaxed.

哑谜：所以我们要在几乎毫无情报的情况下，找一个会变形的“普罗透斯”。
Enigma: So what you're saying is we're hunting a "Proteus" with almost no leads?

缪斯三世：事实确实如此，亲爱的朋友……很遗憾我没有海豹皮给你们。另外——如果拉普拉斯的语言已经成了这个样子，玩具箱里棋子们的状况恐怕只会更糟。
Musae III: Precisely, my friend! I only wish I had some sealskins to give you. Ah, before I forget, if the language in Laplace has become this bad, things inside the toybox are probably even worse.

缪斯三世：你们还有转译器吗？
Musae III: Do you still have a translator?

众人看向始终保持沉默的APPLe。这沉默和往常不同，似乎并非出于一位绅士的谦逊，而是出于某种令人尴尬的困境。
Everyone turns to look at APPLe, whose long silence now feels less like a gentleman's politeness and more like someone caught in an awkward situation.

APPLe：……窝这里还友最厚一个。
APPLe: ... I ... I has last wun.

缪斯三世：噢！那群黑色绒毛小家伙的语言。真有意思，这的确也称得上是一种符号系统。
Musae III: Interesting! He's speaking like one of those little black fuzzy creatures. I suppose Critter communication does qualify as some kind of symbolic system.

苹果表皮的色泽从未如此红润。它飞快地将转译器递给乌尔里希，细小的爪子与磁流体郑重交握。
The apple's skin has never looked so red. He quickly hands the translator to Ulrich.

APPLe：请尽筷把哎剖某从困镜中解酒出来吧，拜脱了，蓬友。
APPLe: Please, help this APPLe soon, my friend.

磁流体深深颤动了一下，这大概等同于人类的深呼吸，能在糟透了的状况中带来一点聊胜于无的自我宽慰。
The ferrofluid trembles deeply in its tank—probably the equivalent to a deep breath for a human—in an attempt to stay calm.

乌尔里希：好吧，转译器、“普罗透斯”、语言与认知紊乱……听起来和“暴雨”前的混乱没什么两样，不过至少我已经学会了怎么和棋子打交道。
Ulrich: Alright then. Translators, a "Proteus," language and cognitive disorders ... Sounds just like the chaos before the "Storm," but at least now I know how to deal with the pieces.

乌尔里希：我准备好了。
Ulrich: I'm ready.

哑谜：不，是我准备好了。
Enigma: No. I'M ready.

哑谜黑着脸上前一步。
Enigma steps forward, a dark look on his face.

哑谜：以防你不合时宜的人道主义精神再次复发，这次我来。
Enigma: Considering how humanitarianism seems to overtake you at the worst possible times, it's safer if I handle it this time.
